# p15 spiral-turn stable interleave of two contiguous runs (#3207)

**Decision:** reject the frozen, exploratory two-run whole-group stable interleave as a sufficient reading-order receiver for p15:20 through p55. It is perfectly invertible, but its original inverse has no native-like length-order lift and disrupts the observed seam suppression. No unknown plaintext or authentic key recovered. H-shuffle before a value cipher and H-decoy remain unresolved.

## Source and fixed operation

The established [p15 number-grid spiral](../results/lp2-p15-fibonacci-prime-spiral.md) begins at the original red 3299 and takes unit moves ESWWNNEEESSSWWW. The consecutive changes of direction give the binary string **11010100100100**, six bends and eight straight transitions. The [independent Fibonacci-word gate](lp2-p15-fibonacci-word-geometry-gate.md) already establishes these directions, but rejects the historical Fibonacci-word drawing correspondence; this proposal does not use that correspondence. The separate infinity mark motivates repeating the finite route. Its weakest premise is that bend vs straight selects two input runs and that the route advances once per printed group. No author instruction authenticates this typing, even though the routing bits and start are fixed independently of language scores.

Freeze the frame at the first black-body group p15 rune20 (global complete-group ordinal 801, zero-based), continuing through p55 inclusive, no resets. The source provides the observable frame of 2,095 complete groups, 9,333 runes. For mask m_i = bit[i mod 14], let a=sum(m)=899. The sender takes the next group from contiguous run P[0:a] at each bend, and from P[a:N] at each straight. Both runs are monotone, all groups conserved, and exactly a and N-a groups consumed. The receiver stably extracts bend slots then straight slots and concatenates them; its inverse is unique because N and the route are public. This is a nonuniform merge of two long contiguous runs, not a 14-column transpose, BWT, cycle-leader rank, per-group endpoint class or unconstrained word permutation. The sender is different from a two-queue variable delay network with interdependent writes. It does not preserve original word-boundary semantics under H-decoy.

## Exact original inverse and independent controls

The reproducer uses the pinned image-corrected transcription, asserts every physical page rune tape against canonical JSON, and excludes both p15 red headings. It decodes all 2,095 actual groups into two contiguous runs (899/1,196) and forward replay is exact. No cipher key, phase, lane order, receiver or group definition was fitted to original language scores. It is an order-only inverse; original groups remain ciphertext.

Use the existing 8-bin GP word-length transition LLR convention (1–7,8+; Laplace one) trained on the first 37,356 Emerson words. Seventeen independent contiguous literary length windows of matching size (six held-out Emerson, five Gibran, six Shakespeare) are exactly recovered after this route; all recover higher transition LLR than their independently permuted same-lane-count wrong-route rival and same-inventory fully shuffled rival. This calibration is length-only; Shakespeare's first window has a negative LLR even in correct order. Nine authentic native GP sections are separately encrypted with the known LP1 Atbash sender, then interleaved, and all native groups recover exactly with the declared inverse and known key. Wrong masks preserve lane sizes but recover only 79/740 native groups in place. These are planted implementation and discriminative diagnostics, not historical route evidence.

Its 64 restored equal seams are high relative to the same-capacity route null (upper-tail p=.006); the extracted runs contribute 32 each, and their joining seam is unequal. This is descriptive of the original receiver, not a calibrated family-wise probability or proof that every postcipher routing is impossible. The printed 17 equal seams are the suppression that a genuine postcipher inverse would need to explain; this candidate does not restore suppressed seams to the putative pretransport cipher.

No known-key transfer to unknown pages is asserted: the Atbash operation was a native calibration only. No rune language or key search follows from this failed order-only gate. Since no authentic typed selector or restored order was obtained, do not tune route origin, lane order, period, arbitrary mask or chapter resets. A new independently source-fixed route or genuine native crib would be needed to revisit. H-decoy and pre-encryption transport remain distinct alternatives.
