## Exact new consequence for the #2665 key-ranking instrument

The uniform-plaintext marginal likelihood that discriminates keyskip1 streams cannot discriminate M04 hold-key or spend-key repair, including fixed ciphertext-history-dependent T6 replacement. Let P_i be independent uniform GP29 and J_i the pre-emission cursor. For fixed deterministic key K and any history of plaintext, coins, emitted ciphertext and cursor, U_i=P_i+K[J_i] is uniform. A collision replacement kernel depending only on U_i and emitted ciphertext history therefore yields an output distribution independent of K, whether a repair holds or advances J_i. In particular for uniform-other repairs, conditional repeat probability is (1-q)/29 and each distinct symbol has probability (1+q/28)/29. The argument also holds for a deterministic affine target of emitted history with uniform fallback. The exact marginal C likelihood is consequently identical for **every fixed key** under this prior, even though language-dependent decoders can distinguish correct keys. Unlike keyskip1, no extra draw reuses the same plaintext rune; thus no adjacent-key-difference constraints arise. This is a mathematical identifiability boundary, not an LP2 mechanism exclusion.

Independent tiny exhaustive enumerator (alphabet5, six emissions, two distinct deterministic keys, hold/spend and uniform/history-target repairs) sums all paths into 15,625 distinct emitted sequences; all total masses within 4.7e-14 of 1 and maximum absolute between-key difference 4.34e-19. [Reproducer](../sims/lp2_m04_uniform_key_nonidentifiability_sft.cjs). No language-free candidate ranking extension of #2665 to M04/R4 is authorized by this calculation. Use a matched language model and independent source selection if that channel is revisited.
