The remaining premise is why the sourced `[3,27]` pair should act separately on the exact p28 center singleton and adjacent five-rune word. P28's geometry and literal DH line motivate that split, while the cross-object selectors remain post-discovery corroboration.

```text

            = [9,28,24,10,1,20]

These equalities motivate the candidate. They are not treated as independent discovery probabilities.

## Full pre-output transform family

The audit then charged the transform choices available before seeing `READ OUT`.

### Whole-object cube orbit

The cube map on nonzero GP29 values has the six-element exponent orbit:

`{1,3,9,27,25,19}`.

The predecessor/center rune and the five-rune word are allowed to choose these independently, giving **36** uniform exponent pairs. This upper-bounds cube versus inverse-cube direction and any number of whole-object cube iterations.

### Literal per-shell alternatives

The phrase "three nested layers" also admits a different natural implementation: apply one cube step per actual shell depth rather than applying the deepest exponent uniformly to every word rune.

The audit charges:

- boundary base cube or inverse cube;
- word base cube or inverse cube;
- outer→inner or inner→outer shell depth.

This contributes **8** per-shell transforms.

### Direction and boundary side

Every transform is charged in:

- forward and reversed output orientation;
- five-rune word with predecessor boundary;
- five-rune word with successor boundary.

Total family size is therefore **176 choices per five-rune printed word with both boundaries present**.

The winning uniform transform `u-3-27` is also exactly:

- cube the boundary/center rune;
- take the GP29 multiplicative reciprocal of each rune in the five-rune word, because `x^27=x^-1 mod29` for nonzero values.

Agent11 tested a source-simple post-discovery variant that chooses the boundary exponent from the same page's visible period count and always reciprocates the five-rune word. P28 has exactly three periods, so its rule is the winning `(3,27)` transform. Across all **314** complete five-rune predecessor objects in unknown LP2, this rule produces exactly one finite-register phrase: **READ OUT** on p28. Across the **nine** one-based page-numbered five-rune words, p28 is again the sole phrase hit.

This is a useful simplification and familywise selectivity result. It is not an independent discovery probability because the rule was formulated after READ OUT was known, and the three periods occur away from the selected line. Its main value is mechanistic: the exact transform need not be described as three uniform cube iterations on the word.
