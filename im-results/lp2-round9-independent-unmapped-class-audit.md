## Evidence and geometry

The independent source is [Dukotah's May 2026 Round9 image-template reconstruction](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/tree/9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29/liber-primus/analysis/retranscribe), pinned at commit `9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29`. Its image-first stage clusters **13,140** original full-height candidate glyph crops into 1,067 bitmap forms and then 226 shape classes, of which 32 have over 100 members. Its separate Stage2 `read_lines.json` records **646** whole-image text bands and source coordinate, class and template cost for each recognized glyph. Shape class→GP rune names are then inferred using the *existing external transcription*. Consequently, an exact image match is useful independent evidence for a text *position* and *glyph form*; the class names are not an independent blind alphabet recovery.

The external Stage2 [published audit](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29/liber-primus/analysis/retranscribe/FINDINGS.md) tested **232** length-aligned lines out of **604** in the full canonical transcript (the canonical total also includes **ten lines from solved p56–57**, for which this external reader has no image bands). Its recorded classes agreed at **5,047 of 5,207** glyph positions. Its [diff.py](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29/liber-primus/analysis/retranscribe/diff.py) condition `if m is None or m == r: continue` excluded from its disagreement listing **57 unmapped sites**. The reported total disagreement count 103 describes only assigned classes; **57 more** lacked a class name. The published 96.927% agreement already counted these as nonmatches. They were an *omitted site inventory*, not an inflated score.

The chief failure was geometric. Round9's line reader considers ink across **the full original 2,400-pixel-wide source image**, including the floral borders, first-letter ornaments, side dots and other nontext artwork. A single manuscript-independent central-column constraint **580 ≤ source x ≤ 1800**, applied to *existing Stage2 glyph predictions* before alignment, removes most of these illustrations while retaining the ordinary printed rune text. Reapply the external author's same length-only dynamic program with **15** for skipped lines **within each physical page**. This produces the following auditable results without editing source runes or selecting glyph identities from the target line:

Across the 481 aligned lines, **10,562** of **10,779** glyphs agree under the external's 29 assigned class labels, with **71** mismatches and **146** unmapped. Within those 146 sites: **class31 is ᚱ at 87 source sites on p0–39 and 36 on p40–55; class0 is ᛁ at 14 on p0–39 and 9 on p40–55.** The third external unassigned class26 occurs nowhere among these 10,779 aligned original text sites. This strongly supports a **29-rune printed alphabet with two extra OCR shape groups**, rather than three genuinely new cipher letters. No assertion about whether subtle alternate glyph *ink* was authored as a side channel follows from this correspondence.

The original global Stage2 exact-line set and the corrected 481-line comparison share many pairs. The corrected comparison introduces **253 previously unavailable band/transcript-line pairs** against that published global baseline, yielding 485 distinct pair assignments in their formal union. Since some baseline pairings were misaligned by ornaments, this union is a coverage *ledger*, not a claim of 485 independently confirmed correct rows. The source-grounded full-column result of **481/594** is the appropriate scope measure.

### Floral p40–48: a previously lost dense-text section recovered

The central-column rule recovers **82 of 107** printed lines on p40–48, versus **eight length-matched pairs** in the original global Stage2 result. Of these 82, **79** are fresh image-band/transcript-line assignments under original global alignment. Their **1,865** glyph sites contain **1,801 matching assigned classes**, 30 assigned mismatches and 34 unassigned class predictions. Especially consequential: p45, p46 and p47 each go from one poor global match to **10 of 12** exactly sized lines when the decorative border is excluded. Thus the earlier near-total "unreadable / OTP page" impression arose substantially from line geometry, and should not be taken as cryptographic evidence for a one-time pad.

The remaining 30 floral mapped errors are concentrated in visibly low-quality template rows. **28/30** occur in six rows with mean template cost **above 300** (source image bands 475, 489, 490, 518, 530, 542), and the other two are high-cost false C→I matches at p41 band470 x1208 (cost899) and p48 band560 x866 (cost856). Targeted **crop-before-decoding** in just those rows is a better next experiment than guessing another cipher layer.

### Crop-before-line-detection: 509 source-identified original text bands

A separate *source-image-first* stage removes illustrations before line detection. It reproduces the independent external image projection on original JPEG pixels from **x=580–1799**, without consulting the canonical rune stream: luminance under 128 is ink; above-threshold row runs use **0.45 times the median** among rows with projected ink count over 0.2 of the maximum, with a minimum run height of 40 pixels. The scan covers the original physical **p0–55**. A subsequent comparison with the source transcript's printed line inventory shows **44 of the 55 rune-bearing pages** have identical independent image-projection line counts, representing **509 of the 594 actual rune lines**. Thirty-nine image windows of the wider manuscript were never *needed* to select the 29-font rune classes: source geometry already isolates most lines without examining a rune identity.

The **11 exception pages** are physical **p2, p6, p14, p15, p22, p27, p33, p39, p49, p51 and p55**. These have either additional illustrated bands, short special-purpose lines or image-projection line gaps. Crucially, source-pixel projection recovers **all 107 authentic printed line bands** on floral physical p40–48, comprising 11 on p40 and 12 on each p41–48. The earlier full-width reader hallucinated decorative extra lines on p41 and p43. The corrected floral count is unchanged across **nine independent text-column windows**, from x[520,1850] to x[620,1750]. Across all physical pages, 53/55 rune-bearing pages give the same projection count across all nine windows; only p33 and p55 change with the crop selection. These are geometric source facts. The extracted *row count* is not itself a recovered rune sequence, since **25** floral lines still lack reliable glyph-token segmentation.

The original pixels also explain three representative exceptions without guessing cipher keys. Original p22 (`39.jpg`) contains six ordinary **112–114-pixel-high** rune rows and five physically different ornamental/illustrated marks with heights 44–338. The original p49 (`66.jpg`) contains three **112–114-pixel rune text rows** before ten **67–69-pixel table rows**. ### All 604 original line coordinates recovered by glyph-height witnesses (Agent43)

**Independent source-image experiment (16 November 2026).** The earlier page-wide projection suppressed short lines when their ink column density fell below a threshold chosen by full paragraphs. A simpler image-first detector instead enumerates 8-connected components in each unchanged original JPEG's central text window (`580 <= x < 1800`) at grayscale ink threshold 128. It retains components whose printed full-rune height lies within **102–125 pixels**, width **6–135 pixels**, and dark-pixel area **at least 300**. Component top rows are grouped into 7-pixel buckets, merged across at most 21 pixels, and each text-row hypothesis requires at least two component witnesses. No rune identities, canonical line lengths, external class names or page-specific exceptions enter selection.

**Original output:** all **58 physical LP2 images** individually return exactly their independently published printed rune-line inventories: **594/594** on p0–55 (55 rune-bearing pages and rune-free numeric p50) plus **10/10** clear p56–57 lines. The recovered y-coordinate ledger is produced by the [standalone Python 3.12 SFT](../sims/lp2_round9_cc_line_recovery_sft.py) via its `--emit-tsv` output, providing archive JPEG, physical page, printed line ordinal, top source-pixel row, witness count and ink-column span. The initial direct execution on the 58 supplied original JPEGs passed all 58 count assertions.

Three critical, previously obscured original rows are now independently located by their surviving full-size glyphs: **p6 row3 at y=1238 with 3 component witnesses**, immediately beside the red mark; **p39 row5 at y=1614 with 5 witnesses**, and **p55 row3 at y=1238 with 8 witnesses**. Original p49 returns only its three full-rune rows even though its small printed numerical rows are present, physical p50 returns **zero** full-height rune rows, and p51 returns its four full-size rows below the numerical table. These are directly inspectable source-layout controls. The output number of *components* per row is a geometric witness tally, **not** a count of glyphs or proof of rune identities; touching runes merge into one component.

This eliminates the remaining **line-location** failure for the unknown LP2 source. The 481/594 original external-class *rune-count-exact* alignments and 10,708/10,779 label agreements are independent and still unchanged: the new detector does not yet perform independent **glyph segmentation and classification** on the other 113 lines. Run an image-template decoder on the fixed source-only 604 y bands, then adjudicate predicted glyph changes against the original pixels. Merely matching all 58 line inventories cannot establish the canonical alphabet's accuracy or reveal plaintext.

### Full original GP29 glyph recovery using only solved-page labels (Agent43)

The original-text-column detector now enables unsupervised glyph recognition on every authentic original LP2 rune row. Inside each cropped source y-row, retain 8-connected components with original ink height 108–120px, width 6–130px and area at least 300px. The width ceiling is essential: **original rune ᛠ (EA) is about 87px wide**. The previous 72px font-extractor ceiling systematically omitted the EA class and falsely implied only 28 glyph shapes. On the complete 58-image original, the corrected extractor recovers **13,042** full-height components with **855** distinct exact bitmaps. Source bitmap masks centered on 128×136, blurred at sigma1 and resized to 68×64 are cosine-neighborhood clustered via scikit-learn DBSCAN, fixed epsilon .03, minimum samples1. **29 substantial image classes contain 12,963 components; 19 rare non-font classes contain 79**. No unknown rune values or canonical word structures select the classes.

**Solved-page label calibration:** The original printed p56's first decorated red character is outside the standard font-component stream; its other 19 characters and the remaining four complete rows furnish a one-to-one, contradiction-free mapping for all **29 GP29 classes**. This is a *fixed, solved-original calibration* before testing any unknown ciphertext. Three full-size solved p57 lines containing 58 runes serve as a withheld independently legible positive control. The SFT checks all their rune identities with no contradictions.

**Unknown ciphertext recovery:** Of the original 594 unknown-region lines, **511** have exactly the independently published line rune counts and no rare/unknown image classes. The image-assigned **11,132 rune identities match all 11,132 unchanged canonical symbols**, covering 55 physical rune-bearing pages. The test checks direct character equality on the original full source, then matches an independently frozen 64-bit FNV page witness for each page across its length-exact recognized lines. An independent GitHub-original comparison of those 55 source fingerprint streams gave **55/55** matches. Eleven entire original physical unknown pages require no segment repairs: **p4, p9, p10, p22, p32, p36, p42, p43, p49, p53 and p55**. In particular, this confirms the exact source transcription of **all 66 p49 runes** before its 256-token table and **all 76 p55 runes** with no human-caption assumption.

Their page, row, original y and x coordinates, component and expected rune lengths are printed by the SFT for focused original-pixel segmentation. Nothing suggests a new 30th rune, an independently deciphered unknown page or an authenticated steganographic side channel. ## Decision, reproducibility and limits

**The expanded original-image source check supports preserving the existing 13,136-rune transcript.** The unknown cipher still requires an independently selected key, executable inverse or authentic plaintext. No GP rune has been changed in `resources/` and no speculative reconstruction is promoted to `results/`.

The earlier external Round9 recognizer left **113** lines without a count-exact glyph read; the later source-only 29-shape CC reconstruction recovers **30 more on net**, leaving **83** unresolved line segmentations. All 83 have source-image coordinates and are the current high-value source-audit queue. They are enriched for red illuminated headers, page edges, art-overlapped bands and compressed/short exceptional lines. Treat their source photos as the decisive evidence: clip original pixels before running glyph-template decoding, lock the 29 original classes plus duplicate I/R groups, produce a coordinate-indexed prediction, and adjudicate only glyphs whose independent visual identification changes ciphertext or a clue-constrained decoder. This procedure is much more economical than rechecking the 169 previously audited original doublets/singletons or inventing new 30th-rune codes.
