## Evidence and geometry

The independent source is [Dukotah's May 2026 Round9 image-template reconstruction](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/tree/9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29/liber-primus/analysis/retranscribe), pinned at commit `9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29`. Its image-first stage clusters **13,140** original full-height candidate glyph crops into 1,067 bitmap forms and then 226 shape classes, of which 32 have over 100 members. Its separate Stage2 `read_lines.json` records **646** whole-image text bands and source coordinate, class and template cost for each recognized glyph. Shape class→GP rune names are then inferred using the *existing external transcription*. Consequently, an exact image match is useful independent evidence for a text *position* and *glyph form*; the class names are not an independent blind alphabet recovery.

The external Stage2 [published audit](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29/liber-primus/analysis/retranscribe/FINDINGS.md) tested **232** length-aligned lines out of **604** in the full canonical transcript (the canonical total also includes **ten lines from solved p56–57**, for which this external reader has no image bands). Its recorded classes agreed at **5,047 of 5,207** glyph positions. Its [diff.py](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29/liber-primus/analysis/retranscribe/diff.py) condition `if m is None or m == r: continue` excluded from its disagreement listing **57 unmapped sites**. The reported total disagreement count 103 describes only assigned classes; **57 more** lacked a class name. The published 96.927% agreement already counted these as nonmatches. They were an *omitted site inventory*, not an inflated score.

The chief failure was geometric. Round9's line reader considers ink across **the full original 2,400-pixel-wide source image**, including the floral borders, first-letter ornaments, side dots and other nontext artwork. A single manuscript-independent central-column constraint **580 ≤ source x ≤ 1800**, applied to *existing Stage2 glyph predictions* before alignment, removes most of these illustrations while retaining the ordinary printed rune text. Reapply the external author's same length-only dynamic program with **15** for skipped lines **within each physical page**. This produces the following auditable results without editing source runes or selecting glyph identities from the target line:

Within those 146 sites: **class31 is ᚱ at 87 source sites on p0–39 and 36 on p40–55; class0 is ᛁ at 14 on p0–39 and 9 on p40–55.** The third external unassigned class26 occurs nowhere among these 10,779 aligned original text sites. ### All 604 original line coordinates recovered by glyph-height witnesses (Agent43)

**Independent source-image experiment (16 November 2026).** The earlier page-wide projection suppressed short lines when their ink column density fell below a threshold chosen by full paragraphs. A simpler image-first detector instead enumerates 8-connected components in each unchanged original JPEG's central text window (`580 <= x < 1800`) at grayscale ink threshold 128. It retains components whose printed full-rune height lies within **102–125 pixels**, width **6–135 pixels**, and dark-pixel area **at least 300**. Component top rows are grouped into 7-pixel buckets, merged across at most 21 pixels, and each text-row hypothesis requires at least two component witnesses. No rune identities, canonical line lengths, external class names or page-specific exceptions enter selection.

**Original output:** all **58 physical LP2 images** individually return exactly their independently published printed rune-line inventories: **594/594** on p0–55 (55 rune-bearing pages and rune-free numeric p50) plus **10/10** clear p56–57 lines. The recovered y-coordinate ledger is produced by the [standalone Python 3.12 SFT](../sims/lp2_round9_cc_line_recovery_sft.py) via its `--emit-tsv` output, providing archive JPEG, physical page, printed line ordinal, top source-pixel row, witness count and ink-column span. The initial direct execution on the 58 supplied original JPEGs passed all 58 count assertions.

Three critical, previously obscured original rows are now independently located by their surviving full-size glyphs: **p6 row3 at y=1238 with 3 component witnesses**, immediately beside the red mark; **p39 row5 at y=1614 with 5 witnesses**, and **p55 row3 at y=1238 with 8 witnesses**. Original p49 returns only its three full-rune rows even though its small printed numerical rows are present, physical p50 returns **zero** full-height rune rows, and p51 returns its four full-size rows below the numerical table. These are directly inspectable source-layout controls. The output number of *components* per row is a geometric witness tally, **not** a count of glyphs or proof of rune identities; touching runes merge into one component.

This eliminates the remaining **line-location** failure for the unknown LP2 source. The 481/594 original external-class *rune-count-exact* alignments and 10,708/10,779 label agreements are independent and still unchanged: the new detector does not yet perform independent **glyph segmentation and classification** on the other 113 lines. Run an image-template decoder on the fixed source-only 604 y bands, then adjudicate predicted glyph changes against the original pixels. Merely matching all 58 line inventories cannot establish the canonical alphabet's accuracy or reveal plaintext.
