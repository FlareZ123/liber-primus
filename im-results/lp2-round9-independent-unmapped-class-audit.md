# Round 9 independent glyph-class audit: the omitted 57 exact-line sites

**Decision (16 November 2026, Agent8, director-task #1178).** The external Dukotah Round9 image-first reconstruction is a useful *partial* independent check of the canonical GP29 text. Its original Stage2 labels agree on **5,047 of 5,207 glyph positions** in **232/604** exact-count lines, but its published per-glyph disagreement list silently omits **57 actual matched-position sites** whose image-derived classes lack a canonical label. All 57 are now localized in the [coordinate manifest](lp2-round9-unmapped-source-sites.tsv). **55** are class31 aligned to an ordinary canonical R (ᚱ), and two lie in demonstrably failed high-cost read lines (class31→F on p45 and class0→D on p47). No verified changed rune or unknown plaintext follows. The 372 lines absent from this strict comparison remain unaudited by the external exact-count method.

## Why this is a consequential limitation

The external [FINDINGS.md](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29/liber-primus/analysis/retranscribe/FINDINGS.md) reported 103 localized differences: 15 leading-edge artifacts, three high-cost C→I misreads and 85 errors on four failed lines p45–47. The actual [diff.py](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29/liber-primus/analysis/retranscribe/diff.py) contains the condition `if m is None or m == r: continue`; an unassigned class therefore vanishes from its disagreement report. Its [diff_report.json](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29/liber-primus/analysis/retranscribe/diff_report.json) assigns **29 of 32** image shape classes to GP29 indices and leaves classes **0, 26, 31** without a value. It correctly counts the 57 as *nonmatches* in its published agreement denominator; the omission affects *site coverage and interpretation*, rather than the 96.927% headline statistic.

We independently replayed the external line-length dynamic program, with the same **15** skip cost and exact line filter, against this repository's unchanged `resources/liber-primus/lp2-transcription.txt`. Counts are **646 source image bands**, **604 original rune lines**, **232** exact-count aligned lines and **5,207** glyph positions. Of these, **5,047** have assigned matching classes, **103** assigned mismatch and **57** unassigned. Unassigned class31 is strongly identified with **R** on 55 count-exact source sites. The omitted sites are given as physical LP2 page, original JPEG number, external image band, global transcription line, zero-based within-line rune index, original x coordinate, pixel-template cost and canonical rune. The other **two** unmatched unassigned class sites are embedded in the four globally failed p45–47 lines and should not be promoted to letters.

The external reconstruction's own dependency and status are important: its [templates.py](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29/liber-primus/analysis/retranscribe/templates.py) clusters **13,140** full-height glyph crops into **1,067** bitmap variants; it retains **32** high-frequency classes among **226** agglomerative classes at a 0.10 difference threshold. In `diff.py`, class→rune naming then uses the *canonical transcript*, so the glyph segmentation is image-first while alphabet naming is a constrained alignment to a source transcript. No GP alphabet expansion follows merely from having 32 printed-shape classes.

## Targeted original-ink adjudications

- **Repeated R silhouettes:** Original `34.jpg`, physical p17, has primary image class15 at approximately **(x1043, y861)** and class31 at approximately **(x1605, y861)**; a second class31 example lies near **(x1620, y673)**. Binary original-ink glyph crops at threshold 128 give **1,991** dark pixels for the class15 example and **1,983** for the class31 example, differing by **68** pixels in a nominal 37×114 comparison (IoU ~**0.9664**). Two examples within each class are pixel-identical at this threshold. Both display the same legible ᚱ skeleton. The minor branch-edge differences could arise from font/rendering phase or authored alternate forms; this inspection cannot identify a steganographic binary channel. Across 646 source bands, class15 occurs **281** times and class31 **140** times, but the 232 count-exact lines locate 55 class31 sites specifically at ordinary R in the source text. Preserve class identity as potential evidence, without declaring a 30th rune.
- **External p29, p34, p35 C→I claims:** Original p29 source `46.jpg`, count-exact band 336, index9, x1111, external cost **955** corresponds to the printed thin ᛁ near punctuation, supporting the canonical I rather than its external template C. The external analysis's reference to p29/p34/p35 as “cryptographically solved” is inaccurate for LP2; these are unknown physical pages. Their image/template failures should be judged from ink, not from nonexistent known plaintext.
- **Exactly two word-boundary/punctuation differences:** The two repository transcript lineages have *identical raw rune identities in identical order*, **13,136/13,136** across LP2 p0–57 including solved pages. They differ as plain text only at the dot/space separators before rune indices **1,886** (physical p8, archive `25.jpg`, first printed line `…ᚩᛒ.ᛡᚾ…`) and **9,648** (physical p40, archive `57.jpg`, first printed line `…ᚫᚻ.ᚪᛈ…`). Our canonical source uses `.` at both; external `krisyotam_runes.txt` uses `-`. Both printed originals show a clear square dot at the boundary. Preserve the original-image **dot**, because a period can mark a meaningful reset/instruction boundary even though the GP rune stream is unchanged.

## Cryptanalytic decision and follow-up

No new authenticated LP2 plaintext, key or reversible original-cipher operation was recovered by this limited image audit. **Do not replace the canonical rune data from external Stage2 predictions.** The full text and 32 cross-page carried words must be retained. The next decisive independent image test is to compare the **372 lines omitted by the external exact-count filter**, especially the dense p40–48 text, with connected-component-free, forced-count glyph segmentation and a *locked* class dictionary. Report each discrepancy as original JPEG, x/y, image glyph class, canonical GP rune, original pixel adjudication, and effect on a previously constrained decoder. The R class15/class31 split should be treated as a predeclared potential source channel, with an independent test distinguishing fractional-pixel font placement from a genuine creator-selected binary symbol.

The file `resources/liber-primus/lp2-transcription.txt` remains unchanged; the [57-row evidence manifest](lp2-round9-unmapped-source-sites.tsv) preserves the only new external-audit loci. This is an evidence-scope correction and candidate source-layer refinement, **not a decryption**.
