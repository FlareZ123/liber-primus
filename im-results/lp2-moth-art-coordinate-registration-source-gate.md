# Original moth artwork does not select a native rune-coordinate overlay

**Decision (15 November 2026, Director #1056).** The original moth on LP1 p05 is the same source drawing used on LP2 physical p53 and p55, but a single artwork-registered similarity transform **cannot** also register the ordinary text or original LP1 5×5 square. The actual reuse is translation/scaling without an observable flip or nonzero rotation. The repeated picture remains an authentic visual motif; these exact source images supply **no unique rune-index traversal, secret operand, value-changing cipher inverse or unknown plaintext**. This retires the literal art-alignment/overlay route, without excluding an instruction independently connected to the moth elsewhere.

**Prior-work qualification.** The repeated p53→p55 logo, its **(+66,+67) displacement**, its near-identical original-pixel appearance, and its pre-May-2014 identity as Cicada 3301's public logo were **already established** in [the terminal moth reuse and period source study](tree-outline-blur-crosspage.md#cicada-emblem-original-p53p55-artwork-and-a-pre-lp2-period-reproduction) (including [Director #354](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/354)). The present 66/67 and pixel results independently reproduce and extend **that existing image control** to the all-16-page stationary floral margins; they are not new source evidence for a cryptographic cipher. The **new, bounded decision for #1056** is the p05→p53/p55 2.33× artwork fit, the invariant 114px GP glyph height, and the p05 5×5 square projecting off-page, which specifically tests and rejects the proposed cross-release original art-to-rune coordinate overlay.

Original JPEGs in `resources/liber-primus/`: `05.jpg` (LP1 p05, SHA-256 `2db94921ae5216d8a589890495fa5432446cd3227c45fbeb4934cbb0525112f7`), `70.jpg` (LP2 p53, `4ec7293ba72bd52384787646d204c8a68fefaf2d22673c40d27f23812d424548`) and `72.jpg` (LP2 p55, `a80191592add93a86ed3868f9e4e6ba3944ec20c9ffccc7d3e221b461344beba`). All are 2400×3600 source pixels. This experiment neither modifies the originals nor constructs a rune reading from a fitting score.

`sims/lp2_moth_registration_source_sft.py` is a self-contained source test (Python with NumPy and OpenCV). It runs SIFT feature extraction on predeclared moth/ornament regions, 0.68 Lowe ratio, a fixed-seed 3px RANSAC partial-affine registration, converts local fits to global source coordinates, then independently checks source text height and the location of the LP1 square. All three moth pairs strongly favor the same orientation, with no observed 90°/180° rotation or reflection. The **moth alone** is reused at source-raster scale but moved 66 pixels right and 67 pixels down relative to LP2 p53. The **flanking floral vines stay in place**. Independent original-image side-region SIFT registration gives exact identity on the left (356/356 matches) and effectively identity on the right (366/366); the 1,551,200 original decoded grayscale pixels in the left-vine sample are *all identical* between p53 and p55, while the right-region 1,606,600 pixels have mean absolute difference 0.00644, with only a small nearby text-edge contribution. This means two original artwork layers have different spatial behavior and prevents treating the complete floral frame as a translated unit. The first rune lines in **all three untransformed pages** occupy exact original y673–786, height **114px**, showing normal text layout did not undergo the artwork scale. A moth-aligned LP1 114px letter would become approximately **266px** high, 2.33 times the actual LP2 letter height, and cannot coincide with ordinary rune glyph slots.

The first physical row of the five-by-five number/rune table *below* the LP1 p05 moth occupies original y2224–2314. Its earliest row, projected using the independently registered moth frame, begins at original LP2 y≈**4153** for p53 and y≈**4220** for p55. Those coordinates are below the 3600px image bounds. Thus the author-visible moth does not supply a direct transparent-overlay alignment of the LP1 printed square onto p53/p55 runes.

## Source-raster difference controls across the floral chapter

The reused artwork was also compared directly at the ink level on the **unchanged original source scans**, rather than interpreting SIFT correspondences alone. For each of the 16 floral-chapter scans `57.jpg`–`72.jpg` (LP2 p40–p55), the left-border rectangle `x=0..559, y=450..3249` is **pixel-for-pixel identical** to p53's decoded 8-bit grayscale rectangle, across 1,568,000 pixels per scan. The right-border rectangle `x=1820..2399, y=450..3249` is also exact across the first 15 scans; p55 alone has a minor near-text difference. In the narrower 1,551,200-pixel left and 1,606,600-pixel right p53↔p55 comparison, left is fully identical and right mean absolute difference is 0.00644. These repeated margins contain no changing high-contrast drawing that could provide a page-varying visual cipher by direct overlay.

For the source p53 moth `x=600..1709,y=2740..3149`, compare p55 at exactly `(+66,+67)`. Threshold at source-grayscale intensity `<120`: the original moth regions have **70,064** and **70,040** dark pixels, with only **464** XOR pixels across the 455,100-pixel crop. A distance-transform test finds **zero** original dark pixels more than three pixels away from the aligned partner's dark ink, in either direction. The differences are consistent with raster/JPEG edge variation; no separate high-contrast stroke, marked wing cell or additive dark dot emerges. This does **not** exclude a deliberately subpixel/LSB steganographic payload, for which no human-visible instruction or source codec has been identified.

The updated SFT reproduces these controls and retains the independently sourced original-image hashes. The numbers support one source graphic moved against a static frame, without a selected cryptographic consumer for its displacement.

## Cryptanalytic consequence and scope

A human solver could notice the repeated drawing, and the **independent 66/67 moth displacement relative to fixed vines**, and reasonably attempt placing the LP1 sheet on top of LP2. This displacement is a measurable image-editing difference; no source text selects 66/67 as a rune address, key, clock or transposition. That operation has a uniquely measurable art fit, but breaks the source's shared rune grid and moves the only nearby LP1 numerical 5×5 structure off-page. A different mapping would require manually discarding the art scale or inventing a fresh affine transform, traversal, flip, displacement or secret that the visible source does not identify. A positional permutation alone would also leave uniformly distributed GP29 ciphertext values unchanged and supplies no value-changing inverse.

This is a **bounded geometric exclusion**, not a proof that the creators intended only decoration. The former p55 seven-word moth crib was already rejected under its source-conditioned null by #1024, and its assumptions are not reused here. Genuine next progress would require a *separate original instruction* naming a wing/center coordinate, an extraction rule and a reversible cipher/key consumer that produces coherent unknown-page plaintext on withheld source runes. Do not expand into arbitrary fit-selected image rotations or keys on the strength of the reused moth alone.
