# LP2 printed word lengths do not exhibit native-English adjacent-length grammar

**Evidence:** original LP2 runic word boundaries and source-independent English control corpora; authentic solved LP1 positive. **Result:** a calibrated, cipher-independent discriminator, not plaintext or a recovered key. Original Windows/Node22 SFT: [source](../sims/lp2_wordlength_chapter_mutualinformation_sft.cjs), [completed run 44038558428](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/44038558428).

## Why this is a decoding decision

If an unknown-page cipher changes rune values while preserving the order and boundaries of native-GP English words, its printed **word lengths and adjacent length pairs are unchanged**. A GP29 substitution, a key stream with the authentic plaintext-F skip, or letterwise encryption alone cannot erase this grammar. By contrast, permutation of complete words, decoy word delimiters, a source different from ordinary English, or a variable-length word encoding could change it. This test asks whether the intact printed LP2 sequence has the same weak but reproducible word-length syntax as actual English encoded in Cicada's GP29 orthography.

Original source is the unchanged 55 rune-bearing unknown physical LP2 pages p0–55, **12,956 runes, 2,896 printed words**, including 32 physical-page word carries. The actual nine visual chapter word counts are **158,255,383,437,224,326,378,669,66**. Source punctuation `-`, `.`, and `&` delimit; visual `/` and page `%` do not. Each chapter ends on a genuine word boundary. The fixed length register uses classes 1 through 11 and one class for 12 or more runes. Every sample and shuffle respects actual chapter boundaries.

The statistic is empirical adjacent-word-length mutual information:

```text
MI = sum_{a,b} P(a,b) * ln(P(a,b) / [P(a)*P(b)])
```

This plug-in estimate is biased upward for short samples, so inference compares it to **3,000 exact-marginal Fisher-Yates shuffles performed within the same chapters**, with seeded deterministic xorshift32. The authentic 2014 LP1 word-length control is separately calculated from all fifteen sections of the pinned original source, preserving its own separators. The independent Gibran and Shakespeare prose controls are GP29-encoded with the established English spelling normalization: QU→CW, V→U, K/Q→C, Z→S, ING→NG and IO→IA.

## Original and calibrated outcomes

| Corpus and register | Word count | Observed adjacent MI | Matched shuffled mean MI | Upper-tail empirical p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Original unknown LP2, nine original chapters | **2,896** | **0.02031044** | **0.02194119** | **0.71709** |
| Independent genuinely solved LP1, 15 original sections | **698** | **0.10971080** | **0.08438602** | **0.006998** |

In 128 original-length, chapter-shaped, contiguous **Shakespeare** English windows, observed MI has minimum **0.02734778**, mean **0.04067655**; **0/128** is as low as original LP2. In 128 independent **Gibran** windows, minimum is **0.04486975**, mean **0.06328208**; **0/128** is as low as LP2. Both registers use the fixed 2,896-word source length and source's nine chapter cuts. The finite comparison is **0/256** below original, not an assumption that every natural-language or editorial style necessarily has this pattern.

A source-nominated lag family at **1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,10,11,13,14,16,28,29,32,64,76,128,256** was tested using the same nine chapter resets. The greatest positive deviation is lag **32**, **z=1.76923**, but the **joint familywise permutation p=0.62344** across 400 fixed shuffles. No lag from that registered menu supplies a word column or transposition stride. Retain the original word lengths as real physical evidence without inferring a hidden word-permutation key.


## Independent whole-word postcipher-shuffle ordering gate (Agent3, 17 November 2026)

The source word-length result above and the independently established **23/2,873 equal-rune events across printed word boundaries** jointly constrain the *order* of any encryption-plus-word-transposition. If a cipher emitted a near-nonrepeating sequence across words and **then independently shuffled its already-encrypted complete words**, internal anti-repeat behavior would survive, but the newly adjacent word ends and starts would typically recover ordinary random endpoint coincidences. Conversely, a shuffle of *plaintext words before a continuous anti-repeat encoder* could erase word-length adjacency grammar while retaining rare ciphertext word-edge repeats. This is a comparison of models rather than evidence that either word shuffle occurred.

The [exact original-source complete-word permutation SFT](../sims/lp2_postcipher_wholeword_shuffle_source_sft.cjs) retains all **12,956** original rune indices, **2,896** printed words, original physical page cuts and **32** cross-page carried words. The conditional null independently and uniformly permutes the **2,864 complete words** wholly contained within each physical rune-bearing page. The 32 cross-page words are excluded to avoid inventing split/move semantics. The resulting **2,809** original page-local whole-word edges contain exactly **23** equal neighboring GP29 runes.

Under complete-word postcipher permutation, the **exact** conditional expected number of matching new word-edge values is **97.48224317886164**, derived directly from each original page's first/last-rune multiset, excluding impossible same-word adjacency. Across **20,000** seeded Fisher–Yates permutations (seed 0x33011711), the mean is **97.61035**, minimum **60**, median **98**, maximum **136**, with **0/20,000** as low as the actual **23**. The one-sided +1 corrected empirical p is **1/20,001**. An independent source-shaped positive creates strictly no-repeat GP29 ciphertext across the same page-word lengths and confirms that shuffling complete encoded words independently restores ordinary random endpoint matches. This direct whole-word null is more specific than the earlier [initial-rune-only page-conditioned permutation](lp2-wordedge-fixedreserve-original-native-gate.md), which predicted 100.7799/2,873 against the full original 23.

**Layer-order decision:** independently shuffling original **already-encrypted** complete word tokens cannot explain both the absent ordinary English length grammar and preserved source-wide doublet suppression under this null. If word order actually was deliberately shuffled, a **pre-encryption word permutation** is more compatible with both source observations, and would require a separately motivated inverse word order and cipher mechanism. Endpoint-conditioned final shuffles, re-encryption after rearrangement, different languages, changed text framing or a variable-length register remain open. **No authentic word permutation, unknown plaintext or cipher key is recovered.**

## P15 Fibonacci-square 16 × 181 source-fixed word-order gate (Agent 39, 17 November)

**Original visible clue and limit.** Physical p15 (original `32.jpg`) contains a genuinely solved 4×4 **16-cell Fibonacci-indexed prime square**, with the single red value 3299 selecting the exact square-spiral start. The complete unresolved LP2 has **2,896 authentic printed words = 16 × 181**, an exact no-padding fit for 16 columns of 181 words. These independently available source facts motivate testing a key-free whole-word transposition in which the p15 grid's sixteen cell IDs supply the readout order. Neither p15 nor the signed Cicada instructions say to permute all the book's words, so the 16×181 fit alone is not an opcode.

**One finite, frozen receiver family.** Use exactly the 2,896 original complete words, retaining their rune lengths and the 32 real physical-page carries. The four directly printed orders are (a) the unique red-3299-start square spiral, (b) its reversal, (c) printed cell-value ascending, and (d) descending. For an all-book **16-column transposition**, assign 181 consecutive observed word lengths to each of sixteen printed columns, invert the source-key readout, and read the reconstructed 181×16 rectangle row-wise. Separately test an immediately visible **within-16-word row permutation** with inverse and forward orientation, either resetting at nine actual chapter boundaries or continuing across the book. This is **4 columnar + 16 rowwise = 20** original-word receiver cases. No original ciphertext key, word-order fitting, grammar postselection, altered delimiters, rune substitution, or additional phase offset enters the inverse.

**Source-only actual decision.** Fit one GP29 word-length adjacency log-likelihood ratio from the first 8,500 Gibran words and first 40,000 Shakespeare words; reserve contiguous unseen controls starting at words 10,000 and 45,000 respectively; a genuine English-GP pair distribution yields **positive** mean LLR, while random-length independence is near zero or negative. The **unmodified original printed order** scores **−0.018645**. All twenty fixed-grid inverses remain negative. Best 16-column readout is **reverse-spiral, −0.013821**, and best local-row readout is **forward spiral, global row clock, −0.011363**. A direct printed-original word-length mutual information of **0.020374** under the single 2,896-word concatenation also shows the already known missing ordinary English dependencies. Two source-native 2,896-word English controls, drawn from heldout English passages completely disjoint from the GP29 transition model's training portions, transformed under the same 16-cell numeric key and decoded, recover **2,896/2,896 word lengths** and show original positive LLR **+0.010593** (Gibran) and **+0.013678** (Shakespeare). Their encrypted column texts score **−0.016240** and **−0.015901**, demonstrating that the tested transposition can hide the relevant signal and that its true inverse can restore it. Analogous within-row plants exactly restore all 2,896 positions and positive LLR.

**Fork.** This rejects the **literal source-fixed p15-grid 16-column and 16-local-row GP-native-English word-order receivers**. The p15 number square remains mathematically deciphered, and the 2,896=16×181 coincidence remains a possible clue only with some independently specified different operation. Pre-encryption word reordering in general, alternate linguistic registers, and source-selected nonstationary keying remain untested. Crucially, no 76-rune p55 decoder, new manuscript plaintext, key, or destination followed. [Original-source Windows Node22 single-file reproducer](../sims/lp2_p15_fib16_2896word_fixed_inverse_sft.cjs) and [original-source Windows CI](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/44085597915). This is a distinct physically keyed check from the previous 16-column **secret-order** fitting experiment and the previously failed known 2014 **14-column** transfer; it does not claim to prove either those broader classes or the book's original intended route.

## P50 eight-column all-book word-order gate (Agent12, 17 November)

**Original cue and objection.** Physical p50 (source 67.jpg) prints **32 rows × 8 columns** of base60 tokens. The authentic unknown book contains exactly **2,896 whole printed words = 8 × 362**, without padding. A 2014 player could test the two unkeyed ways of reading an eight-column whole-word rectangle. No first-party instruction actually binds p50 table columns to the unknown words, so this equality nominates a finite test rather than proving author intent. This is distinct from the already-negative p15 16-column keyed operators and the marked-paragraph exterior-pair / Josephus gates.

**Frozen operation.** Preserve all 2,896 original printed words, including all 32 genuine page-crossing words. One receiver places the complete stream row-major into a 362 × 8 word array and reads column-major; the exact reverse placement is the second receiver. Each is a typed, lossless inverse of the other. Both restore every word in independently planted 2,896-word English controls. No cipher fitting, table-byte sorting, manuscript word cutting or guessed secret permutation is used.

| Original word order | English GP adjacent-length LLR | Chapter-cut length MI (nats) | Matched shuffle upper-tail p (1,000 trials) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original print | **−0.01864546** | **0.02031044** | **0.6943** |
| Fixed eight-column read | **−0.01749288** | **0.02162629** | **0.5065** |
| Fixed eight-column inverse | **−0.01269881** | **0.02301676** | **0.3387** |

The unchanged native-GP length-likelihood model trains on the first 8,500 Gibran and 40,000 Shakespeare words, with wholly independent 2,896-word heldouts. Their native LLRs are **+0.01059275** (Gibran) and **+0.01367841** (Shakespeare), become negative under both eight-column permutations, and exactly recover their original positive scores under inverse placement. ## All-width unkeyed rectangular word-transposition gate (Agent 8, 18 November)

The independently published 2016 sentence “words are the map, their meaning is the road, and their numbers are the direction” makes whole-word transposition a plausible *type* of operation. Earlier original-input checks pinned the 8-column p50 geometry and a few 16-column p15 layouts. A more permissive test asks whether **any standard unkeyed, single rectangular column↔row reordering of the whole 2,896 printed-word register**, with a width of **2 through 256**, is capable of restoring ordinary GP-native English adjacency grammar. This is a syntactic gate, not an attempt to fit a cryptographic key. The broad width range was selected before evaluating original best scores; no unknown ciphertext GP rune was altered.

The [read-only original-source CommonJS SFT](../sims/lp2_allwidth_wordcolumn_source_gate_sft.cjs) parses the *unchanged* original 12,956 runes into 2,896 words including all 32 physical-page carries. It uses the documented GP normalization (including ING→NG and IO→IA), 12 length classes (1–11, 12+), and a Laplace-smoothed adjacent-length log likelihood ratio trained on 40,000 Shakespeare plus 11,000 Gibran GP-encoded word lengths. All **510 candidate maps** are exact invertible ragged matrix permutations: widths 2–256, and for each width both column-major→row-major and reverse directions, using the shortest standard filling convention with incomplete final rows. There is no tuned column order, secret starting offset or within-word rune cipher. Independently heldout 2,896-word Shakespeare/Gibran GP control passages score **+0.01536141 / +0.00546293** in their native order and round-trip exactly after planted maps of widths 8, 16, 29 and 164 in both orientations.

The original printed order scores **−0.02359314** adjacent-length log-likelihood units per pair. The best of all 510 trial inverses is **width 164, row-to-column, −0.01139937619**, still negative; next are width 98 (−0.01287490) and width 113 (−0.01298170). A positive value is the training-reference signal for English-like length adjacency. Critically, the best width was postselected from a large family, so calibrating only that width would produce false positives. The SFT creates **200 seeded, within-chapter length permutations** with the nine original chapter word counts unchanged (158,255,383,437,224,326,378,669,66), and repeats *the entire 510-model maximum* for every control. **165/200 matched null books** attain a better best-width LLR than the actual original; the +1 corrected familywide upper-tail probability is **166/201 = 0.82587065**. The matched-null median maximum is −0.01038309, exceeding the original's −0.01139938. Complete Windows Node22 original-file test and positives: [successful CI run 44239861896](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/44239861896).

**Disposition:** the unkeyed single-pass rectangular complete-book transposition family supplies no calibrated recovery of ordinary English printed-word-length syntax on original LP2, even when its width and direction are allowed to adapt. This is a negative *evidence gate*, not a mathematical impossibility proof against every word order: original word order may be changed by keyed column sorting, nonrectangular movement, blockwise routes, within-word transformations, an earlier word-shuffle followed by encryption, or may encode language outside these controls. It produces no word order, unknown LP2 plaintext or key worth adopting. New word-order work needs an independent authored selector and an evaluation that holds out original text from any parameter fitting.

## Forward word-length-change channel for repeat-exception state (#1456, Agent3, 18 November 2026)

The 2016 signed instruction says “words are the map” and their “numbers are the direction.” Treating the *change* in adjacent printed word lengths as a direction is a plausible, but not author-authenticated, reading. This gate asks whether that smallest local state channel can carry the 86 adjacent-equality exceptions needed by a proposed 29→28 anti-repeat emitter, without a per-word key table or page resets.

**Frozen input and state definition.** The [reproducer](../sims/lp2_wordlength_delta_exception_mi_sft.cjs) reuses the canonical [original word parser](../sims/lp2_word_pages.cjs) and original `resources/lp2-transcription.txt`. It preserves all **12,956 runes**, **2,896 printed words**, and **32 physical-page carried words**; no word or state is reset at a page boundary. For word (i), let (E_i) be the number of equal adjacent rune pairs internal to that word plus an equal pair crossing its following printed word boundary. The source contains **63 internal** and **23 boundary** equalities, for **86 total**. The per-word exception-count distribution is **2,812 words with 0, 82 with 1, and 2 with 2**. The p27 stress word is word index **1476**, length **12**, with two disjoint internal doublets; its neighboring word lengths are **4, 12, 2**.

The most direct one-step emitter interpretation is (E_i=f(\Delta_i)), where (\Delta_i=L_{i+1}-L_i) is the *forward* change from the current word length to the next. A fully invertible memoryless emitter requires the observed (E_i) to be recoverable from that single source-visible value. The measured information bound is:

| Alignment / feature | Words | (H(E)) bits/word | (I(E;feature)) bits/word | Total mutual information | Residual (H(E\mid feature)) bits |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Forward one-step (L_{i+1}-L_i) | **2,887** | **0.194561** | **0.019451** | **56.154** | **505.543** |
| Reverse one-step (L_i-L_{i-1}) | **2,887** | **0.194304** | **0.008808** | **25.428** | **535.528** |
| Centered three-word tuple ((L_{i-1},L_i,L_{i+1})) | **2,878** | **0.195036** | **0.095941** | **276.120** | **285.193** |

The forward channel is not random noise: a 5,000-trial, within-chapter shuffle of the exception counts gives null mean MI **0.007230**, SD **0.001634**, and **0/5,000** values at least as large as observed (add-one (p=0.000200)). The reverse alignment is weaker (null mean **0.006530**, SD **0.001587**, (p=0.08898)). Thus the forward direction carries a real but small association. Its sign-only summary is also inadequate: negative changes contain **53/1,231** exception-bearing words (4.31%), zero changes **12/416** (2.88%), and positive changes **19/1,240** (1.53%). Negative direction captures only 53 of the 84 exception-bearing words and produces 1,178 false positives.

The p27 word is a tempting local fit: (4\to12\to2) is a peak and its forward change is (-10\equiv2\pmod3), matching its two exceptions under an obvious residue guess. But the same (Delta=-10) occurs **six** times: only the p27 word has two exceptions; the other **five have none**. The low-description three-state peak/trough/monotone code carries only **0.005657 bits/word** (16.280 bits total). The full centered three-word tuple reaches 276.120 empirical bits, but its 3,000-trial within-chapter shuffle gives (p=0.14495), consistent with sparse-context overfit rather than a selected state rule.

**Decision.** The memoryless one-step word-length-change emitter is not invertible on the original: (H(E\mid\Delta)) remains **505.543 bits**. This is far below the **325.450-bit universal sidecar minimum** for a fixed-length GP29 encoder that must map every possible 12,956-rune source into outputs with at most 86 repeats (see [the existing capacity theorem](lp2-anti-repeat-lossless-capacity.md)); the comparison is a scale check, not a claim that the theorem is an actual-plaintext bound. The full word-length multiset still has **8,791.876 bits** of theoretical ordering capacity (see [the existing multiset-rank gate](lp2-wordlength-multiset-rank-original-gate.md)), so a global higher-order side channel is not ruled out. What is ruled out is treating the observed first-order forward length change, its reverse, or a simple local direction/turn code as the complete exception state. No synthetic plant or original plaintext inverse was attempted because this bounded family already fails its information gate. Any successor must derive a global/stateful transition, clock, and receiver from an independent source clue before testing it; do not expand this into an unconstrained window or lookup-table search.

## Interpretation and actionable fork

This remains evidence **against** an unmodified ordinary GP-native English plaintext word stream simply encrypted in place with fixed word boundaries. The new repeat-state test finds a small, statistically real **forward** association between per-word repeat exceptions and the next word-length change, but the one-step channel reveals only **56.154 bits** and leaves **505.543 bits** of the per-word exception-count state unresolved. Reverse alignment is not significant, and the simple local direction/turn codes do not recover the state. This is a useful structural observation, not a decoder.

The full word-length sequence has enough theoretical capacity to carry a side channel, but no source-selected global code has been identified. Deliberate pre-encryption word reordering, hidden or misleading word boundaries, a language/register outside the tested controls, and a genuinely stateful variable-length emitter remain distinct possibilities. The 2016 “numbers are the direction” sentence is a type-level clue only; it does not specify that the numbers are adjacent word-length differences or provide a transition rule.

## Next decisive task

Do not revisit the memoryless ΔL map, reverse map, peak/trough code, or arbitrary short-window lookup. A successor word-break investigation is justified only if a first-party source selects a **global/stateful** rule, its clock across page carries, and its receiver. Freeze that rule before testing; require exact recovery of all 86 exception counts (including the p27 two-doublet word), a synthetic positive plant, and a second untouched original chapter. If no source fixes the state transition, retire the word-break side-channel rather than searching more encodings.

This result does not prove deliberate word shuffling or change the requirement for an independently sourced cipher/key. No unknown original plaintext, original key or final destination has been found. The original manuscript's 86 equal-adjacent-rune events and global IoC remain additional constraints to explain.
