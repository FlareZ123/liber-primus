# LP2 p0-p2 three-coordinate Trifid: the historical Instar title fails a fixed cube reading

**Decision, Agent 10, 13 November 2026:** Sixteen explicitly specified reversals of original physical LP2 pp0-2 under a 27-cell ternary Trifid cipher produce no source-native English-GP signal. The best decoded 729-rune index of coincidence (IoC) is **0.03542034**, while the smallest IoC among all **46,975** length-729 GP windows of the local Gibran control is **0.05593994**. This is an exclusion for the finite, source-locked keys and clocks below. No new authentic plaintext, secret cube, full decryption, or destination has been obtained.

## Original clue and its weakest premise

Original LP2 physical **p0, p1, p2** contain **262 + 266 + 201 = 729 = 27 squared** rune symbols. The pages repeat two illustrations with three-barred crosses, and p2 (original image 19.jpg) has an apparent cicada-nymph side-view drawing at its end. These are source-visible reasons to consider a three-coordinate cipher, though the crosses may be ordinary Christian/Orthodox imagery and the art itself does not prescribe a method.

The already recovered historical **2013 761.MP3** track is titled **THE INSTAR EMERGENCE**. Encoding the title by the published Gematria Primus rune alphabet yields **17** rune tokens with exact GP-prime sum **761**. The matching audio filename is independent source evidence for this title's earlier significance; the claim that it is an LP2 encryption keyword remains an unverified inference. Its insect/emergence theme motivates this specific chapter without selecting a cube.

A classical **Trifid** cipher maps a 27-symbol alphabet to a **3×3×3** coordinate cube. It fractionates each source rune into three ternary digits, concatenates the first digits then the second then third digits within each chosen period, and regroups triples into output cube symbols. This is a length-preserving bijection for a fixed cube and period. A 27-symbol cube clashes with the original **29-symbol GP register**. To make the proposed reading executable, we **hypothetically exempt GP-F (index 0)** because the solved LP1/LP2 ciphers sometimes carry F literally, and **GP-EA (index 28)** because it completes the required two-symbol exception count. The EA selection has **no historical authorization**. This leaves **667** of the original 729 positions in the cube stream, with **34 F + 28 EA** unchanged and not advancing the fractionation clock. The exact 729-to-27² equality therefore no longer defines a full 27×27 payload after these exclusions.

The keyed cube is obtained by removing duplicate title runes in their authentic GP order and then appending all still-unused values from canonical GP indices 1 through 27. The comparison cube is the same GP index order without a keyword.

## Frozen original-source experiment

The [standalone CommonJS reproducer](../sims/lp2_p0_instar_trifid27_sft.cjs) reads original canonical GP indices, rather than converting a later textual conjecture. It tests **two cube orders × two fractionation periods (3 and 27) × two clock scopes (whole p0-p2 chapter or each physical page) × both Trifid directions**, totaling 16 concrete source-derived variants. All 16 outputs invert back to the exact original 729 published ciphertext runes.

All outputs remain well below even the lowest tested control window, and the title-keyed period-27 chapter-local inverse gives **IoC 0.03425210**. The title-keyed direct reading begins in GP transliteration as \`ECOJWXNFTHOGCTHOAESFBB...\`, without a recognizable original chapter opening. No candidate supplies a downstream plaintext/key consequence.

To check that a viable scheme would be detected, the SFT enciphers a **729-rune source-native GP window** under the historical-title cube using period 27 and the two exactly declared passthrough symbols. The same untouched inverse recovers **all 729** native plaintext runes exactly. This is a code-control and a finite original-input language gate, not evidence that the original ciphertext was constructed that way.

This particular experiment differs from [earlier all-secret *two-coordinate* fractionation](coordinate-fractionation-pair-capacity.md) and the [small-width *fixed-block* collision invariant](polygraphic-block-collision-invariant.md). Period 27 Trifid mixes **27 rune inputs per independently reset fractionation block**, so it does not inherit the latter's width-3 block-collision exclusion.
