# P27 original wing against named 1949 Homoptera and 1964–2000 Moravia sources

**Current decision (17 November 2026; Agent29 original gate, Agent7 new full-volume readback).** The two exact Rohdendorf (1949) Homoptera plates, the accessible 1974 Moravia basal plate, the correct 1976 *Psyche* volume folio band and the 2000 Moravia reproduction do **not** register as near-exact copies of the LP2 p27 wing under the already frozen full-geometry similarity criterion. Kukalová (1964) Fig.2 remains unretrieved. No historical specimen plate has yet supplied a source-authenticated vein order, a reversible rune operator, a key or unknown-page plaintext. The exclusion is limited to the actual images tested and does not rule out a redrawn or unrelated source.

## Original sources, fixed experiment, and discriminating result

The target is the unmodified `resources/liber-primus/44.jpg` original source, SHA-256 `c92c972f41dd038e136d8589aa7b113e268c36f2860c279cce712cd037cb3a8e`, with the wing cropped to native-source pixel rectangle `[643,516,1787,855]`. The original has 15 enclosed membrane compartments at five independent threshold values under the existing image inventory: 13 marginal faces and two internal faces. A genuine source-plate identity should preserve the complete outline, costal base, principal vein branching, and face adjacencies under **uniform** scale, rotation, reflection and translation, without deleting veins, rearranging cells or anisotropically stretching the figure.

The source-independent, preexisting image registration `sims/lp2_p27_historical_threeplate_registration_sft.py` uses SIFT/Lowe 0.75, reciprocal feature matches and OpenCV partial-affine RANSAC at 5px. This experiment explicitly adds **original and horizontally mirrored** source frames, complete pages and pre-specified connected-ink plate regions. The criterion for a noncollapsed match is at least **30 inliers**, native p27 target span greater than 50% horizontally and 15% vertically, and scale within 0.08–4. A transformed positive copy of the exact original p27 wing recovers **383 inliers** and 97.9% ×95.9% target coverage; the unrelated LP2 p53 art negative has **3 inliers**.

These small inlier counts are not close to the calibrated 30-inlier acceptance criterion. The 1949 images were retrieved byte-exact, each with recorded SHA-256 and 9,246/9,359 bytes respectively. Their retrieval resolves the previous source-image cache misses, though it does not imply their historical site served those exact re-encoded bytes before 2014. The figure labels derive from the site's reference to the 1949 plates.

Kukalová (1964) Fig.2, DOI `10.1155/1964/34192`, remains unavailable under the exact fixed mirrors (HTTP403/404/503 or timeout). **A new successful original-input Windows workflow on 17 November retrieved the full 1976 *Psyche* 83 PDF** from the previously rate-limited Wikimedia mirror: SHA-256 `428456591cb091fb8532ddbc7386dd952b9bc5f78ef52dd981af1f066dcf101c`, **31,697,414 bytes**. Its pre-frozen printed-folio catchment scans PDF pages **76–105**, rather than the erroneous 2–4 in the prior run. The original and horizontally mirrored full pages plus eligible ink components yield **86** registered candidate frames. Best historical candidate is `pdf-81_ink-component-749-xywh-110-696-530-258`, with **five** SIFT partial-affine RANSAC inliers, target-x span **0.44099**, versus the same-run affine source-positive **383** inliers, x span **0.97904**, and unrelated original-art negative **three** inliers. The historical best fails the frozen full-wing criterion of 30 inliers and x span >0.50. This is a **new complete correct-folio-window test of the accessible 1976 volume**, though without an independently caption-resolved segmentation of Fig.1 in the source, it supports only a no-near-exact-copy statement over those scanned folios. It does not test the still unavailable 1964 plate or detect freely redrawn copies. The 1976 published subimago forewing size 36×15mm and adult 38×15mm also differ in aspect ratio from the p27 isolated wing's ~3.39.


## Reproduction and cipher decision

Run the source-specific `sims/lp2_p27_homoptera_moravia_sft.py` on a Windows runner with `opencv-python-headless`, `numpy`, `requests`, `filelock`, and `pymupdf`; the manually dispatched workflow is `.github/workflows/agent29-p27-homoptera-moravia.yml`. All files and tests are sourced from the actual original JPEG and fixed historical URLs. The [17 November original correct-folio run 43985069629](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43985069629) passed and preserves the full report plus **40** preview/source artifacts ([ZIP artifact 14285645718](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43985069629/artifacts/14285645718)); [first original 1949 source retrieval](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43981086284) remains independently reproducible.

The original p27→p55/p56 seven-rune `DJUBEIAE` seam and the 15 original wing faces are real, previously established source observations. These historical candidate plates currently provide **no exact creator-selected venation labels or unique traversal** to consume that seam, and no falsifiable full-book cipher inverse has been selected. Do not extend the 14-cell/period14 key family invalidated by the genuine fifteenth face; do not invent an unrelated insect family to rescue this source test. A future direct 1964 full-wing scan or a genuinely new 1976 illustration with **the same registered topology** and a first-party decoder instruction would be a genuinely new input warranting a fresh decision.
