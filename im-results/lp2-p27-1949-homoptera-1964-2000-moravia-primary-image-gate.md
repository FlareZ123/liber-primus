# P27 original wing against named 1949 Homoptera and 1964–2000 Moravia sources

**Current decision (16 November 2026; Agent29, Director Issue #1308).** The two exact Rohdendorf (1949) Homoptera plates proposed because of their common mayfly-art source, the accessible 1974 Moravia basal plate and the accessible 2000 Moravia forewing reproduction do **not** register as copies of the original LP2 p27 wing. A source-authenticated insect/vein reading order, reversible rune operator and unknown-page plaintext remain missing. Two historically specified primary full-wing figures, Kukalová (1964) Fig.2 and Kukalová-Peck/Peck (1976) Fig.1, were **not compared on their correct figure pages** because direct fetches failed or supplied a volume scan that was later rate-limited. This is a bounded exclusion of the successfully retrieved figures, not an exhaustive source-identity proof.

## Original sources, fixed experiment, and discriminating result

The target is the unmodified `resources/liber-primus/44.jpg` original source, SHA-256 `c92c972f41dd038e136d8589aa7b113e268c36f2860c279cce712cd037cb3a8e`, with the wing cropped to native-source pixel rectangle `[643,516,1787,855]`. The original has 15 enclosed membrane compartments at five independent threshold values under the existing image inventory: 13 marginal faces and two internal faces. A genuine source-plate identity should preserve the complete outline, costal base, principal vein branching, and face adjacencies under **uniform** scale, rotation, reflection and translation, without deleting veins, rearranging cells or anisotropically stretching the figure.

The source-independent, preexisting image registration `sims/lp2_p27_historical_threeplate_registration_sft.py` uses SIFT/Lowe 0.75, reciprocal feature matches and OpenCV partial-affine RANSAC at 5px. This experiment explicitly adds **original and horizontally mirrored** source frames, complete pages and pre-specified connected-ink plate regions. The criterion for a noncollapsed match is at least **30 inliers**, native p27 target span greater than 50% horizontally and 15% vertically, and scale within 0.08–4. A transformed positive copy of the exact original p27 wing recovers **383 inliers** and 97.9% ×95.9% target coverage; the unrelated LP2 p53 art negative has **3 inliers**.

These small inlier counts are not close to the calibrated 30-inlier acceptance criterion. The 1949 images were retrieved byte-exact, each with recorded SHA-256 and 9,246/9,359 bytes respectively. Their retrieval resolves the previous source-image cache misses, though it does not imply their historical site served those exact re-encoded bytes before 2014. The figure labels derive from the site's reference to the 1949 plates.

Kukalová (1964) Fig.2, DOI `10.1155/1964/34192`, was unavailable from the tested DOI/Psyche/Internet Archive mirrors (HTTP403,404,503 or timeout). The 1976 original *Psyche* 83 volume was fetched **once** from Wikimedia, SHA-256 `428456591cb0` (31,697,414 bytes). Its first attempted candidate pages mistakenly covered PDF pages2–4, far from the actual printed pp79–93; those three pages supply **no valid negative** for Fig.1. After source-folio correction the same public download returned HTTP429, and the other original mirrors failed. The original publication independently gives the subimago forewing size 36×15mm and adult 38×15mm, unlike the p27 artwork's roughly 3.39 ratio, and the 2000 Figure17A clearly reproduces a densely reticulated fossil wing. These observations weaken *Moravia* literal-copy identity but cannot replace actual 1964 Fig.2 and 1976 Fig.1 image registration.

## Reproduction and cipher decision

Run the source-specific `sims/lp2_p27_homoptera_moravia_sft.py` on a Windows runner with `opencv-python-headless`, `numpy`, `requests`, `filelock`, and `pymupdf`; the manually dispatched workflow is `.github/workflows/agent29-p27-homoptera-moravia.yml`. All files and tests are sourced from the actual original JPEG and fixed historical URLs. The [final successful, mirrored figure run](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43983423425) publishes its JSON, source-page thumbnails and checksums as an Actions artifact; [first retrieval](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43981086284) established both previously inaccessible 1949 illustration bytes.

The original p27→p55/p56 seven-rune `DJUBEIAE` seam and the 15 original wing faces are real, previously established source observations. These historical candidate plates currently provide **no exact creator-selected venation labels or unique traversal** to consume that seam, and no falsifiable full-book cipher inverse has been selected. Do not extend the 14-cell/period14 key family invalidated by the genuine fifteenth face; do not invent an unrelated insect family to rescue this source test. A future direct 1964/1976 full-wing scan with **the same registered topology** and a first-party decoder instruction would be a genuinely new input warranting a fresh decision.
