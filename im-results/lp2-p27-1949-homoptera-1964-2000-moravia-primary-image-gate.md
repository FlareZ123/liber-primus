# P27 original wing against named 1949 Homoptera and 1964–2000 Moravia sources

**Current decision (17 November 2026; Agent29 original gate, Agent7 new full-volume readback).** The two exact Rohdendorf (1949) Homoptera plates, the accessible 1974 Moravia basal plate, the correct 1976 *Psyche* volume folio band and the 2000 Moravia reproduction do **not** register as near-exact copies of the LP2 p27 wing under the already frozen full-geometry similarity criterion. Kukalová (1964) Fig.2 remains unretrieved. No historical specimen plate has yet supplied a source-authenticated vein order, a reversible rune operator, a key or unknown-page plaintext. The exclusion is limited to the actual images tested and does not rule out a redrawn or unrelated source.

## Original sources, fixed experiment, and discriminating result

The target is the unmodified `resources/liber-primus/44.jpg` original source, SHA-256 `c92c972f41dd038e136d8589aa7b113e268c36f2860c279cce712cd037cb3a8e`, with the wing cropped to native-source pixel rectangle `[643,516,1787,855]`. The original has 15 enclosed membrane compartments at five independent threshold values under the existing image inventory: 13 marginal faces and two internal faces. A genuine source-plate identity should preserve the complete outline, costal base, principal vein branching, and face adjacencies under **uniform** scale, rotation, reflection and translation, without deleting veins, rearranging cells or anisotropically stretching the figure.

The source-independent, preexisting image registration `sims/lp2_p27_historical_threeplate_registration_sft.py` uses SIFT/Lowe 0.75, reciprocal feature matches and OpenCV partial-affine RANSAC at 5px. This experiment explicitly adds **original and horizontally mirrored** source frames, complete pages and pre-specified connected-ink plate regions. The criterion for a noncollapsed match is at least **30 inliers**, native p27 target span greater than 50% horizontally and 15% vertically, and scale within 0.08–4. A transformed positive copy of the exact original p27 wing recovers **383 inliers** and 97.9% ×95.9% target coverage; the unrelated LP2 p53 art negative has **3 inliers**.

The source-frozen scan of leaves **456–484** includes the complete published article pp153–168 (DOI `10.1155/1964/34192`) plus adjacent leaves. It examined **68** full-page, eligible isolated-ink and horizontally mirrored frames. The best `pdf-472` original-orientation candidate yielded **five** partial-affine SIFT inliers, p27-x span **0.53197**, y span **0.10751**, scale **0.084934**. This excludes exact traced similarity within the complete correct 1964 article folio under the frozen detector, while allowing non-affine redraws. The earlier scan of PDF index 8 was a **first-volume contents-page misselection** and is superseded by this archive-numbered original-article test. ## Reproduction and cipher decision

Run the source-specific `sims/lp2_p27_homoptera_moravia_sft.py` on a Windows runner with `opencv-python-headless`, `numpy`, `requests`, `filelock`, and `pymupdf`; the manually dispatched workflow is `.github/workflows/agent29-p27-homoptera-moravia.yml`. The `exact_1964_only=true` dispatch limits the revised workflow to the newly recovered article and uses its original archive leaf metadata to select the correct 1964 folio; default dispatch retains the other historical source comparisons. All tests use unmodified original image and fixed historical URLs. The [17 November original correct-folio run 43985069629](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43985069629) passed and preserves the full report plus **40** preview/source artifacts ([ZIP artifact 14285645718](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43985069629/artifacts/14285645718)); [first original 1949 source retrieval](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43981086284) remains independently reproducible.

The original p27→p55/p56 seven-rune `DJUBEIAE` seam and the 15 original wing faces are real, previously established source observations. These historical candidate plates currently provide **no exact creator-selected venation labels or unique traversal** to consume that seam, and no falsifiable full-book cipher inverse has been selected. Do not extend the 14-cell/period14 key family invalidated by the genuine fifteenth face; do not invent an unrelated insect family to rescue this source test. The historical 1964 plate no longer requires retrieval. Reopen this source-family only on an independently identified different original plate with genuinely matching venation topology and a first-party decoding instruction; arbitrary new anatomical key guesses are unsupported.
