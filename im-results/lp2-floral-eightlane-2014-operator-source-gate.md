## Independent original inputs and historical reconstruction

The canonical physical LP2 corpus, `resources/liber-primus/lp2-corpus.json`, records **3,008** consecutive GP29 runes across the original floral p40–53 images. P40 begins with exactly eight highlighted runes (three plus five printed title runes), followed by **3,000** chapter-body runes. The separate actual p50 page has zero runes and an eight-column, 32-row table. These lengths make an eight-lane hypothesis imaginable: body length 3,000 = eight lanes × 375 positions, with a possible eight-symbol title seed. The geometry is source-visible, but neither a column-major/row-major reading nor a value-changing state law appears in these facts.

The issue describes an eight-column **2014 Stage05** transposition as authentic precedent. Inspection of the preserved historic reconstruction finds a **different** operator width:

- [`micheloosterhof/cicada-2014`, archived stage03 README](https://github.com/micheloosterhof/cicada-2014/blob/master/stage03/README.md) explicitly identifies a **14-column** transposition, column order `2 8 9 1 12 13 11 4 5 7 3 0 6 10`, recovering the signed `GOOD WORK ULTIMATE TRUTH ...` address.
- [Archived stage06 README](https://github.com/micheloosterhof/cicada-2014/blob/master/stage06/README.md) records the subsequent avowy... image extraction and solved phrase; the independently detailed [2014 stage walkthrough](https://github.com/scream314/cicada3301/blob/master/2014.md) specifies **period 7**, key `1736254`, for the column transposition of the hidden p8 image payload, recovering `TO BELIEVE TRUTH IS TO DESTROY POSSIBILITY ...`.
- The stage labels in this historical archive are folder-path conventions, so the absence of an eight-column precedent does not depend on whether a separate account calls the avowy stage five or six. Both documented columnar operations have known, **non-eight** dimensions. They are **positional permutations of a signed message**, not a supplied eight-register arithmetic update rule.

Thus the premise that an original 2014 Stage05 **eight-column operation** independently selects eight coupled evolving lanes is not sustained by these preserved walkthroughs. This correction is important because interpreting an arbitrary eight-rune p40 header as both the number of cipher lanes and its entire numeric seed would otherwise grant unwarranted source authority to an invented cipher.
