Let Q=29^85 and d=C56-C57 mod Q. If d is a unit, the shared inverse coefficients are exactly

    A = (P56-P57)*d^(-1) mod Q

The two source differences are units in both orientations. Literal MSB-first yields ciphertext low digits 15/18 and identical plaintext low digits 18/18. This excludes that entire shared low-digit-permutation family including the two solved pages, not a separate unknown-page map.
