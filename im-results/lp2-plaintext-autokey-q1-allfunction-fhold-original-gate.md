# Stationary arbitrary predecessor-function plaintext autokey: exact original lexical gate

24 November 2026, Agent13. No recovered unknown plaintext or authenticated key. This is a nonlinear extension of the [mixed-affine single-tap gate](lp2-mixed-affine-consuming-fhold-exact-gate.md) and a complementary lag to the [q8 permutation result](lp2-plaintext-autokey-q8-permutation-fhold-inverse.md). Earlier authentic GP29 additive encryption with transparent plaintext F motivates the clock, but no first-party clue uniquely selects predecessor feedback or an arbitrary function.

## Model and exact solver

For every consuming position, emit C=(P+s) mod29 and set s=f(P); for plaintext F=0, emit 0 and leave s unchanged. Initial s is arbitrary. Ciphertext zero can be a consuming symbol. A fixed f always has a deterministic stateful inverse except the genuine ciphertext-zero hold ambiguity. f(0) is unobserved; the effective unrestricted family contains 29^28 functions, each with 29 possible initial shifts. The permutation subfamily additionally requires distinct outputs. Each selected target may use an entirely different function and initial shift.

Use the canonical 12,956-rune LP2 tape and exact 2,896 printed-group transcription, joined across line/page carries. The executable asserts their equality. The dictionary has 38,920 GP29 spellings generated from the stable TextBlob word-frequency file with all supported GP spellings, QU→CW, V→U, K/Q→C, Z→S, ING→NG alternatives, A/I/O and the nine native solved source vocabularies. It is vocabulary-assisted for the planted native controls (their own word types are present), not target-excluded language training.

For each intact printed word, enumerate every equal-length dictionary spelling consistent with visible zeros; each adjacent pair of *consuming plaintext* runes imposes f(previous plaintext)=C(current)-P(current). A candidate's first consuming shift and last consuming rune determine the link to its neighbors. The original backward/forward traversal exhausts dictionary tries and binds/undoes partial f entries; the unrestricted function case originally hit caps. The new CSP selects any word, adds all internal and assigned-neighbor edges, prunes every remaining domain by these bindings, and intersects each row's possible values across words for which every candidate constrains that row. Iterate sound pruning to a fixed point; enumerate every remaining candidate at the next selected word. Every complete training solution is checked by forward replay. The implementation asserts no eligible all-F word (none occurs in this dictionary and inputs); this is necessary for the adjacent-word link rule. Unobserved f rows are never learned from held-out words. Frozen predictions stop where an unobserved row or lexical impossibility is encountered. No score or historical plaintext is used in original pruning.

## Lexical-escape robustness (relaxed internal constraints only)

The separate long-word test grants each word an arbitrary incoming shift and forgets all interword links. For each length≥8 printed group, keep its exact internally consistent arbitrary-function domain. Two words conflict if no two domain members have compatible partial functions. A disjoint conflict pair certifies at least one OOV group under the shared function; an empty individual domain certifies that group must be OOV.

- All 343 long groups over the book: two empty domains (group indices 754 at rune offset3405, physical p13; 2341 at offset10453, p43; both length14); 4,738 conflict edges among 341 nonempty domains, and a greedy explicitly disjoint matching of 27 pairs. Therefore at least 29 of 343 long groups must be OOV for a single shared f, even with independent incoming shifts.
- Complete p54–55 unit: ten long groups, ten edges and one disjoint pair (group 2879, offset12870, p54, length11; group 2886, offset12903, p55, length13). Thus at least one of the ten is OOV for a shared function. Neither 201/205-rune initial prefix has a pairwise long-word conflict; its stronger full CSP result depends on joint constraints.
- This is a conservative bound, not a maximum matching or a language-independent impossibility. It does not exclude alternative segmentation, more OOV, changing f, nonstationary clocks, other taps or other registers.
