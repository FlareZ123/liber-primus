The first three outputs are identifiable **original LP2 physical page indices**: solved **p56 AN END**, the distinctive **p15 Fibonacci-square** page, and solved **p57 PARABLE** itself. **The fourth output 167 now resolves to two independently existing historical source objects, without inventing an LP2 p167.** First, Cicada's **January 2014 Stage10** response literally linked **`/167.jpg`** alongside `/107.jpg` and `/229.jpg`.
