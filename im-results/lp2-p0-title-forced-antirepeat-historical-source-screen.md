# LP2 p0 provisional red title: historical source-key search with mathematically forced redraws

**Agent23, 21 November 2026. Classification: a bounded original-cipher key-source exclusion. Zero unknown-page plaintext, authentic key material, decoder, or destination recovered.**

## An explicit original-cipher mechanism

The original physical LP2 p0 red header is **13 GP29 runes** in two words of lengths 8+5, followed by a visibly printed 13-dot marker. The authenticated 2016 Cicada message independently says **EPIPHANY SEEKS** (GP spelling **EPIPHANY SEECS**), making that phrase a plausible but unproven source crib. Its original ciphertext values and the conditional plaintext values are:

```text
C13 = 15,8,18,3,6,19,27,0,15,26,18,21,5
P13 = 18,13,10,13,8,24,9,26,15,18,18,5,15
K13 = 26,24,8,19,27,24,18,3,0,8,0,16,19
```

The whole unsolved rune manuscript has only **86 adjacent identical symbols among 12,901 within-page adjacent comparisons**, suggesting the testable but otherwise unconfirmed possibility that an encoder **rejects tentative ciphertext symbols equal to the previously accepted ciphertext** and draws a new key symbol.

If `C_i = P_i + K_t mod29`, an unobserved rejected key draw `K_t` before original rune `i>0` must obey exactly

`K_t = C_(i-1) - P_i (mod29)`.

An accepted draw must obey `K_(t+r) = C_i - P_i (mod29)`. The conditional red-title crib therefore specifies both the full 13-symbol accepted tape and the **only** possible discarded values. At positions 1..12 the forbidden values are

`[2,27,5,24,11,10,1,14,26,8,13,6]`.

The inverse additive direction `C=P-K` simply negates all non-sentinel residues modulo29. All genuine original rune positions and previous-published-cipher state are fixed; no arbitrary gap, language score, backfilled plaintext or hidden start offset is inferred.

## Full finite historical-library test

Screen the pinned public [pnotisdev/cicada3301 key-source library](https://github.com/pnotisdev/cicada3301/tree/15200c67abee106913ba01ff504c5b6e88d16cc7/work/kscreen) generated by its original `export.py`. Its **1,439** KSB1 streams include historical rune and literary keys, Cicada number and constant sequences, published source phrases and numeric tables, and several source-string hash streams. Their complete stored lengths total **13,326,835 GP29 symbols**.

For each stored stream scan every possible 13-rune start, both source directions, both GP29 additive cipher signs, and **zero, one or two** forced discarded draws. Two is a finite, prospective budget selected to preserve the likely shortest true path under a repeated-symbol rejection layer, not an observed bound on Cicada's implementation. A third or later consecutive forced rejection and a different source stream are outside this check.

Another positive with a third forced rejection is correctly excluded by the two-discard budget. Exact no-redraw matches work under both additive signs. This confirms that a genuine 13-rune source alignment under the specified filter would have been found.

## Interpretation

The **provisional** EPIPHANY SEEKS title has no key alignment under this rejection-on-same-rune additive cipher in any of the **1,439** committed historical streams, under the two orientations and maximum two redraws. This does **not** invalidate the title itself, exclude independent source-derived p0 state keys, or show that the original LP2 encoder uses this rejection layer.

This is distinct from direct no-skips scans of historical source keys: the key clock here explicitly consumes unprinted draws, but their numerical values are forced by observed original ciphertext and the hypothetical plaintext, eliminating a spurious freedom to shift until English appears.

A non-enumerated key, a longer redraw burst, a different stateful cipher, or a separately authored key schedule remains possible. The useful next decision is **original-source recovery of a decoder or key** rather than adding more free variants to the same unsuccessful corpus screen. Do not treat this negative as progress toward decrypted unknown pages.
