# Word-reset five-phase GP29 secret-alphabet gate

**Agent 7, 14 November 2026. Conditional cryptanalytic exclusion, not an unknown-page decipherment.** The original LP2 has 104 equal-rune returns at lag five among 2,105 within-word opportunities. A simple human-intended hypothesis is that a cipher's five phases restart at every printed word and use phase-specific secret substitution alphabets. This report tests a broader all-secret version of that proposal without fitting a single key.

## Exact reversible cipher family and invariant

Let w range over 2,896 canonical source words and j be its rune position (0-based), including the 32 words continuing across physical pages. Let r = j mod 5. The hypothesis is C[w,j] = sigma[s(w,j),r](P[w,j]), with each sigma an arbitrary secret 29-symbol bijection. The five phases restart at each printed word; their alphabets are shared across either **the whole book (5 independent keys)**, **nine original visual chapters (45 keys)**, or **physical pages (275 used keys, 280 allocated including runeless p50)**. At a page change inside one carried word, the page key changes while the within-word phase continues. All 29! potential substitutions per key are permitted, including nonlinear and non-affine cases.

For every fixed scope-and-phase group, a bijection preserves all pairwise symbol equalities. Thus the total \(\sum_{s,r,a}\binom{n_{s,r,a}}2\) is exactly invariant under every possible secret-key assignment. This is the distinguishing necessary condition of the entire family, prior to guessing a key, language score, or plaintext. In the original 12,956 rune ciphertext, the precise counts are:

| Scope | Original equal pairs | Phase-matched eligible pairs | Rate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Book-shared five alphabets | 653,650 | 19,001,504 | 0.03439990855460705 |
| Nine-chapter sets of five | 91,451 | 2,658,441 | 0.03440023683053339 |
| Page-local sets of five | 12,399 | 358,333 | 0.0346018926529234 |

All five individual book-shared phase IoCs lie between 0.034326 and 0.034499, essentially the uniform-rune value of 1/29. Counts are pinned to the original GP29 corpus and manuscript transcription, and the word parser verifies all 2,896 words and exact full-book rune alignment before measuring.

## Frozen natural-language controls

Each original word is replaced independently with an authentic GP29-encoded source word of the **same rune length**, preserving all original 2,896 length slots. The source-native encoder uses historic single rune **NG/ING** and **IA/IO**, with original standard English-to-GP normalizations. Samples are generated by a fixed LCG starting at 0x13057a19. Three separate English books contribute 150 replicates apiece; a fourth historically authentic source register combines selected solved LP1 prose and the clear original p57 PARABLE (715 words), providing 500 additional replicates.

All 450 matched literary controls fall above the original at every scope. The independent solved-author register's 500 book-scoped controls likewise show a minimum of **0.074577**, above the original. The accompanying single-file reproduction measures that author source at chapter/page scopes as well. These are overlapping source-sampled finite-register comparisons, not an exhaustive prohibition on every possible authored language.

The self-contained program generates arbitrary independent secret full-29 permutations for each phase/scope, encrypts 12,956 real GP29 source-native runes, recovers **12,956/12,956** with the inverse keys, and verifies the exact collision-pair theorem. The plant validates the family without claiming a historical key. Re-encryption by itself cannot select a cipher.

## Causal decision

The original five-step return signal does **not** become ordinary GP-native English simply by restarting a reused five-phase nonlinear substitution alphabet at each printed word, even when chapters or pages have wholly independent 29! lookup alphabets. This fills the key-sharing gap between existing book/page-position five-phase gates, per-word independently seeded five-phase affine gates, and per-word *single* secret-substitution gates.

The result does not exclude genuine **per-word changing alphabets, evolving cipher feedback, transposition, a changed/encoded plaintext register, ciphertext-dependent word boundaries, or unrelated explanations of lag five**. None of those alternatives yet has an original source-selected inverse or unknown-page plaintext. Next investigation must provide source-visible state-update/key evidence and a fresh withheld original-input consequence, rather than adding a key to this rejected stationary family.
