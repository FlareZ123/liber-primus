**Classification (Agent45, 16 November 2026): one clue-motivated, bounded negative. No unknown plaintext, key or source-authenticated algorithm.**

Cicada's signed January 2012 message says **“Patience is a virtue.”** The 2012 Cicada emblem appeared on the contemporaneous coordinate/clock page and was reused, image-register-identically, on LP1 p05 and LP2 p53/p55. The signed phrase had an obvious historical waiting-clock use in 2012; assigning it an additional cipher role in 2014 is a **competing conjecture**, not a creator instruction.

There is a specific pre-2014 cipher alternative worth testing once: “patience” is a generic name for card solitaire, and Bruce Schneier's 1999 **Solitaire/Pontifex** stream cipher uses a deck of 52 cards and two jokers. The original, full signed phrase **PATIENCEISAVIRTUE** supplies a fixed candidate passphrase. The exact historic deck-keying procedure produces 1..26 output values. The narrow GP29 adaptation tested here **retains that 1..26 stream unchanged** and uses the manuscript's 29-index rune algebra, `C[i]=(P[i]+K[i]) mod29`, reset at the beginning of physical LP2 p55 (76 runes). No F skip, word reset, phase fit, tuned deck, or other secret is introduced. The **only** change to original 1999 Solitaire is the final addition modulus 29 instead of Latin's 26; no primary source explicitly prescribes it.

The original p55 ciphertext decoded with the literal full phrase begins GP-rune romanization **`I A S R F F A NG N G EA E C AE TH G Y W X M`**, contains no coherent paragraph, and has a complete 76-rune IoC of **0.03614035087719298**. By comparison the first 76 runes of genuine clear LP2 p57 have IoC **0.05964912280701754**. There is no independently readable word-contiguous p55 plaintext or recoverable key. The p55 first printed rune completes a word from preceding physical p54; this fixed page-start mode does not solve the need for an author-selected key clock.
