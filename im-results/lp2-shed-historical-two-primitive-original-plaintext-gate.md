## Human-visible clue and two-step receiver

Already solved LP2 p57 states **"WE MUST SHED OUR OWN CIRCUMFERENCES / FIND THE DIVINITY WITHIN AND EMERGE."** The literal reversible reading takes each complete printed word, retains its first and last glyphs as an exterior sidecar, and processes the intervening runes in book order. The interior lengths are computed after rejoining original page-edge carried words. Singletons and doublets have empty interiors. Previous p55-specific word-core studies are distinct from the complete distant original chapters tested here.

A 2014 solver also had documented GP29 operators used on already solved Cicada pages: the DIVINITY Vigenère keyword, the FIRFUMFERENFE Vigenère keyword, and the prime-totient sequence decoded from p56. Source p57 itself repeats the word DIVINITY. The precise strongest objection is **no first-party text instructs transfer of these historical operators to the unknown chapters' interior stream**. The competing interpretation has **independent 2013 historical evidence**: the exact same Instar/Circumferences/Divinity sentence appears in the authentic Cicada OS 761.mp3 metadata, where the period solution bytewise-XORed that MP3 with a separate Twitter binary before OutGuess. That original operation is documented in [the 2013 source contract](../resources/external/2013-instar-xor-source.md). The quote itself therefore does not authenticate LP2 word-edge stripping, and the original historical XOR is byte-typed with no LP2 rune serialization or second source operand. A philosophical reading or a different intended receiver also remains plausible.

The grammar freezes the two operations before reading plaintext: (1) word-interior extraction with unchanged exterior glyphs at their exact source coordinates, then (2) a single historical GP29 subtraction key advancing once per interior rune. At each chapter's source-defined start the key phase is zero. All output rune positions, punctuation and page carries remain unchanged, and the inverse adds exactly the same key to the same positions. These are the only tested program instances:

| Program | GP29 source key | Clock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SHED + LP1 DIVINITY | [23,10,1,10,9,10,16,26] | 8 |
| SHED + LP1 FIRFUMFERENFE | [0,10,4,0,1,19,0,18,4,18,9,0,18] | 13 |
| SHED + solved p56 prime-totient | (prime(i)-1) mod29, starting at prime2 | Each interior rune consumes a prime |

In historical LP1/p56 some **literal F** symbols bypass encryption without advancing their key clock. Those positions are explicit in independently solved plaintext, but a corresponding original skip mask for unsolved LP2 is absent. This specific missing author input prevents a source-locked transfer of F bypasses; the frozen programs do not invent skips or optimize F placements.
