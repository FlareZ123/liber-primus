# PARABLE 55 page seeds: GP29 substitution and additive-IV original-cipher exclusion

**17 November 2026 · Agent49.** All 56 LP2 unknown pages remain unsolved. This original-source test rules out two finite structural readings of the **55 strict interior runes** of clear p57 PARABLE as seeds for the **55 rune-bearing encrypted pages**. In particular, a changing, unknown 29-ary additive key stream may be different at every rune offset and may have an **independent hidden constant additive IV on each physical page**. These freedoms cannot rescue this one-seed-per-page interpretation when equal p57 runes restart the identical stream. The test never guesses a key or requires an implementation of its generator.

## Source-chosen premise and strongest competitor

The clear p57 instructs readers to "shed our own circumferences." Removing the first and last GP29 rune of each of its 20 printed words leaves 55 rune symbols. A human solver might assign them in order to the 55 encrypted physical pages (p0–p49 and p51–p55), perhaps restarting a page-specific keystream at each page boundary. Two frozen assignments are tested: original **title-first 5 + body 50**, and conditional **body 50 + title 5**. Each contains **106 unordered pairs of different pages bearing the same proposed seed**. The 2016 authenticated "numbers are the direction" message is suggestive, but no Cicada-authored instruction demands this seed assignment, addition, a common key stream for repeated seeds, or a page-independent derivation. A page-number-dependent key schedule, a nonlinear stateful receiver, and a source register radically unlike GP-native English remain serious competitors.

This branch differs from the [fixed-reserved-rune, 28-nonzero-step chain and its changing secret alphabets](lp2-parable55-cyclic-chain28-original-gate.md). The present trial directly consumes **all 29 original GP29 glyphs**, permits adjacent doubles without special handling, and allows a new hidden additive page IV for every one of the 55 pages.

## Exact invariants and native benchmark

Write page-local ciphertext glyphs as C_{i,t}, plaintext as P_{i,t}, the p57 seed as s_i, and t as the original rune offset from that page's start.

**A. An arbitrary source-seed-only, position-changing secret bijection.** If C_{i,t} = f_{s_i,t}(P_{i,t}) with each f any secret permutation of all 29 glyphs, then for two pages with s_i=s_j, C_{i,t}=C_{j,t} exactly when P_{i,t}=P_{j,t}. Count same-position equality across all 106 seed-matched pairs. A different, secret permutation may be used at *every* position. This invariant does **not** survive independently shifted page alphabets/IVs, so the no-IV assumption is explicit.

**B. A changing unknown additive GP29 key and an arbitrary per-page constant IV.** If C_{i,t}=P_{i,t}+K_{s_i,t}+IV_i (mod29), then for seed-equal pages the cross-page difference D_{ij,t}=(C_{i,t}-C_{j,t}) mod29 equals (P_{i,t}-P_{j,t}+IV_i-IV_j) mod29. For each page pair, the entire 29-bin histogram of D may rotate by a hidden constant. Its collision count **sum over residues d of n_d(n_d−1)** is unchanged. Pool these ordered difference collisions and ordered pairs across the 106 page pairs, then scale by 29. This eliminates *every* possible unknown, changing, aperiodic key stream shared by equal seeds and *every* constant page IV without recovering either. Position-dependent IVs, page-index-derived keystreams, cipher-feedback evolution, and a second transform are outside the tested class.

The benchmark is **44 nonoverlapping 12,956-GP29-rune native-English controls**, drawn from Gibran, Shakespeare and Emerson, deterministically transliterated with the project's established GP29 digraph renderer and partitioned into exactly the 55 original physical-page lengths. They preserve the same seed groupings, original page-offset alignment, and whole-page matching-window lengths. They are distributional English controls, not Cicada-authored samples or proof of the plaintext language.

The separation is a family-dependent distributional exclusion, conditional on the native-English GP29 source profile. It is not a mathematical impossibility for adversarially chosen non-English plaintext.

## Secret-key positive controls and decision

The [standalone Windows-compatible CommonJS SFT](../sims/lp2_parable55_page_seed_additive_iv_invariant_sft.cjs) reconstructs both 55-rune source tapes, the exact 55 original physical-page glyph arrays, and every benchmark partition. It independently plants and recovers **all 12,956 native-GP29 English glyphs** with a seed-and-position-dependent, nonperiodic polynomial additive key schedule and a **different constant page IV**. The exact additive-IV ciphertext difference-collision score equals the planted known plaintext's score for both tape orders. A separate secret GP29 affine permutation changing with seed and position preserves same-position equality identically. The original counts are pinned as exact assertions; they cannot be produced by fitting secret parameters to the source ciphertext.

**Decision:** Retire p57's 55 rune interiors as a literal one-seed-per-rune-bearing-page **deterministic repeated-substitution schedule without per-page IV**, or as a repeated **additive GP29 schedule with any constant page IV**, for ordinary GP-native English plaintext. Unlike a direct key search, this covers **all unknown keys**, including high-entropy and aperiodic streams, within precisely those architectures. The stronger 28-step chain result remains separate. Neither result disproves source-derived **unique per-page key material**, altered page clocks, ciphertext-dependent state, non-GP-native language, or other as-yet-unspecified puzzle mechanisms. New work on this 55-seed connection needs an original source-selected operation that genuinely escapes these constraints and can recover a whole withheld original paragraph.
