# 3301/1033: source roles and LP2 transfer decision

**Decision (11 November 2026): no single source-authenticated operation uses both 3301 and 1033 to select a unique LP2 interval.** There are real, distinct uses of each value. The only signed numeric operation with a direct LP2 algebraic consequence is the 2013 BASE29 example on 3301, which yields the p50 H factor. The 1033 BASE29 control does not divide p50; the original 2014 page-05 same-artifact rune-word-to-number reconstruction has already failed its proposed LP2 row-key transfer. No plaintext or key is recovered.

This audit distinguishes (a) signed/authenticated message content, (b) original puzzle artifacts that are not PGP-signed, and (c) participant/archive reconstructions. “Player-visible” does not mean “signed.” The archive manifest [1033-3301-historical-operator-chain.json](../resources/external/1033-3301-historical-operator-chain.json) explicitly identifies itself as a secondary reconstruction; it is useful for locating leads, not for upgrading them into authenticated Cicada instructions.

## Frozen operation tests against LP2

### BASE29: 3301 succeeds as one factor; 1033 is a negative control

The signed 2013 example fixes the operation and operand for 3301: BASE29(3301) = 3QO, whose coefficient vector gives H(X) = 3 + 26X + 24X². The existing [GF(29) grid-factor reproduction](2013-base29-gf29-grid-factor.md) shows H divides the original p50 256-byte table exactly; the independently source-derived p39 red triad supplies G(X) = 27 + 5X + 15X², and p50 is divisible by both. This is an exact structural relation, not yet a reversible cipher or plaintext.

Applying the same BASE29 conversion to 1033 gives 16I, with coefficient vectors [1,6,18] and reversed [18,6,1]. The existing original-grid test gives nonzero remainders [3,11] and [24,3], respectively. Thus the same BASE29-to-polynomial operation does **not** carry both constants into the p50 factorization. The 1033 input is present in the unsigned participant test vector; it is a useful control, not a second signed operand.

### Prime-sum: exact same-2014-binary reconstruction, without an earlier key handoff

The **2014** original LP1 page-05 word-cell prime-sum map reconstructs the 5×5 numerical trailer, 25/25. The two encodings are components of one preserved 2014 Stage04 object. This establishes the arithmetic reading of that page while providing **no independent 2013→2014 transformation**. The source-selected LP2 candidate is the five red-numbered passages on p36–p38, a 553-rune interval bounded by the original 1–5 labels and 10-dot delimiters. The existing [five-numbered wisdom inversion gate](lp2-cuneiform-five-numbered-wisdom-inversion.md) tested the row-wise φ(cell) mod 29 transfer, both orientations, and the solved-p56 prime/totient shift with exact original-input round trips and controls. None produces language. The proposed direct transfer from the 2014 square to unknown LP2 is negative.

## p33 header versus the original 2014 square form (Director Issue #522)

The purported “Stage11 form” is misidentified. The original form HTML is the 2014 sixth-onion/Stage10 artifact [ut3qtzbrvs7dtvzp.onion.v2-form.html](https://github.com/neuroretransmit/cicada/blob/ade9a02889ef8acbc8cde8edbfd0a785c369f4c3/2014/008-sixth-onion/ut3qtzbrvs7dtvzp.onion.v2-form.html). Its exact ordered fields are **Order 5 Square, 3301; Order 7 Square, 1033; Order 5 Square, 1033**. The task's proposed order (5,3301),(5,1033),(7,1033) swaps the last two fields. The preserved [Stage11 index](../resources/historical/stage11-2014-index.html) is not a square form at all: it is a 58-image index (0.jpg through 57.jpg). The Stage11 signed message concerns participants hosting a CGI upload service, as detailed in the [upload-direction correction](2014-stage11-upload-direction-correction.md).

The signed form message asks participants to paste their three squares and supply their own onion-service URL; it does not mention p33, LP2, a checksum, or a two-number header. The signed 2013 TCP challenge authenticates BASE29 3301, but contains no 1033 operand; the preserved 1033 BASE29 request is an unsigned participant live vector. The 2013 prime_echo pauses at both values, but does not connect them to the 2014 form or the 2016 p33 header.

**Issue #522 decision:** the original p33 header visibly contains 1033 and 3301, and the original 2014 Stage10 form visibly requests three ordered square/value pairs. No primary source says the p33 pair is a checksum for those three fields, defines a mapping from two values to three requests, or supplies a validation consequence. The task premise also mislabels the form and misorders its fields. Treat the recurrence as a cross-round observation, not an authenticated checksum or an LP2 decipherment instruction.

## Human solve-path assessment and decision

A contemporary solver could reasonably notice the 2013 signed BASE29 worked example on 3301, the LP2 p50 numeric table, and the p39 red structure. That route has a concrete algebraic consequence (H/G divisibility), so it remains worth retaining as a structural lead. The same solver could also notice the 1033/3301 reversed-prime pair in the archived 2013 services and later LP2 cuneiform; however, the service grammar is not in the signed TCP challenge, the ordinal normalization is not source-selected here, and the pair does not specify a reversible LP2 operation.

The two values do not form one common key, modulus pair, recurrence, or row-key rule in the preserved sources. **Source-boundary and diagonal certificate for Director #459:** the signed January 2013 text contains the worked `BASE29 3301 → 3QO` and a separate **DH modulus 23** example, while the unsigned March participant transcript records `BASE29 1033 → 16I` and `DH 3301` (in which 3301 is the DH modulus). The original 2014 square's five row sums and five column sums are all **1033**; its *both* principal diagonal sums are **1033**, and the sum of all 25 cells is **5165**. It has **no diagonal sum 3301**. Therefore the assignment premise claiming an authenticated 2013 `BASE29 1033, DH 3301` pair plus 2014 square diagonal 3301 is factually false. No source-authenticated 2013→2014 protocol-to-square operation remains to freeze or invert. 3301's signed BASE29 route and 1033's 2014 prime-sum/magic-square role are distinct. The 1033 BASE29 control, the source-selected 553-rune row transfer, and the paired-value LCG/page-walk candidates all fail to produce plaintext. **Close the combined-constant hypothesis as unsupported; keep the 3301→H/p50 and p39→G relationships as an intermediate structural candidate, not a solved cipher.**
