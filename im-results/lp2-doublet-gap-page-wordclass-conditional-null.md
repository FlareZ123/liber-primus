# Original LP2 doublet-gap correlation after exact page and word-shape conditioning

**Agent 40 · 14 November 2026.** A source-original, independent *conditional calibration* of an externally identified ciphertext anomaly. **No unknown plaintext, GP29 key, cipher inverse, or final destination has been recovered.** The result narrows what the lag-one doublet-gap correlation can mean.

## Why this gate matters

The external [Dukotah Round 44 synthesis](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/master/liber-primus/analysis/round44/SYNTHESIS.md#2-the-one-live-result--b3--t7-doublet-rate-non-stationarity) reports that the spacing between the 86 original repeated-ciphertext-rune sites has consecutive-gap Pearson correlation around **+0.380**, with a 20,000-draw gap-permutation tail about 0.00025. That is a *ciphertext fact* potentially consistent with a drifting anti-repeat parameter or key-session state. The source itself also has changing page lengths, word-length inventories and different event counts on individual pages. Permuting abstract gap values breaks all of those physical constraints. We ask the more discriminating question: **does the spacing anomaly remain after those original layout counts are fixed?**

This is an independent null construction using original source inputs, not a new discovery of the 86-event signal. It does not presuppose any GP29 plaintext, additive cipher, key stream, or decryption register.

## Exact original site inventory and null

The [reproducer](../sims/lp2_doublet_page_wordclass_gap_autocorr_sft.cjs) reads both the original numeric GP29 corpus and its independently maintained literal source transcription. It asserts rune identity across **12,956/12,956 original rune positions**, the **2,896 exact complete words**, and the **55 rune-bearing physical pages** p0–55 (the numeral-only physical p50 contributes no ciphertext). There are **12,901** adjacent positions *inside a physical page* and exactly **86** equal-rune destinations. The first rune on each page is excluded from repeat eligibility, but successive observed repeat sites are ordered globally when defining the inter-event gaps. The original physical page breaks are never edited.

For each eligible destination rune index, assign one fully observed, source-defined stratum:

- its original physical page;
- if the preceding rune is inside the same printed word, the exact **printed word length** and destination rune's **zero-based position inside that word**;
- otherwise, the distinct **cross-word boundary** stratum for that page.

All printed word boundaries are preserved, including line/page-continuing words; no artificial word split is introduced at a line wrap. Across the book, **81 strata contain at least one of the original 86 repeats**. All remaining source strata have zero events and stay zero. For each of **20,000** deterministic controls, choose *uniformly without replacement* exactly the observed count of repeated-rune sites from the eligible positions **within each stratum**, then merge and sort those sites in printed global order. This conditions on **all 55 original per-page repeat totals and the entire exact word-length/slot event inventory**, while permitting event positions to move among word instances that share that printed shape.

For successive original repeat-event positions \(t_0,\ldots,t_{85}\), define 85 positive gaps \(\Delta_i=t_i-t_{i-1}\). Each of the three frozen descriptive statistics is a Pearson correlation between \((z_1,\ldots,z_{84})\) and \((z_2,\ldots,z_{85})\), for \(z=\Delta\), \(z=\log\Delta\), and \(z=\operatorname{midrank}(\Delta)\). Midranks average tied integer-gap ranks. All three are scored under the **same** 20,000 layout-preserving draws, xorshift32 seed \`0x44553301\`, without fitting a cipher model.

The base original statistic **0.3800196892** exactly reproduces the independent Round 44 reported value. Conditioning the null on original physical-page and word-position counts raises its expected raw correlation from a naively zero-centered reference to **+0.24693**. Much of the observed consecutive-gap correlation can therefore be generated by source-layout heterogeneity *without a dedicated long-memory ciphertext filter*. The original remains somewhat high under that conditional null.

The largest observed gap is **712** runes. Removing it and recomputing the raw correlation lowers the observed value to approximately **0.2784**, so a long-gap pair contributes materially to the Pearson effect. The log-gap and midrank comparisons address that concern directly. The most resistant of these three statistics is the originally reported raw Pearson score; the log-gap result is borderline.

## Decision and limits

**Interpretation:** an event-order residual survives a particularly strict physical-page and exact word-shape conditional redistribution: original Pearson exceeds **19,770/20,000** source-matched controls. The effect is substantially weaker than in the externally reported gap-permutation null, so the previously inferred nonstationarity should **not** be treated as proof of a changing cryptographic key or per-rune rejection rate. A simple source-free renewal null is insufficient; a page/word-aware model is mandatory.

The null makes event identities exchangeable within each fixed source stratum. It deliberately does **not** preserve ciphertext-rune bigram content, the previously discovered lag-five/lag-six echo features, or exact within-word symbol counts. Those could explain part of the residual without hidden state. The raw Pearson anomaly was selected in prior work, and the two robustness alternatives were evaluated after seeing that selection. These are *conditional model diagnostics*, not globally corrected discovery probabilities or independent evidence for a historical Cicada mechanism.

**Causal next step:** require a reversible candidate anti-repeat cipher to predict *both* original doublet scarcity and the remaining page/word-conditioned event-order pattern using independently source-selected key/state instructions. The strongest missing ingredient remains an actual inverse yielding native source-backed plaintext. Without that, further unconstrained lag, shift or key guessing is unjustified.
