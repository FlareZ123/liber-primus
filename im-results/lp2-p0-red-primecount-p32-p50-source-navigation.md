## Original visible input, and the operation under consideration

Physical LP2 p0 starts with two words of 8 and 5 rune glyphs: **both are red**, separated by a small ordinary red word dot; the large red 13-dot marker follows the five-rune second word, at offset13. Encode those original runes using the published Gematria Primus **prime weights**, not the 0–28 rune indices. The first red word sums to **347**, and the second red word sums to **315**. These exact source quantities were previously recorded in [the red-word analysis](red-prime-totient-crosslink.md) and [the opening-selector report](lp2-p0-27x27-opening-selector.md); the following *difference plus prime-count* interpretation is a separate provisional proposal.

The two-word sum and difference are:

- `347 − 315 = 32`, directly selecting **physical p32** if the difference is a page number.
- `347 + 315 = 662`, and the ordinary prime-counting function `π(662) = 121`; physical **p32 contains exactly 121 original runes**. Equivalently `662 = p_121 + 1 = 661 + 1`.
- Their arithmetic mean is **331**, the authenticated 2014 Onion7 **7×7 magic-square center** already recognized in prior research. Additionally, `331 = p_67`, so its one-based prime ordinal is **67**: source image **67.jpg** is the exceptional **rune-free physical p50**, in the three-page **256-byte base-60 numerical table**. Source archive indices are `17 + LP2 physical page`.
- The separate counts `π(315)=65` and `π(347)=69` bracket image number **67** symmetrically. These are dependent calculations on the same two words, not extra statistical confirmations.
- The two opening **word lengths** give `8 × 5 = 40`. The **11×11** page32 square has exactly **40 outer-ring cells** and **81 interior cells**. Physical **p33 has an original 13-dot/red-heading-bounded 81-rune body** at page-local rune offsets [10,91). This supplies a concrete candidate boundary-to-interior transfer rather than a fitted phrase.

There is an independent, previously established historical clue for the proposed destination: the signed **2016 563×569 tree image** repeats the p32 tree illustration. Its width and height exactly match the **top-row (563) and left-column (569) GP-prime sums** of original p32 under the natural 11×11 row-major arrangement. See [the tree image and source axis report](lp2-tree121-signed2016-source-gate.md). The current link only supplies an *additional possible origin at the opening phrase*; it does not upgrade that image linkage to a cipher key.

The initial red pair is the **only qualifying pair among these three at a complete two-word red manuscript heading**. The other two demonstrate that the numeric identity can also arise elsewhere. This is a finite candidate count under a *retrospectively selected function*, **not a discovery-wide probability**. The complete result remains conditional on the author using a word-sum difference and prime counting, rather than any of many other available operations. All possible individual-page and crossing-page differences were included via the canonical 2,896-word transcript.

## Direct p0-heading → p49–51 five-cell table-key gate (Agent2, 17 November)

The 2016 signed message says that LP's words are the map and their numbers the direction. One direct candidate is to use the p0 red-heading prime sums **347/315** as addresses in the 256-cell p49–51 table. Since `347−315=32` and the table is 32×8, modulo256 endpoints 91→59 lie in one column four rows apart; the inclusive path contains five cells. The 8-rune first word matches the table width and the 5-rune second word matches the path length. This is a motivated but unauthenticated reading: no first-party instruction specifies modulo256, index base, base60, mod29 or additive-key use.

The selected cells were visually checked against original [66.jpg](../resources/liber-primus/66.jpg) and [67.jpg](../resources/liber-primus/67.jpg); the heading is on [17.jpg](../resources/liber-primus/17.jpg). The two standard indexing conventions yield:

| Convention | Array indices, first heading to second | Tokens | Base60 values | GP29 key (`value mod29`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zero-based | `91,83,75,67,59` | `0n,3D,2v,3C,19` | `49,193,177,192,69` | `20,19,3,18,11` |
| One-based cell numbers | `90,82,74,66,58` | `3I,3L,3P,0P,2Q` | `198,201,205,25,146` | `24,27,2,25,1` |

The minimal receiver is a repeating five-symbol GP29 additive key, phase zero at the first black p0 rune. Both arithmetic directions were tested, with and without the known p56 plaintext-F bypass (plaintext F is transmitted verbatim and does not consume key). The F-transparent inverse is exactly impossible for all four combinations:

| Index convention | Inverse | First impossible body offset | Original p0 rune index | Forced plaintext-F equation |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Zero-based | `P=C−K` | 7 | 20 | `3−3 ≡ 0 (mod29)` |
| Zero-based | `P=C+K` | 21 | 34 | `9+20 ≡ 0 (mod29)` |
| One-based | `P=C−K` | 3 | 16 | `25−25 ≡ 0 (mod29)` |
| One-based | `P=C+K` | 220 | 233 | `4+25 ≡ 0 (mod29)` |

At each first dead symbol, the only reachable key phase would turn a **nonzero ciphertext rune** into plaintext F, which is forbidden because plaintext F would have been transmitted unchanged. A source-native SFT also encrypts the 95-rune clear p57 passage under each candidate key/sign as a planted control; all four planted paths remain reachable. For ordinary no-skip addition, the full 12,943-rune unknown body has scaled GP29 IC values:

| Index convention | `P=C−K` | `P=C+K` |
|---|---:|---:|
| Zero-based | 0.999965 | 1.000431 |
| One-based | 1.000219 | 1.000306 |

These are effectively the uniform GP29 baseline of 1.0, not evidence of language recovery. Reproduction: [source-native CommonJS SFT](../sims/lp2_red_heading_p50_column_key_sft.cjs).

**Decision:** reject the direct five-cell base60-byte-mod29 repeating additive key under both index conventions and both signs. This does not show that p50 is irrelevant, nor exclude another table role, radix or source-selected operation. Do not expand this route into arbitrary phases or transformations without a new authorial selector.

## Research decision

The p0 red-heading arithmetic offers a **candidate entry point** that independently names p32 (page and rune count) and original numerical image67/p50, while its word lengths reproduce p32's outer-vs-inner 40/81 geometry. The page32 tree image and p33 81-rune body preexist this discovery and supply a potential next step, **but the navigation rule remains an unverified interpretation**. The simplest p32-ring consumer and the direct modulo256 five-cell p50 additive consumer are both negative; the latter fails under both index bases and arithmetic signs. The strongest missing input remains an author-attributable instruction fixing another exact readout from the chosen p32/p50 sources. No actual ciphertext key, decryption algorithm, unknown LP2 plaintext or destination is established. Prefer discriminating source-controlled consumers of this combined p0→p32/p50 pointer to repeated exact-number coincidences.
