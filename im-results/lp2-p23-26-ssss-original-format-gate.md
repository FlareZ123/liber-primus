# LP2 pp23–26: literal 2013 \`ssss\` five-strand format gate

## Decision

**The original 2013 5-of-10 \`ssss\` shares cannot literally be the five full 201-rune strands.** The historical share payloads are fixed **176-bit / 22-byte values**, while an unrestricted 201-digit GP29 string requires **977 bits / 123 bytes** for an injective positional serialization. The historical \`ssss\` 0.5 executable *can* represent larger fields, including a 984-bit field, so this is an exclusion of the **observed 2013 format**, not of every possible Shamir construction. No original Cicada instruction identifies a larger-field GP29-to-byte packing, exact field setting, five decimal share indices or its output register. The viable large-field interpretation lacks the independently selected inputs required to run a meaningful original-cipher inversion.

The five-dot image remains a legitimate structural observation. The \`ssss\` analogy is a conjecture; **no unknown LP2 plaintext, key, or decoder is recovered**.

## Independent source and original partition

- Original archive scan \`40.jpg\` / LP2 physical p23 shows five unlabeled black dots above a red16 heading; pp24–26 continue the same body. The source page-aligned record \`resources/liber-primus/lp2-corpus.json\` has lengths **[213, 270, 273, 265]**, summing to **1,021**. Remove precisely the first **16** rubric runes to obtain **1,005** body runes, or five complete interleaved columns of **201** GP29 indices each. These numbers are asserted directly by \`sims/lp2_p23_26_ssss_format_capacity_sft.cjs\`.
- The independently attested 2013 pklmx continuation uses Poettering's **\`ssss\` version 0.5**. Five preserved decimal-ID shares, reproduced in \`sims/2013_pklmx_reproduce.py\`, each carry **44 hexadecimal digits** (176 bits), and correctly recover the 22-byte ASCII onion address \`p7amjopgric7dfdi.onion\` using a five-of-ten threshold. The successful contemporary execution supplies a positive implementation control for the **historical 176-bit field**, but neither its 2013 identifiers nor bytes are known LP2 inputs.
- The original \`ssss.c\` uses decimal \`atoi\` for the share x-coordinate. Its \`combine\` uses \`4 * hex_length\` for binary-field degree, requires a multiple of eight up to 1024 bits, constructs an irreducible polynomial from its static table, subtracts the known monic \(x^5\) term, solves the interpolation system and reverses its XTEA-based diffusion. The documented historical field is \(GF(2^{176})\) with polynomial \(x^{176}+x^{11}+x^3+x^2+1\). ## Exact capacity obstruction

A complete 201-rune GP29 strand has \(29^{201}\) possible values. The maximum positional value is \(29^{201}-1\), which occupies **977 binary bits**; the information entropy is **976.454180020642 bits**. Therefore any lossless *fixed-width byte serialization of all possible strands* needs at least **123 bytes**, equivalent to a **984-bit** \`ssss\` field. The published **22-byte** share can hold at most \(2^{176}\) possibilities: a 201-rune string cannot be mapped into it injectively without an extra constraint that the source does not supply. Treating each rune as one byte yields **201 bytes**, above the historical program's **128-byte** maximum field size, and is likewise invalid.

A mathematically possible *new* serialization is to interpret each of the 201 GP29 indices as a base-29 digit, with the first printed digit most significant, and zero-extend the resulting integer to 123 big-endian bytes. The SFT constructs this reversible map on all five untouched original source columns and round-trips every rune including leading zeros. Under the original \`ssss\` 0.5 irreducible-polynomial table, the corresponding 984-bit field has polynomial \(x^{984}+x^{24}+x^9+x^3+1\). This demonstrates **compatibility of the field size only**. It does not show that the creator used that positional codec, reading direction, 984-bit degree, or any output interpretation.

A uniformly random 984-bit share falls into the 201-digit base29 image with probability exactly \(29^{201}/2^{984}\), approximately **0.00535157781049**, so requiring all five random historical-style shares to fit this fixed-width image without rejection sampling has probability approximately **4.38944 × 10⁻¹²**, conditional on independent uniform share values. This provides a strong *format prediction* against an incidental five-share visual coincidence, although a deliberate encoder could generate restricted shares or introduce another layer. It is not a cryptanalytic p-value.

## Why no unrestricted five-share test is meaningful

Five shares is exactly the SSSS threshold. For **any** five distinct chosen nonzero share IDs and **any** five field values, there is a unique degree-at-most-four interpolation polynomial after removal of the known degree-five monic term. Thus all five original strand values would always satisfy the same five-share interpolation equations. Perfect forward reconstruction proves only correct arithmetic, exactly as for planted shares. An unconstrained fifth-degree fit and round trip would be vacuous evidence of plaintext.

The original p23 artwork has **five unlabeled dots**, with no literal **1–10** IDs, hex separators, 123-byte length or positional-base29 instruction. A human solver could know about the 2013 SSSS stage, but this would leave both the binary codec and five x-values unspecified. There are **30,240** ordered distinct selections from the historical 1–10 IDs before choices of strand direction, interleaved/contiguous reading, byte endianness and diffusion interpretation. Searching these alternatives for anything vaguely text-like would contradict the independent-source gate and duplicate the unselected-key problem already studied in #541's *different* GF29 family.

## Handoff and decision

**Close #568 at its source/serialization gate.** The authentic 2013 format is capacity-incompatible with any direct, reversible encoding of a full 201-rune strand. The larger 984-bit option is supported by \`ssss\` software but has no LP2-selected binary serialization or x-coordinate assignment. Therefore no original p23–26 SSSS reconstruction should be promoted or submitted to #41. If a genuinely original LP2 instruction later specifies **both** the reversible GP29-to-984-bit serialization and exact five decimal indices, reopen a single frozen 984-bit inversion and require an independently recognizable decoded file/text or key, withheld page decryption, and authentic 2013 plus wrong-source controls.

This study does **not** exclude encoded *partial* shares embedded across pages, a source-derived secret from elsewhere, other five-input ciphers, or an unknown LP2 byte intermediate. These need independent evidence and a distinct proposed consumer.
