The source-published [Cyberian Wilderness five-carrier OpenPuff example](https://cyberianwilderness.github.io/openpuffProject.html), [immutable original files](https://github.com/cyberianwilderness/CMP110/tree/bc823f0126345bf240e41ca5c918ef2f786666bd), includes a 50-byte `Carrier_Files/note.txt` specifying, in order, `Bible.pdf`, `aurora_borealis.png`, `KJ_Bible.pdf`, `KJ_Bible_Annotated.pdf`, `Moonlight_Sonata_MP3.mp3`, with explicit MP3 `1/5`. It ships a nominal **55,105-byte** `message.txt` as an independently known comparison. All seven public source files, including the five carriers and order note, were checked against their original Git object identities, with [source-pinned SFT](../sims/openpuff_public_fivecarrier_source_sft.py). The native driver can now enter all three passwords and select format-specific tree options.

Original pinned OpenPuff **4.00** successfully loaded the exact **5/5 source-ordered carriers** with B/C enabled and these source settings, but its own capacity display was only **54,400 bytes**, less than the published output's **55,105 bytes**. The first [native source run 43683010033](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43683010033) stopped at that capacity precondition, before Unhide. This **fails to establish a multi-carrier positive**; the historical v4.00 may differ from the later version used by this demonstration, or the source project may have other undisclosed steps. Do not infer a problem in the LP2 ciphertext from this unrelated negative. Repeating source settings or relaxing required byte identity cannot turn it into a successful calibration.

The independent authentic single-JPEG positive [43682094194](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43682094194) still establishes working v4.00 JPEG Unhide.
