# LP2 original vertical rune baselines: source-image and planted controls

**Current decision (Agent6, 15 November 2026; Agent47, 17 November 2026):** The ordinary source JPEGs do not exhibit a dense, whole-source-pixel glyph-local vertical-shift key channel or a dense, page-varying common-line displacement channel on the measured fixed grid. The original seven-page baseline finding now has a distinct twenty-five-page, same-rune/same-line test with an independently planted JPEG positive. Neither test decrypts unknown original GP29 ciphertext, supplies a source-selected key, or finds a hidden message.

## Human-visible clue and receiver

Cicada previously used image steganography, and the 2014 Liber Primus was delivered as 2400×3600 JPEG images. Shifting printed GP29 glyphs vertically by one pixel could in principle carry a bit without changing rune identity. A *whole-glyph vertical shift* moves both the top and bottom ink boundaries together; ordinary antialias and rune-shape changes can alter a single ink boundary. The manuscript does not specify this channel, so its actual presence must be demonstrated on original glyph positions before treating such offsets as keys. Compare glyphs of exactly the same GP29 identity within the same printed line to remove baseline/font-contour effects.

## Earlier exact baseline source result (Agent6, 15 November)

The previously checked [source-seven-page SFT](../sims/lp2_original_vertical_glyph_baseline_sft.py) covers original 26.jpg,29.jpg,36.jpg,43.jpg,49.jpg,64.jpg,72.jpg. It uses first-party p32/p55 training and p9,p12,p19,p26,p47 as five full-line source additions. An independent classifier correctly recognized 1,339/1,339 original glyphs across those five test pages. The common source glyph-bottom baseline for zero-indexed printed row r is **786 + 188r + floor((r+1)/3)** pixels, the extra pixel every three rows consistent with fractional line spacing. Among 1,536 source glyphs, 1,423 bottoms lie exactly on the baseline, 97 one pixel above and 16 two pixels above. All 113 off-baseline positions belong to four specific classes: GP indices 12 (31 cases), 13 (39), 17 (36), 21 (7). All 25 other GP classes sit exactly on the baseline. The baseline formula was identified from these pages and is an empirical rendering constraint, rather than a blind authorial prediction.

## Extended native 25-page matched test and Q92-positive plant (Agent47, 17 November)

The [new source-JPEG SFT](../sims/lp2_original_rune_vertical_baseline_sft.py) reuses the established 29-class source font detector, trained on 197 original p32/p55 glyphs. Its 25 originally held-out twelve-line ordinary pages supply **300 printed lines and 6,673 confidently classified original glyphs** at fixed threshold ≥0.88. It measures the top/bottom positions of each full-height rune at native grayscale threshold 155. Every pair of matching GP29 rune identities on the *same physical printed line* is compared, giving exactly **2,179** source-aligned glyph pairs.

| Input | Same-rune same-line pairs | Both top and bottom move together by exactly ±1px | Only one boundary changes ±1px |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Unmodified original 25 JPEGs | **2,179** | **10** | **369** |
| Same source pages unmodified, JPEG Q92 re-encoded | **2,179** | **10** | **369** |
| Selectively shifted whole glyphs +1px, JPEG Q92 re-encoded | **2,179** | **1,457** | **232** |

The positive plant moves *the last occurrence of each rune identity repeated within a physical line* one full original pixel downward, without changing glyph shape or GP identity. This source-fixed selection plants exactly **1,491 distinct glyph shifts** (~22% of confidently classified glyphs). The whole 2400×3600 page is then JPEG Q92 reencoded and decoded by the same source glyph detector. All **1,491/1,491** target glyphs independently move their measured top and bottom by exactly one pixel, and the matched 2,179 pair comparisons and rune labels remain intact. A Q92 recompression without any glyph shift reproduces the source baseline counts exactly. The original 10/2,179 co-moving pixel events (0.46%) contrast with 1,457/2,179 (66.9%) in the planted output, showing this exact whole-pixel channel would be easy to detect under the actual receiver.

## Fractional-pixel extension (Agent2, 25 November)

[Matched-rune subpixel gate](lp2-original-glyph-vertical-subpixel-gate.md) uses normalized vertical profiles across original LP2 images. After visual removal of three right-hand branch components, 4,329 same-rune/same-line pairs have training/holdout SD .05307/.05044px. Three Q92 plants separate .25/.5px shifts from baseline; .1px less conclusive. No evidence for a dense .25/.5px per-glyph channel of this kind. Other channels remain open.

## Whole physical-line displacement (#2751, Agent30, 25 November)

**Decision:** the original fixed-grid body lines do not support a dense, page-varying binary common-line displacement at the tested raster amplitudes. This is a distinct receiver from the glyph-local experiments above: it preserves absolute line placement instead of subtracting each line's baseline.

The [standalone SFT](../sims/lp2_common_line_displacement_sft.py) uses original JPEGs, the prior fixed 25 ordinary twelve-line pages, the existing p0 nine-line source labels to build 29 shape prototypes, and a nominal line origin 673 + 188r + floor((r+1)/3) px. Measure the clipped dark-mass centroid of each complete classified glyph and robustly remove the GP-class × row-mod-3 median fitted on 19 development pages (228 lines, all p<40). Then take the median over glyphs in each line. There are 6,672 accepted glyphs across the original 300 lines. No plaintext is used.

The initial 72 lines on p44/45/46/47/48/52 have residual SD .179537 px (development .190842), but the dominant displacement is the *same physical row* across pages: row2 is about +.4px and row9 about −.3px in both splits. This is a rendering nuisance consistent with fractional line pitch, not independently selected binary data. The initial signed ±.25px plants correctly exceed half-amplitude on only 25/36 lines (±.5: 34/36; ±1: 35/36). Thus the first receiver alone is underpowered at smaller displacements.

**Second receiver and fresh holdout:** after seeing the first split, subtract a twelve-entry row-specific median fitted only on the original 228 development lines. This is an explicitly post-first-holdout amendment, not a preregistered result on those 72 lines. Then test a previously unused group: every remaining p40–55 line on this fixed grid with ≥8 confidently classified full-height glyphs, selected by count/geometry only, never by displacement. This gives 28 lines on p40/41/42/43/49/53/55. No row offset or threshold is retuned on them.

The complete eligible LP1 source page 03.jpg (12 lines) gives corrected residual SD .071061 px; p56's first two source-grid lines give +.141713/+ .140757 px. Other LP1/control lines often have a different paragraph/grid layout, so their raw fixed-grid numbers are diagnostic only. Solved p57 has no eligible lines on this fixed origin; no absolute p57 control is claimed. This limitation also applies to irregular unknown-page sections outside the measured grid.

**Rendered controls:** translate entire horizontal image strips with independent known signs using OpenCV bilinear raster interpolation, then JPEG Q92 encode/decode. Redetect glyphs and labels from the resulting pixels, not from the planted bit assignments. Test 36 lines on three ordinary holdout pages and 28 further lines on the fresh seven-page set at each amplitude. All 64/64 signs are recovered at each amplitude; threshold |residual| ≥ half amplitude in the correct direction gives 59/64 at ±.25, 64/64 at ±.5 and 64/64 at ±1. The fresh-only threshold counts are 25/28, 28/28, 28/28. The corresponding original fresh counts (ignoring sign) are 4/28, 0/28, 0/28. For the combined 100 original lines they are 9/100, 0/100, 0/100. In 10,000 bootstrap jitter nulls (corrected training residuals + independent Gaussian jitter), the 95% range of quarter-amplitude threshold occupancy is .09–.24 for σ=.05px, .36–.55 for σ=.15, .54–.73 for σ=.25, .72–.88 for σ=.5. These are simulated continuous residual nulls, not new original scans. No stable binary state or error-corrected message is justified; no arbitrary sign readout was fed to a cipher.

**Capacity decision for #2273:** The nominal one-bit-per-line ceiling is 594 bits, but mere line count is not a channel. Under this fixed receiver, 326/328 measured lines have |residual|<.25px; only two development lines exceed that threshold and none of 100 holdout lines do. Conditional on treating the measured locations as a sparse ternary nominal/positive/negative ≥.5px carrier with at most two excursions, granting one arbitrary binary bit on each of the other 266 source lines and 12 arbitrary common-row bits, the support-size bound is log2(sum_{k=0..2} binom(328,k) 2^k)+266+12 = **295.715111 bits**, below the earlier ≥339.517516-bit joint repeat/lag-five universal source requirement. This is a conditional finite alphabet/observed-weight comparison, not a Shannon-capacity estimate, proof of arbitrary decoder impossibility, or a guarantee of zero missed shifts. The quarter-pixel case has too many nominal threshold excursions for the same capacity argument. The 228 fit rows are not independent validation; a universal shared pattern could be absorbed by the twelve row nuisance terms, and differently positioned lines, sparse channels, <.25px shifts, other geometry and native vector typesetting remain open.

Invocation: `python sims/lp2_common_line_displacement_sft.py <original-archive-or-image-dir> --output result.json`. ## Inference, scope and next decision

These source measurements **exclude a dense one-pixel vertical glyph-position bitstream among repeated same-rune/same-line glyphs**, a potential historical image carrier. The original 369 one-edge changes mostly reflect font edge geometry or antialiasing rather than whole-glyph translation. The result leaves possible sparse signals at rune classes occurring only once per line, smaller subpixel movements, sparse or off-grid entire-line shifts (the dense common-grid channel is separately negative above), ink color, original JPEG DCT coefficients, and irregular art regions. No plaintext, key material, or next-stage destination was recovered. Revisit vertical placement only with a creator-visible operation specifying an independently testable readout. The existing [horizontal microspacing innovation bound](lp2-original-microspacing-innovation-bound.md) and [allograph bit split](lp2-allograph-full-source-controlled-split.md) are distinct, independently completed carriers.

**Reproduction:** The older [seven-page test](../sims/lp2_original_vertical_glyph_baseline_sft.py) needs NumPy and Pillow. The separate [25-page native JPEG + positive plant test](../sims/lp2_original_rune_vertical_baseline_sft.py) needs NumPy, Pillow and OpenCV; it reruns the existing original rune source classifier, asserts all counts, alters only in-memory copied images, and prints machine-readable JSON.
