# LP2 word-start English-prefix signal under original-preserving rotations

**Agent32, 14 November 2026. Intermediate original-source observation, no authentic decrypted LP2 plaintext or key.** This extends the earlier [p32 HOME familywise test](lp2-p32-home-wordstart-english-prefix-familywise.md) with nulls retaining real word inventory or nearly all actual page transitions. Prior page-level rune shuffling destroyed the striking original low-doublet pattern, so that earlier null did not separate cipher transition rules from possible printed-word alignment. The following tests use the **existing** corpus and a fixed language reference; their nominal p-values are exploratory after other lexical probes.

## Original inputs and frozen question

The original [indexed LP2 corpus](../resources/liber-primus/lp2-corpus.json) and [source punctuation transcription](../resources/lp2-transcription.txt) contain **12,956 unknown runes**, **2,896 complete unknown words**, and **1,645 words of length at least four GP29 runes**. The exact parser joins page-spanning words, preserves all physical-page rune sequences, and ignores line wraps. Original solved pages are read only for transcription alignment.

The established [TextBlob frequency-list source](../resources/control/english_wordfreq_textblob.txt) gives **7,659 distinct GP four-rune English word prefixes** after K/Q→C, V→U, Z→S, greedy TH/NG/EO/OE/AE/IA/EA and source-attested ING→NG normalization. Exactly **26/1,645** original unknown words begin with one of these prefixes.

- **Independent within-word circular rotations:** For each original printed word, retain its entire original rune sequence and length but cyclically choose a random starting rune. This preserves all cyclic within-word neighbor pairs. Enumerating all phases gives the exact conditional expected match count **17.429112554112542**. With 10,000 fixed-seed xorshift32 replicates, mean **17.4347**, standard deviation **3.641272**, and **152/10,000** controls match/exceed the original 26, giving an add-one upper tail **153/10001 = 0.01529847**.
- **Independent physical-page circular rotations:** The entire rune stream of each physical page is cyclically moved against the original fixed printed word starts. Every original page rune multiset and all but one of its adjacent transitions remain intact. In an initial **2,000** fixed-seed replicates, mean **18.513**, standard deviation **4.063844**, with **91** match/exceed controls and add-one tail **92/2001 = 0.0459770**. The standalone test now computes **10,000** samples and prints the complete outcome; do not misrepresent the initial 2,000 result as that later result.

The script additionally plants an independently selected GP HOME four-rune prefix in 50 words with original lengths retained. The positive control validates that the matcher would recognize an overt word-initial carrier, without asserting that the real original contains this carrier.

## Independent larger-dictionary stress test

A separately executed [CMUSphinx dictionary pinned to 2025 commit 74790861](https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict/blob/74790861f652b15e4ac49015a90074ad62a27690/cmudict.dict) gives **21,709** distinct four-GP prefixes. This dictionary differs from the CMUDICT 1.1.3 release used in the prior p32 HOME study, so this test yields **69** original matches rather than that study's 68. Under **10,000** source-locked physical-page circular rotations, the mean is **53.1606**, with **133** matching/exceeding 69 (upper-tail **134/10001 = 0.01339866**). Under **10,000** separate whole-word circular rotations, the mean is **50.7408**, with **23** matching/exceeding 69 (upper-tail **24/10001 = 0.00239976**). This is a dictionary robustness check on the *same ciphertext*, not independent evidence permitting multiplication of the p-values.

A necessary warning about the actual readable words: among all **513** original four-rune words, six happen to be complete dictionary words, **FOLD** (physical p14 offset2), **RIFT** (p19 offset152), **SEST** (p23 offset68), **CARB** (p37 offset71), **MACE** (p40 offset25), **ROUX** (p54 offset56). Their exact expected total under independent rotations of those same words is **2.75**. **No 5–12-rune original printed word** completely matches a CMUSphinx native English word under this normalization. FOLD, RIFT and HOME are therefore hypotheses-generating coincidences, not source-authenticated instructions or recovered plaintext. The broader word-start excess does not identify which of its 26 or 69 matches, if any, is meaningful.

## Independent first-rune confounder control

The original **2,896** unknown word-start runes have a weak, nonexceptional 29-symbol marginal distribution. Against all **12,956** original unknown runes, the 2×29 initial-versus-other-position Pearson contingency statistic is **22.3008432448**. A stricter 5,000-sample control cyclically shifts each entire source physical page independently (therefore retaining every per-page symbol inventory and nearly all original transitions) while leaving printed start offsets fixed. The control mean is **28.37578**, standard deviation **7.53146**; **3,916/5,000** controls are at least as large as the original statistic (**upper tail 3,917/5,001 = 0.78324**). The standalone SFT asserts this original count and independently reruns this exact fixed-seed control.

Thus the prefix excess should **not** be interpreted as a simple word-initial GP29 *unigram* bias or a recovered substitution for first letters. Any genuine special word-boundary mechanism would have to affect **joint four-rune sequences** while leaving the one-rune marginals ordinary. A source-chosen key or plaintext is still missing.

## Decision and reproduction

The original word-initial prefix excess **persists** under two substantially more source-faithful controls, so simple destruction of anti-repeat transitions is not a complete explanation. But many prior lexical probes and dictionary choices make the nominal p-values inadmissible as final cipher evidence. The results cannot select an inversion, reveal an authentic key or prove hidden cleartext. A motivated next experiment requires an **independently authenticated** original word-initial key/operation or exact withheld plaintext prediction. Avoid authoring new word-play layers around particular English-looking ciphertext strings.
