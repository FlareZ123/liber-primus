# p57 19-body-word modular direction graph: two 5-cycles sharing WE

**Classification:** candidate human solve-path structure; exploratory, not a decryption. The raw-marker start/target route below is independent of the H/+ factor-index reduction; the p55 fold → TWO P convergence is conditional.

p57 contains **20 words overall**: the red heading `PARABLE` plus 19 body words. The graph below deliberately treats the heading as a separate selector/seed (its GP-prime sum is 449, used in the conditional NUMBER(449)→944→59→95=5×19 chain) and uses the 19 body words as nodes. ## Provisional H/+ factor-index anchors (secondary)

The corrected 2013 first-onion transcript/server has its double-space markers after **29** and **3257**. Under NUMBER, 29 reverses to 92, whose distinct prime factors 2 and 23 reduce by the project's established factor-index rule to GP rune **H**; 3257 reverses to prime 7523 and returns **+**. The rule and marker correction are documented in [the word-signature note](2013-number-markers-p57-word-signature.md).

Applying the same GP-prime-sum / NUMBER factor-index reduction to p57's 19 body words gives unique matches:
- **LIKE**, body-word position 1, sum 184 = 2³×23, reduces to **H**.
- **FIND**, body-word position 14, sum 151 is prime and reverses to itself, so it returns **+**.

The ordered marker signatures H, + therefore select LIKE, FIND in the same order. They select candidate anchors, not both directions: the literal `+` supports FIND-plus, while the H signature does not independently mean subtraction or select LIKE-minus. The 19-word sum table is in the linked note above.

Separately, the 2013 NUMBER operation on the PARABLE sum 449 reverses to 944, whose divisors include 59. Earlier work then chose 59 for a derived follow-up, obtaining 95 = 5×19, but **59 was not independently selected from the many divisors of 944**.

A p55 route was proposed as the missing selector, but independent controls leave it conditional. Under conventional base60, p50 token `3D` is 193 and historical NUMBER(193) reverses to **391 = 17×23**. The 43 strict p55 word-interior runes do contain a striking exact geometry: ordinal GP23 **D** occurs at one-based positions **5 and23**, with **17** runes strictly between and a **19**-rune inclusive span. D is the only GP rune whose occurrence position equals its own zero-based ordinal (23), and it is the only rune occurring specifically in page-local strict-interior segments 5 and9.

Those facts do not source-select the navigation chain. NUMBER returns 17 and23 as mathematical prime factors. Reading them as GP prime weights gives **G/H**; reading raw 23 as zero-based alphabet ordinal gives **D**; the separate factor-index reduction instead maps 17/23 to indices 6+8 = GP14 **X**. No original instruction selects this change of representation. The same 43-rune interior also has **X at positions 13 and31**, again with 17 runes strictly between, a 19-rune inclusive span, and the identical fold mask **100000000₂ = 256**. D's span uniquely centers on I/prime31, but the gap and fold-mask structure are not D-only.

The source-compatible radix59 reading of the same printed token is **190**. Under the historical NUMBER semantics, composite 190 is factored directly as **2×5×19**. Thus the graph's 5 and19 do not discriminate the radix60/193 route.

Most importantly, the stronger proposed 59 derivation is circular as evidence: D's first position and span length are multiplied to make **5×19=95**, 95 is reversed to **59**, and NUMBER(59) then returns factors **5 and19**. Once 5 and19 have been selected, that loop is guaranteed by construction. It does not independently choose the formerly free divisor59 of NUMBER(449)=944.

### Conditional minus-FOLD exposes “TWO P” and selects WE

The D…D span leaves **17 runes strictly between** its two markers. The source route has already selected **FOLD**, and DIVINITY is the anchor of the graph's **minus** five-cycle. Under the conditional reading that this minus sign also specifies the fold arithmetic, combine the 17 runes outer-to-inner as **left minus right modulo29**, carrying the center forward. The first fold level begins with the exact GP runes **T W O**. Repeating the same fold recursively until one rune remains ends at **P**.

The complete strict-interior register of the 55-page unknown corpus contains **7,247 runes**. Across every 17-rune window in that register, the conjunction “first fold begins TWO” plus “recursive survivor is P” occurs **exactly once**, at this p55 D-between-D window. This is a corpus uniqueness statement for the selected register, not a discovery-wide p-value.

The natural numerical read “TWO P” is unusually well connected to the independently selected sources. P has GP prime43, so two P values total **86**. On p57, **WE** is the unique body word with GP-prime sum86. WE is also position8, the sole node shared by the two five-cycles, and it begins the actionable sentence “WE MUST SHED OUR OWN CIRCUMFERENCES. FIND THE DIVINITY WITHIN AND EMERGE.” Independently, the p27 title's selected outer **P/P** pair has the same total86. Thus p55's fold signature and p27's P/P converge on the p57 junction WE. Treat “TWO P” as a candidate routing instruction rather than recovered LP2 plaintext because the left-minus-right fold sign is inferred from the DIVINITY minus cycle.

Two source gates remain weak. First, the 2013 H/+ reduction identifies LIKE and FIND as anchors, but H does not select the minus sign; only FIND-plus has a literal plus-marker interpretation. **Decision:** retain the cycle intersection as a conditional map-structure clue, but do not claim a unique directed route, a source-fixed two-cycle operation, a key, or recovered plaintext. ## Reproducible map operation

Number the 19 body words 1–19 in source order. Let S_i be the sum of the GP prime weights of word i. Use the source-selected modulus 19 and the directed step (with residue 0 represented by position 19):

[
f_{+}(i)=i+S_i\pmod{19},qquad
f_{-}(i)=i-S_i\pmod{19}.
]

The marker-selected LIKE and FIND anchors produce:

- **LIKE (1) under +:** (184\bmod19=13), so 1→14, landing exactly on **FIND**.
- **FIND (14) under +:** (151\bmod19=18), so 14→13, landing on **CIRCUMFERENCES**.
- Continuing the + map gives the 5-cycle:

[
	ext{FIND}_{14}\to
	ext{CIRCUMFERENCES}_{13}\to
	ext{WE}_{8}\to
	ext{AND}_{18}\to
	ext{TO}_{5}\to
	ext{FIND}_{14}.
]

The opposite direction from LIKE gives a second, conditional 5-cycle; the H anchor does not itself select this minus sign:

[
	ext{LIKE}_{1}\to
	ext{SURFACE}_{7}\to
	ext{WE}_{8}\to
	ext{WITHIN}_{17}\to
	ext{DIVINITY}_{16}\to
	ext{LIKE}_{1}.
]

The cycles are both length 5 and share exactly one node: **WE**, position 8. Thus the 19-word graph has a figure-eight structure around WE under opposite directions. The positive path from LIKE feeds directly into the FIND cycle.

The two cycles' word meanings are strikingly consonant with the parable's key phrases (“like ... to the surface”, “we ... find the divinity within”, “shed ... circumferences”), but this semantic reading is interpretive, not a recovered plaintext.

## Raw 2013 marker start/target route (new candidate)

The same first-onion source gives raw whitespace-selected integers 29 and 3257. Before applying the historical NUMBER reversal or the later H/+ factor-index reduction, reduce these raw values by the 19 body-word count:

- 29 mod 19 = 10: **SHED**, the start node.
- 3257 mod 19 = 8: **WE**, the target node.

This uses a different interpretation of the same markers from the H/+ → LIKE/FIND association above. It is independent of the p55 fold and does not rely on H/+.

With the GP-prime word-sum map already defined, the forward walk is a locked three-step prediction:

- SHED(10): 232 mod 19 = 4, so 10 → 14 (**FIND**).
- FIND(14): 151 mod 19 = 18, so 14 → 13 (**CIRCUMFERENCES**).
- CIRCUMFERENCES(13): 451 mod 19 = 14, so 13 → 8 (**WE**).

The reverse map instead enters SHED(10) ↔ THE(6), never reaching WE. The raw marker target therefore selects the forward direction without requiring a post-hoc H→negative convention.

Controls:
- Including the heading PARABLE as a twentieth node and using modulus 20 gives start 9 (WE) and target 17 (DIVINITY); neither direction reaches the target, and both enter the INSTAR fixed point.
- Zero-based and one-based GP-rune ordinal sums do not reach WE from SHED in either direction.
- Rune-count steps do not reach WE in the forward direction; the reverse count-map reaches it in four steps. The signed 2016 wording points to numerical word values, and Gematria Primus supplies prime weights, making GP-prime sums the better-motivated measure.
- A fixed-start shuffle of the other 18 word sums gives the exact forward-three-step / reverse-nonarrival event in 3,855 of 100,000 trials (3.855%). This is modest, so the raw path is a source-motivated check, not proof by itself.

The 2016 signed message's “words are the map” / “numbers are the direction” wording supports the broad construction, but does not explicitly prescribe the addition rule or modulus. Keep this as a candidate intended route, not an established cipher.

## Alternative numeric controls

### All-20-word map (heading included): a competing route

If the red heading PARABLE is included as a 20th node and the total-word count 20 is used as modulus, the selected anchors converge to **INSTAR** under both directions:

- In the + map, LIKE (position 2, 184 mod 20 = 4) and FIND (position 15, 151 mod 20 = 11) both land on **TO** (position 6). The shared path is TO → OUR → OWN → SURFACE → CIRCUMFERENCES → TUNNELING → INSTAR.
- In the − map, LIKE → WITHIN → THE → SHED → AND → INSTAR, while FIND → INSTAR directly.
- INSTAR is a fixed point because 280 mod 20 = 0. EMERGE (300 mod 20 = 0) is another fixed point, but neither selected anchor reaches it.

A separate 100,000-permutation null fixes LIKE/FIND positions and sums and shuffles the other 18 word sums. Both anchors reach INSTAR under both signs in 604/100,000 (0.604%); under the − map alone the frequency is 2.867%, and under the + map alone 2.344%. This is an exploratory null, not a formal p-value; word sums are not guaranteed exchangeable.

The 20-node LIKE/FIND map remains a competing structure, but the raw-marker start/target check favors the 19-body map: modulo 19 selects SHED(10) and WE(8), and only the forward prime-sum walk reaches that target. The 20-word raw-marker control fails. This is source-motivated but not conclusive; the heading/body distinction and conditional 95=5×19 chain also support the 19-body interpretation.

### Alternative numeric controls

The two 19-node 5-cycles do not appear when the body-word values are replaced by the sum of rune ordinals or by rune count:
- With ordinal sums, the negative walk from LIKE reaches the fixed point TO, and the positive walk from FIND reaches the fixed point WITHIN.
- With rune counts, the negative walk from LIKE has a different 4-cycle (DIVINITY → WE → THE → TUNNELING), while the positive walk from FIND has a different 5-cycle (FIND → AND → THE → TUNNELING → OUR). They do not form the observed pair of 5-cycles sharing WE.

This supports the specificity of GP-prime word sums as the tested numeric representation, but it does not independently prove that this is the intended representation.

## Permutation null

The reproducible script [`sims/p57_word_map_19_direction_cycles.py`](../sims/p57_word_map_19_direction_cycles.py) fixes the two marker-selected anchor sums at LIKE position 1 and FIND position 14, shuffles the other 17 word sums, and tests all four sign assignments for 100,000 permutations (seed 20261112).

- Both selected nodes lie in 5-cycles for some sign assignment in 24,721/100,000 permutations (24.721%): not rare by itself.
- Both lie in 5-cycles that intersect at exactly one node in 860/100,000 (0.860%).
- Both lie in 5-cycles whose shared node is specifically WE in 32/100,000 (0.032%).

The conservative relevant frequency is **0.860%**, because the sign assignment and shared-node identity were not independently fixed before observing the graph. The 0.032% WE-specific frequency is descriptive and post-selected. This is a simple permutation diagnostic, not a formal p-value: word sums are not guaranteed exchangeable, and the 5-cycle length was motivated by the conditional 5×19 factorization.
