## External 2026 ledger: avoid turning partial coverage into a false premise

The external ledger says its Python 2.7 `random.seed(w)` **`exact` / `keyskip1`** experiment reached **2,059,308,008 of 2^32** seeds, or **47.947%**, by 31 October 2026. Crucially, **44.138 percentage points** were screened under the earlier nine-register Round19 panel and **3.809 percentage points** under the later panel-R36; the earlier mass was *not* re-adjudicated under panel-R36 and its original claim threshold was not recalibrated for the 12-register panel. The separate Python **`pair` / `keyskip2`** row did cover 2^32 but under its own limited reducer/clock/control assumptions, with English-only planted power explicitly recorded. The PHP `mt_rand` **uniform** tier covered approximately **1%** of 2^32 per variant, not 1% of an author-selected input space. In every case the tested relation includes its own choices of offset, reduction, sign, skipped draws and language register; no Cicada-authenticated text fixes them.

These figures are valuable *conditional* performance measurements, not an independently motivated target for fresh CPU-days. They cannot identify what generator Cicada used. Do not silently combine the 47.947% mixed-instrument number into a uniformly high-power negative; do not construe the other ~52% as a promising space without an authorial reason to privilege MT.

## Stop decision and reopening contract

**Do not launch a new MT/PHP/LCG/Bash seed extension or rename an existing near-miss as a key.** There is presently no historical joint selector to freeze an original-input model for the `>=100 contiguous + >=40 withheld` requirement in #924. A positive plant under a researcher-selected RNG would only confirm the implementation.

Reopen this exact branch upon discovery of an authenticated pre/contemporary LP2 clue or preserved author-operated executable that specifies, *independently of the unknown ciphertext*: (i) generator/version; (ii) original bytes fixing seed/nonce to a small domain; (iii) GP29 digit conversion; (iv) stream initialization/advancement and treatment of original printed word/page/F state. Then freeze a complete decoder, test solved p56 or GP-native withheld positive, and make the issue's original 100+40-rune prediction. A newly found **participant-authored** tool cannot supply the missing authorial choice without a separate Cicada-authenticated link.

**Decision for Director:** retire #924 as an unlicensed search branch under current evidence and allocate effort to source-led recovery of an actual unknown-page key, reversible operation, or original ciphertext-bearing continuation. Preserve the external measured ceiling only as scoped prior computational evidence.
