# A source-exact 16 × 203 black-rune register in floral LP2, and the p15 value 3299

**Status (23 November 2026): established source-selection arithmetic, with a negative direct Hill16 consumer; no unknown plaintext, source-authenticated key, or solved LP2 page.** A new bounded test treated the original 256-token p49–51 table as a 16×16 GF29 Hill matrix acting on the 3248 black-floral runes. Its exact original-source inverse gave four near-uniform outputs with IoCs 0.03440–0.03456 despite full matrix invertibility and a 6496-rune native positive-control roundtrip. The formal original-cipher decision is in [the focused Hill16 gate](lp2-floral-p50-16hill-original-gate.md). The source itself does not authorize the reshaping, so this is a finite exclusion of a plausible source-shaped candidate rather than proof that the floral grid has no role. Original pages 40–55 are the continuous sixteen flower-framed physical pages. Two alternative, carefully distinguished rune selections have exact factorizations but do not supply a standalone decipherment instruction.

## Frozen original-cipher experiments

The [read-only CommonJS SFT](../sims/lp2_floral16_3299_source_geometry_sft.cjs), **validated successfully on original repository inputs by [Windows Node 22 CI run 45161417589](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/45161417589)**, fixes the exact original inputs above before testing: (i) whether the 3,248 black symbols, placed into sixteen consecutive 203-rune rows, exhibit common-column equality compatible with one reused key; (ii) the full-length index permutation **[0, 2^0 mod 3299, …, 2^3297 mod 3299]**, which visits all 3,299 positions because 2 has multiplicative order 3,298 modulo 3299; (iii) the literal finite GP29 key **K[i]=(2^i mod 3299) mod 29** applied in original position order. These two p15-to-3299 transfer operators are hypotheses introduced in this experiment and have no independent original-page opcode.

For the 16×203 arrangement, the original same-column equality count is **857/24,360 pairs**, collision probability **0.035180624**. In **2,048** deterministic, seeded, independent circular row-rotation controls (which preserve every source row's symbols and internal order), the mean is **840.458**, with range **757–932**. **574/2,048** controls equal or exceed 857; the plus-one one-sided Monte Carlo tail is **575/2049 ≈ 0.28062**. Thus there is no meaningful positive indication of a shared, unshifted 203-position monoalphabetic key from this statistic.

The complete unmodified 3299-tape's GP29 IoC is **0.034464934**. Reordering its positions by modular doubling preserves that exactly, yields **124 adjacent-equal glyph pairs**, and does not reveal GP-native prose. The direct modular-doubling residue-subtraction candidate yields IoC **0.034515669** and nonlinguistic output. For comparison, the solved p57 control's GP29 IoC is approximately **0.062709966**. The SFT includes complete invertible synthetic p57-text plants as a regression, which establishes correctness of the proposed machinery but does not validate its historical use.

## Independent physical-page-order discriminator

One natural human proposal is to lay out the sixteen flower pages in a 4×4 sheet and read them in the p15 spiral order, treating the sixteen 2014 pages as the sixteen source square cells. The original [page-preserving transcription](../resources/liber-primus/lp2-transcription.txt) supplies a direct falsifier for interpreting that as a *literal continuous prose reading*: **12** flower-section physically adjacent page seams carry an unfinished runic word across the boundary (40→41 through 48→49, 51→52, 52→53, 54→55). The original order preserves all **12/12** joins. With pages mapped row-major to the 4×4 p15 grid, its source-center-first spiral gives page order **45,46,50,49,48,44,40,41,42,43,47,51,55,54,53,52**, preserving only **4/12** original word joins. The SFT parses the unmodified source transcript and asserts the full list before evaluating that order.

That strongly rejects a bare **physical-page spiral rearrangement as a plaintext reading order**; it does not exclude a secret-position transposition that first destroys and later reconstructs word breaks. The source itself does not indicate such reconstruction.

## Decision for further research

**New usable observation:** the manuscript supports a 16×203 full-black register, and a separate 3299-rune register spanning the same physical pages. These are precisely specified alternative source selections, not interchangeable ciphertexts.

**Strongest objection:** the 3299 selection was noticed after inspecting p15, and the idea to convert 3299 into a modular doubling permutation is not printed as a rule. The register count alone might reflect composition and editorial segment lengths, while 16×203 may be coincidental. The observed original equality profile provides no positive evidence for one simple 203-clock.

**Practical fork:** obtain a period-authentic or original-page visual instruction that selects 203 columns, the p53 coda inclusion, and a reversible alphabet/key operation, then apply that operator unchanged to a substantial native original page with an independent holdout. In its absence, do not enlarge into a 203-period key sweep, p50 AES/IV menu, or other adjusted cipher family. Keep this precise registration as an intermediate structural lead. **No GP29 plaintext, solved page or destination recovered.**
