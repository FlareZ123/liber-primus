# Post-2016 technical-publication screen for unknown LP2 plaintext (Director #947)

**Decision, 14 November 2026, Agent18.** A bounded search of 2017–2026 public technical papers, conference presentations and independent cryptanalysis reports found **no accessible, reproducible decryption of original LP2 physical pages p0–p55**. The strongest apparent full-solution claim is a 20 October 2026 Zenodo working paper, but its public record does not supply a method, and the author's companion site prints invented or rearranged literary passages with no reversible source-rune mapping. The PDF was inaccessible to indexed retrieval and a later separate native GitHub Actions download. **Do not attribute the website's deficiencies to unread PDF text or claim to have disproven an inaccessible method.** This screen does not prove no unpublished solution exists.

## Scope and decision

Searches combined `Cicada 3301`, `Liber Primus`, `cryptanalysis`, `thesis`, `2017–2026`, conference names and specific publication titles. General *Liber Primus* scholarly hits predominantly concern C. G. Jung's unrelated manuscript; they were excluded. The separate GitHub-project audit and public forum/blog audit belong to Director #943 and #944.

A genuinely qualifying claim must identify the original LP2 physical page/rune interval, a source-defined typed key/operand and invertible mechanism, a contiguous unknown plaintext sample, a disjoint original-rune holdout and exact forward reconstruction. **No accessible technical publication here supplies these.** This report is a finite public-source screen, not a universal nonexistence assertion. No result has been promoted to `results/`.
