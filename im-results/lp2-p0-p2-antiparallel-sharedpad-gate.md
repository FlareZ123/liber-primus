# Opening cross motif: exact antiparallel shared-pad inverse

The original p0–2 scans share opposing 180° rotational triple-bar cross artwork. The [forward shared-pad gate](lp2-p0-p2-orthodox-cross-source-plate-gate.md) compares same-start pads. This experiment tests a distinct executable consequence of opposite orientation: a page starts consuming a common additive pad at its opposite physical end. The illustration alone does not establish pad reuse.

For each pair, align oriented page starts, with A forward/B reversed and vice versa. If C_A=P_A+K and C_B=P_B+K in GF29, P_A-P_B=C_A-C_B. The inverse considers every pad without enumerating 29^n possibilities. Whole printed words from the two oriented pages become interval variables; each lexical candidate implies an exact key substring. A wildcard costs one OOV and permits arbitrary key symbols. Page-edge fragments and overlap-cut words impose no constraint.

**Constructive exact algorithm:** the overlap graph of the two disjoint-interval families is a forest. Sweeping from left to right, every overlap edge introduced at a word start joins a previously unseen interval to at most one active opposite interval; simultaneous starts form a new component. Hence no cycle. For each vertex, compute a minimum-cost subtree assignment for every lexical key substring and wildcard. For every child, index candidates by the substring on its shared interval. A fixed parent candidate uses the cheapest identical substring or child wildcard; a wildcard parent can independently select each child's cheapest candidate because its child overlap intervals are disjoint. The parent overlap is also disjoint from all child overlaps. This proves the optimum over the entire finite dictionary plus unconstrained OOV options. A backtrace constructs actual common pads and plaintexts; every witness re-encrypts both streams and independently recounts its exact OOV cost. This replaces the slower per-rune product-trie enumeration; the initial full product-trie run timed out without results. Three independent 29^2 exhaustive tiny controls agree with the forest solver.

## Independent literal-F consuming clocks — Agent2, 26 November 2026

Retain the same p0–2 full physical-page starts, opposite traversal directions and shared arbitrary GP29 additive key. In each oriented stream separately, P=F emits C=F without consuming a key digit; a non-F P consumes K[t] and emits C=(P+K[t]) mod29, including possibly C=F. This is the authentic literal-F convention added to the existing source-motivated reflection hypothesis, not a newly authenticated cipher instruction. The pad prefixes align in **consuming** time rather than physical-rune time; both page-end fragments are free, and once either stream ends the remaining key suffix is unconstrained by that stream. The same 29,061-form TextBlob GP29 lexicon and original printed word boundaries are used. All complete words on both pages are scored, including those beyond the common prefix (the individual minimum there is included; each original individual relaxation costs zero). The red p0 heading is treated as ordinary text, as in the previous test.

**Exact bounded inverse.** Build forward/reverse finite lexical tries by word length, merge equivalent suffix subtrees into a 47,340-node acyclic automaton. State (i,j,u,v) tracks physical positions and the two lexical prefix states. A literal-F edge advances only its stream at C=0. A consuming edge advances both, tries P_A=1..28 and fixes P_B=P_A+C_B-C_A mod29, excluding zero. If a word first leaves its dictionary trie, charge one OOV and allow arbitrary legal plaintext until its boundary; it cannot charge again. Hence cost is exactly the number of nonlexical complete words. Free page fragments never charge. Memoize the greatest exhausted remaining budget for each state; each edge advances one or both physical positions, making the graph acyclic. Independent single-stream tail optima provide an admissible additive lower bound; after either stream ends they give the exact remaining cost. Iterative budgets 0..4 decide feasibility for all keys and all independent F histories within that cost. Successful witnesses replay the shared consuming tape and independently recount OOVs. This is a constructive feasibility receiver, **not** an identified-key solver. The earlier exact table-flank trie receiver is the unweighted zero-OOV special case; this extends it to charged lexical exceptions and antiparallel orientation.

## Reflected and arbitrary proportional pads — Agent1, 26 November 2026

The physical opposition of the p0–2 crosses might reverse the **sign** of the key as well as the reading direction. This is a concrete untested alternative to the equal-sign ordinary receiver above. The exact forest inverse grants every relative coefficient r=1..28, with r=28 the reflected sign. No source authenticates that coefficient, an arbitrary scaling, or pad reuse. The fixed physical starts, opposite page directions, overlap cuts, printed group boundaries, and native dictionary are inherited unchanged; no independent offsets or latent F holds are granted in this extension.

For C_A=P_A+K, C_B=P_B+rK in GF29, an A-side lexical word supplies a key substring K=C_A−P_A; a B-side word supplies K=r⁻¹(C_B−P_B). This substitutes a bijective field scaling in the existing interval-forest dynamic program. It retains exactness for each r and all possible key strings, with one OOV exception per wildcard. Exhausting the 28 coefficients yields the exact global lexical minimum over this family. The backtrace constructs both plaintexts and a pad; each saved witness is independently recounted and re-encrypted. Nine 29² exhaustive toy cases, over three input pairs and coefficients 1,2,28, agree.
