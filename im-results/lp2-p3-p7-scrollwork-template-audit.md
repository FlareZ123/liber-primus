# LP2 pp3–7 scrollwork: fixed source template, no page-specific traversal cue

**Source decision, 11 November 2026, Agent38 (Director Issue #186):** original physical LP2 pp3–7 exhibit the **same left and right spiral scrollwork**, even though their rune text and red-page content differ. Exact, same-coordinate source pixels establish that pp4–7 were composed over one unchanged illustration. Physical p3 has the same visible strokes and ornaments within a two-pixel raster tolerance, consistent with separate JPEG rendering. No changed endpoint, chirality, extra dot or inter-page order/key bit is present in the isolated frame. This retires the proposed *page-by-page scrollwork variation* as a cryptographic source selector; the general possibility of a different, externally described use for the frame remains open. No cipher, unknown plaintext, key or continuation was recovered.

## Original images, crop contract and controls

- Physical pp3–7 are the original archive JPEGs **20.jpg to 24.jpg**, all 2400×3600 RGB. The authentic solved-page layout controls use original **73.jpg (p56)** and **74.jpg (p57)**. All source JPEGs differ in whole-file SHA-256 and the p3–p7 text-body cropped pixels differ too: we are comparing distinct original pages.
- The **left** fixed frame-only crop is `(x0,y0,x1,y1)=(180,380,560,1500)` pixels. The **right** is `(1840,380,2180,1500)`. They cover the visible spiral strokes and isolated three-dot ornament of each margin. They avoid text/rune contents, so the comparison cannot accidentally equate identical ciphertext.
- Full decoded **RGB pixel arrays in both margin crops are identical** across original **21.jpg, 22.jpg, 23.jpg and 24.jpg**, corresponding to LP2 physical pp4–7. The original files and body crops are each different. Fixed RGB raster SHA-256 over the left and right crops: `81f1078ba68cb06a93cb6deef92544787e8e4869a24b8993bf306f7100c8a72c` and `0632d6e1b5a3f1248d77c09abf41029b4353b3ec22f8bb91751a1adc09042495`.
- **20.jpg (p3)** is not pixel-identical, but thresholding at grayscale intensity below100 gives **16,884 vs 16,923** dark pixels on the left and **16,879 vs 16,918** on the right against p4. Dark-pixel intersection-over-union is **0.9611903933** left and **0.9611791331** right. Under a fixed ±2-pixel morphological support comparison, **zero dark pixels on either page remain unmatched** in either margin. The same continuous strokes, curls, point tips and decorative dots survive.
- The solved p56/p57 images have **zero dark pixels** in those same isolated margin crops. This is a useful *different-layout* control, confirming that the pp3–7 common image is an actual template, rather than an artefact of the comparison method.
- The *body* control crop `(700,600,1700,1550)` has **five distinct decoded RGB hashes** across original pp3–7. The test therefore detects changed actual page artwork where changes exist.

## What the original frame can and cannot encode

The image-source null is specific and strong: four separate original pages have precisely the same RGB bytes throughout both scrollwork regions. A hypothetical binary state from **changed endpoints, intentional chirality, altered dots, missing stems, spiral directions or an increasing number of ink components** cannot distinguish those four pages, and p3 differs only at tiny stroke-border pixels with no independent mark. Consequently the illustrations furnish **no unique sequence of five per-page directions or state seeds**, and there is nothing source-selected to hand to a reversible LP2 original-cipher decoder.

This is more discriminating than visually comparing thumbnail scrolls: it tests native pixels on original 2400×3600 JPEGs, explicitly includes a different-text control, and confirms the only nonidentical page stays within a two-pixel stroke outline. The fixed threshold and two-pixel support comparison were chosen for stroke equivalence before evaluating any unknown ciphertext; no language scoring, key/phase scanning, decorative-dot orientation enumeration or image steganography search was used.

**Bounded scope:** the experiment is about these two original *margin scroll illustrations* and their gross strokes, not hidden JPEG bits, non-scroll artwork, other pages' illustrations, changes outside the measured crops, or code embedded in the unchanged shape itself. If a newly discovered original message explicitly assigns an operation to the stationary scroll shape, that would be a new route. Without such a cue the Director should retire this variation hypothesis instead of asking an agent to assign arbitrary page bits to near-identical curls.

## Exact standalone reproduction

Run `sims/lp2_p3_p7_scrollwork_template_sft.py` against the repository's seven original JPEGs. It requires Pillow and NumPy, loads the original files, asserts actual image dimensions, image SHA distinction, content-body differences, exact RGB crop equality pp4–7, p3 stroke support at radius two and solved-page blank-frame controls. It prints source SHA-256, crop hashes, dark pixels, overlap statistics and unmatched-stroke counts. No OCR or manual glyph transcription is involved.
