## Frozen new original-input handoff: copied moth → solved prose key

The LP1 p5 moth/primes/totient illustration is replicated in LP2 physical **p53** and **p55**. Together with the original 2014 successful **text-guided** rune Vigenère stage, this motivates one complete low-assumption test: select the *entire* LP1 p5 SOME WISDOM solved prose through KNOW THIS, excluding its separate numeric square, serialize as **82 canonical GP tokens**, and use it as a repeating additive key at offset zero for each image-matched physical LP2 page. Correct historical GP subtraction consumes every F, requires no guessing, and re-encodes every original cipher rune.

**Original p53:** 179 ciphertext runes, decoded IoC **0.03383341** versus source **0.03351955**, with GP opening `YOENWMLRMJLEALMEEATCTONG...`. **Original p55:** 76 rune positions, decoded IoC **0.03578947** versus source **0.03789474**, with opening `GOEFNGFCTHLTAEIBIIASINNOE...`. Neither gives coherent plaintext. The exact historical 2014 18-rune onion-address positive and a synthetic encryption/decryption of genuine solved p57 GP prose both succeed in the same [single-file test](../sims/lp2_lp1p5_moth_textguide_original_sft.cjs).

The **strongest unsupported premise** is that a copied illustration both selects the source prose as a key and instructs the Stage04 operation on a later manuscript. The moth occurs twice; p55's first physical-page rune continues a word from p54, so even key position zero is not guaranteed by a word boundary. The source image supplies no text-key selection rule comparable to “Let the text guide you.” Once the complete literal reading fails, adjusting key start, red-only sections, skip masks, direction or phase for better English would defeat the frozen claim.

## Decision for future original-LP2 cryptanalysis

No source-authentic creator-era rule above supplies **all three necessary inputs** for an additional unbroken LP2 passage: an unambiguous **target/ordering**, a **rune-changing operation**, and a **source-derived key with reset/exception clock**. Previous stationary-symbol, period-7/14 permutation and unconstrained Vigenère tests cannot supply missing original instructions, even when they produce favorable synthetic controls. Recover an independent authored clue, missing historical payload or authentic plaintext crib that selects at least one of those missing inputs before extending a dependent decoder. A successful candidate must yield **at least 40 previously unknown original GP rune symbols as readable plaintext** and reproduce the source ciphertext without postselection. Current research has no such recovery.
