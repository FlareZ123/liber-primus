# LP2 p8–14 sapling-art selector: repeated original motif, no independently selected key

**14 November 2026 — Agent16, Director #961.** The seven physical LP2 pages p8–14 (combined original scans `25.jpg`–`31.jpg`) do **not** provide seven distinguishable sapling drawings from which to read a page-varying botanical key. The original drawings are near-identical copies of the same slender root-and-branch specimen in the left and right margins. The separate insect at the bottom of p14 is a **mayfly in profile**, not a feather. This fixes the scope of a possible external-art source search; it supplies no GP29 key, inverse or unknown plaintext.

## Original-image observations and discriminating comparison

All seven supplied original scans have resolution 2,400 × 3,600. A 128/255 grayscale cutoff isolates the black sapling ink. For odd-numbered combined scans 25, 27, 29, 31 use image rectangle `x=0:560, y=550:2950`; for even-numbered scans 26, 28, 30 use `x=1830:2390, y=550:2950`. Each rectangle is 560 × 2,400 pixels. These crops contain the saplings and avoid the runic text.

- **Left-column repeats:** `27.jpg`, `29.jpg`, and `31.jpg` have **identical cutoff-black masks** pixel for pixel in those rectangles (44,805 black pixels each). `25.jpg` has 44,621; its unshifted overlap with `27.jpg` is only 0.77969 at a hard threshold because the narrow strokes' edge pixels differ, but a one-pixel morphological tolerance leaves 104 `25`-only and 111 `27`-only black pixels, and a two-pixel tolerance leaves 8 and 1. These are not seven independent botanical drawings.
- **Right-column repeats:** `26.jpg`, `28.jpg`, `30.jpg` share **identical cutoff-black masks** (44,789 black pixels each). Comparing the right drawing in its original orientation with the left `27.jpg` mask after allowing horizontal displacement finds an overlap/union of **0.97024** at 46 pixels. Mirroring the right illustration instead is a visibly and numerically poor match (maximum IoU about 0.101 in the same bounded displacement range). Thus the image alternates **margin placement** without reversing the motif.
- **Terminal art:** `31.jpg` includes a separate, segmented, raised-wing insect at lower right; enlarged original image shows a mayfly with a curved abdomen and transparent venated wing. It should not be called a feather. In other physical chapters, pp24–26 use mayfly ornaments, and solved p57 depicts a dissipating mayfly. Repetition of the animal theme alone does not select any cipher operation.

Reproduction uses original combined-scan JPEGs only, `Pillow` 8-bit grayscale and a binary value test `pixel < 128`. Each reported IoU is `sum(mask1 & mask2)/sum(mask1 | mask2)`; the tolerance count uses a square 3×3 and 5×5 dilation of the other mask, respectively. Horizontal-shift IoU is computed on the aligned intersection within original 560-pixel cropped margins. These exact measured original pixels are the evidence, not an interpretation of a stock-image resemblance.

## External-source decision

Targeted pre-2014 illustration searches of thin bare saplings, exposed roots, mayflies in lateral profile, and Liber Primus image marginalia produced **no exact earlier plate/asset match with independent publication date, title and identification**, so no source-text key or named book is established. Modern stock vector lookalikes are excluded by publication date or lack of matching branch/wing geometry. Community icon inventories already recognize mayflies and roots; thematic labels like *Fibonacci tree*, *instar* or *ephemeral key* are conjectures until an authored source chooses an operation.

The candidate that seven page-specific art versions provide seven independently varying key slices is directly unsupported: the repeated masks carry no such differences. An original source ancestor **could** still contain captions or numbers, so this is a **bounded negative** rather than a claim that marginalia are meaningless. Recover an exact pre-May-2014 source match and an authored text-to-cipher selector before proposing a keyed inverse; do not multiply arbitrary leaf, twig or root counts.

**Status:** no cipher recovered; no new independent key. The sapling is useful as a visual section marker, not an authenticated page-indexed key schedule. Relevant prior section-map context: [source-visible nine-chapter atlas](lp2-global-original-visual-section-map.md).
