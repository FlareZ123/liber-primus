# Source-original chained mod-28 emitter: low-bit linear-state gate

## Exact decision (13 November 2026)

The original 55 rune-bearing unknown LP2 pages contain 12,956 GP29 cipher runes and only 86 identical page-local adjacent pairs. This makes a no-repeat *chained emitter* worth testing as one simple implementation-level candidate, but the source supplies no instruction to choose it.

Freeze the following **specific** direct model for a non-exception step, using canonical GP29 rune indices `C_i ∈ {0,…,28}`:

```text
C_i = (C_(i-1) + 1 + (R_i mod 28)) mod 29
```

where `R_i` is one directly emitted 32-bit word of a GF(2)-linear pseudorandom generator. The 86 original zero increments are *exceptions* to this restricted model, and this experiment does not assign them any explanatory meaning. Consecutive nonzero increments within one original physical page require no assumptions about what happens on exception sites or at a page reset.

For a nonzero increment, the observed value `D_i = (C_i - C_(i-1)) mod 29` is in 1..28. Hence `D_i - 1 = R_i mod 28`. Because 28 is divisible by 4, the **two least significant bits** of `D_i - 1` are *exactly* the two least significant bits of `R_i`. An autonomous GF(2)-linear emitter with at most 128 internal state bits necessarily has binary Berlekamp–Massey linear complexity at most 128 in either output bitstream, regardless of the unknown starting state, output tap or initial time.

The original LP2 **p17** has a contiguous sequence of **272 nonzero within-page differences**, at local difference indices 1..272, and the observed two bitstreams have linear complexities **138** and **140**. One sequence already contradicts every **≤128-state-bit autonomous GF(2)-linear generator under the frozen direct emission model**. Four additional source-local sequences corroborate the incompatibility:

| Physical page | Start difference index | Uninterrupted steps | Low-bit 0 LC | Low-bit 1 LC |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 5 | 1 | 262 | 131 | 132 |
| 13 | 1 | 271 | 135 | 136 |
| 17 | 1 | 272 | 138 | 140 |
| 28 | 1 | 268 | 134 | 129 |
| 31 | 1 | 268 | 135 | 134 |

All 31 original uninterrupted page-local sequences of length ≥150 were evaluated without training a parameter on the corpus. The table shows only the five that independently exceed 128 in both low-bit lanes; their exact indices and outputs are frozen in the test.

## Positive calibration and limits

[Single-file Node.js CommonJS test](../sims/lp2_chain_mod28_lowbit_sft.cjs) reads the repository's complete [canonical LP2 rune-index corpus](../resources/liber-primus/lp2-corpus.json), reconstructs page-local increments, asserts both original counts and p17's 272-step exact result, and checks source-native 29-rune chained plants generated by deterministic Marsaglia xorshift32 and xorshift128. The inverse recovers every planted 28-step value exactly. Their 272-step two-lane complexities are respectively **(32,32)** and **(128,128)**. The saved CJS file was run against the original, current repository dataset through a JavaScript environment and **passed all assertions**.

This is a precise exclusion of a *hypothetical* direct incremental emitter and is not a decipherment. It does **not** exclude nonlinear cryptographic generators, MT19937 (19,937-bit linear state), changed output projections, hidden draws between runes, a 29-ary GP message with an independent escape channel, or another stateful/lossless cipher architecture. It cannot establish that the original used a PRNG at all. The F-shaped escape interpretation remains unselected, and no authentic plaintext, historical state or secret key has been recovered.

**Follow-on decision:** do not branch into arbitrary fitted PRNG/tap/output variants. Reopen this lane only if a period-authentic source specifies a generator, its output projection and the chained arithmetic, or supplies independently testable key/state material. A direct plaintext-bearing inverse remains the objective.
