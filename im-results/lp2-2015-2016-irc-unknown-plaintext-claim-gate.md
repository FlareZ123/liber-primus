A text-mode candidate screen combined 2015–16 date and message provenance with finite literal claims such as "I solved/decrypted/cracked", "key is", "page", "runes", "LP", "plaintext", code/paste references, and checked surrounding chat turns for disavowals, explanations and output. Twelve materially distinct conversations were then examined with exact line numbers below. This is candidate-driven source triage rather than proof that all 306,983 dated lines lack hidden evidence. Earlier fully reconstructed **4 February 2015** p0–2 prime-shift-then-difference result, already solved-page text, the separately indexed 2016 Titanpad versions, and previous 2014 material are excluded from the twelve.
