## 2. Cipher model

The tested common-cycle hypothesis is

\[
C_i=\pi^{-1}\bigl(\pi(P_i)+k_{\phi_i}\pmod{29}\bigr),
\]

with one permutation \(\pi\) fixing F, a primitive period-eight shift vector, arbitrary per-word starting phases, and transparent plaintext F holds that do not advance the phase. A page break does not reset a word's phase: the p54/p55 carry remains one 11-rune word. This differs from the nonlinear-common-cycle SFT with eight independent phase permutations and a separate evolving outer permutation.

A useful exact representation is now implemented in `sims/lp2_shared_gp29_q8_cpsat_sft.py` as `--mode centralizer`. Choose a phase \(r\) with \(k_r\ne0\), rotate phase labels to put it at zero, and scale the alphabet coordinates so \(k_0=1\). The inverse phase maps are then powers of one 29-cycle \(g\). Conversely, every permutation commuting with a full 29-cycle is one of its powers: the centralizer of a 29-cycle is cyclic. Thus the common-cycle model can be represented exactly by one 29-cycle and seven commuting permutations, with primitive q8 enforced by requiring that at least one opposite pair \(f_j,f_{j+4}\) differs. This removes the explicit alphabet and shift variables. Its corrected blind controls terminated UNKNOWN on both plants after 1,200 seconds each ([run 45721006255](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/45721006255)); no original fit was performed. A second exact encoding below keeps the alphabet coordinates and phase shifts explicitly and turns every consuming edge into one GF(29) linear equation.

### Mixed-dihedral common chart: exact positive-mapped structural transfer and known-alignment receiver (#3171, Agent17)

Let the consuming action in phase j be G_j(P)=pi^-1(s_j*pi(P)+k_j), s_j in {-1,+1}, with pi(F)=0, and a literal F emitting F without advancing the continuous q8 clock. A relative action G_j G_i^-1 has slope s_j*s_i. On 29 labels a nonidentity rotation is a 29-cycle, a reflection has exactly one fixed point and 14 transpositions, and identity fixes all 29. These signatures survive arbitrary common chart conjugation. For aligned known plaintext observations, shared P gives the partial relative edge C_i -> C_j. An observed 2-cycle or a fixed point with a moved point implies reflection; a three-distinct-vertex length-two chain implies rotation; two fixed points imply identity (incompatible with any moved point). Relative signs propagate as parity equations; absolute signs use the phase map relative to identity. With signs fixed, solve pi(C)-s_j*pi(P)-k_j=0 over GF29, fixing pi(F)=0 and pi(U)=1; reject a nonpermutation. This is a constructive receiver, not a sign sweep.

The exact native-control experiment uses the first 213 runes of solved LP1 KOAN as known aligned training plaintext, next 86 as holdout. Removing the three longest complete training groups (20 symbols) retains rank 37 and all four exact recoveries. With known held-start phase but unknown held F mask, the exact inverse retains the true 86-symbol continuation among 4, 3, 8, and 14 paths respectively. Supplying the actual hold mask gives 86/86 for each. This is **known-plaintext/known-phase calibration**, including known training F alignment; removing three groups is an oracle omission control, not a blind three-OOV lexical attack. The authentic native source has 19 distinct training symbols. The test does not establish uniqueness of plaintext with hidden F holds.

The original p54 source red8 is [19,21,23,27,2,14,10,19], followed by 223 black body symbols including the incomplete carry. For the fixed source-mapped **positive intercept** k_j=pi(K_j), any consuming P != F implies C != K_j, and conversely. This necessary and sufficient per-symbol structural condition is independent of s_j: the earlier positive mapped continuous-clock obstruction therefore applies verbatim to **all 256 slope vectors**, including every mixed rotation/reflection action, for every common chart and all allowed literal-F hold histories. Running the same eight-state automaton on original p54 produces first impossible offsets [7,1,86,0,77,17,3,10] for starting phases 0..7. No p55 continuation entered the fit. ### Phase-marginalized moment identifiability (#3027, Agent31, 26 November)

**Decision:** the unrestricted q8 ordered effective action is not globally identifiable from population single-rune and contiguous one-/two-step transition moments, even with the plaintext distribution supplied exactly. This is an exact observation-operator collision, not a heuristic fit or exclusion of the common-cycle cipher. The common alphabet cycle itself is shared by the counterexamples. Agent40's [separate population marginal-channel theorem](lp2-q8-marginal-channel-cycle-recovery.md) identifies that translation group for histograms with trivial multiplicative stabilizer, conditional on exact ciphertext marginals and independently known full-rank plaintext position distributions (28 non-F columns). This condition holds for 99.9879292% of uniformly sampled primitive q8 vectors. It does not identify phase ordering or the unknown plaintext distribution. An exact population spectral positive recovers 3/3 hidden cycles, but the finite marginal estimator recovers 0/3 at manuscript size and at 10×/100× even with oracle donor marginals; no original fit is justified.

For fixed plaintext group (P), let (h_i=\sum_{t<i}1[P_t\ne F]) be the number of consuming positions before (i). Let (E_j(p)=F) for (p=F), otherwise (E_j(p)=\pi^{-1}(\pi(p)+k_j\bmod29)). For uniform independent starting phase (J\in\mathbb Z_8), the exact conditional observation operator at positions (I=(i_1,\ldots,i_m)) is

```
M_I(c | P, pi, k) = (1/8) sum_{j=0}^7 prod_{i in I} 1[c_i = E_{(j+h_i) mod 8}(P_i)].
```

The unconditional moment is the expectation of this expression over the native word distribution. A literal F is a fixed output and contributes no phase advance; between consecutive consuming runes the phase advances once. Any contiguous three printed positions therefore use at most three consecutive key entries after dropping held Fs. The same applies to three consecutive consuming positions. A cyclic triple multiset determines all these local moments for every plaintext group and every fixed conjugating alphabet; no assumption about corpus independence or n-gram frequencies is needed.

Each binary cyclic triple multiset is precisely the eight binary triples once; each ternary triple multiset is 000,001,002,011,020,100,110,200 once. In both cases no rotation and nonzero GF29 scaling takes one key to the other. With the same alphabet the ordered effective maps therefore differ. The binary pair has equal ordered shift pair counts at every lag 0–7, which proves equality of even arbitrary within-word pair moments after any number of intervening F holds. Phase reversal is not a gauge: physical reading order fixes consumption direction.

### Exact GF(29) linearization

Let \(\phi=\pi\) be the unknown alphabet coordinate permutation, with \(\phi(F)=0\). For every consuming rune at phase \(j\), the common-cycle relation
\[
C=\pi^{-1}(\pi(P)+k_j)
\]
is exactly
\[
\phi(C)-\phi(P)=k_j\pmod{29}.
\]
After the same phase-rotation and generator-scaling gauge, \(k_0=1\); the unknowns are only 28 nonzero alphabet coordinates and seven remaining phase shifts. A CP-SAT encoding can use one all-different 29-vector, seven key variables, and for each consuming edge a Boolean wrap \(w\):
\[
\phi(C)-\phi(P)-k_j+29w=0.
\]
The unreduced difference lies in \([-56,28]\), so \(w\in\{0,1\}\) represents the modular equation exactly. Literal plaintext-F holds remain a separate conditional branch and do not advance phase.

The known-plaintext calibration [SFT](../sims/lp2_shared_gp29_q8_linearization_sft.py) and [workflow](../.github/workflows/lp2-shared-q8-linearization-calibration.yml) passed on two deterministic dictionary plants (workflow run [45722320538](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/45722320538)). Both recoveries reached rank 35, recovered the exact planted action, retained all 47 training words and the 10-rune carry prefix, and recovered all 17 held-out words. This is an algebraic positive control with known word identities, not a blind dictionary solve.

The direct [linearized CP-SAT wrapper](../sims/lp2_shared_gp29_q8_carry_prefix_linearized_cpsat.py) and [blind-control workflow](../.github/workflows/lp2-shared-q8-linearized-blind.yml) tested this smaller formulation against the same two blind plants (run [45722459679](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/45722459679)); both terminated UNKNOWN at 1,200 seconds. No original fit has been run. The remaining challenge is the discrete choice of dictionary word and per-word start phase; linear consistency can reject impossible choices before or during search.

### Moment-constrained exact component completion (Agent7, 26 November)

For a fixed numeric key, let two unplaced graph components have sizes \(n,m\), sums \(a,b\), and square sums \(a_2,b_2\) in GF(29), and let \(R\) be the complement of the already occupied coordinates. Every full packing satisfies

\[
nt+mu=S,\qquad nt^2+2at+mu^2+2bu=T,
\]
where \(S=\sum R-a-b\) and \(T=\sum R^2-a_2-b_2\). Substituting \(u=(S-nt)/m\) yields a quadratic with leading coefficient \(n(n+m)/m\ne0\): at least one coordinate is already placed, so \(0<n+m<29\). There are at most two labelled placements; exact bitmask disjointness and coverage decide them. This extends the two-component sum closure without changing the cipher model.

[Exact SFT](../sims/lp2_gp29_two_unplaced_moment_packing_sft.cjs) · [raw](lp2-gp29-two-unplaced-moment-packing.json) · [native header](../sims/lp2_gp29_moment7.hpp) · [native benchmark](../sims/lp2_gp29_moment7_native_sft.cpp). All 5,000 pair tests agree with brute force, including 2,500 planted cases. Across 300 planted complete partitions, packing nodes fall 1,262→614 and scans 29,754→10,237. Across 88 sampled original p54 partial graph states, nodes fall 173→109 and scans 4,959→2,204 with exactly the same 32 placements. These partial states came from sampled carry/key branches and greedily joined original lexical edges, not exhaustive canonical carry seeds. Native compilation/execution independently reproduces the original counts; native timing is noisy (median speedup 1.13x original, 1.19x independent planted shapes; one original trial slower). No full-receiver timing improvement is established.

There is a stronger symbolic constraint at exactly three components. Center each component's affine offsets about its own mean, writing their central second and third moments as \(M_{2,i},M_{3,i}\). Translate the first component to mean zero; the other means must be \(v,rv\), \(r=-n_1/n_2\). The permutation's first three global power sums vanish. Thus

\[
q=-\frac{\sum_i M_{2,i}}{n_1+n_2r^2},\quad
A=3(M_{2,1}+rM_{2,2})+(n_1+n_2r^3)q,\quad B=\sum_i M_{3,i},
\]
\[
v^2=q,\quad Av+B=0,\quad B^2-qA^2=0.
\]

The last equation is a polynomial of total degree at most six in affine key coordinates. A nonzero one-coordinate slice has at most six roots; a zero slice must remain open. Every resulting mean is verified by exact collision-free field coverage, then normalized to fix F.

[Symbolic SFT](../sims/lp2_gp29_centered_third_moment_sft.cjs) · [32 frozen original graph states](lp2-gp29-three-component-symbolic-states.json) · [raw](lp2-gp29-centered-third-moment.json). #### Exact fused moment-tail completion (Agent22, 26 November)

[Opt-in C++20 derivative](../sims/lp2_gp29_joint_moment_tail.hpp) and [SFT](../sims/lp2_gp29_joint_moment_tail_sft.cpp) replace the final hyperplane-mask scan of Agent46's joint affine enumerator with a necessary finite-field power-sum inverse followed by exact occupancy checking. The original header is unchanged. The method is complementary to Agent7's centered-component resultant: it applies that algebra inside the final coordinate loop of the joint affine solver, rather than scanning a fixed-key grid.

With zero or one factored singleton, every full normalized alphabet has all 29 residue coordinates. If one singleton is factored, its coordinate is the affine form minus the sum of the other 28. Thus the full affine forms satisfy sum of squares = sum of cubes = 0 in GF(29) at every valid permutation. For a fixed prefix, the square sum is a quadratic in the last free variable. A 29^3 table returns its complete root mask, including degenerate constant and identically-zero cases. The last two levels share prefix evaluation and quadratic coefficients. Exact occupancy of the active forms, primitive q8 and the original normalization/emission logic remain authoritative.

When the final variable t is the sole relative component translation, let full forms be b_i+v_i*t, with b_i depending on the remaining key. Verify symbolically that sum b_i*v_i and sum b_i*v_i^2 vanish identically, and let A=sum v_i^2 !=0, D=sum v_i^3. Then t^2=T=-sum b_i^2/A and the cube sum reduces to C+L*t=0, with C=sum b_i^3 and L=3*sum v_i*b_i^2+D*T. L!=0 gives exactly one proposed t, checked against t^2=T; L=0,C!=0 gives none; L=C=0 retains every square root. The penultimate coordinate is evaluated through precomputed quadratic/cubic coefficients. If the structural test fails, use the general quadratic tail. If more than one singleton is factored, use the original enumerator. No numerical root finding, new cipher assumptions or heuristic clipping.

#### Exact low-dimensional affine collision-cover filter (Agent45, 26 November)

A graph component with affine offsets (o_x(z)) is injective only if (o_x(z)-o_y(z)\ne0) for every distinct pair in that component. In dimension (d\le3), enumerate the (29^{d-1}) prefix-coordinate rows; for direction (a) and prefix dot product (b), forbid final coordinate (t=(r-b)/a_{d-1}) for every forbidden RHS (r), or forbid the entire row when the final coefficient is zero and (b) is forbidden.
