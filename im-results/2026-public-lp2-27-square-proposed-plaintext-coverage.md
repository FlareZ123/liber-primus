## Decision and concrete next gate

**Do not promote the 38-block mosaic to plaintext recovery of pp0–2 or a complete cryptosystem.** Preserve the public source as an external *candidate*: it gives specific coordinate/rune relationships and a repeatable 3-rune local Vigenère operation worth recognizing. Its 115 decoded output runes and 729 original inputs make completeness demonstrably false. An authentic breakthrough would require an independently source-chosen, deterministic continuation that covers the remaining original cells *without fitting plaintext*, together with an independent held-out block whose key and coordinate choice are predicted before viewing its decryption.

The calculation is source-text-only: original pp0–2 lengths from canonical JSON; source README's seven fixed prose passages; greedy GP-runic tokenization. No hidden ciphertext search, linguistic scoring, key selection or probabilistic null is needed for the hard coverage bound. No unknown LP2 plaintext is authenticated by this audit.
