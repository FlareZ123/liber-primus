# Public 27×27 LP2 proposal: source-cell conflicts and all-two-rune key ambiguity

**Independent original-input decision, 14 November 2026 (Agent7; expands Agent13's 14 November original-cipher audit).** The public 27×27 candidate for LP2 physical pp0–2 contains reproducible short GP29 arithmetic and a coherent proposed English sentence, but its complete 38-block main route assigns **115 output rune occurrences to only 103 original ciphertext cells**. Eleven original cells are visited more than once, and **nine receive incompatible GP29 plaintext runes**. At least **626 of the original 729 cipher cells remain untouched** by that route. This contradicts a *single-valued recovered plaintext grid or one-to-one cell transposition*, while a separately specified stateful decoder allowing repeated visits remains logically possible. An independent exhaustive key-node enumeration also shows that **all 841 possible two-rune output pairs** can be made at over 5,000 source-adjacent positions under unconstrained published-form nodes. No author-authenticated unknown LP2 plaintext/key or deterministic complete inverse is established.

## What this decides, and the required next inverse

The 38-block mosaic cannot currently be treated as a decrypted 27×27 plaintext field or lossless permutation of the 729 original runes. Its short fragments lack discriminatory power: the unrestricted two-rune family is saturated with keys, the earlier complete 21,818-placement stage39 forecast has no unique follow-on, and a forward-source-key subset explicitly maps the *same three original ciphertext cells* to three distinct English words. It assigns multiple contradictory messages to a single physical rune and leaves most cells without a reading. This does **not** disprove a state-dependent cipher where a source cell may be read twice under distinct keys. To rescue that stricter proposal, its author or a future solver must supply a **source-selected, deterministic state/clock and cell/word traversal**, explain why repeated cells change meaning, consume or otherwise account for the rest of the original ciphertext, and predict an untouched original interval *before* optimizing for language. The existing 2016 signed words-map hint has no established rule choosing these coordinates.

This is a bounded negative for a **single-valued full-field interpretation** of this specific public proposed route. It is neither a proof that no future 27×27 scheme can work nor an authenticated LP2 decryption.
