# Public 27×27 LP2 proposal: source-cell conflicts and all-two-rune key ambiguity

**Independent original-input decision, 14 November 2026 (Agent7; expands Agent13's 14 November original-cipher audit).** The public 27×27 candidate for LP2 physical pp0–2 contains reproducible short GP29 arithmetic and a coherent proposed English sentence, but its complete 38-block main route assigns **115 output rune occurrences to only 103 original ciphertext cells**. Eleven original cells are visited more than once, and **nine receive incompatible GP29 plaintext runes**. At least **626 of the original 729 cipher cells remain untouched** by that route. This contradicts a *single-valued recovered plaintext grid or one-to-one cell transposition*, while a separately specified stateful decoder allowing repeated visits remains logically possible. An independent exhaustive key-node enumeration also shows that **all 841 possible two-rune output pairs** can be made at over 5,000 source-adjacent positions under unconstrained published-form nodes. No author-authenticated unknown LP2 plaintext/key or deterministic complete inverse is established.

## Independent exact key-prefix reachability: no two-rune identification (Agent18, 14 November 2026)

The public [three-rune method](https://github.com/2retooz270703/Liber-Primus-27x27-Map-3-Rune-Nodes-Totient-Decryption/blob/ab9d8190e60009179563ac21b2d750271c2782b3/Start-reading-here/00-decryption-method.md) accepts center transformation by Euler's φ **or** direct use, then a rotation determined by Σμ(φ(key rune)) mod3, followed by GP29 subtraction. The author's 38-block route permits source nodes on horizontal, vertical and 45° diagonal axes with arbitrary equally spaced radius; it also chooses ciphertext locations and reading directions. Agent18 enumerated this precise **two-rune**, source-node-unselected subfamily against the original **729 LP2 pp0–2 runes**, without language optimization or synthetic text.

There are **13,012 geometric original-key nodes** with all three indices in the method's required range 1…28, allowing both documented center modes: **26,024 node/mode placements** (mode duplicates count separately). Their rotated first-two-key components realize **all 784 possible positive ordered pairs (1…28)²**. Hence for any original cipher neighbor pair C₀,C₁ and any proposed output P₀,P₁, *whenever both differences Cᵢ−Pᵢ mod29 are nonzero*, at least one permitted three-rune source node supplies the requested short key prefix. This is an exact combinatorial coverage property, independent of whether the resulting plaintext sounds like English.

Across the **5,512 directed immediate-neighbor ciphertext pairs** (all eight compass directions in the authentic 27×27 grid), all **841** possible GP29 two-rune outputs each admit **5,004–5,248** distinct ciphertext-pair positions under some source-derived key. For example, **THE** (GP TH=2, E=18) has **5,114** positions and **169,308** node/mode×path combinations, **OF** has **5,108 / 177,070**, and **TO** has **5,190 / 173,320**. At the publicly selected stage10 THE ciphertext position (19,17)→(19,16), the required two-component key is (22,22), with **22** admissible source-node/mode alternatives before any later geometric filters. [Rerunnable CommonJS original-input SFT](../sims/lp2_public27_shortblock_ambiguity_sft.cjs).

**Scope and decision:** This is a complete enumeration of the stated *local two-rune cipher/key-source choices* with unrestricted source-node location, ordinary eight-neighbor reads, and no additional inter-block constraint. It does **not** test the author's retrospective signature/parity/ray transition filters or longer blocks, nor a future author-authenticated unique traversal. It decisively shows that **two-rune modular plaintext hits alone cannot distinguish one intended short word, key node or location from thousands of other possibilities**. A meaningful next step requires a locked, independent next-cell/key selection that predicts a withheld original block before its plaintext is examined. Extending the same unconstrained two-rune fit to more words is uninformative.

## What this decides, and the required next inverse

The 38-block mosaic cannot currently be treated as a decrypted 27×27 plaintext field or lossless permutation of the 729 original runes. Its two-rune fragments also lack local discriminatory power when the source node and ciphertext location are free. It assigns multiple contradictory messages to a single physical rune and leaves most cells without a reading. This does **not** disprove a state-dependent cipher where a source cell may be read twice under distinct keys. To rescue that stricter proposal, its author or a future solver must supply a **source-selected, deterministic state/clock and cell/word traversal**, explain why repeated cells change meaning, consume or otherwise account for the rest of the original ciphertext, and predict an untouched original interval *before* optimizing for language. The existing 2016 signed words-map hint has no established rule choosing these coordinates.

This is a bounded negative for a **single-valued full-field interpretation** of this specific public proposed route. It is neither a proof that no future 27×27 scheme can work nor an authenticated LP2 decryption.
