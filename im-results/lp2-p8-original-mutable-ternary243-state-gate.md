## First-party operand, real seam, and missing author operation

Reducing modulo3 gives **[2,2,2,0,2]**, a verified primitive 242-state fifth-order ternary register. The immediately following p8 original has **12 red runes and 243 ordinary black runes**. P8 black text ends inside a six-rune **printed word** whose other five runes open p9. The decoder retains this original page continuation and seeds its five-rune address history from the last five unchanged p8 runes. It then consumes all **1,474 untouched p9–p14 runes continuously**, including the carried first five p9 runes.

No original source establishes that p8's black prose is a lookup table, that the p7 derived register addresses it, that ciphertext GP29 residue modulo3 selects a cell, or how that cell should mutate. The test therefore evaluates bounded clue-shaped hypotheses, not historically authenticated decipherment.

## Frozen lossless decoder

Read the 243 p8 black rune indices in source order and assign them to the unique 243 five-trit state addresses generated by the p7 primitive register with the **208** missing-zero splice. The independent valid alternative **87** provides a predeclared rival ordering.

At each following ciphertext rune, convert the five most recent **ciphertext** rune indices modulo3 to a base-three cell address \(a_i\). Let \(k_i=T[a_i]\). Encrypt or decrypt by

\[
C_i=(P_i+\sigma k_i)\bmod29,\qquad P_i=(C_i-\sigma k_i)\bmod29,\quad\sigma\in\{-1,+1\}.
\]

After emission, mutate the single addressed cell with one of three complete rules: **cube**, \(T[a_i]\gets k_i^3\bmod29\), motivated by the authentic adjacent p8 red product3/cubing orbit; **cipher overwrite**, \(T[a_i]\gets C_i\), the simplest ciphertext-written table; or **plaintext overwrite**, \(T[a_i]\gets P_i\), a conventional autokey alternative. The advance uses recovered original ciphertext only and is exactly invertible. No private unknown symbol or fitted phase is needed. All **2 splices × 3 mutations × 2 signs = 12** variants are predeclared. Earlier work already tested the stationary table and is not repeated.
