## What the receiver actually requires

The existing [seven-crib conditional receiver](../sims/agent31_q8_seven_crib_gf29_receiver_sft.py), with [four-world calibration output](lp2-q8-seven-crib-conditional-chart-recovery.json), is not a generic dictionary search. Its test source gives seven correctly *located* solved-native plaintext words (CIRCUMFERENCES, INSTRUCTION, PILGRIM, DISCOVER, INSIDE, WITHIN, EVERY), totaling **55 GP29 runes**, across **three independent frames** (`jpg229`, `p57_parable`, `p56_an_end`). Three start phases are normalized as gauges and four are withheld. The two actual common-chart plants yield one exact planted action each, while two private-chart rivals yield none; all planted truth is oracle-supplied.

For nontransparent runes, an identified plaintext/ciphertext pair `p,c`, active frame `f`, and word-start phase `s` generate a GF29 equation:

```text
π(c) − π(p) − k[f,(s+t) mod 8] = 0
```

with three 8-entry frame keys and 27 unknown non-gauge chart entries. The solver has **51 unknown variables**; `π(0)=0`, `π(1)=1` fix chart gauge, and the permutation sum adds one global equation. An original ciphertext value `c` *without independent knowledge of `p`* yields **no equation of this form**. The chart-permutation invariant by itself does not reveal plaintext.

Even unlimited supplied known words from only one of three independent frames leave at least **16 unmentioned key variables** (8 for each other frame); from two frames at least **8**. This is an exact symbolic underidentification, irrespective of word count or language score. The calibrated 55-rune example establishes *sufficiency on those oracle plants*, not a universal minimum or proof that any seven convenient LP2 words will work. A prospective LP2 application first needs evidence for common action, actual receiving frames, word boundaries, their visible ciphertext slices, and source-authenticated plaintext spellings; it cannot import the native donors' known words as if they were unknown-page cribs.
