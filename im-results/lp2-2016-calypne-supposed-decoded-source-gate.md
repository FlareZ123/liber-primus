# The March 2016 "supposed decoded Liber Primus" post is a rune transcription

**Decision (14 November, extended 17 November 2026): source-selector negative for issue #926 and the later-page witness gap in #1431.** The thread is genuine, historically dated participant discussion. Its March original post supplies a 51 KB page-by-page **Latin-label transliteration** of Liber Primus ciphertext, old solver notes, and an explicitly questioned rune-to-prime output. The complete 2016 posts on page two add informal participant discussion and one exact external archive link, without a cipher solution. It provides **no specified plaintext for unknown LP2 p0–55, no author-selected unknown key, and no byte-level decoder**. No original-cipher inverse can be frozen from this object. The thread's suggestive title therefore cannot qualify as a decryption claim.

## Original-cipher discrimination

The first unknown page of the source is written as S-H-E-O-G-M-IA-F S-Y-E-NG-C ..., matching the opening of the authentic original LP2 p0 rune sequence after the source-era IA label is used for ᛡ. The extractor independently converts the [project's original p0 transcription](../resources/liber-primus/lp2-transcription.txt) into the same labels and obtains **98 consecutive matching original rune labels** before encountering a transcription/format mismatch. This establishes that the passage is **original ciphertext represented in another alphabet**, not an independently derived plaintext passage. The comparison is a known-input identification, not a claimed 98-rune decipherment.

The poster's prime-number reference is expressly described as **runes converted into prime numbers**. That operation is the public Gematria Primus numbering convention. The first post expressly warns that a preceding pasted script's output was wrong. No complete original p0–55 plaintext span, key, state, IV, reset clock, F policy, GP-to-plaintext operation, or encoder is specified, and the later 2016 page-two replies do not supply them.

**Decision:** #926's gate for independently replaying a claimed decoder on 100 original runes plus 40 untouched positions does not activate because the source supplies no decoder or claimed original plaintext requiring such a test. The new 2016 page-two evidence reaches the same decision. Retire this exact thread as an apparent decipherment source. The distinct historical Titanpad and individual source programs already have separate evidence gates. A future exact-byte independently dated decoder would be a new source object, requiring its own frozen original-cipher test.
