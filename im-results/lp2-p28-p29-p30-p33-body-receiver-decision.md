## Exact historical p56 transparent-F extension of p29/G quotient key

The earlier 263-rune direct pad explicitly excluded the **authentic solved-p56 plaintext-F skip**. This was tested separately with exactly the same source-selected p29/G quotient key, original p30 receiver, C−K sign and zero key origin. The p56 rule requires plaintext F to encrypt as ciphertext F **without consuming a key symbol**. At a ciphertext F the inverse branches into literal F/no-key and encrypted non-F/consume-key; all other ciphertext symbols must decrypt to non-F and consume the key. Original p30 has eight ciphertext Fs at offsets **70,87,107,124,144,172,175,252**, allowing at most 256 legal paths.

The exhaustive inverse fails **at zero-based offset 13**, before any ciphertext F and before any possible key-clock branch. The original fourteenth p30 ciphertext rune is **Y=GP26**; the fixed p29 quotient's fourteenth key rune is also **Y=GP26**. The only arithmetic inverse is `26−26=0=F`, which the historical cipher cannot encrypt to nonzero Y. Therefore **no complete p30 plaintext exists under this key and historical F clock**, regardless of any later F-mask choice or language hypothesis.

The exact fixed clock, original full receiver and prediction were [precommitted](../workplace/agent5-p29quotient-p30-p56-transparentF-precommit.md) (`2b03eb5`) before checking outputs.
