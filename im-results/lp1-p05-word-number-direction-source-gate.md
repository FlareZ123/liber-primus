## Direction-grammar prerequisite and explicit stop

The period-original February 2014 Onion6 form explicitly instructed players to **paste the three numerical magic squares into the appropriate textareas** and submit a Tor hidden-service URL, with the third square entered into field \`51033\`. That documented action uses the converted numbers as an admission/submission operand. The saved success page leads to solved LP1 pages14–16; it does not direct a movement across these 25 cells. This was independently resolved in [Director #854](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/854) using the [pinned original stage10 Onion6 HTML](https://github.com/micheloosterhof/cicada-2014/blob/3b15257c5a9efada85b8ccfdb876e2ec934d5b90/stage10/index.html) and its separately preserved success response; those sources are stronger evidence for the historical role than a guessed grid walk.\n\nThe signed 2016 message says the book's *words are the map, their meaning the road, and their numbers the direction*. In isolation it does not identify this particular p05 mixed grid, a compass bearing encoding, or a chosen step origin. The original p05 prose says **KNOW THIS** and explicitly praises prime numbers and Euler's totient; it supplies the **arithmetic operand** but no **movement rule**. Solved p57's “shed our own circumferences” and “find the divinity within” are plausible metaphors for a center-directed path, yet neither specifies a reversible traversal.

Four necessary rule slots remain unselected by an original first-party instruction:

1. **Starting cell:** the center FORM=91, any corner 272/151, or the first printed cell are different plausible readings, with no author-issued start marker selecting one.
2. **Direction conversion:** the same values 18–366 could represent residues modulo 4 or 8, compass angles, prime indices, turn counts or something unrelated. Both arithmetic and glyph-wise interpretations are plausible; no published p05 legend fixes a mapping.
3. **Word/number handling:** either use every converted numeric cell, use only printed words, use only printed numbers, or treat words as labels and integers as steps. The source-authenticated conversion alone cannot choose one.
4. **Stopping condition:** the center, all 25 cells, return to the starting square, exit an edge, or a puzzle-controlled terminal phrase have no first-party selection.

**Decision:** the original word→number conversion is reproducible and strongly corroborated. It is insufficient to define even the *first* unambiguous movement, so there is no source-locked trajectory on which to run a meaningful solved-page or original-cipher test. Per the Issue #861 gate, **do not enumerate rotations, direction residues, skip rules or plaintext-scored paths**. A valid successor requires a dated first-party route instruction selecting a start, numeric movement grammar and stop *before* testing any unknown LP2 ciphertext. The now-completed historical #854 source audit has identified the actual three-square form submission, and found no authored square→LP2 direction/clock instruction. Any future traversal proposal must first supply an **additional original instruction** distinguishing its operation from this verified solved-stage submission.

This conclusion does not disprove the presence of navigation in the square; it identifies the unprovided authored operation needed to turn the verified table into an independently checkable decoder.
