# Authenticated 2014 three-square payload: p55 and solved-p56 key gates

**Status, 16 November 2026:** Neither of two independently defined, source-motivated ways to transfer the original 2014 OpenPuff three-square numbers into LP2 recovers unknown plaintext or the already-known p56 key. The historical *key source* is authentic, but the book does not independently prescribe either tested decoder. The 2014 source prints three squares of sizes **5×5, 7×7, 5×5**, totaling **99 numerals in 17 rows**. Each test is limited to its explicit arithmetic and reading order, with exact source-native forward reconstruction; no broader claim excludes every square-derived cipher.

## p55, 17 page-local segments: eight frozen ciphertext-difference decoders

The original LP2 physical p55 has **76 GP29 runes in 17 page-local printed word segments**. Its first segment is the single rune **ᛝ**, continuing the last word of p54, which ends with **ᚣ (Y)**; therefore these are **not 17 independent complete words**. The full source transcription, after joining printed line wraps but retaining physical page cuts, makes p55 the only one of the **57 rune-bearing LP2 pages** (including solved p56–57) with precisely 17 page-local segments. This weaker numerical match to the 17 historical square rows motivates a possible row-to-segment key, while supplying no authenticated cipher instruction. An earlier [direct repeated-row Vigenère test](../sims/lp2_p55_2014_three_squares_17row_full76_sft.cjs) and [inner43 permutation invariant](../sims/lp2_p55_inner43_square_permutation_invariant_sft.cjs) separately bound straightforward alternatives.

For the new conditional inversion, freeze the original printed rune order and separators. Set `delta[i] = (C[i]−C[i−1]) mod29`, fixing `C[−1]=0` at page start or separately at each word start. Decode `P[i] = (delta[i] ± (K[i] mod29)) mod29`. The original square entries supply K by cyclic continuation of all 99 numerals, or by the corresponding original source row cyclically reused inside each of the 17 printed words. Two feedback restarts × two key clocks × two signs give **eight exact reversible** complete p55 inverses without plaintext selection.

All eight original outputs remain nonlinguistic with GP29 IoC **0.02912–0.03579**; none produces a sentence, instruction or key. Full 76-rune outputs and forward reencryption: [the original-input CJS SFT](../sims/lp2_p55_2014_threesquares_cipherdelta_sft.cjs). This retires only those eight difference-plus-square modes.

## Independent exact 75-rune triple-Hill receiver after shedding the 7×7 ring

A different, mechanically complete consumer is now tested: the authentic 2014 5×5 / 7×7 / 5×5 source has 99 entries; removing the 24-cell **outer circumference of only its middle 7×7 square** leaves three actual 5×5 matrices, or 75 entries. The original p55 begins with a **one-rune carry fragment ᛝ (NG)**, which completes a word spanning p54–55, followed by exactly **75 further runes**. This gives a finite candidate with a source-visible three-matrix key and three 25-rune blocks without guessing any missing padding. Its weakest assumption is that the later solved p57 instruction to shed circumferences refers backward to this 2014 numeric payload, and that the initial page-carry fragment can act as a cipher header or state despite its ordinary cross-page word-continuation role.

Both standard, fixed Hill-cipher conventions were inverted over the actual GP29 field: column vectors `C=KP`, and the row-vector counterpart `C=PK` (implemented with transposed matrices). The three original-key determinants modulo29 are **10, 8, 3**, so both are fully invertible. Each variant correctly recovers **75/75** source-clear p57 rune positives after authentic encryption and exactly re-enciphers all **75/75** original unknown p55 runes after decoding. Nevertheless the complete outputs are nonlinguistic: original p55 IoC **0.037894736842**, decrypted column IoC **0.040360360360**, decrypted row IoC **0.038198198198**; neither yields a recognisable phrase or instruction. This **retires only this precise three-block Hill interpretation**; it does not disprove the 75-cell clue alignment or an independently selected value-changing consumer. The original source, exact matrices, controls and complete output are frozen in [`sims/lp2_p55_2014_three5x5_circumference_hill_sft.cjs`](../sims/lp2_p55_2014_three5x5_circumference_hill_sft.cjs).

## Solved p56, 85 runes: the circumference-removed 2014 square sequence

The next original solved LP2 page, **p56, contains 85 GP29 ciphertext symbols**. A source-visible alternative reading takes all five numbers from each of the first five rows, **only the interior five** from each of the seven 7-number rows, then all five from each of the last five rows. This makes exactly **25+35+25=85** values in the original printed source order, motivated conditionally by the already-solved p57 instruction to **shed circumferences**. The instruction does not explicitly mention these historical matrices, so the alignment is a testable hypothesis.

A direct fixed mapping `K[i] = sourceValue[i] mod29` and `P[i]=(C[i]−K[i]) mod29` is run against the **independently authenticated p56 plaintext**. Its historical true decryption key is the successive prime's Euler totient `φ(prime)=prime−1` modulo29; the sole literal plaintext F at zero-based original offset56 is transparent and consumes no key. The candidate square-derived residues agree with only **2 of the other 84 true p56 key positions** (at offsets14,31), and their decoded p56 output remains nonlinguistic with IoC **0.031652661064**. The authentic known p56 plaintext has IoC **0.070028011204**. Both the known prime-totient decoder and square-derived candidate reencrypt correctly with their own keys; internal roundtrips alone therefore do not establish decryption success.

There is also a **key-independent exact contradiction** to every *fixed pointwise transformation* `K[i]=f(sourceValue[i])` under that source order. The historical square numeral **434** appears at zero-based offsets **0 and 24**, while the known p56 prime-totient decryption requires shifts **1 and 9** there. Any function f of the single numeral must give one value at both occurrences. Across the entire 85-number sequence there are **44 distinct source numerals**, **41 repeated-value classes**, and **38 classes whose same numeral requires contradictory known key shifts**. This rejects all simple value-only transforms, including arbitrary scalar lookup, modulus, totient or affine transformations, **for this exact 85-position key claim** without scanning those functions. It does not exclude a position-dependent keyed schedule or a different book traversal, none of which is selected by the source.

The complete independent original-2014 file read, known-p56 key replay, 44/38 collision count, exact witnesses and 85-rune forward reconstruction are in [the source-frozen single-file CJS test](../sims/lp2_magic557_inner5_known_p56_source_gate_sft.cjs).

## Decision and unsolved input

The 17-row/17-page-local-segment match and 85-interior-number/85-rune match are useful *possible* cross-artifact clues. Neither selects a successful original decoding operation or an authentic unknown-page key. The complete p55 encrypted page and all other original unknown LP2 pages remain undeciphered. Further tuning numeral functions, phases or square orders against plaintext scores has no justified causal path unless new creator-era evidence independently selects them.
