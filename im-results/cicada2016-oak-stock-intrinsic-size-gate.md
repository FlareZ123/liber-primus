## Newly measured source-to-signed oak orientation

To test if the named source file is actually the composited faint oak in the signed artifact, the [Python/OpenCV original-image SFT](../sims/cicada2016_oak_stock_shape_registration_sft.py) downloads and SHA-verifies the stock JPEG and independently SHA-verifies the signed target. It converts both into grayscale, inverts stock white/black polarity to the signed dark-background display, zeroes bright text regions in the target, and applies the *same* Gaussian blur to the source templates and target. For each of 46 isotropic source scaling factors 0.125–0.350 (within target size), it optimizes only integer x/y placement by normalized template matching. The three orientations are unmodified, horizontal mirror and vertical mirror; **no skew, nonuniform scaling, arbitrary rotation, crop or fitted rune key** is used. This is an image-registration test, not a steganographic decoder or a language-optimized search.

**Retract the repeated historical assertion that the identical p32 tree appears behind the 2016 text.** The original p32 121 runes still yield GP-prime sums **563** across the first row and **569** along the first column under a **derived, unprinted 11×11 reshaping**. This is exact arithmetic, but the picture's source identity no longer independently singles out physical p32. The coincidence could still be a designed *thematic* connection. New p32-to-2016 decoding proposals require an independent layout/key selector, rather than the disproved identical-artifact premise.

## Cryptanalytic decision

1. The **currently hosted** source JPEG does not natively have the signed carrier's **563×569** dimensions. Its recognizable tree appears horizontally reflected and downscaled inside that prime-sized canvas. This excludes unchanged reuse of the present source's image dimensions, while ordinary crop/resize/editing and undated stock-file version drift remain viable alternatives.
2. The signed-image / physical **p32 11×11** GP prime-weight checksum relation `row0=563, col0=569` remains exact **as arithmetic**, but no original tree-image identity selects p32: the trees have different sources. **Do not flip p32's original rune text** because the artist reflected an unrelated background stock oak; neither image supplies a cipher-key operation.
3. Mirror is a real **image operation**, but ordinary mirroring of a GP29 sequence, with or without Atbash rune-label reversal, is a permutation of the original symbol multiset and preserves its index of coincidence exactly. Original p32 has IoC **0.0341597796** for 121 GP runes, regardless of these permutations; the known solved p57 native-GP control has **0.0627099664**. A simple visual flip alone cannot change the cipher's unusual unigram statistics or nominate a plaintext key. It could only contribute as part of a separately sourced cipher rule.
4. The authentic 2012 telephone rule `3301×503×509` would yield proposed `1057465447.com` if transferred, via `3301×563×569`. The source-size and mirror findings make deliberate 2016 recomposition credible but **do not establish** that the 2012 phone rule applies to 2016; no authenticated capture of that candidate destination or new page exists. See the [consolidated phone-domain route](cicada2012-2016-phone-image-dimension-transfer-gate.md).

**Research consequence:** Preserve the source-byte and physical-mirror facts. Retire further unselected image flip/crop sweeps and p32 Atbash fantasies. A usable solve route would require an authenticated rule selecting a ciphertext transformation from the *observed background mirror*, or an independently dated archive establishing the exact candidate destination and signed key. Neither is provided by this finite image-alignment experiment.
