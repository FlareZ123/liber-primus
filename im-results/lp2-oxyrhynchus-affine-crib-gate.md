The solved LP1 inward journey, knowing oneself and finding divinity motivate a new historical crib source: P.Oxy.654 sayings I and II and P.Oxy.1's stone-and-wood saying, in M. R. James's 1924 public-domain translation and quoted Evelyn White / Lagrange restorations. Source: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Apocryphal_New_Testament_(1924)/Fragments_of_Gospels/The_Oxyrhynchus_Sayings_of_Jesus (revision 14215505). The parallels are thematic, not evidence of actual borrowing. The two alternative restorations are expressly hypothetical editorial reconstructions of lacunose Greek.

This is an exact all-key placement search on the original 12,956 unsolved rune tape, using C = a_phase P+b_phase mod29, a!=0, q=8 or13, ordinary consuming and plaintext-F-transparent clocks. A repeated plaintext in any phase must produce the same ciphertext; a second distinct plaintext determines the nonzero slope and intercept, and subsequent positions must match. Free phases are retained, not set arbitrarily. Within-word GP tokenizations and historical spellings follow the prior source-crib normalization. All candidate placements with at least 3q+10 active runes were eligible. Shared prefixes of exactly that many active runes were deduplicated before searching; any prefix match would be extended to each complete crib, preserving exact completeness.
