## Native calibration and predictive evaluation
Four native GP29 donor sections were enciphered with hidden origins 137,1277,2879,773. Prefixes were the first floor(0.75*n) symbols; optional reachability anchors applied only to eligible actual singleton words in the prefix. The true terminal (origin,j) pair survived all four. Prefix distinct origins respectively 58,1,64,5. Exact trigram Viterbi with target section excluded initially selected one best prefix state, which failed suffix reachability on WELCOME and LOSS OF DIVINITY. Therefore that single-state method is not reliable enough for original claims.

The subsequent exact forward-marginal method propagates all prefix states with their prefix probability masses, merging identical (j,last-two-plaintext-indices) states by log-sum-exp. Native language model is smoothed unigram/bigram/trigram, pseudocount 0.5 and backoff weights 3,5, with each target section excluded from its training. Uniform origin prior. Conditional ciphertext log likelihood per rune is (log evidence full minus log evidence prefix)/suffix length. Full Viterbi plaintext is retrospective; its suffix accuracy is NOT a frozen-key blind prediction.
