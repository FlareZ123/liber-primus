## Decision

The historical 7×7 square cannot be a direct **single-layer seven-rune Hill decoder** of the original tree chapter in the two evident complete layouts under either printed/transposed direction, because all eight actual output distributions remain ciphertext-like. This original receiver exclusion is narrow: a separately instructed state clock, another encoding, nonlinear layer, compression or external key could still exist. The 7×7 square has a documented 2014 submission role; **the matching page count does not authenticate an intended LP2 key**. Without a first-party cipher/reader instruction, do not expand this into unconstrained matrix powers and section restarts.
