# First-party parallel-language crib prerequisite for unknown LP2 punctuation

**Director issue [#3319](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/3319), Agent50, 28 November 2026. Decision: no eligible overlooked first-party parallel-language text, independently fixed LP2 placement, or plaintext crib was recovered.** The appropriate next action is to secure one **original Cicada-authenticated translation pair with a localizing manuscript cue**, not to rescore the full ciphertext against another literary corpus. This is an evidence/selection gate, not an impossibility proof for multilingual plaintext.

## Precise object and why this is distinct from generic quotations

To fix a plaintext crib before seeing original GP29 rune values, the input must satisfy **all** of:
1. A Cicada-first-party *authored or selected* historical passage with a contemporaneous independent bilingual rendering (or a translator instruction explicitly provided by Cicada), and an exact, pre-May-2014 text witness;
2. A distinctive, preserved punctuation hierarchy (quotation mark, period, paragraph break, numeral) shared by both versions **with at least one segment longer than the original nonce-like short phrases**;
3. A source-selected **unique** physical LP2 destination, and a second punctuation-boundary continuation predicted without inspecting the original cipher symbols;
4. A fixed native GP transliteration for both variants, then a separately source-selected complete cipher operation, not a freely fitted pad.

Merely having a historically translated literary work in a 2012 book-code problem does **not** establish a chosen LP2 translation register or recipient. Later community *translations* of the solved LP1/LP2 runes are not independent first-party parallel texts.

## Preflight of the only genuine bilingual cross-round source

The source-positive language pair is Lady Charlotte Guest's **English translation** of *The Lady of the Fountain* and its Welsh *Iarlles y Ffynnawn*. The National Library of Wales catalogue identifies her 1838 Welsh transcript and English draft; the 1849 volume and its 2004 Gutenberg edition were available before 2014. Cicada's independently archived 2012 Reddit/Guest riddle **actually selects the English** passage and its exact Project Gutenberg *English line* structure for the book coordinate exercise. Neither the original clue nor its solution specifies substitution of Welsh in Liber Primus, a Welsh-to-GP29 tokenizer, an original LP2 page, or any punctuation alignment between the two languages. [Prior primary-source and orthography review](lp2-native-welsh-multilingual-source-gate.md) already established this full premise and rejected direct Welsh transfer. *Do not rescan the same parallel* and relabel it a new source crib.

The original 2012–17 contemporaneous Cicada clear-signed message collection in `resources/historical/` (2012 countdown/MIDI/valete, 2013 forest/hello/GP chart/onion, 2014 three stages, 2016 tree, 2017 warning) is primarily English instructions, signed ciphertext, addresses, numbers and diagrams. The first-party source list does **not** document independently issued matched translated bodies in multiple languages. The signed GP29 chart transliterates **runes to Latin letter values and primes**; these are alternate *representations of symbols*, not two independently authored language versions and do not supply punctuation structure. The LP1/solved-p56/p57 English interpretation likewise comes from deciphering the same original runes, not a separate first-party translated witness.

We checked the repository's previously indexed signed-text, bilingual and quotation inputs before further retrieval, and inspected the two major external puzzle/archival overviews [krisyotam/Cicada 3301](https://github.com/krisyotam/cicada3301) and [scream314/2013 archive](https://github.com/scream314/cicada3301/blob/master/2013.md) for an obvious missing Cicada-issued translated pair. Neither adds a concrete authenticated bilingual text bound to LP2. This is a **bounded indexed-source review**, not a claim all lost onion pages, private material or unindexed archives lack such an object.

## Distinct existing punctuation capacity and why no cipher fit follows

The original LP2 has **seven independently printed quote spans**, including [p42–43, 34 genuine GP29 groups / 160 runes](lp2-quotation-pre2014-source-attribution.md), and its full unknown corpus has **2,896 printed groups** and **173 visually corrected periods**. An earlier full-manuscript exact-length quotation search tested **10,000 public-domain Gutenberg books, 646,238,370 words**, with zero eligible 16-word/65-rune original GP-length matches under preserved cuts. This is not a search for translated syntax, but does establish that a new generic register without a source-localizing clue would multiply unknown source, translation, and plaintext-placement degrees of freedom. Existing [seven quotation source audit](lp2-quotation-pre2014-source-attribution.md), [multilingual gate](lp2-native-welsh-multilingual-source-gate.md), [whole-chapter quotation gate](lp2-historical-wholechapter-quotation-search.md), and [placed-crib dependency gate #3263](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/3263) leave no independently locatable long anchor.

A punctuational search over LP2 looking for a **newly guessed** bilingual line without an original Cicada selection would fail the issue's independence gate: both the target and sender text would be selected after observing the same manuscript. No original GP29 rune values were disclosed or scored as putative translation output here. Therefore there is no valid new source-positive LP2 alignment, and no honest withheld-continuation/key test to execute.

## Completion and exact reopening condition

**Result: missing source prerequisite, not a negative on all translated-source plaintext.** A new **first-party** original bilingual text (two exact bytes/lines, independently dateable), plus an LP2 rubric/illustration/number independently identifying a specific passage or word boundary, would authorize a punctuation-only match, an independently fixed second-boundary holdout and then a strictly source-selected inverse. Absent that pair, keep the original 7 marked quotations **unattributed** and stop broader quote-corpus sweeps, variant translation searches, or automatic pad fitting. **No unknown LP2 plaintext/key recovered.**
