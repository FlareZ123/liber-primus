# Source gate: p15 spiral versus p55 sixteen complete words

**Decision, 11 November 2026 (Agent 38; Director Issue #179): retire the proposed 16-to-16 p15→p55 cipher handoff as unselected.** The two counts are genuine source facts. The original artifact does not select a cell-to-word pairing, a traversal of p55 words, or an executable lossless cryptographic operation. No unknown plaintext or key was recovered. This is a source-selection decision; it does not disprove that an as-yet-unobserved instruction could connect the pages.

## Original input and source contract

The original scan `resources/liber-primus/32.jpg` (physical LP2 p15, SHA-256 `5543cfce96493b86eece3195b3f5ea6e779ff41551676600b0c2479fc18c0ec5`) prints the exact four-by-four grid below a separate red heading, with **3299 in red at row 2, column 2**. Its 16 cells are generated by `abs(3301-prime(F+1))` for the sixteen distinct Fibonacci inputs, beginning `F=0` and spiralling right then down around the highlighted cell. The full exact cell/prime table and independently executable source check already exist in `results/lp2-p15-fibonacci-prime-spiral.md` and `sims/lp2_p15_fibonacci_prime_spiral_sft.cjs`. No new p15 numeric work was needed.

The original `resources/liber-primus/72.jpg` (physical LP2 p55, SHA-256 `a80191592add93a86ed3868f9e4e6ba3944ec20c9ffccc7d3e221b461344beba`) contains four printed rune lines between symmetrical decorative trees, a pale central tree, a cicada and a red terminal marker. It has **no red word-start mark, printed 4×4 array, numbered words or word-to-cell connecting strokes**. Its 76 runes have four source line counts **24/22/22/8**. ## The causal decision

The 2016 signature-verified instruction states that Liber Primus's words are the map and their numbers the direction (`resources/cicada2016-4gq25.md`); it does not name p15, p55, a 16-word group, or a mapping rule. The solved p57 PARABLE's mention of circumferences is compatible with numerous round/rectangular designs and does not choose an assignment between these two pages.

One can align the 16 p15 spiral cells with complete p55 words in ordinary reading order (start at offset 1), or pair the same spiral with the 16 words in reverse (end at offset 75, nearest the solved pages). Both preserve *all* printed word boundaries and the source-generated spiral, and neither is excluded by any printed instruction. The red starting square at p15 gives an origin **within the grid only**; p55 contains no corresponding marked word origin. Treating the incoming carry as its own p55 word instead creates seventeen units and arbitrarily requires dropping one. A row-major pairing requires ignoring the source-visible p15 spiral order, and a printed-line 4×4 pairing requires regrouping the p55 words against its 7/2/5/2 line layout.

Even after arbitrarily fixing an alignment, the 16 p15 values can supply differences from 3301, prime values, prime indices, Fibonacci inputs, spatial addresses or modular shifts. No original mark specifies which of these is an operand, whether it applies per word or per rune, or how a 16-entry association acts on **all 76 original ciphertext runes** while respecting the cross-page word. The needed decoding state and reversible forward transformation remain unspecified. An implementation choosing those decisions would be post hoc, regardless of its resulting language score.

**Result:** the genuine cardinality match does not pass the requested independent source gate. Per Issue #179, do not run orientation, modulus, language, row/group or arbitrary key scans and do not create a dependent p15/p55 decoder. Reopen only when a new original-source instruction uniquely determines the association **and** the first reversible operation. Preserve the corrected original p55 boundary census for other independently motivated research.

## Reproduction without a duplicate pipeline

Read original `32.jpg` and `72.jpg`, then run the existing `sims/lp2_p15_fibonacci_prime_spiral_sft.cjs` and `sims/lp2_original_page_boundary_audit.cjs --csv` against canonical `resources/lp2-transcription.txt`; compare p55 CSV rows `segment_index_zero_based=0..16` with the table above and check the distinct incoming-carry role. This document relies on these previously audited source contracts and a direct visual reinspection of both original scans. The decision depends on absence of a unique source-selected coupling, **not on optimized ciphertext fit**.
