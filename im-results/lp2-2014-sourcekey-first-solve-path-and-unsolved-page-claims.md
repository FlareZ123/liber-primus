# Original 2014 unsolved-page algorithm claims: targeted primary-source screen

**Agent 27, 14 November 2026. Classification:** original-period participant-claim triage using unmodified Freenode source bytes. **No unknown-page LP2 plaintext, executable key, verified cipher or p56 hash preimage recovered.**

## Research decision

An original 1 August 2014 speaker says that one of the group's Pastebins has the **“page54 algorithm.”** Reading the complete, time-adjacent exchange changes its meaning: this is a **search script** for OEIS integer sequences under an assumed first-rune `A`. The same speaker had scanned **93% of the sequences without a p54 match** and had not attempted the list's Caesar-cipher entries. No decrypted p54 or key is asserted. Do **not** commission a new page54-inverse branch based only on the phrase “page54 algorithm.”

The original 25 June 2014 exchange also gives a concrete, historically proposed **p0–15 alternate prime register**: `2,3,5,7,13,23,43,79,149,263,563...`, taken from the original p15 Fibonacci/Ulam index. Its proposer anticipated a per-chapter mod29 prime stream using the first signed upload as a clue. An independent solver immediately objected that the candidate offsets had already been brute-forced, and the proposer supplied no original plaintext. This is a contemporaneous hypothesis, **not an author-chosen operation or a new result**.

A stronger separate **19 May 2014 first-person p56 solve transcript** has since been retrieved by Agent40, under original lines L180878–L181131. It establishes that the genuine p56 solution began with the prime sequence associated with the original upload and an `AN END` crib, followed by the consecutive-prime-minus-one rule and plaintext-F skip. That primary account **supersedes** the weaker 21 May retrospective statement found in this screen. Its source-level conclusion, caveat about unsigned trailing whitespace and exact reproduction are in [the authoritative original Freenode reference](../resources/external/2014-cicadasolvers-freenode-log-corpus.md). The present source screen claims **no rediscovery of p56 or its human solve path**.

## Next useful decision

The original 2014 first-person p56 account now establishes one genuine *historical route* to a known decryption. To transfer a mechanism to an unknown chapter, a new source needs to select the **alternate key sequence, variable-rate rule, or other missing operation** and produce a held-out original ciphertext prediction. Neither the p54 OEIS program nor the p0–15 sequence proposal supplies it. This screen changes the interpretation of a misleading participant claim, and offers a bounded reproducible source index for new actual evidence. It recovers **zero** unknown LP2 rune positions.
