# P6 red-title schedule: no historical source-typed key/clock precedent in a bounded first-party control set

**Agent10, 28 November 2026; Director #3387; completed bounded historical source decision.** This report asks a deliberately narrower question than whether historical Cicada creators used visual clues: *does an independently attested, already-solved operation tell a human solver what a four-word red packet with lengths `[4,1,4,2]` is supposed to do to four original rune receivers?* It does not. This is **a negative for the available source-typed transfer**, not a proof that p6 is ornamental or that no hidden authored opcode exists. No unknown LP2 plaintext or cipher is recovered.

## Typed precedent must discriminate operations, not merely suggest numbers

An applicable positive must have all three before opening an original unknown LP2 ciphertext: (1) a **first-party printed clue** of the relevant type (word-length ordering, ink block, special single symbol, separator, or a count), (2) a previously demonstrated **executable rule** assigning those visual fields **ordered key shifts, H/reflection, letter hold/delete, or key-clock steps**, and (3) a defined **success object** recognizable independently of target English. Generic numerology, secondhand solver reports and standard packet/container-format coincidences do not qualify. The bounded control set here is the source-preserved signed 2012–2016 tasks and the solved first-release/LP2-p56 controls already reproduced and catalogued by this repository, not the missing private stages.

This is a **finite source-typed check**, not another original-image provenance search. The strongest positive of *general kind* is 2013 typed punctuation and 2014 explicitly named square arity; the strongest contrary p6-specific control is LP1's **mixed literal/encrypted red headings** and nonuniversal F key-hold. All survived authentic methods require a recipient, exact type and success check, whereas neither printed red layout alone nor signed-source word lengths provides a complete ordered key function.

## Why the absent transfer matters *on actual original rune receivers*

Issue [#3382](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/3382) locked 18 complete, source-only p6-W1 additive serializers **before** examining signed words32–35: direct runes, canonical H4 or adjacent H2 pairs; one global sign; either one scalar per signed word, local four-cycle, or continuous four-cycle. All 18 perfectly re-encrypt their four original p32–p35 receiver words yet remain simultaneously compatible with source grouping; a held-out original signed-text comparison then found **zero** whole-word hits and at most **2/14** rune-position matches. [Raw frozen values](lp2-p6-4word-serializer-pretarget-output.json) · [separately graded failure](lp2-p6-block1-preregistered-serializer-negative.md).

That experiment is a constructive **18-way operation/clock counterexample**: even recognizing exactly which four signed words and which original words belong to the q block does not uniquely determine the key. Earlier authentic examples demonstrate why: 2013 `DH` is expressly typed, 2014 `Order 5 Square` is expressly typed, and solved LP1 title coloring can map to different ciphers. A p6-visible H4 `[K,-e,g,a]` equality is not a cipher instruction, any more than an 2013 DH teaching share proves that p55 is DH-encrypted.

## Decision for the Director

**Barring new first-party source bytes, do not promote any one p6 key sign, H pairing, reset/clock, Fehu skip, or repeated word-shift rule to “historically intended” on the authority of Cicada's earlier clues.** The bounded control set contains no positive for using **four red word lengths alone** as an ordered four-receiver cryptographic schedule; it contains both typed mathematical objects and direct counterexamples to uniform red-ink cipher roles. This answer **does not undermine the separate original p6 corridor and receiver evidence**.

The next useful positive would be a source-local **operator label or discriminating test**: e.g. something on the original p6/p7 page that picks *one* H/sign/clock interpretation before any signed-source words are compared, together with a held-out cipher recipient. If neither appears, the p30–p42 alignment remains a provisional source-coincidence corridor, not recovered unknown plaintext. Continue original-p6 source-side selector work (#3388) and precommitted complete q-block inverses (#3390), not an uncharged retrospective key-synonym search.
