# 2016 A-road / Inveraray reading: bounded route audit

**Status:** weak, source-motivated endpoint hypothesis; not a recovered LP2 key, plaintext, or destination.

## Proposed chain

The 2016 signed Cicada message says: “Liber Primus is the way. Its words are the map, their meaning is the road, and their numbers are the direction.” The proposed interpretation starts at the oversized **Ac** rune on LP2 physical p54, takes its GP prime weight 97, and repeatedly advances through the 29-stanza rune-poem ring by \u03c6(p) mod 29. The proposed six-rune chain is:

| Rune | Proposed meaning | GP prime | A-road |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Ac | oak | 97 | A97 |
| Rad | ride/journey | 11 | A11 |
| Eolh | elk-sedge | 47 | A47 |
| Thorn | thorn | 5 | A5 |
| Gyfu | gift | 17 | A17 |
| Ethel | estate | 83 | A83 |

The arithmetic/rune sequence is inherited from the proposer and should be treated as a reproducible hypothesis, not a source-authenticated instruction.

## What genuinely matches

Historic Environment Scotland's official Inveraray Castle designed-landscape record confirms that:
- the A83 runs along Loch Fyne at Inveraray;
- the site is a designed estate with the castle as its focal point;
- the Oak Walk is a northward avenue through the policies towards Carloonan;
- the Carloonan Dovecot terminates the vista;
- the extended oak avenue leading to the dovecot was probably removed between 1750 and 1800.

This is a real thematic fit for the proposed terminal **A83 / Ethel (estate)** and initial **Ac (oak)**. It makes Inveraray worth recording as a candidate, but does not establish that the route is intended.

## Main discriminants against the route reading

1. **The six A-road numbers do not form the proposed contiguous route.** A97 runs Banff–Dinnet in Aberdeenshire; A11 is the London–Norwich corridor; A47 is an East of England route; A5 is the London–Holyhead corridor; A17 runs Newark–King's Lynn and joins the A47 near King's Lynn; A83 runs Tarbet–Campbeltown in Argyll. Only A11/A47 and A17/A47 share the same East Anglia network, and the proposed order inserts A5 between A47 and A17. The sequence jumps Scotland → East Anglia → England/Wales → East Anglia → Scotland, rather than specifying a continuous path.
2. **The road-zone numbering does not repair this.** Great Britain's A-road first digit indicates one of nine broad numbering zones. The proposed sequence has zone digits 9, 1, 4, 5, 1, 8; no source rule explains those jumps or turns them into a direction.
3. **The number-to-road match has a large base rate.** Every proposed number is a small prime below 100, in a range densely populated by two-digit A-road designations. Merely finding an A-road with each number is not a discriminating result. A proper test must compare all 29 GP prime weights and all source-allowed starting positions, not just the six values selected by the attractive endpoint.
4. **The endpoint is semantically post-selected.** Oak, estate, path, and dovecot all fit the Inveraray landscape, but the “epiphany” → dove/dovecot association is conjectural, and no independently authenticated 2016 image metadata or LP2 coordinate selects Inveraray. The A97/ Banff–Duff House alternative also has a designed estate and dovecot motif, weakening uniqueness.

The broad “numbers are direction” walk/sieve families in prior research do not automatically falsify this exact A-road proposal; however, this proposal has not yet passed a route-graph or familywise null test.

## Decision and next useful test

**Current decision:** retain as a low-confidence thematic endpoint lead; reject the claim that the six listed A-roads constitute an executable route. Do not promote it to a solution or expand into more arbitrary place-name searches.

A decisive follow-up would freeze the exact 29-rune transition rule and compare every allowed start against:
- the complete official A-road graph, requiring consecutive road IDs to meet in the proposed order;
- all 29 GP prime values, not only the six selected;
- matched alternative estates, oak walks, and dovecots.

Only a unique connected route, selected before inspecting place names and ending at one independently supported destination, would materially advance the solve.
