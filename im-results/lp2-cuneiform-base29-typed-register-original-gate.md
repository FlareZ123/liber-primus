# Cuneiform BASE29 as a typed byte/register receiver: original-input decision

**Agent 6, 17 November 2026. Classification (corrected 19 November by Agent34):** historical mixed-span negative. This does not decipher any unknown LP2 text or authenticate a source-selected codec. **Boundary correction:** the visually marked cuneiform body ends at p39's first `&` separator after 119 p39 runes, so its exact size is 1,468 runes / 326 words. The 1,589-rune calculations below included the separate 121-rune red-X tail on p39 and must not be cited as exact-body exclusions.

## Upstream evidence and the exact human question

Physical pp33–39 have the cuneiform values **1033** and **3301** and 1,680 original GP29 runes. The 91-rune p33 preface is followed by the **1,468-rune marked cuneiform body**, which ends at p39's first `&`; the later 121-rune red-X section is a separate source unit. The authentically Cicada-signed 2013 TCP challenge specifies an actual **BASE29** integer-to-ASCII-numeral operation with digit alphabet `0123456789ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRS`: its printed example is **3301→3QO**, and arithmetic gives **1033→16I**. The same source specifies typed ASCII command responses `01 OK` with dot terminator; contemporaneous signed Cicada material also uses OpenPGP ASCII armor or binary packets.

The historical command does **not** identify Gematria Primus runes with its numeral characters, define a rune-to-byte serializer, or instruct any reading of LP2. To give the typed-register possibility the lowest-complexity test, we *conditionally* identify GP rune index 0–28 with historical BASE29 digit value 0–28 and preserve original printed order and word boundaries. The mappings below are explicitly **investigator models**, not creator-selected rules. The [earlier six-rune GP additive/f-transparent gate](lp2-cuneiform-base29-literal6-original-gate.md) is a separate failed candidate and is not rerun.

In the whole-number and word-number interpretations, ordinary numeric decoding runs but produces no complete typed first-party object. These are exact properties of the source under the declared maps.

The chapter has **12 adjacent equal rune pairs in 1,582 within-section links**. A *conditional* independent uniform BASE29 digit model expects **54.551724** and has exact `Pr(X≤12) = 2.081697079493993 × 10⁻¹²`. This strong anti-adjacency is already a known **book-wide** feature; it weakens a direct high-entropy packet-as-BASE29 interpretation, while **not excluding** an added source-selected anti-repeat transport. It is neither a newly discovered key nor a model-free randomness proof.

## Genuine signed-format positive and decision

The SFT verifies the published **3301→3QO** and the independently computed **1033→16I**. A fully specified exemplar TCP response `01 OK\\n3QO\\n.\\n` encoded as big-endian bytes, then as GP29 index-digits, decodes back **byte-for-byte** under the same whole-integer procedure. That is an implementation positive only; no original LP2 ciphertext is selected as its input by the 2013 challenge.

**Conclusion:** The table above retires those typed-register models only on the historical 1,589-rune mixed span. It does **not** establish the same finite exclusion on the exact 1,468-rune marked cuneiform body. Re-running arbitrary byte packers is low priority because the p28 route now supplies a stronger, source-selected BASE29/DH command path; a future exact-body typed test should require an independent receiver clue first. The specific **GP-index digit → direct BASE29 integer/printed-word integer → raw ASCII/TCP/OpenPGP byte emitter** and the two displayed fixed-width packers remain unsupported. The historical source supplies a numeral *encoder*, but supplies no GP digit correspondence, byte-length convention, packet framing, original cipher inverse, or key state. A fresh original clue selecting such a consumer would change the decision; otherwise an arbitrary alternative radix, phase, offset, page grouping or hidden byte transform would only add unlicensed parameters. **No unknown plaintext or typed original output was recovered.**
