## Executed search boundary

1. Eight quoted expressions with `before:2014-01-01` in fast web retrieval.
2. Eight identical expressions without a date restriction in a separate search engine (contamination check).
3. Eight restricted queries excluding Cicada / Liber Primus terms, with Q5 shortened to the exact rarer clause; the engine still surfaced explicit LP transcriptions. Search operators are advisory, not proof of dates.
4. Six targeted Google Books domain searches for the central phrases (zero returned results in this retrieval interface).
5. Focused WIDSOM / BELEIVE variants and Eckhart edition/source check. No unknown LP2 rune values used, no cipher sweep, no author profiling. Retrieval is finite and nonexhaustive; indexing, ranking and date metadata vary. Some original pages returned cache/402 errors. Counts above describe submitted queries, not complete hit totals.

## Concrete near hit: Eckhart

C. de B. Evans's English translation of Franz Pfeiffer's *Meister Eckhart*, volume I, London: Watkins, 1924 (edition documented in [National Library of Ireland catalog](https://catalogue.nli.ie/Record/vtls000397556) and [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL6679019M/Meister_Eckhart)). Page 31, [CCEL reflow of scanned page 0055](https://ccel.org/ccel/pfeiffer/eckhart1/eckhart1.31.reflow), contains the literal fragment "with this widsom she discerns the Father". Several derivative digitizations carry it too; [Ad Fontes](https://adfonteslibrary.org/eckhart) expressly identifies imperfect scan transcription. The CCEL page image and archive scan were not retrievable in the current web interface. Therefore the existence of WIDSOM in the 1924 **printed page** and in a demonstrably pre-2014 digital copy remains unverified. Other visible OCR errors on the CCEL page further weaken inference from this one token. Even if the printed error is authentic, its surrounding Father/soul sentence has no exact rare LP1 clause overlap, and WIDSOM alone is a single adjacent D/S transposition; no independent LP2 alignment or operation follows. Do not use this as an Eckhart running-key selector.

The second-koan voice-in-head theme also has independent analogues, e.g. [Ruiz and Mills, The Voice of Knowledge (2004), digitized text](https://pdfcoffee.com/don-miguel-ruiz-janet-mills-the-voice-of-knowledge-a-practical-guide-to-inner-peace-pdf-free.html), but no recovered distinctive exact clause. Thematic resemblance is insufficient.
