# Ciphertext-only permutation-autokey marginal inverse (Agent45, 25 Nov 2026)

**New constructive result, not a source-selected cipher.** For the candidate consuming-clock law C_i=P_i+f(P_{i-8}) mod 29 with a single arbitrary permutation f, the permutation can be recovered without an intact-word dictionary. This is distinct from the exact word-domain equivalence SAT in [the earlier report](lp2-plaintext-autokey-q8-permutation-fhold-inverse.md).

## Noiseless theorem and finite-data attack

Assume independent identically distributed plaintext with known rational nonuniform GP29 prior pi, positive on all 29 values. Put u_z=pi_{f^{-1}(z)}. In stationarity h=pi*u. For adjacent ciphertexts on a q-strand, J(c,d)=sum_y pi_y u[c-y] pi[d-f(y)]. Let A and B be the circulant matrices of u and pi. Then J=A diag(pi) P B^T, where P_{y,z}=1 iff f(y)=z. Recover u by deconvolving h, then recover P exactly as diag(pi)^(-1) A^(-1) J B^(-T). Both circulants are invertible: a nonuniform rational probability vector on a prime-length cyclic group has no zero Fourier coefficient, since a rational polynomial of degree at most 28 vanishing at a primitive 29th root must be a scalar multiple of 1+x+...+x^28. This proves noiseless identifiability of every permutation under the stated iid model. It does not prove finite-sample or non-iid identifiability.

Use ridge deconvolution and random starts, permutation-constrained EM (Hungarian assignment of expected latent sufficient counts), exact all-transposition pair-likelihood refinement, then q-strand chain composite likelihood at lengths 3,4,6,8,12,16. A chain marginal sums over 29 initial plaintext states; remaining states are deterministic given f. Select the 8 initial states on the fit prefix only, then decode the untouched suffix with the frozen key and state. No dictionary or target plaintext in fitting. Ordinary clock only for exact frozen-state controls below.

For the Fehu hold law (plaintext zero emits zero without advancing), condition on physical intervals containing no zero ciphertext; those intervals necessarily consume. Use the active prior pi_0=0 and normalize each chain marginal by its exact nonzero-observation probability (DP). The other q-1 strands are independent under iid and cancel in conditioning. Censored pair EM imputes expected rejected pairs from the current model, and uniform imputation is only a spectral initializer. This extension deliberately does not equate all observed F with holds.

## Decision

The ordinary model has a practical ciphertext-only inverse on workload-matched controls, but the frozen original output is qualitatively incoherent and provides no independently identified plaintext. The cross-entropy comparison alone does not exclude this model; the later independent repeat obstruction supplies a separate conditional finite-register exclusion. The active-clock adaptation nearly identifies hidden map rows on literary plants but requires a robust rare-row completion and global hold-mask recovery before a negative or positive original decision. Do not repeat restricted word SAT or promote any fitted output to plaintext. No independently authenticated source rule chooses q8 permutation feedback.

## Follow-up: blind rare-row completion and held-F inversion

The censored active-chain estimator loses information from windows containing ciphertext F. A second composite likelihood integrates all possible hold/consume labels in each physical window of lengths 33,49,65,81, stride four, starting at physical index 32. For every candidate map, the probability of a mask is the product of q=8 stationary consumed-chain marginals times h^H(1-h)^(L-H); the exact sum over masks gives the window likelihood. Cache distinct strand strings. Optimize all single transpositions with at most 12 iterations per length and two EM updates for h. This improves hold plant 0 to 27/29 map entries but worsens hold plant 1 to 25/29, so retain both estimates.

To recover rare rows without oracle selection, generate both base maps and all single swaps among the eight lowest-prior active rows (including Fehu's unused row): [0,12,22,25,11,14,27,28]. Evaluate each with a width-32 hold beam on the FIRST 10,000 ciphertext runes only. Each strand carries a distribution over its last consumed plaintext; deterministic consume update and literal-hold branches are exact conditional on the chosen mask. Process blocks of eight visible zeros, merging histories once all eight strand states are singletons and their last plaintexts plus phase agree. Select by best surviving prefix likelihood and recover primer via exact single-strand conditional likelihood. No suffix or true plaintext in selection. "No retained path" is beam failure, not UNSAT.

A second decoder with the frozen selected key and primer uses queue plus last two physical plaintext runes as state; merge equal states by best score, width 2048. The native trigram prior has 10 pseudo-counts from the lower-order model at each backoff. Adapted variant weighs the 2,979 independent solved native runes by 5 and the predicted first-pass fit plaintext by 1. Re-decode prefix, freeze selected boundary state, then decode suffix. Forward replay was exact for every reported decode. The uncapped-in-principle DP with 100,000-state hard abort reached the cap (134,665 and 102,552 states), hence no exact optimality claim for the beam.
