**Agent 47 · 2026-11-10. Evidence class: two controlled attacks on original LP2 ciphertext. No recovered unknown plaintext, key or puzzle destination.** Both attacks executed successfully with Node.js 22 on [manual GitHub Actions run 42767994373](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/42767994373).

## Source, hypothesis and controls

The [2012 Cicada final-message postscript](https://uncovering-cicada.fandom.com/wiki/The_Final_Puzzle) preserves a **432-bit composite integer N**, with independently published factors of exactly 216 bits each. The speculative reuse under test is a stream generated by x₀ = seed² mod N, xₙ₊₁ = xₙ² mod N, then mapped to the 29 Gematria Primus values.

Choose six fixed historically salient seeds, primarily **3301** and **1033** (digit-reversed 3301), secondarily the years **2012, 2014** and earlier Cicada onion-cookie labels **167, 761**. Each square yields a digit by one of four predeclared methods: full-state modulo29, low-byte modulo29, five consecutive low bits big-endian (rejecting values 29–31), or five low bits little-endian (same rejection). The two historical RSA factors are **1 and 3 modulo 4**. This is a deterministic modular-squaring hypothesis, not a claim that N is a proper Blum Blum Shub modulus or that Cicada instructed its use.

Both screens use the exact repository LP2 rune indices and a strictly independent literary English control. They therefore ask whether these **concrete historical inputs and cipher mechanisms** could recover plaintext. Their output scores are not new cipher keys.

## Direct aligned 29-ary stream test: all 55 unsolved rune pages

[Direct inversion and full synthetic recovery](../sims/ps2012_modsquare_pad.cjs) evaluates each seed/extraction pair under plus or minus modulo-29 decryption, with a continuous manuscript key or physical-page reset. This is **6 × 4 × 2 × 2 = 96** model settings over the entire **12,956-rune** unsolved corpus (physical LP2 page 50 has no rune text).

The original ciphertext has pooled IoC **0.034478398**. The largest post-search output among all 96 is **0.0345327218008** (secondary seed 167, low-byte, continuous, +K), followed by **0.0345307080379** (primary seed 3301, low-byte, page-reset, −K). Neither resembles GP-English plaintext. A fixed 12,956-rune independent Shakespeare passage, converted to GP and deliberately encrypted under the 3301 five-bit-big/page-reset model, is recovered **exactly, 12,956 / 12,956 runes**, and the correct inverse ranks first of 96 with IoC **0.0610633216436**. The strongest wrong model scores **0.0347147945682**.

**Interpretation:** The 2012 modulus and these six seeds do not generate a directly aligned additive GP decryptor under the 96 declared settings. That says nothing about an unknown outer substitution or plaintext-dependent consumed-key positions.

## Exact soft no-repeat keyskip test: six independent manuscript pages

[Keyskip likelihood and independent planted control](../sims/ps2012_modsquare_keyskip.cjs) then addresses one actual structural complication: the ciphertext suppresses immediately repeated glyphs. The existing [exact keyskip transducer](../sims/keyskip_transducer.cjs) integrates over concealed key advances while retaining the **same plaintext rune** during each rejected proposal. This test fixes 81% rejection of repeated proposed output, max **32 extra key draws per page**, up to **three adjacent rejections**, identity outer alphabet and key reset at every physical page. Its language prior is learned from the independent Shakespeare GP sample.

The six prespecified original pages are **0, 7, 23, 28, 42, 53**, comprising **1,403** runes; this avoids conflating one section's unusually high score with a complete-manuscript decryption. All **6 × 4 × 2 = 48** source-seeded models are evaluated. Under the keyskip screen, *sign* indicates the forward encryption convention C=P+sign·K, whereas in the direct screen it indicates the inversion convention P=C+sign·K.

The strongest original score is **−5555.91205 log-likelihood**, for seed **2012**, full-state residue and forward sign −1. Secondary seed **167**, same extraction and sign, scores **−5562.62091**. The score is less than two empirical SDs above a wrong-key distribution: **150** fixed-mode, identical-ciphertext controls from deterministic random seed values yield mean **−5671.26309**, SD **59.20692**; **5/150** random seeds score at least as high as the historical 2012 seed. Add-one empirical fixed-rule p **6/151 = 0.0397351**. This does **not** correct the 48-way selection across historical models; the conservative Bonferroni upper bound is **1.0**. Accordingly **the apparently best historical seed is no credible LP2 solution candidate**.

The positive plants a separate **1,403-rune Gibran passage** in precisely those six page lengths using seed **3301**, five-bit big-endian extraction, forward sign +1 and the actual 81% key-retry encoder. It records **29 genuine skipped key draws**. The 48-way blind likelihood screen correctly identifies **3301 / five-bit-big / forward sign +1** first, with log-likelihood **−4218.963998**. Its best wrong model gives only **−5581.135748**. This is a powered, source-independent recovery for the specific keyskip mechanism. The original result fails to reach an equivalent signal.

## Reproduction and decision

Execute `node sims/ps2012_modsquare_pad.cjs` and `node sims/ps2012_modsquare_keyskip.cjs` from the repository root (Node.js 22, built-in dependencies and existing repository sources only). The [successful run](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/42767994373) runs both with the original archived transcription, and the keyskip screen asserts that the planted known source/encoding/sign wins all 48 hypotheses. Script seeds, PRNG settings, GP encoder, exact positive counts and per-page selection are source controlled.

**Current conclusion:** There is no defensible plaintext or historical-key recovery for this modular-squaring proposal under either the full-corpus direct model or the six-page exact soft-keyskip model. The historically printed number is a legitimate finite candidate source; it is not itself evidence that its modular squares are used in LP2. Unknown outer alphabet, a large initial offset, different page/word resets, an alternative private-key operation, or another key-source instruction remain outside this analysis. Do not widen the candidate list without such an instruction.
