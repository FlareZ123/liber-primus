# Original LP2 chain-step / period-256 all-secret gate

**12 November 2026, Agent 13.** An independent original-input test of a concrete reversible candidate: the 256 source-visible bytes printed across LP2 physical pp49–51, combined with the 28-step nonzero ciphertext-difference alphabet. **No unknown LP2 plaintext or authentic historical key was recovered.** This report excludes a source-motivated cipher *family*, rather than another guess at a specific key.

## Original-source operation and key-independent prediction

The manuscript's 256 numerical cells form 32 rows of eight two-character tokens. Their conventional base60 reading supplies a candidate 256-byte key; that parse remains conditional. Under the separately proposed primitive GP29 multiplier 15, let C be the 12,956 original GP29 rune indexes from physical p0–55, with C before the first rune taken as 0. Compute D[i] = (C[i]-C[i-1]) mod29, and let E[i] = log_15(D[i]) in 0..27 for nonzero D. For D=0, emit reserved GP symbol EA/28 and do **not** consume the key clock. Exactly **86** zero steps occur. The generator 15 has exact order 28 in the nonzero residue group mod29. Neither the zero-step interpretation nor the log mapping is established as the historical cipher.

An attractive first consumer, now rejected, repeats the printed row-major 256 bytes (B) as a 28-way additive key on *only* nonzero steps: P[i]=(E[i] ± (B[keyClock mod256] mod28)) mod28, while zero steps emit EA. Both signs are nonlinguistic; output whole-book rune IoCs are approximately 0.03527 versus approximately 0.03448 for the original ciphertext. This is only a fixed specific key, so the main test below grants **every possible unknown phase-specific 28-symbol bijection**.

Suppose instead that each nonzero position at clock phase t mod256 is encoded by an arbitrary *bijective* substitution f_t:P→E, and that EA uses the zero-step escape. For two positions in the same phase, P_i=P_j if and only if E_i=E_j. Consequently the total same-phase equal-pair count, after removing EA and using the exact reset segmentation, is a **key-independent necessary condition** for *every* one of the (28!)^256 phase-key combinations. This does not rely on guessing a key or on any language model.

## Fixed-escape symbol word-position gate on original printed words (Agent43, 12 November 2026)

**Exact necessary condition, not a recovered cipher.** A strict GP29-to-28 lossless predecessor-exclusion encoding could reserve the zero ciphertext difference for **one fixed plaintext rune**, often proposed to be GP index28 `EA`. Under that literal-escape contract, each same-page immediate ciphertext repeat must represent that one plaintext rune. Secret permutations or keys governing the other 28 outputs cannot alter this marker's number of occurrences or its word locations, assuming printed word boundaries survive decipherment.

The exact original p0–55 runic stream has **12,956 rune positions and 2,896 printed words**, including uninterrupted words across printed lines and page turns. Excluding the beginning of all 55 rune-bearing physical pages, it has **86 same-page adjacent repeats, 23 at beginnings of complete printed words, and three at beginnings of two-rune words**. A single fixed reserve symbol must match precisely the joint signature `(86,23,3)` on those same source positions. A repeated symbol at a word beginning can copy the last symbol of the preceding word.

**The one-sided count/start rejection is language-sensitive.** In Latin, the `F` symbol often has about the right total count but many *more* than 23 word starts, so 645 Latin samples satisfy a deliberately weak condition `starts≥23`. Other symbols under loose ±10/±5 two-sided thresholds yield two Emerson and five Latin matches. Exact symbolic replication of all three original measurements occurred **0 times in 7,500 independent samples**, even allowing each control the best choice of **all 29 rune identities**. The uncorrected one-sided `0/6,000` result for the first four sources remains true but is no longer the cross-language conclusion. These are illustrative finite-source controls and cannot establish a universal probability bound or eliminate a plaintext in another register. The stricter three-coordinate equality is a mechanical requirement; its frequency alone is not a proof of intent or cipher impossibility.

**Native Cicada plaintext:** Four independently printed clear-GP passages in the original master transcript (`SOME WISDOM`, `THE LOSS OF DIVINITY`, `AN INSTRUCTION`, solved `PARABLE`) have **1,096 genuine clear runes, 250 printed words, eight literal `EA` occurrences and zero `EA` word starts**. This authentic source-language observation independently challenges using `EA` as the one literal marker behind 23 original word-initial repeats. It does not show what the encrypted passages actually say.

**Positive implementation plant:** The [reproducible CommonJS SFT](../sims/lp2_zero_repeat_marker_wordstart_sft.cjs) implements a complete bijective reserved-symbol encoder/decoder on all **12,956** exact original word/page positions. For three different fixed markers (`F`, `J`, `EA`) it encodes native plaintext by copying the predecessor precisely for the marker, mapping the other 28 to nonzero differences; inversion recovers every planted rune and every marker location count. All **7,500** source controls and original-source assertions were executed against pinned repository bytes with deterministic fixed seeds. This differs from prior 256-phase exclusion tests, which imposed all 86 original marker sites on native controls and therefore could not challenge their linguistic locations.

**Research decision:** Deprioritize a **single literal fixed plaintext rune** as the explanation for every repeat, especially `EA` under the solved manuscript's English register, pending an independent source rule predicting the full observed (86,23,3) signature. The stronger possible alternatives include conditional or state-varying reserved runes, nonliteral repeated-output events, and different word/register representations. They are possibilities needing creator-selected operations, not currently justified decryption proposals. **No unknown LP2 plaintext or authentic key was recovered.**

## Decision and limits

**Reject direct native-GP English plaintext behind the 256-phase stationary bijective predecessor-rank families tested: the original log-step with book/nine-chapter reset and EA zero markers, plus cyclic/sorted ranks under book/page reset with held/advanced erasure clocks and F/EA marker reservations.** In every declared clock and rank convention, the original same-phase equality rates are far below the corresponding original-length native-GP controls, before any key selection. The original exact source grid modulo28 Vigenère consumer is a strict subfamily and need not be retried with different phase permutations.

This excludes neither a cipher with state that evolves when the same key phase recurs, nor alternate plaintext alphabets, a changed zero-step meaning, insertions/erasures, different key clocks, or an independently selected 256-byte stream-cipher expansion. It also does not prove that the original 256 cells were intended as a repeating key. Any proposed descendant should specify **which source instruction changes one of these assumptions**; searching all 256-phase keys would otherwise be unproductive.
