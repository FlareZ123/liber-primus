# LP2 figure-eight ornament versus pre-2014 rotating-alphabet devices: source-selector gate

**Issue #469, Agent 37, 12 November 2026.** **Decision: no independently sourced, fully parameterized rotating-alphabet decoder.** This is a bounded source/geometry exclusion, not a demonstration that all rotating-alphabet mechanisms are mathematically impossible. It yields **no unknown LP2 plaintext, key or historical destination**.

## The original-source observation and strongest objection

The 58 original LP2 images are in `resources/liber-primus/`; the relevant physical pages are p0/p1/p2/p15 (archive JPEG `17.jpg`, `18.jpg`, `19.jpg`, `32.jpg`). The prior, repeatable direct-pixel census is `im-results/lp2-original-infinity-ornament-source-gate.md` with `sims/lp2_infinity_motif_source_sft.py`.

- On pp0–2 there are **six** small horizontal, open figure-eight strokes; pp1–2 top instances are pixel-identical, and all three bottom instances are pixel-identical. Source boxes are given in the earlier report. The p15 551×187-pixel drawing is the same-handed enlarged type, between the 16-number table with red **3299** and the runic prose. The same-handed scale/translation fit has IoU 0.764432; mirrored fits are much lower. There is no encoded sequence of changing orientation states across pp0–2.
- Native-pixel inspection of those seven crops shows **one broad ink stroke, open near the crossing**. The image has two asymmetrically shaped loops but **no marked alphabet cells, letters along a track, paired concentric disks, fixed index, notch, hub or directed start/stop indicator**. Its outline suggests the ordinary infinity sign. The corpus itself has 29 GP rune classes, but neither **29 marks** nor a page-specific rotation address are placed on the ornament.
- **Strongest objection:** perhaps an infinity/Möbius-like flourish is a hint to think cyclically and a cipher disk is then the intended operation. That is plausible as an association, but it does not specify one inverse: the device family requires a starting alignment and at least one further independent choice (alphabet permutation, turning schedule, or signaling rule).

## Period-known candidate devices, exact mismatch

| Pre-2014 device or notation | Actual source-indicated geometry and operation | Missing correspondence to original LP2 image |
|---|---|---|
| **Alberti cipher disk**, `De cifris` (c. 1467; quoted in historical cryptology literature) | Two concentric rings, one fixed and one movable; mixed inner letter order; changing relative positions announced using index letters in the message. | Neither concentric rings nor a specified pair of alphabets; no index letter or keyed alignment; a flat sideways-eight stroke does not indicate a reset or a degree increment. |
| **17th-century Italian ciphering instrument**, surviving Museo Galileo inventory **1312** | Two superposed disks of unequal diameters; **three** concentric 24-cell rings on each, movable arm and an index. | No 24/29 cells, concentric rings, pointer or supplied initial phase. |
| **1865 Myer / later revolving alphabet disks; Jefferson/Bazeries cylinders** | Turning alphabets on circular disk/wheel geometry with keyed starting state, wheel order or changing-row choice. | No disks, ring letters, wheel sequence, step counter or indicator. A figure-eight silhouette alone cannot choose among these incompatible systems. |
| **Möbius strip** (one-sided physical band) | A two-dimensional ribbon with **two longitudinal edges joined after a half twist**. It can motivate an alternate traversal but does not by itself define a cipher. | Original penstroke is a **single open line**, with no ribbon width, seam, glued ends, side labeling, travel direction or transfer rule. |
| **Mathematical infinity sign**, attested in John Wallis (1655) | Horizontal figure-eight `∞` conventionally denotes infinity, sometimes indefinite repetition. | Matches the visual motif class with fewer added assumptions; defines no alphabet pairing, initial state or cryptographic recurrence. |

The historical device descriptions are verifiable independently of LP2: Museo Galileo, [17th-c. ciphering instrument](https://catalogue.museogalileo.it/object/CipheringDevice.html); contemporary/historical Alberti description in [Rutgers cryptography history](https://people.cs.rutgers.edu/~pxk/classes/419/notes/crypto-1.html), quoting the indexed movable ring; [C. W. Friedman historical lecture](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-D-PURL-gpo52787/pdf/GOVPUB-D-PURL-gpo52787.pdf) (older text with original disk figures); [MacTutor historical infinity notation](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Miller/mathsym/calculus/). These sources **predate or discuss hardware predating 2014**, but **no direct Cicada primary-source link to any of the devices was found**.

## Fixed mechanism and withheld original-input decision

The issue requires that a historical source **and** the original geometry freeze, *before seeing LP2 output*, (a) the two GP29 alphabet orders, (b) which is outer/fixed, (c) the first index and offset, (d) clockwise/counterclockwise orientation, (e) step schedule, (f) page/word reset, and (g) an independently withheld prediction.

**Gate fails before any cipher test:** the device source and original ink together fix **zero** of the two 29-symbol ring orders, **zero** initial key alignments, and **zero** stepping/reset rules. The modest statement “it resembles ∞” does not pick between Alberti's changing index-letter model, fixed-rate rotor stepping, a Möbius two-sided traversal, or plain ornament. Even if both alphabet orders are assumed canonical GP29, the initial phase and step schedule remain free, and imposing them would repeat the previously tested generic cyclic/Vigenère branch. There is therefore no source-frozen inverse to run, no legitimate withheld word prediction, and no original-input round trip that could identify the true mechanism. **Running candidate settings and selecting the most English-looking output would violate the issue's gate.**

### Completion and Director consequence

Close #469 as **no unique historical geometry-to-state selector**, with explicit scope limited to this ornament, Alberti-family double-disk models, and a literal Möbius-strip reading. Keep the authentic repeated figure-eight ornament in the source atlas: its location beside the Fibonacci-number table may serve as an ordinary “unbounded/continuing sequence” mathematical signal or decoration, but that interpretation also predicts **no LP2 cipher**. Reopen the rotating-alphabet branch only if a preserved contemporaneous Cicada source supplies a missing state rule or a previously unidentified distinctive mechanism graphic emerges from original ink. Do not commission arbitrary wheel, offset, motif-period or cyclic-key sweeps as a consequence of this observation.
