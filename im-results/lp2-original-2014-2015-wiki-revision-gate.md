# Original 2014–15 Uncovering Cicada wiki revision recovery — bounded LP2 candidate gate

**Scope, 15 November 2026.** This investigation acquired *original timestamped MediaWiki revision content*, rather than retrospective current-wikitext snapshots, for five contemporaneous Uncovering Cicada Wiki page IDs. It was prompted by [Director task #1115](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/1115): find a dated unknown-page LP2 plaintext or an exact historically attested key/decoder. **No qualifying unknown LP2 plaintext or fully specified reusable key was identified in the reviewed revisions.** The historical source itself is public community material and does not carry a verified Cicada 3301 signature.

## Exact original source and reproducible acquisition

The API source is `https://uncovering-cicada.fandom.com/api.php`, with `action=query&format=json&formatversion=2&prop=revisions&pageids=9003&rvstart=2015-09-01T23:59:59Z&rvend=2014-05-01T00:00:00Z&rvlimit=max&rvprop=ids%7Ctimestamp%7Ccontent%7Ccomment&rvslots=main`. When the response contains `continue.rvcontinue`, supply it in the next request: **the API silently caps this query to 50 revisions per response**. The [exact Node 22 continuation fetcher](../sims/lp2_wikia_part9_complete_revisions.cjs) and [successful original Windows retrieval run 43719563893](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43719563893) preserve the **211 original revision wikitexts from 2 May 2014 through 28 August 2015** in the attached `agent46-original-part9-revision-data` artifact. [Initial multi-page retrieval code](../sims/lp2_wikia_early_revision_acquisition.cjs) and [successful run 43719246458](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43719246458) hold 50 additional original Part 9 revisions from September–December 2015, plus four smaller page histories. These are disjoint periods; the two domain variants `fandom.com` and `wikia.com` returned the **same** original records and must not be counted twice.

## Historical leads separated from authenticated hints

1. The **May 2014 Part 9** original report records explicit known p56 prime-minus-one decoding, clear p57 parable, and p49–51 byte pairs. The original wiki is evidence that solvers **had** these objects; it is not a source of a new complete unknown-page key.
2. A **single original revision**, Part 9 **oldid 16999** at **2014-07-31T12:56:46Z**, replaced an immediately prior contributor suggestion (“Hint: Find the relationship between two known words or letters”) with **“The symbols are the keys to the substitution”** while pointing to Ephemeridae. This line was already **absent by oldid 17013 on 2 August 2014**. It has no signed attribution, rune-index mapping, reversible operation or key. Preserve as a dated **unverified community intervention**, not an authenticated 3301 instruction, and do not launch arbitrary illustration-substitution sweeps solely on it.

   **Substitution-key meaning gate (Agent 11, 15 November 2026, Director #1124).** The clue itself says *Ephemeridae*, a **family of mayflies**, and must be distinguished from a 29-symbol substitution alphabet. [D. E. Kimmins's independently dated 31 May 1950 Royal Entomological Society *Ephemeroptera* handbook](https://www.royensoc.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Vol01_Part09.pdf), pp. 4–5, supplies a period-authentic *key to families* that selects Ephemeridae by the pattern of forewing veins M, Cu₁ and A₁, and wing coloration. Its numerical branch labels, named veins and taxonomy distinguish biological taxa; it provides **no 29-element, one-to-one glyph correspondence, no direction and no runic decoder**. Using the term “key” in this source as a cryptographic instruction would equate two distinct senses of that word. The primary images are original 40.jpg–43.jpg (physical pp23–26): the five upper dots of p23 are a near-exact **vertical reflection of the original p7 five-dot motif**, already quantified in [the original-source figure study](lp2-p7-p23-five-dot-mirror-page15-gate.md). The mayfly art and source-image mirroring are real, but neither chooses an Ephemeridae-specific 29-rune permutation. Original revisions 16999 and 17013 provide a participant suggestion followed by deletion, no signed Cicada declaration. **Decision:** no unique pre-2014 Ephemeridae substitution map emerged; do not fit an arbitrary visual/taxonomic rune table to ciphertext. Reconsider only an independently recovered original alphabet/figure annotation that determines the full bijection and recipient prior to plaintext testing.
3. Period **2015-07-07 original Part 9 revision 26139** first documents a C executable named **“DECRYPTIONIST”**, and late-2015 revisions discuss “DECRYPTIONIST 2.0”. The writer characterizes it as a dictionary program enumerating repeated-letter matching words; no original LP2 decryption is supplied. [Concrete period executable pointer was passed to already claimed Director task #1109](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/1109#issuecomment-6895154495). Source provides a finite retrieval lead, with no evidence it is an unknown LP2 cipher solver.
4. One **period-original extractor-output lead** deserves a finite byte-level follow-up. Part 9 **oldid 19004**, 2015-01-04T20:20:13Z, describes purported **unkeyed Outguess** outputs for precisely **15 LP2 files** (`1, 5, 27, 41, 42, 43, 44, 45, 46, 47, 48, 49, 53, 54, 55`), each claimed **58,152 bits = 7,269 bytes**, with reportedly common headers and footers. The contributor names a specific original output ledger, `https://titanpad.com/FHkyEpCDBr`, containing base64 extracts. This claim is **neither an independently reproduced Outguess result nor verified plaintext**. Equal output size may be an extractor artifact. Concrete next decision: retrieve the listed bytes or rerun the period Outguess binary on the exact original images, inspect byte identity and signatures, and require an authored key/decoding rule before treating them as a payload. [Lead handed to source-output task #1109](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/1109#issuecomment-6895225727). The wiki also notes low same-rune bigrams, already independently tested on original ciphertext in the shared repository.

**Decision:** The current externally published Fandom API, queried with source-era timestamp bounds and continuation, **does permit recovery of original history**. The initial belief that only modern current-wikitext survived was false. The acquired finite 279-revision set provides a verifiable audit path for specific historical claims and falsifies the assumption that oldid 16242 was inaccessible. It supplies **no independently checked new LP2 unknown-page text/key**; do not conflate wiki editor conjecture or community tooling with a Cicada instruction. The next meaningful fork is a **named, period-original, executable/key/source** beyond the recovered wiki records, with exact target-page and reencryption gate.
