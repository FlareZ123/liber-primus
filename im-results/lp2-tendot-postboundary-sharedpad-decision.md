## Research consequence

The physical marker alignment is real, but no absolute consuming-clock equality is independently known. Two-stream exact lexical constraints do not reliably separate partial reuse from genuine reuse or recover an entire held-out key even on plants. Retire this particular marker-reset branch, not every nonperiodic reuse model. Reopen only with a new independently selected cross-text clock relation and receiver that demonstrably predicts independent native continuation, or independently sourced known plaintext. Avoid additional width/offset/dictionary sweeps here.
