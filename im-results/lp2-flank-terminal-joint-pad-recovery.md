## Shared arbitrary additive pad: exact joint lexical recovery
Freeze the 10,849-form GP dictionary from the first 80% of three donor texts plus solved native LP1, and preserve both printed word partitions. For each word occurrence and candidate plaintext word, derive the pad substring. The two interval partitions induce an overlap forest: an edge equates the shared pad substring, and every simultaneous word boundary disconnects the graph. Exact bottom-up sum-product messages count all assignments per component; top-down messages identify forced plaintext and key coordinates. The method is unrestricted in pad values, not a keyword or finite-period sweep. No choice of score or arbitrary search cap is involved.

Three held-out 308-rune donor pairs with hidden shared pads survive and recover respectively 183, 209, 147 forced true key/plaintext coordinates (all verified, not unique complete solutions). Independent-pad negatives retain only 67, 9, 53 feasible runes across their components. Original pair: only two components (7 runes total) survive; ten components are impossible. No original plaintext can be inferred from the surviving fragments because the global hypothesis is inconsistent.

## Authentic literal-F transparent clock
Both physical tapes have 308 runes; respectively 14 and 6 observed zeros. A shared consuming tape permits any hold-mask pair of equal count, giving C(20,6)=38,760 mask pairs. For each word, enforce the exact F pattern implied by a mask, remove held positions, and align the compressed consuming intervals. Every remaining overlap requires equal pad substrings; arc consistency on the resulting forest is exact. All-zero held words are checked lexically before omission. Memoized local compatibility avoids repeating the same constraint.

Three fresh 120-rune held donor pairs, each with exactly three plaintext F holds per tape, retain 1,3,1 feasible masks with genuine shared pads; paired independent-pad nulls retain zero. Original: 29,939 of 38,760 pairs have nonempty individual lexical domains; zero pass all pairwise overlap constraints, so zero globally feasible masks. This proves infeasibility for the frozen register and preserved groups, not for unknown/OOV words, word permutation, unrelated pad, or a different cipher. Positive feasibility does not mean the true F mask or all key coordinates were recovered.
