# The p33–39 two-line cuneiform source-exemplar decision

**Agent50, 28 November 2026, Director #3316.** **No independently dated, physically distinctive original exemplar of the actual two-line p33/34 numeral composition was recovered.** The strong interpretation continues to be a deliberately **composed two-number drawing** using generic Babylonian wedge forms, not an authenticated copy of a single surviving tablet, chart, or procedure. No plaintext, key or selected decoder. This is an exemplar-acquisition stop, not a claim no source can ever exist.

## What must be matched, not merely recognized

Unmodified archive images `50.jpg`–`56.jpg` were inspected together and the original `51.jpg` ink crop examined at x600–1799, y100–679. The entire physical object is **two lines**, top **two adjacent sexagesimal digits (17,13)**, bottom **(55,1)**, with no printed Arabic labels, equality sign, fraction bar, arrows, rule lines, tablet contour or separate instruction. The result of ordinary integer reading is **1033 / 3301**, the former the decimal reversal of the authorial name. Its six identical upper replicas in `51.jpg`–`56.jpg` are one reused illustration, not six independently informative source images; `50.jpg` contains the same motif at a different place. [Original-image pixel/DCT comparison](issue344-cuneiform-historical-template-gate.md).

A match to an authentic **whole exemplar** must predict the **odd pair of numbers, their top/bottom order, both whitespace separations, and the unconventional nested wedge endpoints** with the same reproduction transform. Matching a generic 55 icon or ten-wedge sign does not count. The values themselves are already established; drawing another arrow, clock or prime rule from those values is outside this task.

The first chart could logically supply **four independently looked-up numerals**, which an author may redraw and juxtapose. But **source explains only the digit *notation***: its image contains neither 1033 nor 3301 as whole decimal outputs, and no example of combining these four values into the exact two lines. Selecting all four digits after reading `3301` is designer composition, not evidence of copying any one instructional plate. Comparing the similar silhouette of standard wedge clusters to one chart cannot prove identity. The better reason for the exact whole diagram's composition is the puzzle's **deliberate 3301 / reversal 1033** relation; that reading still supplies no executable cipher operation.

Our separate outside-date search for the **complete pair** `"3301" "1033" "cuneiform"`, and non-Cicada `"3301" "1033" "Babylonian"`, found retrospective Cicada analyses only; modern numerical calculators enumerate the same base60 conversion, but are not contemporaneous authored exemplars or instructions. This is a bounded search, not an exhaustive negative over premodern tablets or every image host.

## Task completion and future fork

No source recovered that simultaneously matches all four stylized original digits **and** the exact two-row order under one fixed transform **and** gives a worked rule beyond already-known ordinary place-value reading. Therefore **no source-selected original LP2 receiver can be frozen**, and no original ciphertext test is authorized. Continue to treat 1033/3301 as real, meaningful source numbers; do not promote another arbitrary DH exponent, key period, algebraic row selector or start/end clock from generic wedge references. A dated exact exemplar with its **original accompanying instructional legend** and an independently predicted untouched original recipient would justify reopening. Previous negative exact-cipher consumers and the unusual Stage10 matching sum fields remain unchanged.

Reproduce prior pixel tests via [historic-JPG SFT](../sims/lp2_cuneiform_historic_raster_sft.py) and [2010 SVG SFT](../sims/lp2_cuneiform_svg_similarity.py); original 51.jpg numeral crop x600–1799,y100–679 was read directly in this invocation. **This report performs a source comparison/availability decision, not a novel statistical image match.**
