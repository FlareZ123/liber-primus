# July 2025 “Unlocking the Liber Primus” page-28 plaintext claim: original-source gate

**Decision, 18 November 2026.** The eleven-page outside guide [*Cicada 3301's Liber Primus: A Beginner's Guide to Solving the Puzzle* (5 July 2025)](https://epicalienstorytelling.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/unlocking-the-liber-primus.pdf) asserts “Page 28 is solved” (PDF pp. 4, 7) and presents the supposed ciphertext transliteration `KHLTKAFWTDTKAFG`, a Vigenère key `DOUBT`, an intermediate `ITAFOTAFOT...`, and a resulting sentence `THE KEY TO KNOWLEDGE IS DOUBT`. It gives no exact ciphertext-aligned full inverse or a second unseen runic passage. This document is a self-described beginner's guide, not a primary Cicada artifact.

**Source-cipher contradiction.** The canonical [LP2 corpus](../resources/liber-primus/lp2-corpus.json) has **269** runes on physical p28. Its first 15 rune-index transliterations, in source order, are `P C J TH B I W D W NG EA IA W L W`. The guide's fifteen Latin letters `K H L T K A F W T D T K A F G` are not that source opening, even allowing GP digraph tokens to be written without spaces. In the standard GP29 table, bare `K` is not a token at all. The figure therefore cannot be the claimed direct transliteration of original physical p28.

**Internal arithmetic contradiction.** The guide says “K (10) − D (3) = I (7)” and “L (11) − U (20) = I (17).” With its own A=0 Vigenère shift convention, the actual modulo-26 results are `K−D=H` (7) and `L−U=R` (17). The other example `H−O=T` (19) is correct. No following deterministic transposition or rune alignment is specified to obtain the asserted sentence. The guide also attributes the clear `PARABLE` passage to p56; the original [physical mapping](../resources/liber-primus/README.md) places that clear passage on **p57** (archive `74.jpg`), whereas p56 (`73.jpg`) is the 85-rune prime-totient/F-bypass solved cipher.

**Result and limit.** Treat this outside 2025 page-28/page-55 plaintext table as unsupported, rather than a newly solved LP2 passage. This is a fast source-identity rejection of this *particular publication's stated example*, not an exclusion of Vigenère or transposition generally. No unknown LP2 plaintext or key was recovered.

Reproduction: compare the source `pages.find(p=>p.page===28).indices.slice(0,15).map(i=>corpus.translit[i])` to the guide's literal claim; for its Latin example use `(cipherIndex-keyIndex+26)%26`. No training, key search, or original-cipher fitting is involved.
