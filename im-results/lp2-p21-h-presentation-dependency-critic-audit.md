## Expanded finite charge

- all three perfect pairings of four coordinates: adjacent, cross, outside-in;
- both pair traversal orders;
- both subtraction orientations;

This gives **48 distinct sum/difference presentations**. For each of the repository's 10 self-indexed four-rune objects, I then reused the p21 report's full 32-way omission/orientation/page-insertion family, for **15,360 configurations** total.

Result: **4 hits**, all on **p21**, all using the **outside-in** pairing and **left-minus-right** differences, all omitting **6**, and all reconstructing the same consecutive sequence:


This is useful evidence: the correlation is robustly specific and, inside this bounded family, it identifies the outside-in / left-minus-right structure. But that identification is made **by the semantic hit itself**. It cannot also be counted as independent evidence that H was source-selected or historically authored.
