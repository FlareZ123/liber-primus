### Key-independent all-key logic

For repeated additive and affine models, fix a possible 11-rune authentic-GP plaintext fourth word at red rune positions 13..23. It supplies `K[0..10] = C[13..23] - a P[13..23] (mod29)`. Thus the first red word at positions0..4 and second at positions5..8 are both fully determined, independent of unknown K[11..12]. Testing all native-GP word types at this anchor and every scalar `a` exactly rules out the dictionary-consistent full solution under this model. For plaintext autokey, the fourth word instead determines `P[0..10]` exactly by the feedback equation; the same first-two-word gate applies. For ciphertext autokey, the entire fourth plaintext word is already fixed from the observable original cipher differences at lag13. For multiplicative pads, the original fourth ciphertext has no zero symbols, so a zero-valued anchor plaintext is impossible and each nonzero original anchor fixes eleven invertible key units; first two words are again fixed. No 29^13 search is necessary.

**Decision:** retire these exact 13-local classical cipher families. A human would have a plausible motive to try 13 from the original red-dot sign, but this particular operation does not recover a GP English sentence even in the unusually discriminating fourth-word position. The result is conditional on the predeclared finite source-native dictionary, authentic printed word spaces, start phase0 at the red color change, and the specified fixed-rate GP29 operations. It does not reject a non-English source, alternative spelling, a different period, a historical text transformation, nonperiodic steganographic payload, or a key selected elsewhere. Adding exception clocks or arbitrary phases solely to fit the negative would weaken rather than advance the original solve path.
