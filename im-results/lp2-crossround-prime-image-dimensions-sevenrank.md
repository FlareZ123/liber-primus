## A compact source-grounded route

A participant in the 2012 Cicada challenge was explicitly told to multiply three primes associated with the **original opening JPEG**. The original image is **509 pixels wide and 503 pixels high**, both prime. Its prime dimensions helped produce the historical website 845145127.com via multiplication with 3301 ([original-image catalog and geometry](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cicada3301%27s_First_Message.jpg); [2012 phone instruction and reconstruction](https://uncovering-cicada.fandom.com/wiki/What_Happened_%282012%29)).

Independently, the creator-linked 2014 OpenPuff phase presented three number squares of orders **5, 7, 5**, including a complete 7×7 square with row sum1033, recorded in [the preserved square text](../resources/cicada2014-openpuff-magic-squares.txt). Following submission, the historical Stage10 response exposed three source JPEG files named **107.jpg, 167.jpg and 229.jpg** ([byte-source reconstruction](2014-stage10-three-images-lp1-source-handoff.md)).

The preserved **signed 2016 tree image** at [4gq25.jpg](../resources/historical/cicada2016-4gq25.jpg) is **563 pixels wide and 569 high**. Original unknown LP2 physical p32 contains exactly **121 GP29 runes**. Reflowing them in an 11×11 square and summing the first printed-order row and column in **Gematria Primus prime weights** independently reproduces **563** and **569**, respectively ([previously established original-image relation](tree-prime-canvas-partition.md)). This strongly supports a p32↔2016-image link, while the reflow and any proposed image-key consumer remain hypotheses.

The additional, previously unindexed cross-round equality is an **exact seven-step shift in ordinary-prime ordinal**, with original image axes exchanged:

| Earlier original 2012 JPEG axis | Value / prime ordinal | Add seven prime ordinals | Later signed 2016 JPEG axis |

The two numerical axis increments are exactly **60 pixels** after exchanging horizontal and vertical axes. The source's unique **7×7** 2014 square supplies a physically published number **seven**, so a human participant could in principle notice the ordinal step without selecting a wholly unconstrained integer. This remains a backward-looking connection: the historical 2014 or 2016 instructions do **not** direct participants to transpose old image dimensions, advance prime ranks by seven, or derive a rune key from the output.

A separate, strictly weaker arithmetic match is **107+167+229=503**, so the three Stage10 prime JPEG filenames sum to the original 2012 image height. Their actual contents are the already-solved **LP1 physical pages14–16**, not LP2 prime-ordinal pages28/39/50; that distinction was established independently in the Stage10 source audit. No signed stage tells the solver to add their names, and this relation does not by itself identify an LP2 decoding operation.

## What is genuinely reproducible, and what it does not establish

The [self-contained original-input CJS SFT](../sims/lp2_crossround_prime_image_dimensions_sft.cjs) reads the canonical p32 runes, the original signed 2016 JPEG, and the actual 2014 5/7/5 magic-square text; it checks the independent GP-prime axis sums, actual JPEG dimensions, all four ordinary-prime ordinals **96, 97, 103, 104**, seven-step pair, nearby five/six/eight rank controls, and the historical three-name sum. The 2012 dimension pair is sourced to the independent preserved first-image catalog, not to a downloaded byte-exact image inside this repository. This distinction prevents the original 2012 source from being overstated as locally hash-pinned.

The prime-ranking operation and the 2014 side length are mathematically simple and period-appropriate. Yet the correspondence was found **after seeing both image sizes** and after reviewing many numerical alignments in this repository. Its conditional exactness is not a corrected probability of intentional authorship. Both 2012 and 2016 JPEG dimensions are already primes, and the 2016 pair was independently aligned to p32; a retrospective numerical transform can arise by selection. A rotated image with a literal 60px border is **not** supported by these facts: the two images depict different subjects and no matching pixels have been identified.

One additionally tested source-name hypothesis did **not** recover plaintext. An external commentary notes that Jung's *Red Book: Liber Novus* contains a part titled *Liber Primus*, with its English-language prologue *The Way of What Is to Come* beginning on page229 ([2020 contemporary analysis](https://frankgbosman.wordpress.com/2020/02/11/cicada-3301-the-book-of-daniel/); [published table-of-contents witness](https://toc.library.ethz.ch/objects/pdf/z01_978-0-393-06567-1_01.pdf)). The original LP2 p22 reclining illustration is a mirrored copy of LP1 pp14–16 ([original-image match](lp1-lp2-illustration-reuse-and-cipher-transfer-gate.md)); the claimed Jung illustration resemblance is **not a pixel-certified source attribution**. On all **131** unchanged p22 rune values, applying either add/subtract GP29 Vigenère with fixed Jung-prologue English key `THEWAYOFWHATISTOCOME` (19 GP tokens) yields original candidate IoCs **0.03264827** and **0.03394011**, with no recognizable plaintext. The corresponding German-title key `DERWEGDESKOMMENDEN` (18 GP tokens) gives **0.03217851** in both directions. No page, key clock or plaintext was chosen from the output. The Jung interpretation is thus a tentative external textual association, not a new solution.
