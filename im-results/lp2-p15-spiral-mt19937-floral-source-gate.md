A known difficulty for any decoder is the marked suppression of repeated ciphertext runes. To accommodate that *without altering a single original symbol*, the test uses a reversible previous-ciphertext difference alphabet: at each page reset the preceding rune is GP index 0, and for each original ciphertext rune compute `d=(current-previous) mod29`.
