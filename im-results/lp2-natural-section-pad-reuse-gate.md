# Natural-section GP29 pad-reuse and chain-step reuse gate: sections 4 and 5

**Result (11 November 2026):** An exploratory language signal on two adjacent original LP2 sections prompted two independent exact candidate inverses. Across their source-aligned 1,021-rune overlap, a **direct reused additive GP29 pad** requires at least **55 non-dictionary plaintext words**, and a **reused GP29 chain-step pad** requires at least **57**. Both exact tests pass whole-overlap positive controls with **zero** exceptions. These bounded results strongly disfavor normal-English, identical-key, aligned section-start encryption under either of these frozen operations. No authentic unknown plaintext or key is recovered. They do **not** exclude different vocabulary, altered segmentation, key skips, independent pads, or a different cipher law.

## Concrete original-input question

The canonical 12,956 unknown runes divide into source-defined sections of lengths `[729,1145,1729,9,1894,1021,1524,1589,3316]`. Section 4 begins at global index **3,612** and has 1,894 runes, spanning p15's body and pp16–22. Section 5 begins at global index **5,506** and has 1,021 runes, spanning pp23–26. Both begin at printed word starts. An exploratory start-aligned differential screen of section pairs found this adjacent pair unusually compatible with a shared additive pad, but language-score selection alone does not identify a key. The decisive follow-up was therefore performed with the pair and clock frozen.

**Direct-pad hypothesis:** If `C4[t] = (P4[t]+K[t]) mod29` and `C5[t] = (P5[t]+K[t]) mod29`, then `D[t]=(C4[t]-C5[t]) mod29 = (P4[t]-P5[t]) mod29`, eliminating the key.

**Chain-step hypothesis:** The manuscript's unusual scarcity of adjacent repeated runes also motivates a shared pad on ciphertext *steps*: `C_s[t] = (C_s[t-1] + P_s[t] + K[t]) mod29` for sections `s=4,5`. Consequently `Dstep[t]=(C4[t]-C4[t-1]-C5[t]+C5[t-1]) mod29 = (P4[t]-P5[t]) mod29`. The frozen test sets the predecessor to zero at each printed section start. Any unknown initial predecessor changes only the **first rune** constraint, within the first printed word, so cannot explain dozens of forced exceptions. Both difference streams can be tested jointly against source-visible word separators without guessing a pad.

## Exact test and controls

- Original inputs: `resources/liber-primus/lp2-corpus.json` and `lp2-transcription.txt`. Parsing rune positions while treating `-` and `.` as printed word separators reproduces **12,956/12,956** source runes and the established **2,896** words. Section 4/5's first 1,021 positions end at complete printed words in both streams.
- Candidate language: the existing `resources/control/english_wordfreq_textblob.txt`, 29,157 entries, becomes **29,061 distinct GP29 word forms** in a 74,946-node trie. Transliteration is longest matching GP digraph, with the conventional K→C, V→U, Z→S, Q→C simplification. This generous general-English vocabulary covers **15,441/15,733** tokens of the separate Gibran control (`resources/control/gibran_prophet_58585.txt`), leaving **292 / 15,733 = 1.856%** outside the vocabulary.
- At each rune position, consider **all 29** possible first-plaintext values. The second value is fixed by `D`. A joint exact trie dynamic program carries both current dictionary prefixes and minimizes the total number of words that depart from the vocabulary. Once a word is outside, its remaining runes are arbitrary, charged **once** for that word. At a printed word end, any unfinished dictionary prefix is also charged once. This is the minimum possible non-vocabulary count, not the result of a heuristic beam search.
- **Direct-pad minimum:** **55** out-of-lexicon words; first forced exception at zero-based rune index **23**. Intermediate minima after 200/400/600/800/1,000 positions are **11/20/32/43/53**.
- **Chain-step minimum:** **57** out-of-lexicon words under the source-start reset. Intermediate minima after 200/400/600/800/1,000 positions are **8/20/32/42/54**. Both original streams have **239 and 224** printed word starts, respectively, or 463 words together.
- **Matched positive controls:** independently selected complete GP dictionary words of exactly the same printed word-length layouts are encrypted with an identical artificial direct pad, and again with an identical additive pad on their ciphertext first differences. Exact replay finds **zero** forced nonlexicon words in either 1,021-rune plant.

**Reproducer:** [exact standalone dynamic-programming SFT](../sims/lp2_section_reused_pad_dictionary_sft.cjs). It asserts the original corpus alignment, word count, dictionary/trie sizes, both original minima, independent native word coverage, and both planted controls, then prints their checkpoints. The DP uses original printed section starts and **no fitted offset, key phase, dictionary adaptation, crib selection, or reranking after observing output**.

## Interpretation and next decision

The original section-pair effect was an exploratory high score across natural boundaries. The independent, exact whole-word feasibility checks disfavor both candidate mechanisms: **55/463** or **57/463** forced unknown words, while the separate original-language-like reference has **292/15,733** unknown words. A coherent ordinary GP-English two-time-pad decryption under either frozen relation should have produced a largely lexicon-consistent pair, rather than dozens of forced exceptions. Neither hypothesis identifies an underlying key or recovers authentic unknown plaintext.

**Retire both natural-start identical-pad hypotheses for this adjacent section pair.** A distinct shared-pad route needs an independently supplied clock or offset, source-motivated vocabulary, and a successful whole-word reconstruction on original ciphertext. Ciphertext-filtered skips change keystream consumption and require recoverable side state. The 56-page LP2 cipher remains unsolved.
