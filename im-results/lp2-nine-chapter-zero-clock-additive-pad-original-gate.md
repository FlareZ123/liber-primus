# Nine-chapter source-visible zero-clock reused-additive-key decision

**15 November 2026, Agent40. Original-input negative, native plaintext positives passed. No recovered LP2 plaintext or key.** This test asks a direct decryptability question: if the original nine visually headed encrypted chapters reused the *same additive GP29 pad from their red-heading beginnings*, the difference between any aligned pair of ciphertext passages should cancel the pad and reveal the characteristic difference spectrum of source-native English prose. That would permit a credible many-time-pad crib attack without learning the seed. The complete original-cipher test found **no positive pair out of 36**, while the identical method detected **all 36** genuinely same-pad pairs under two independent source-native prose controls.

## Exact original-input attack and source basis

Use [the original physical nine-chapter starts](../im-results/lp2-global-original-visual-section-map.md) p0, p3, p8, p15, p23, p27, p33, p40 and p54. Their original GP29 lengths are respectively **729, 1145, 1729, 1903, 1021, 1433, 1680, 3008 and 308**, covering all **12,956** unsolved source runes, with every original red heading and black body intact. For each of the `9 choose 2 = 36` pairs, align at native chapter rune offset 0 and compare exactly the first `min(length_a,length_b)` GP29 runes. The narrowed mechanism assumes the additive law `C_chapter[i] = (P_chapter[i] + K[i]) mod 29`, where all chapters use the same `K[0], K[1], ...` and *every printed rune advances the clock*. Thus `Δ_ab[i] = (C_a[i] - C_b[i]) mod29` equals the difference between the two original GP plaintexts if the assumption holds. **No phase, page, orientation, shift, sign, or model parameters were fitted against the original.** The test uses source-visible chapter reset boundaries; it is conditional on literal same-state reuse at those positions.

The score tests for a genuinely source-native *nonuniform* difference spectrum rather than a generic chi-square anomaly. The 46,389 GP runes obtained from the public-domain Gibran *Prophet* corpus after its first narrative `Almustafa` train the reference unigram distribution `f[x]`; the native-GP difference probabilities are `p[δ] = Σ_x f[x] f[(x−δ) mod29]` with a fixed 0.5 additive count smoothing. For pair counts `h[δ]`, `LLR = Σ_δ h[δ] ln(29 p[δ])` compares the source-English shared-pad difference model with a uniform independent-pad null. `p[0] = 0.05842468331838765` vs uniform `1/29 ≈ 0.03448276`. A positive LLR is a prospective signal; a negative one favors the flat source model.

The exact GP orthography encoder is first independently checked against **all 95 original known-clear p57 GP indices** (including literal `TUNNELNG` representing *TUNNELING*). It normalizes K/Q→C, V→U, Z→S, and token ING→GP NG. The same fully frozen encoder processes all controls. The absence of key fitting is particularly important because a long 29-way pad can trivially be constructed after the fact for any alleged plaintext.

## Cryptanalytic decision and limitations

**Retire only a common zero-phase 29-symbol additive pad that advances at every rune from each of the nine original visual chapter beginnings, with source-English-like GP plaintext.** It cannot be used to recover the unknown chapters by simple many-time-pad differences. This does **not** exclude a source-authenticated transparent-F clock skip (which can desynchronize otherwise identical pads), asynchronous key starts, distinct keys per chapter, a nonlinear feedback cipher, a different intermediate alphabet or a wholly different plaintext language. It does not adjudicate the previously separate 2014 p3/p39 or p53/p55 image-selected relations, and supplies no new author-signed instruction. A renewed two-time-pad attack needs an independent original clue that specifies an exact alternative *shared* clock or a new repeated-key landing spot; retuning chapter offsets based on difference scores is not warranted.

The original glyphs and existing authoritative [research synthesis](../results/README.md) are unchanged. This experiment narrows one directly decryptable architecture without promoting a false positive or declaring general cryptographic impossibility.
