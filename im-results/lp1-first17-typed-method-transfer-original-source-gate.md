# Original first17 LP1 typed methods: no source-visible transfer selector for unknown LP2

**Agent27, 23 November 2026; Director [#2422](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/2422).** The physical **17 first-release pages** contain **seven** separately encoded/runic source units, but only **three** executable cipher classes: literal GP29 emission, GP29 alphabet reflection (one instance also shifts by 3), and repeating additive GP29 Vigenère subtraction (two different keyed examples). They do **not** specify which original LP2 chapter receives which method, how to derive a new original-unknown key/clock, or whether a single cross-release method transfer is even intended. This is an **upstream selection failure**, not a negative claim that every seven-method permutation was exhaustively tried.

## Source-visible target mapping decision

A human method-transfer path would need **both** a target that is identified before decrypting and the key/mode/reset instructions (or sufficient visible operands to derive them) from the source. The seven LP1 units do not label a seven-stage cycle, an LP2 page/chapter permutation, or any numerical association pairing one of the first17 sections with one distinct unsolved LP2 chapter. LP2 is **nine source-defined illustrated chapters**, not seven decoder slots. Simply pairing section ordinal with chapter ordinal, iterating 7 through 9, choosing a well-known theme word after seeing output, or testing a full method permutation would create an invented mapping.

The most concrete adjacent thematic examples are the **already-solved** LP2 p56 totient cipher and the **literal** LP2 p57 “FIND THE DIVINITY WITHIN” line; neither is a new target/operand pair. For unsolved pages, original LP2's p14 **FOLD** and p15 Fibonacci square are readable on the artifact, but **neither** is the name and parameterization of a demonstrated LP1 transformation. Artwork-correspondence proposals are independently handled in [#2419](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/2419), not a license to assume a unique method when one insect illustration occurs in multiple LP1 solved contexts. The existing source-named keyword and FOLD transfer tests are extensive; no ciphertext-derived key phase or new arbitrary receiver was explored here.

**Decision:** A human reader of these authentic pre-LP2 sources can learn the GP29 alphabet and several possible operations, **but cannot select even one new LP2 unknown-chapter recipient with all necessary state/operand fixed**. In accordance with the upstream gate, no 150/200-rune inverse or new code-search was authorized. This retires the strong first17→LP2 *seven-method instruction schedule* until another first-party source points to a real receiver and key; it does not retire independently clued methods or the original first17 artifact itself.

**Actionable missing input:** one dated creator-authored instruction or artifact marking, for example, an LP2 page/chapter alongside both `FIRFUMFERENFE` and a reset rule, or a source-visible symbol that unambiguously selects that solved LP1 operation. Absent that input, a completed guessed-key search would measure tuning rather than recover original plaintext.


## Separate LP1-derived PRF seed follow-up (Agent20, 24 November)

The participant-suggested first17→last57 route was also tested as a specific derived-key construction, without claiming that the source selected it. Two fixed LP1 GP-prime seed serializations (2,797 rune values; and those values plus 39 printed numbers) were expanded by SHA-256/SHA-512/HMAC-SHA-256/HMAC-SHA-512 counter streams, reduced mod29, both signs, phase zero, continuous clock. All 16 candidates had valid p0 prefix paths, but the top four p0 candidates failed full-corpus re-encryption and yielded nonlinguistic outputs; the other 12 were not full-stream decoded. This is a bounded negative, not an exhaustive exclusion of every LP1-derived method. See [the reproducible PRF seed gate](lp1-first17-prf-seed-gate.md).
