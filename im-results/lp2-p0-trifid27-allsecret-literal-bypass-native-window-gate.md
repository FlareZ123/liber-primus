A classical three-dimensional **Trifid** cube has **27** cells, while Gematria Primus contains **29** distinct runes. One elementary way to use Trifid is to pass two reserved rune identities through unchanged, applying any secret 27-cell cube permutation to the remaining 27. A source-specific previous test used **F (GP index0)** and **EA (GP index28)** as its two literal control runes, taking the natural consecutive interior GP indices1..27 as its cube alphabet; it tested sixteen fixed cube/period/clock options ([prior p0 source gate](lp2-p0-instar-trifid27-source-gate.md)). A genuine F/EA literal passthrough makes **two exact predictions that are independent of every possible cube key, period, direction, secret alphabet order, transposition within cube blocks and page clock**: the original ciphertext's counts of F and EA must equal those of its original plaintext. These counts survive both the forward and inverse reversible operations. An additive operation outside the cube, symbol-name relabeling that changes the two controls, hidden source rewriting, or a different plaintext language lies outside the tested class.

For perspective, each GP27 selection has exactly two control runes, and the 29-symbol inventory yields **406** unordered candidate pairs. The SFT uses the identical original GP29 histogram and checks all406 source-count pairs independently of any cube permutation. An identity pair survives this finite corpus *if at least one reference 729-rune window matches both original counts*. This permissive condition ignores all cryptographic constraints of the 27-symbol core, so it cannot create false exclusions of a genuine literal-bypass plaintext within the fixed controls.

- Canonical GP spelling: **81/406** pairs have a match in the union of three sources, hence **325/406** have none.

These counts describe the *fixed literary registers only*. Shorter or longer opening sections, different prose, foreign languages, replacement alphabets, further cipher layers and nonliteral bypasses can have different symbol frequencies.

**Research decision:** The F/EA-control 27-cube is no longer a credible *direct* decoder into the independently represented GP-English samples, **even if the secret cube key is unknown**, so no 27! cube-order search is justified on this basis.
