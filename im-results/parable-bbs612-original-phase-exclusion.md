# Parable prime clauses as a 612-state Blum–Blum–Shub seed: original-input phase exclusion

**Decision, 12 November 2026: new source-derived period, no recovered LP2 plaintext or key.** For the specific 612-phase GP29 additive and EA-transparent GP28 rank-additive ciphers, the original full-manuscript output fails a key-independent necessary language test. This result addresses a *candidate plaintext consumer*, unlike the prior original-grid squaring test, which assessed whether 32×8 table bytes themselves were generated by modular squaring.

## Plausible human solve path and the unproved premise

The actual solved p57 PARABLE has a GP-prime sum of **449** for its red title and **1,259, 1,031, 1,229** for its three body clauses. The three body sums multiply to **1,595,277,641**, explicitly printed as `Parable 1,595,277,641` in original 2013 `761.MP3` metadata. The author demonstrably associated these numbers with the poem; no historical instruction names a pseudorandom generator.

A solver familiar with the LP1 prime/totient instruction might notice that the **first two ordered body-clause primes** both satisfy `p ≡ 3 (mod 4)` while the third is coprime to their product. This exactly supplies the parameters of a *possible* Blum–Blum–Shub stream:

- Candidate modulus **M = 1,259 × 1,031 = 1,298,029**, a Blum integer.
- Candidate seed **s = 1,229** (the third printed clause sum).
- Standard initial state **x₀ = s² mod M = 212,412**.
- State recurrence **xᵢ₊₁ = xᵢ² mod M**.

The initialized trajectory returns to x₀ after exactly **612** distinct states. Its least significant bit sequence also has minimal period **612**. Therefore using a state-derived GP shift, or packing successive bits into 5-bit words, supplies a period of 612 regardless of key orientation or unknown initial phase (provided the 5-bit stream uses a fixed grouping). The **mathematics is exact**. Interpretation of the factors as a BBS instruction is unverified and should not be presented as creator intent.

## Key-independent original cipher test

A period-T additive pad has an invariant: two ciphertext symbols encrypted with the same pad phase are equal precisely when their underlying plaintext symbols are equal, even when the secret pad values are completely unknown. The period here is fixed **before** consulting ciphertext, by the p57 number and the standard modular-square update.

Apply this to every one of **12,956 canonical unsolved runes** over 55 rune-bearing physical pages:

1. **GP29 direct**: `Cᵢ = (Pᵢ + Kᵢ) mod 29`, any secret `Kᵢ₊₆₁₂ = Kᵢ`.
2. **GP28 predecessor-excluding**: a repeated original ciphertext glyph represents special GP symbol EA; otherwise `rᵢ = (Cᵢ − Cᵢ₋₁ − 1) mod29` is its rank among the 28 *different* glyphs and `rᵢ = (Pᵢ + Kᵢ) mod28`. The 55 physical page initials are arbitrary visible IVs. The 86 same-rune transitions are excluded.
3. The GP28 rank model is evaluated with a book-global clock that **consumes every rune** and with a **compacted clock** consuming only the 12,815 nonrepeating non-IV glyphs. Both allow every one of 612 completely secret phase key values.

| Invariant | Original matching pairs | Eligible same-phase pairs | Original collision rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| GP29, book-global clock | 4,474 | 130,704 | **0.03423002** |
| GP28 rank, global rune clock | 4,481 | 127,880 | **0.03504066** |
| GP28 rank, skip-IV/EA clock | 4,509 | 127,780 | **0.03528721** |

The original values cluster near the independent-uniform 29- or 28-symbol equality rate. They are far lower than prose-like GP29 native-source collision rates.

## Genuine plaintext and secret-key controls

The reproducer parses checked-in Shakespeare, Gibran and Emerson sources using the shared GP encoder and takes **45 disjoint manuscript-length 12,956-rune windows** (16, four and 25 respectively). It retains the original page lengths for page-IV handling. For each source window, it encrypts all symbols with actual period612 BBS-state-derived secret pads and then inverts every symbol exactly:

- Direct GP29: phase612 shifts in the 29-rune register.
- GP28: 28-way predecessor-excluding encryption preserving EA as literal repeated ciphertext, including either global or marker-skipping key clock.

All **135** positive control encryptions recover their entire source input; for every control the within-phase equality numerator and denominator are exactly preserved by the encryption. Every one of the **45** native controls has a collision rate **above 0.055** under all three invariant calculations, while the largest original rate is **0.03529**. Thus even completely unknown, adversarially selected additive pad values at all 612 phases cannot make these original symbols look like prose sampled from the matched references under the declared architecture.

This is a reference-conditioned exclusion of direct GP-native prose. A compressed/binary register, evolving output permutations, extra historical key material, page-specific BBS reinitialization, or a BBS consumer other than additive/rank transport remains possible.

## Research decision

The source factorization uniquely suggests the standard BBS parameter roles by the first two clauses' Blum-prime status, but this candidate **does not directly decrypt the book** under a simple repeating additive or predecessor-rank pad. Avoid additional phase search or secret-pad fitting for these same 612-period families. New work should seek an original instruction tying the p57 numbers to a distinct reversible operation, or an independently testable nonperiodic language representation.
