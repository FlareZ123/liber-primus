# Parable prime clauses as a 612-state Blum–Blum–Shub seed: original-input phase exclusion

**Decision, 12 November 2026: new source-derived period, no recovered LP2 plaintext or key.** For the specific 612-phase GP29 and EA-transparent GP28 rank-substitution ciphers, the original full-manuscript output fails a key-independent necessary language test. This result addresses a *candidate plaintext consumer*, unlike the prior original-grid squaring test, which assessed whether 32×8 table bytes themselves were generated by modular squaring.

## Plausible human solve path and the unproved premise

The actual solved p57 PARABLE has a GP-prime sum of **449** for its red title and **1,259, 1,031, 1,229** for its three body clauses. The three body sums multiply to **1,595,277,641**, explicitly printed as `Parable 1,595,277,641` in original 2013 `761.MP3` metadata. The author demonstrably associated these numbers with the poem; no historical instruction names a pseudorandom generator.

A solver familiar with the LP1 prime/totient instruction might notice that the **first two ordered body-clause primes** both satisfy `p ≡ 3 (mod 4)` while the third is coprime to their product. This exactly supplies the parameters of a *possible* Blum–Blum–Shub stream:

- Candidate modulus **M = 1,259 × 1,031 = 1,298,029**, a Blum integer.
- Candidate seed **s = 1,229** (the third printed clause sum).
- Standard initial state **x₀ = s² mod M = 212,412**.
- State recurrence **xᵢ₊₁ = xᵢ² mod M**.

The initialized trajectory returns to x₀ after exactly **612** distinct states. Its least significant bit sequence also has minimal period **612**. Therefore using a state-derived GP shift, or packing successive bits into 5-bit words, supplies a period of 612 regardless of key orientation or unknown initial phase (provided the 5-bit stream uses a fixed grouping). The **mathematics is exact**. Interpretation of the factors as a BBS instruction is unverified and should not be presented as creator intent.

## Key-independent original cipher test

Any period-T sequence of arbitrary bijections has an invariant: two ciphertext symbols at the same phase are equal precisely when their underlying plaintext symbols are equal. The complete secret substitution tables can be unknown. The period here is fixed **before** consulting ciphertext, by the p57 number and the standard modular-square update.

Apply this to every one of **12,956 canonical unsolved runes** over 55 rune-bearing physical pages:

1. **GP29 direct**: `Cᵢ = σ_(i mod 612)(Pᵢ)`, any 612 unknown permutations σ independently chosen from the entire 29-symbol symmetric group.
2. **GP28 predecessor-excluding**: a repeated original ciphertext glyph represents special GP symbol EA; otherwise `rᵢ = (Cᵢ − Cᵢ₋₁ − 1) mod29` is its rank among the 28 *different* glyphs and `rᵢ = τ_(phase)(Pᵢ)` for any one of 612 entirely unknown 28-way permutations. The 55 physical page initials are arbitrary visible IVs. The 86 same-rune transitions are excluded.
3. The GP28 rank model is evaluated with a book-global clock that **consumes every rune** and with a **compacted clock** consuming only the 12,815 nonrepeating non-IV glyphs. Both allow all 612 independent secret phase alphabets, much broader than an additive keystream.

| Invariant | Original matching pairs | Eligible same-phase pairs | Original collision rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| GP29, book-global clock | 4,474 | 130,704 | **0.03423002** |
| GP28 rank, global rune clock | 4,481 | 127,880 | **0.03504066** |

The original values cluster near the independent-uniform 29- or 28-symbol equality rate. They are far lower than prose-like GP29 native-source collision rates.

## Source-derived bit-packing periods and original reset clocks

The literal BBS least-significant-bit cycle has period612. Packing consecutive cyclic output bits into ordinary **2-bit**, **4-bit**, **5-bit** or **8-bit** units gives respectively **306**, **153**, **612** and **153** output units per full cycle, with independently computed *minimal* periods of exactly those lengths. Therefore 153 and 306 are also source-fixed periods if this speculative BBS output enters a byte or nibble readout.

A separate original-input extension checks the **nine independently visible red-heading chapters** and every physical rune-bearing page as *reset clocks*, using the same arbitrary unknown phase permutation across reset regions. The exact source visual chapter ranges are pp0–2, 3–7, 8–14, 15–22, 23–26, 27–32, 33–39, 40–53 and 54–55. For every period (153, 306, 612), reset (physical page, visual chapter), and direct GP29 or GP28 rank model (marker consumes / does not consume phase), it scores original same-phase equality over all available pair observations. ## Genuine plaintext and secret-key controls

The reproducer parses checked-in Shakespeare, Gibran and Emerson sources using the shared GP encoder and takes **45 disjoint manuscript-length 12,956-rune windows** (16, four and 25 respectively). It retains the original page lengths for page-IV handling. For each source window, it encrypts all symbols using 612 independently shuffled secret phase permutations, and then inverts every symbol exactly:

- Direct GP29: 612 arbitrary secret 29-way tables, each with a complete inverse.
- GP28: 612 arbitrary secret 28-way tables driving predecessor-excluding encryption, preserving EA as literal repeated ciphertext, including either global or marker-skipping phase clocks.

All **135** positive control encryptions recover their entire source input; for every control the within-phase equality numerator and denominator are exactly preserved by the encryption. Every one of the **45** native controls has a collision rate **above 0.055** under all three invariant calculations, while the largest original rate is **0.03529**. Thus even completely unknown and independently selected **permutations** at all 612 phases cannot produce these original equality frequencies from verbatim prose sampled from the matched references under the declared architecture.

This is a reference-conditioned exclusion of direct GP-native prose. A compressed/binary register, evolving output permutations, extra historical key material, page-specific BBS reinitialization, or a BBS consumer other than additive/rank transport remains possible.

## Research decision

The ordered source factorization suggests the standard BBS parameter roles by the first two clauses' Blum-prime status, but those roles are speculative. This candidate **does not directly decrypt the book** under any direct repeating GP29 or predecessor-rank GP28 bijective phase table. Avoid additional phase search or secret-alphabet fitting for these same 612-period families. New work should seek an original instruction tying the p57 numbers to a distinct reversible operation, or an independently testable nonperiodic language representation.
