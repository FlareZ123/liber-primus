Given the `LP3` token triple and that particular radix change, `KO2` follows automatically. The existing [corrected historical-key report](lp2-2014-firfumference-vigenere-gate.md) already exhausts the direct 13-phase p14 key across all unknown pages, while its 13×8 exhaustive **stack of the two historical Vigenère keys** fails on every entire LP2 unknown chapter under genuine F-transparent semantics.
