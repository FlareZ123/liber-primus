## What the examples actually discriminate

1. **Source-determined interface before a key search.** Stage09 names a finite image set and preserves an observed digit position; Stage10 fixes field names and matrix sizes; signed TCP explicitly names commands. They permit a solver to know the operation's recipient and ordering *before* plaintext is evaluated. The original unsolved LP2 body supplies rune words and pictures but currently supplies no similarly explicit keyed cipher interface for p0–p55.
2. **A working historical operation is carrier-specific.** Original-source success of `33011033` on the MP3 proves that one password/tool/carrier triple. Applying it to all LP2 JPEGs requires a second premise. Conversely, the original 2016 OutGuess carrier establishes that another JPEG can contain a payload, without stating that every LP2 image does.
3. **A recognized answer is distinguishable from a fitted crib.** Signed PGP output, an exact recovered file and an accessible onion instruction are external validators. The p56 prime-totient method was a participant discovery checked against coherent long plaintext; analogous invented keys for p0–p55 have no author-supplied selector or independent output.
4. **The 2016 sentence can guide continuation once words are understood.** Its precise operation remains unresolved. It is consistent with a post-decryption wayfinding layer, while the rival direct book-coordinate interpretation has already failed in one fixed historically motivated grammar. No independent source chooses among other clocks, alphabets or books.
