# The p50 perimeter `LP3` and the exact LP1 p03 ↔ LP2 p57 author-text echo

**22 November 2026. Intermediate original-source cross-reference, not recovered unknown plaintext.** Original pointer observation by Agent32, source-text comparison and fixed LP1-page03 key receiver by Agent6. The interpretation is conditional on a conventional base60 table codec and an unprescribed clockwise walk. No first-party instruction yet proves that the three letters were deliberately encoded.

## The literal small source object

The original physical p49–51 numerical table has 32 rows and 8 columns, 256 printed two-character tokens, transcribed in `resources/liber-primus/base60-grid.json`. Under its *conditional* standard base60 digit order, original printed cells at global zero-based offsets **47,55,63** are `1G`, `1K`, `0p`, whose unsigned bytes are **76,80,51** and ASCII letters **`LP3`**. These are one-based **rows6–8 of the rightmost eighth column**. If a reader traverses the complete original 32×8 rectangle's **76-byte outer circumference clockwise starting at its upper-left corner**, these exact three source cells are **one-based perimeter positions13–15**.

The final already solved LP2 physical p57 red heading PARABLE is followed by an **original 13-dot mark**, and its completely solved body says `WE MUST SHED OUR OWN CIRCUMFERENCES`. Source alignment therefore offers a compact conditional human instruction: start reading the 76-cell circumference at dot position13, revealing the literal label `LP3`. Original row/column orientation, direction, zero/one origin and how many cells to read are not fully stated by this clue. The table's naturally zero-based row-major indexing also displays the same three bytes as a right-column substring, so the appearance need not rely on the ring origin.

The independently archived participant 2014 `sexagesimal.bin` record, `resources/cicada2014-sexagesimal-source.json`, has **the identical byte values at offsets47,55,63** despite its documented ten other discrepancies relative to the original printed table. This confirms that the particular three character values are not a 2026 transcription edit. It does **not** make the participant's bytes a first-party authored 256-byte payload.

## Material base59 ambiguity: the same original three tokens read `KO2`

The repository preserves an *unresolved original digit-alphabet question*: the printed 256-cell table has **no lowercase `f`**, so a *conditional* 59-digit source alphabet obtained by deleting `f` remains possible. Under this alternative, the **same original unmodified tokens** `1G 1K 0p` give byte values **75,79,50**, exact ASCII **`KO2`**, because each of the three byte values decreases by exactly one. Thus the apparent `LP3` pointer is **not alphabet-independent**; the 2014 participant's archived base60 conversion cannot by itself authenticate the author's radix.

A speculative but independently intelligible counterpart to `LP3 = Liber Primus Part 1 page03` is **`KO2 = the second original LP1 KOAN`**, whose source original part1 physical page14 opens `A KOAN` and tells the reader:

> THE I IS THE VOICE OF THE CIRCUMFERENCE

The original later p57 imperative `WE MUST SHED OUR OWN CIRCUMFERENCES` echoes the second Koan's *circumference / false self* subject. There are exactly two original LP1 sections explicitly titled **A KOAN** in the pinned solved-page reference (p06 and p14). A conditional two-code reading could therefore select **LP1 p03 via standard base60** (INSTAR / EMERGE) or **the second LP1 Koan via the alternate base59** (CIRCUMFERENCE). Both already solved source passages connect semantically to different parts of original LP2 p57. Such a pattern may be designed, but its deliberate status is **unproved**: `KO2` is **not an author-written identifier or established abbreviation** for that Koan, and the English echo itself is compatible with thematic reuse.

To bound the alternate recipient, freeze `KO2` as the second LP1 Koan in `resources/external/relikd-solved-gp29-native-v1.json`, section `jpg107-167` (**319 native clear GP29 runes** including heading). Without any chosen phase or key advancement, use its first158 original runes as a GP29 key on the complete printed p49→p51 158-rune flank. `C−K` decoded IoC **0.03313714**, `C+K` **0.03345965**; both outputs are nonlinguistic. For original 76-rune p55, `C−K` **0.03543860**, `C+K` **0.03368421**; also nonlinguistic. These are tiny direct key consumers, not a rejection of a genuine source-chosen Koan cipher.

The dual reading is a reason to **require an explicit radix convention before calling `LP3` a pointer**, and to avoid selecting either interpretation merely because its separately known text resembles one clause of p57. The one-base-unit shift of all three output bytes is a mechanical property of the codec ambiguity and therefore cannot independently authenticate two intentionally embedded clues.

The first four English words **LIKE THE INSTAR** occur only in original source records for **LP1 p03** and **LP2 p57** among all **17** solved-page records in the pinned reference, and both passages share the longer WITHIN / EMERGE instruction. This is a real literary/source-text cross-reference between the two genuine original pages. The earlier WELCOME section also used the independently solved **DIVINITY** GP29 Vigenère key, consistent with the later literal DIVINITY word. It supplies a less arbitrary potential recipient for `LP3` than guessed key material from unrelated Cicada stages.

**Alternative:** `LP3` could mean the unsolved LP2 p3, a page identifier in a different numbering scheme, or just a coincidental three-byte occurrence. The creator already reused themes across the book. Even a fully intentional literary echo does not prove a table-embedded pointer. The 13-dot/perimeter interpretation remains retroactively noticed, not a historically signed decoder.

## Decision

The exact LP1 p03 ↔ LP2 p57 **INSTAR/WITHIN/EMERGE** echo is a plausible thematic referent for Agent32's conditional `LP3` label, but the *same printed bytes under source-compatible base59 literally say `KO2`*, with an alternative original LP1 second-Koan / CIRCUMFERENCE echo. Neither reading is selected by an independently first-party codec instruction. The strongest missing evidence is the **creator's original table-reading instruction** and a **source-prescribed reversible cipher or other typed consumer**. Continue only if such an instruction provides deterministic origin, orientation, reference to p03 and cryptographic role. Do not turn thematic recognition or hand-chosen alignment into a plaintext claim.
