## The literal small source object

The original physical p49–51 numerical table has 32 rows and 8 columns, 256 printed two-character tokens, transcribed in `resources/liber-primus/base60-grid.json`. Under its *conditional* standard base60 digit order, original printed cells at global zero-based offsets **47,55,63** are `1G`, `1K`, `0p`, whose unsigned bytes are **76,80,51** and ASCII letters **`LP3`**. These are one-based **rows6–8 of the rightmost eighth column**. If a reader traverses the complete original 32×8 rectangle's **76-byte outer circumference clockwise starting at its upper-left corner**, these exact three source cells are **one-based perimeter positions13–15**.

The final already solved LP2 physical p57 red heading PARABLE is followed by an **original 13-dot mark**, and its completely solved body says `WE MUST SHED OUR OWN CIRCUMFERENCES`. Source alignment therefore offers a compact conditional human instruction: start reading the 76-cell circumference at dot position13, revealing the literal label `LP3`. Original row/column orientation, direction, zero/one origin and how many cells to read are not fully stated by this clue. The table's naturally zero-based row-major indexing also displays the same three bytes as a right-column substring, so the appearance need not rely on the ring origin.

The independently archived participant 2014 `sexagesimal.bin` record, `resources/cicada2014-sexagesimal-source.json`, has **the identical byte values at offsets47,55,63** despite its documented ten other discrepancies relative to the original printed table. This confirms that the particular three character values are not a 2026 transcription edit. It does **not** make the participant's bytes a first-party authored 256-byte payload.

It supplies a less arbitrary potential recipient for `LP3` than guessed key material from unrelated Cicada stages.
