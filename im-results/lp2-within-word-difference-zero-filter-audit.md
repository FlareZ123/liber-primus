# Within-word difference audit: the apparent step signal is the doublet filter

## Decision

The canonical LP2 ciphertext has a strong within-word adjacent-difference anomaly, but the anomaly is concentrated at **difference 0**, the already-known adjacent-equal deficit. After conditioning on nonzero differences, there is no statistically strong residual step distribution. This matters because a deconvolution of the full difference histogram against English can manufacture a seemingly nonuniform key-step distribution by absorbing the collision-repair deficit.

## September-2026 external 28-symbol chain claim: exact reproduction and equivalence

Director #1541 asked whether the external Leo-Y-Zhang/LiberPrimusAnalysis chain-cipher fingerprint adds a new original-cipher invariant. The external source is pinned at commit `c0875c8df069245d83d576243696a8d2f0faacc4` (7 September 2026), whose `fingerprint.py`, `tests3.py`, `tests5.py` and `controls.py` define the claim.

Its primary transform is exactly

```text
Delta_i = (C_i - C_(i-1)) mod 29.
```

On the canonical local 12,956-rune p0-p55 stream, the full continuous 12,955-transition histogram reproduces the external report **exactly**:

```text
[86,459,504,440,438,446,430,470,426,510,497,455,449,504,474,
 470,449,404,434,474,467,463,421,486,468,467,457,476,431]
```

The external previous-rune versus step independence calculation also reproduces exactly: Pearson **chi2=801.4231 on 784 df**, p about **0.325**. Conditioning away the zero-step column gives **chi2=774.5944 on 756 df**, p about **0.312**. Thus the nonzero step value is not detectably dependent on the previous ciphertext rune.

There is one useful calibration correction. The 28 nonzero bins over the **whole continuous stream** give chi2=**41.3803 on 27 df**, p about **0.03785**, so “flat” is a reasonable multiple-tested descriptive summary rather than an exact uniformity result. The already-established source segmentation is more informative: within printed words the same nonzero test is chi2=33.5568, p about 0.179; the complementary 2,895 word-boundary transitions give chi2=33.1365, p about 0.193; the two nonzero histograms are mutually compatible (2x28 homogeneity chi2=24.9521, p about 0.577). No stable nonzero-step feature survives the natural segmentation.

Most importantly, the alleged chain form

```text
C_i = C_(i-1) + s_i (mod 29)
```

with `s_i=Delta_i` is an **identity for every ciphertext stream**. The updated reproducer reconstructs all 12,956 original runes exactly from `C_0` plus the observed differences. Therefore “the ciphertext has a chain representation” does not select an encryption mechanism. The external synthetic chain positive proves its detector can find a chain generated by a known step key; it cannot make the observed difference stream itself evidence that such a key was historically used.

The only empirical content left is already local: zero steps are sharply suppressed, the 28 nonzero values have no strong source-stable residual structure, and step value is approximately independent of the previous ciphertext rune. These are the same collision/anti-repeat constraints documented below and elsewhere in the repository.

The external report's stronger interpretation, “zero step = one rare plaintext rune such as EA,” is additionally under tension with the independent p50 localization. The original 86 zero steps contain **33** events at the source-fixed phase-zero p50 low60/leading-zero class. Even after the exact 85 event-bearing printed word/page groups and their event counts are fixed, the exact conditional expectation is 20.63294 and `P(X>=33)=0.00062425`; conditioning also on the global 23/19/44 word-initial/final/interior totals gives `P=0.00078555`. A plaintext-only rare-letter exception would need an additional p50-clock mechanism or an unexplained plaintext-clock coupling to account for that alignment.

**Director decision for #1541:** the September chain fingerprint adds no new reversible cipher family or key requirement. Preserve it as an independently reproduced description of the same zero-difference deficit and near-flat nonzero differences. Do not create a new chain-key hunt from this report. Any future chain-like proposal must independently source the step generator and explain the p50-localized zero-step positions.

## Original-input measurement

The source parser is the canonical `resources/liber-primus/lp2-transcription.txt` with `resources/liber-primus/lp2-corpus.json`. It reconstructs exactly **2,896 words and 12,956 runes** over physical p0–55, so the audit uses the same word segmentation as the shared corpus.

There are **10,060 within-word transitions**. Their difference counts (mod 29) are:

`[63,356,373,354,338,349,325,372,328,397,377,362,343,381,369,365,333,313,349,368,373,366,334,390,371,352,349,380,330]`.

Under a uniform 29-symbol stream, difference 0 would occur **346.90** times. Only **63** are observed. The retained zero-transition fraction is **0.18161** under an iid-uniform reference. Thus the observed zero bin is **81.839% below that baseline**. This is an effective difference-frequency deficit, **not an estimate of how often the author rejected a candidate symbol**. Collision-suppression mechanisms can produce such a deficit, but no creator-selected mechanism has been identified.

Now remove difference 0 and test the remaining 28 bins against the natural equal-mass collision-repair expectation. There are **9,997 nonzero transitions**, giving **357.04 expected observations per nonzero difference**. The Pearson statistic is **33.5568 on 27 df**, with chi-square survival probability about **0.179**. The largest standardized deviation is difference 17 at **−2.33σ**. A matched 10,000-trial Monte Carlo null using a uniform 29-symbol base plus the measured zero-suppression rate gives mean nonzero chi-square **27.075** and empirical p≈**0.1831** for a statistic at least this large (maximum simulated 71.20). No nonzero difference is remotely comparable to the zero deficit.

## Consequence for the proposed K_STEP route

A recent external analysis proposed deconvolving the within-word difference distribution against an English transition distribution to infer a nonuniform keystream-step law. The canonical source data do not support treating that inference as a new key-generator signal without a collision-aware model. A uniform keystream followed by a rule that suppresses some adjacent equal ciphertext symbols can produce this broad shape: a deep hole at difference 0 and approximately equal mass in the 28 nonzero bins if replacements are spread across nonzero symbols.

The correct conclusion is therefore narrower and more useful:

1. **The zero-difference deficit is real and compatible with anti-repeat filtering; an author-selected filter remains unproved.**
2. **The nonzero difference bins do not provide a strong new key-step fingerprint.**
3. **Any full-histogram Fourier/EM deconvolution that ignores the filter is confounded and should not be used to select a keystream family.**
4. A future step-distribution attack would need to model the collision/rejection mechanism first and then demonstrate a residual nonzero-step structure that survives a held-out null.

This closes the apparent “nonuniform K_STEP” shortcut as a causal route to plaintext. It does not solve or independently prove the anti-repeat mechanism; it simply prevents the difference histogram from being misused as a key-generator oracle.
