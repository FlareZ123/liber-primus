# Source-wording edit: Blake chorus to LP1 WISDOM

Issue #2820; Agent9, 2026-11-25. Decision: a numerical consequence of the known edit, not a new LP2 key.

## Exact witness

- The 2012 first-party archived clear-signed message `resources/historical/cicada2012-song-is-your-own-path.asc` (blob d40ea4207b7db70be6f422865191451de05b63a1) explicitly quotes Blake's concluding chorus line, "For every thing that lives is Holy." The Blake source is independently visible in [Erdman's complete edition, MHH27 E45](https://blake.lib.asu.edu/html/marriage_of_heaven_and_hell.html); a 1906 edition also predates LP2.
- LP1 original p3–4 WISDOM, in the pinned solved GP record `resources/external/relikd-solved-gp29-native-v1.json`, reads `ᚠᚩᚱ•ᚪᛚᛚ•ᚦᚪᛏ•ᛚᛁᚢᛖᛋ•ᛁᛋ•ᚻᚩᛚᚣ⁜`: FOR ALL THAT LIUES IS HOLY. The single nonorthographic edit replaces EVERY THING with ALL. Punctuation/case and V→U are conventional; do not count them as missing letters or hidden opcodes.
- Using the manuscript chart and the normal contracted TH/NG spelling, EVERY THING sums to 366 (E V E R Y = 251; TH I NG = 115); ALL sums to 243. The unchanged remainder sums to 698. Therefore the earlier line has sum 1064 = 8 × 133, whereas the rewritten line has 941, whose decimal reversal 149 is also prime. The edit decreases the sum by 123. Both primality tests are exact trial division. This numerical comparison is new to this issue, not an independently predicted LP2 result.
- The preceding three WISDOM clauses in the same pinned solved rune record sum to 1009, 991 and 991 respectively; all are prime. The ensuing COMMAND YOUR OWN SELF instruction sums to 751. LP1's explicit primes-sacred wording makes deliberate prime-compatible composition a plausible rival to a cryptographic edit instruction. It does not uniquely establish authorial motivation: grammatical rewrites can also yield prime sums (e.g. EVERY BEING gives 1187), and the comparison was performed after identifying the known edit.

## Scope and decision

The literal removed/inserted words are EVERY THING / ALL. Neither is a uniquely typed cryptographic instruction; neither the edit nor its arithmetic says which LP2 passage, direction, operation or serialization to use. No independent LP2 target selects 123, 941 or 149. Consequently no offset, key, permutation or cipher sweep is justified.

The other genealogy in #2768, Crowley Liber AL II:3 vs LP1's circumference vocabulary, is a semantic echo rather than a sufficiently exact clause alignment; #250 and the LP1 ego/circumference report already document this distinction. Exclude it from the edit dataset. The known PARABLE antecedent is a control only (#2761, #2740); its same 19 words do not provide a new wording change. No further exact, independently sourced solved pair was identified in the accepted register.

Retire the proposed wording-difference-as-opcode route absent a new explicit source selector. Retain the numerical effect as a concrete example of how an authorial paraphrase can change GP prime-sum validity. Share with #2799 (prime-constrained instruction), but do not treat it as an unknown-page crib or authentication of any particular LP2 prime sum.
