Consequently this is not the new independent relation demanded by #3212, and should not be retried as a fresh key constraint.

## What would materially change identifiability

If independent keys have q values each in GF(29), a fixed affine key relation B=A M+b, with source-fixed invertible M and b, reduces 29^(2q) candidate pairs to 29^q. The relation is not established by merely choosing a transform after observing two unknown body scores. Indeed for any ordered pair of nonzero difference vectors u,v there exists an invertible linear map taking u to v (extend each to a basis); if offsets are unrestricted, *every* pair of candidate keys can be linked. Such a fitting rule removes no identifiable degrees of freedom. Even fixing M but leaving b as an unrestricted q-vector adds exactly the removed q variables back. The literal artwork and retrospective products do not establish M, b, or even the key dimension.

The authentic #3170 rubric differential has a genuinely independently known alignment and historical enciphering operations. Its success cannot establish the corresponding relation between two unknown LP2 chapter keys. The earlier all-36 chapter-start same-pad test is already negative within its native model.

**Decision:** bounded missing-premise fork. Do not score new unknown body pairs or run synthetic controls for a freely chosen key relation. A new assignment needs a literal independently typed key on each artifact (or a source instruction determining one from the other), plus fixed recipient/clock. No original plaintext recovered.
