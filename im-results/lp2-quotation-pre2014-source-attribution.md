# Seven original LP2 quotation spans: a finite, source-motivated attribution gate

**Original-quotation source gate, 12 November 2026. Director Issues [#417](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/417), [#628](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/628), and [#634](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/634). Result: no independently attributable long quotation, unknown plaintext, authentic key, or cipher-state equation.** The scope is uninterrupted original printed word lengths in seven marked runic quotations against three preselected literary editions, eight original signed-message witnesses, three additional historically Cicada-used literary families, and a complete English OCR/text witness of Hofstadter's *Gödel, Escher, Bach*. Each finite source test preserves independent selection and positive controls; these results cannot exclude a differently worded, edited or unpreserved original source. Other manuscripts, translations and altered word breaks remain viable.

## Why these sources, before trying a key

The seven original marked passages total **405 printed GP runes**, and there are **14 visible quotation marks**. The source marks appear in the originally distributed LP2 JPEGs. The present scan covers exactly three additional author/text families with reasons a 2014 human solver might have chosen them before looking at decrypted text:

1. **Zhuangzi, *Chuang Tzŭ: Mystic, Moralist, and Social Reformer*, Herbert A. Giles translation (1889).** LP2 physical p53 has an insect emblem; the already decrypted p57 uses `INSTAR` and shedding to describe emergence. In Giles's chapter II a mention of **cicada wings** directly neighbors the famous butterfly-dream and metamorphosis passage. The text and the illustrated subject together select the work without inspecting quote-runic lengths. The checked digitization is pinned to Git blob `b8683719e62b919e29821be2b90025dde5ac8406`, [complete pinned source excerpt](https://github.com/yochail/taiku/blob/f13b69879da1dcbe3087166517c64c5e803da9f6/sites/taiku/sources/zhuangzi/chuang_tzu.txt). The repository's own source declaration identifies the 1889 Giles translation and [Project Gutenberg ebook 59709](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/59709).
2. **Lewis Carroll, *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland* (1865), especially chapter V, “Advice from a Caterpillar.”** The source explicitly discusses a caterpillar becoming a chrysalis and a butterfly; the LP2 image and solved p57 insect metaphor make this a specific, finite thematic candidate. The checked full transcription, pinned Git blob `286921e38dfe9f754e35aec78270f1251086283b), is [this preserved edition](https://github.com/brandonprry/wicked_cool_shell_scripts_2e/blob/1f22d91a7b6b0ec986225817716586301f3ad680/alice.txt), corresponding to [Project Gutenberg ebook 11](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11).
3. **John Bunyan, *The Pilgrim's Progress* (1678).** The solved manuscript independently addresses the participant as a `PILGRIM`, and original LP2 p22 shows death/weapon imagery compatible with allegorical pilgrimage. A specific full public-domain digitization, transcribed from a 1942 edition, is pinned as Git blob `91c5f7817aa653c3e7066f3088b56ffb08de2da1`, [original file](https://github.com/benawad/Word-Counter/blob/30924d17500b574f40766e14b727a40ee7500078/bunyan-pilgrims-304.txt).

These are *source-selection hypotheses*, not an attribution of the engraved insect, original rune text or Cicada's authorship. The candidate set was chosen on recognizable source motifs and earlier authenticated `PILGRIM` text. Earlier blanket searches of Shakespeare, Emerson, Gibran, Cicada's 2013-selected *Book of the Law*, Blake, Latin/Old English texts and the King James Bible already have separate repository reports. We did not restart them or the previously negative quotation-period scan.

## Frozen comparison and what it excludes

Before scoring, translate each potential source word into its **GP-rune length** with longest-match multigraphs `ING, TH, NG, EO, OE, AE, IA, EA`, or leave every Latin letter uncontracted as an alternate control. `ING` as a single GP rune is attested in solved LP1 and solved p57. Source word order and punctuation stay fixed; search **every original source word start**, allowing no deleted/added words, no shuffled quotations, no fitted offsets and no key guesses. The comparison uses all text in the three pinned files, including headers, rather than selecting favorable chapters after inspecting scores. The compact [machine-readable exact source word-length fingerprints](quote-pre2014-source-length-fingerprints.json) preserve all 102,565 corpus word lengths separately in both conventions alongside Git blob IDs, so the entire test runs **offline**.

**Full exact matches per original quoted span, corrected GP/ING-aware encoding:**

For all **four long quotations (13,14,34,14 words)**, **all 3 × 2 source/orthographic settings** have zero complete uninterrupted matches. The strongest partial consecutive GP matches for these four quotes are respectively **[6,6,6,6]** source words in Giles, **[6,6,7,6]** in Carroll, and **[8,5,7,5]** in Bunyan. Even the no-contraction alternative provides no complete long attribution. A search of dozens of thousands of windows makes five-to-eight adjacent length equalities weak evidence without accompanying actual ciphertext decoding.

For the three **short** quotations, a complete 2-, 4- or 5-word shape matches repeatedly, sometimes in unrelated passages. Giles has one exact 5-word length-window, but its mere numerical shape does not identify the printed runes as that phrase; competing books and editions supply further alternatives. Do not promote a length-only short match to plaintext or derive a candidate key from its letters.

**Original-input regression:** The historical 2012 book-code source crib reproducer passed on [Windows / Node.js 22 run 43278788293](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43278788293) with the unmodified 13-/34-word spans, immutable five source witnesses and their fixed controls.

## Additional source-authentic 2012 book-code texts: p42–43 originals (Director #628)

The 2012 Cicada puzzle itself directed solvers to texts of **Lady Charlotte Guest's *Mabinogion*, specifically “The Lady of the Fountain”**, **William Blake's *The Marriage of Heaven and Hell*** and **William Gibson's *Agrippa (A Book of the Dead)***. These are genuinely independent, author-associated candidate books, beyond the three literature choices above and the pretested KJV/Emerson/Shakespeare/Gibran/Crowley families. This historical choice supports inspecting them before any LP2 language scoring, although it does **not** assert that Cicada selected them as p42 plaintext or keys. The exact early-puzzle book-code connection is preserved in the 2012 participant archives and [the pinned research-source registry](https://github.com/pnotisdev/cicada3301/blob/main/docs/SOURCES.md).

The original printed p42 quotations are held fixed: quote 4 is rune-global **[10211,10279)**, **13 words / 68 runes**, and the adjacent quote 5 continues across physical p42→43 at **[10279,10439)**, **34 words / 160 runes**. Their original exact rune hashes and word-length arrays are above. The experiment scans every source-word position, with no key or phrase-location fitting, in both longest-match GP29 normalization and raw Latin-letter length. Both orthographies preserve the original word count. Five distinct source witnesses were frozen by Git blob SHA: a surviving 2,425-word 2012 Mabinogion participant excerpt, a 10,400-word full English version of the same translated narrative, a 7,738-word Gutenberg Blake text, a 1,671-word Cicada-2012 *Agrippa* transcript and a separate 1,746-word archival *Agrippa* variant. The particular source paths and exact byte-identifying Git blob SHAs are stored alongside their **length-only** tapes in [the offline witness fingerprints](quote2012-source-length-fingerprints.json). The copyrighted Gibson poem itself is not committed.

The strongest consecutive partial match in any of the **20 locked source/orthography/original-quotation comparisons** is just **seven** words, below the predeclared **12-word** credible-crib threshold. The maximum actual original GP-rune coverage for **any** uninterrupted matching word-length run is **32 runes** (the Mabinogion p42 13-word quotation, seven matching words), below the independent **40-rune** credible-crib criterion. Textual transformation, punctuation edits, alternative translations or a cryptographic code key derived from these books remain untested without a source-selected instruction.

The full read-only [offline original-input CommonJS SFT](../sims/lp2_quote_2012_sources_original_crib_sft.cjs) joins original runes across real printed line/page continuations and verifies the *entire* 13/34 word tapes against canonical GP indices and exact rune SHA-256. This source-selected attempt does **not** recover unknown LP2 plaintext or license a dictionary-fit key. With the 2012 named books now screened, future source crib searches should require a new independently authored text hint and a source-specific word-boundary hypothesis.

## Preserved 2012–May-2014 Cicada signed-message crib control (12 November 2026)

A separate, historically tighter source hypothesis is now tested without revisiting the three literary editions above. Solved LP2 p57 recycles the contemporaneous 2013 761.MP3 emergence wording, establishing that Cicada could reuse earlier challenge text. Accordingly, an unknown LP2 quoted passage might reuse the **original author's own signed messages**. The repository preserves eight clear-signed message witnesses between 2012 and the May 2014 LP2 release: 2012 countdown and MIDI email (including its Blake chorus), 2013 Onion greeting, forest-clue sentence, sky/knock hint and ICMP continuation, the 2014 Stage11 message and May release message. These are fixed, prior-era sources rather than a tuned keyword collection.

The new [source-word-shape SFT](../sims/lp2_signed_messages_wordshape_crib_sft.cjs) strips the armored PGP signature, keeps *only* the original signed body and derives its complete original-order word-length sequence using source-attested GP spellings and the longest-match `ING` contraction. It compares **every complete consecutive source phrase** against the fixed full original **2,896-word, 12,956-rune LP2 transcript**, allowing only identical lengths of uninterrupted printed words; page and printed-line wraps do not break words. No runic candidate letters, English scorer, offset-selected book code or cipher key influence the matches.

The eight messages respectively have maximum exact consecutive length matches of **5,5,6,5,4,5,5,5 words**, in the fixed source order given above. Therefore **none supplies a contiguous seven-word or longer verbatim word-length crib anywhere in the unsolved corpus**. A source-native positive control plants the entire signed-message word-length tape at a fixed original-word position and recovers it for each of the eight sources. This excludes only unaltered GP-word-shape quotation, *not* extracted substrings shorter than seven words, restated/edited passages, transformed word boundaries, or later/unpreserved sources. Short matching phrases occur multiple times and cannot independently authenticate plaintext. The already-tested 2013 forest sentence has its prior dedicated gate; this extension applies the same decision across the other seven available signed witnesses. No new original plaintext or key is claimed.

## Cicada's 2014 GEB book-code source: full English textual witness (Director #634)

Cicada's 2014 puzzle used Hofstadter's *Gödel, Escher, Bach* (GEB) as a **book-code source**, providing historical author-selected reasons to inspect this one book. That does not establish a direct GEB quotation within the later LP2 ciphertext. The previous finite literary and signed-message tests above did not include an independently frozen complete GEB English source.

A complete English text witness was recovered from [kylevedder/JChains, `inputs/GEB.txt`](https://github.com/kylevedder/JChains/blob/master/inputs/GEB.txt), **Git blob SHA-1 `03c403fa3db18dd9ee966e555e4978036b5f6b56` (1,953,906 bytes)**. Its contents and pagination outline resemble the original published English volume and include the text through the index; the OCR/transcription error rate and whether this was the *specific 1979 edition used by 2014 players* remain unverified. The text itself is copyrighted and is **not copied into this repository**. An independent, later 2011 MOBI text witness in `sinxisterrr/Sage-Library` at frozen commit `ff9ec97f4f662d3d0dc8553f59a7b25da74aa95d` contains 65 machine-generated 5,000-word chunks, not original book pagination.

Before comparing, the untouched p42 original quoted segments were fixed as **13 words / 68 true GP runes**, original rune interval `[10211,10279)`, and **34 words / 160 true GP runes**, `[10279,10439)` crossing p42–43. Their full 13- and 34-word GP lengths and exact original rune SHA-256 values are frozen above, and the [full-book read-only SFT](../sims/lp2_geb_original_quote_gate_sft.cjs) verifies them against the canonical transcription. Each English witness was tokenized in unchanged order into maximal ASCII-letter words, removing internal apostrophes, under both bare Latin length and greedy `ING|TH|NG|EO|OE|AE|IA|EA` GP29 lengths. Every uninterrupted source start was compared, with no fitted key, source offset, discarded printed word, or altered LP2 punctuation.

The pinned GEB blob is downloaded only when the SFT is executed, checked against its Git blob identity, and parsed locally without printing source prose. Its detector also checks a source-native 34-word positive plant and an independently shuffled negative. Both SFT jobs passed on [Windows-independent Node 22 GitHub Actions run 43283415184](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43283415184) on 12 November 2026, with all four exact original-GEB rows reproduced from the pinned Git blob. This closes a *verbatim word-boundary GEB quote* hypothesis. It does **not** exclude GEB's separate original 2014 book-code mechanism, extraction of a key from GEB, reworded quotations, or a cipher that changes word boundaries. None of those alternatives has an authored LP2 handoff or an original reversible key/state equation. A new GEB-derived attack needs a source-visible selector before computational tuning.

**Full unsolved manuscript GEB original-word-length extension (14 November 2026, Agent50).** The earlier book-source check confined its exact comparisons to the two p42–43 printed quotations. As a distinct original-cipher test, the complete GEB witness was now matched against **every contiguous word interval across all 2,896 original unknown LP2 printed words** (12,956 runes), preserving physical-page carries and every native printed boundary. The previously pinned 1,953,906-byte book object remains unchanged (Git blob `03c403fa3db18dd9ee966e555e4978036b5f6b56`). Apostrophe-preserving ASCII-letter tokenization exactly reproduces the earlier **318,443 source-word** count; the source-attested GP spelling now explicitly supports single-rune ING and IO, QU→CW and source-normalized consonants. A linear-time rolling-k-gram index checks candidate shapes with *full exact symbol-array equality*, avoiding false hash hits. The strongest surviving original/source shape is only **11 consecutive words**, originating at LP2 printed word **843** (physical page **16**) and GEB word **110,657**; its original GP length vector is `6/3/3/3/3/7/2/2/3/4/4`. There is **no single 14-word-or-longer exact GP-word-shape run anywhere in the whole unknown manuscript**. A source-true **27-consecutive-word planted passage** at fixed original word1200 and book word12345 is detected exactly, proving that the source, native orthography, and matcher could find a genuine long verbatim passage. [Original-source standalone SFT](../sims/lp2_geb_verbatim_native_wordlength_sft.cjs) and [successful manual CI run 43574060726](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43574060726) fix the test and evidence. This broader result excludes **unaltered word-for-word GEB prose as a source for any original contiguous fourteen-word LP2 passage in this pinned edition under this native spelling**. It leaves ciphered book-code references, edited paraphrase and unknown word-boundary transforms untouched; eleven length equalities are expected to occur by chance in a search of hundreds of thousands of source words, and alone cannot be labeled plaintext.

**Director decision:** retire the three prior edition-locked literary quotations and the single independently pinned complete GEB-text witness as **verbatim original-word-boundary crib sources** under their exact tested spellings and editions. The historically used GEB *book code* remains a separate, unsupported LP2 key route. The 2-, 4- and 5-word shape matches are intrinsically non-discriminating, so there is no source-authenticated plaintext crib with which to derive a key/state equation, and a withheld ciphertext prediction is premature. A new quotation approach should begin with a genuinely independent published source clue or a demonstrable text-transformation rule. Investigating the p40/p53 identical five-word-length prefix as a possible *internal authored refrain* is separate from pre-existing literary attribution and is already addressed by [the source-twin equality-invariant gate](lp2-twin-quotation-fiveword-crib-gate.md).
