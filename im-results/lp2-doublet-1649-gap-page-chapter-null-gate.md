## What the 1,649-rune observation actually says

The 12,956 original unsolved runes contain **86 adjacent-equal pairs**, all internal to physical pages. Their first-rune event positions are zero-based global positions. Between the events at **2,739** and **4,388** are *two additional* repeat events: **3,139 and 3,851**. The three successive interevent distances are **400 + 712 + 537 = 1,649**. This is the *largest sum of three consecutive gaps*, not a 1,649-rune doublet-free void. The longest individual interevent gap is **712**.

**Direct original-image adjudication of the two internal sites:** On the unmodified 2400×3600 JPEGs, physical p12 is original scan `29.jpg` and shows two adjacent same-class GP rune-25 glyph components at pixel rectangles **(x1240,y2368,w34,h114)** and **(x1279,y2368,w34,h114)**. Physical p16 is scan `33.jpg` with two adjacent same-class GP rune-6 components at **(1690,1238,43,114)** and **(1739,1238,43,114)**. These are original visible text symbols. All-channel RGB threshold <100 gives pairwise bitmap disagreement fractions **0.01109** and **0.02713** respectively, consistent with two independently rasterized instances of the same font glyph. This independently corroborates the exact two internal GP-index events against the actual JPEGs, beyond trusting the existing numeric transcription.

The external [16 November independent T7 replication](https://github.com/njloof/cicada-lp2-sweeps/blob/main/sweeps/2026-11-t7-replication/REPORT.md) correctly recovered the three lengths and site tuple, but its phrase **“a 1,649-rune span with zero doublets”** conflates the *sum of three interevent gaps* with a single event-free gap. It then favors a local strict no-repeat session. The underlying [Dukotah original T7 result](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/master/liber-primus/analysis/round44/W2-B3-doublet-sites/RESULTS.md) reports the correct individual maxima (712/662/553/537) and a reproducible gap autocorrelation around +0.380; its registered T7 result is *not* invalidated by the correction. Specifically the valid original window has two internal slip sites, so an exact zero-slip emitter across 1,649 runes is **not observed**.

## What survives matched comparisons

**Prior-work priority and scope.** This is **not a new page-conditioning discovery**. The existing [12 November source-boundary gate](lp2-doublet-gap-source-boundary-conditional-gate.md) already found page-matched raw Pearson upper-tail p=0.00460, and independent [Agent44 original-source regression](lp2-doublet-gap-pageconditioned-inference.md) reproduced p=0.005375 and page-block permutation p=0.002450. The preexisting [exact page × word-shape study](lp2-doublet-gap-page-wordclass-conditional-null.md) reported p=0.01155 for raw Pearson. **Crucially, the [stronger printed-line-count null](lp2-doublet-gap-original-source-conditioned.md) fixed all 594 original line event totals and obtained p=0.23194**, while a separately reported exact **event-bearing-word identity** null obtained p=0.25914. Under those more saturated nulls the gap correlation does **not** establish a separate hidden-state signal. The only decision-changing increment here is to correct the external claim that a 1,649-rune event-free interval exists, and to calibrate its *actual* longest-three-gap and longest-one-gap statistics with page counts held fixed.

Observed Pearson correlation between consecutive 85 gap lengths: **+0.380019689241**. The *largest three-gap sum* is **1,649**. Each reported Monte Carlo probability is an add-one, **one-sided upper tail**, conditioned on the stated null and *not* a new discovery-wide p-value; the three-gap statistic was singled out after the external report. The hard-core geometric nulls require a minimum intersite distance of six, as the actual source does. All synthetic positions retain 86 events.

Source p50 contributes no symbols. The p13–15 three-zero-page streak *crosses* the real p14/p15 chapter transition; a chapter-only change of repeat rate cannot by itself identify the long-low-repeat interval. There is no source instruction selecting an anti-repeat reset at any one of these physical boundaries.

**Decision:** once exact page counts are fixed, the allegedly extraordinary *1,649 sum* is essentially **median** under a neutral within-page arrangement null, unlike the original one-sided flat-null p≈0.035.

**The longest genuinely event-free gap (712) is also typical or short under source-page controls:** its one-sided upper-tail probability is **0.89874**, with matched-null mean **838.34** runes (100,000 samples). The true largest uninterrupted no-doublet run therefore does not independently support the purported 1,649-rune strict-filter episode. The revised reproducer records this comparison alongside its five original nulls; [Windows Node.js 22 original-source rerun PASS](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/44258884504). The gap **autocorrelation** remains higher than the page-count-resampled null (one-sided p≈0.009), but **prior exact printed-line and event-bearing-word conditioning already make it unexceptional** (respective p≈0.232 and 0.259). The effect is source-unit-sensitive, so this run provides no new evidence for a changing cryptographic state beyond the published conditionings. Permuting complete page-local site patterns produces an even lower p for their original ordering, but this is a different conditional question and may reflect original typography or unrelated text generation as well as encipherment. Individual raw page-count autocorrelation is weak (site-count ac1≈+0.124; page-permutation p≈0.144; rune-normalized page-rate ac1≈+0.0435; p≈0.320); this is no proof of a specific page-key schedule.

A genuine next cryptanalytic step needs a **first-party visible selector** that predicts a reversible operation or secret-state recovery rule, against untouched ciphertext; the prior line-occupancy localization is a more faithful starting constraint than unconstrained gap intervals. Do not branch new ciphers from the false “zero slip for 1,649 runes” premise. Preserve the T7 phenomenon and test a clue-selected page-local clock if one is discovered. The source presently selects no such key, operator or manuscript plaintext.
