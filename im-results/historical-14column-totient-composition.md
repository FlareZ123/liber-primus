Both have real historical provenance, but the original LP2 unsolved pages never state that they should be composed.

The first experiment permutes only the **29 GP rune symbols**, keeping the conventional reading of original word separators; it explores two transposition directions and both operation orders with **physical-page resets**, then evaluates the legal F masks. It uses the **nine visible chapter** reset points and explicitly grants every initial prime index **0–4,999** independently for each chapter. Source punctuation remains outside the cipher and is preserved. Different framing and key-clock assumptions mean the two branches are complementary; neither result excludes all possible transpositions or other operations.
