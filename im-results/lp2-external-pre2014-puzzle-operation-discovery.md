# External pre-2014 puzzle-operation discovery gate (#2821)

**Agent12, 25 Nov 2026 — bounded negative, no original plaintext, key, or destination recovered.** This is a new external worked-example comparison, not another survey of Cicada's own operations.

## Independent historical evidence

Southampton's dated [25 Sep 2003 announcement](https://www.southampton.ac.uk/news/2003/09/new-cipher-challenge.page) establishes the eight-message Babylon Stone competition and its explicit Babylonian-mathematics tablet theme. Its [official archive](https://www.cipherchallenge.org/boss-case-archive/) preserves that identity. I inspected all eight solution descriptions in the [unofficial BritishNationalCipherChallenge archive, 2003](https://github.com/themaddoctor/BritishNationalCipherChallenge/tree/master/2003) (the solution descriptions are retrospective solver reconstructions, not independently dated 2003 official solution sheets). Stages 1–4 are Caesar/affine/keyword substitutions, stage 5 block transposition, stage 6 Egyptian-symbol substitution followed by reversal of six-letter blocks, stages 7–8 ADFGVX variants with block transposition. The specifically interesting example is [stage 6](https://github.com/themaddoctor/BritishNationalCipherChallenge/blob/master/2003/6/solution.txt): a recognizable ancient-script register and fixed six-block reversal. [Stage 7](https://github.com/themaddoctor/BritishNationalCipherChallenge/blob/master/2003/7/solution.txt) and [stage 8](https://github.com/themaddoctor/BritishNationalCipherChallenge/blob/master/2003/8/solution.txt) use a six-symbol coordinate alphabet and separately keyed block rearrangement.

I also found MIT Mystery Hunt 2004's [A Scholarly Lesson](https://devjoe.appspot.com/huntindex/puzzle/mit20045415timbuktu04), indexed as Babylonian/cuneiform numerals, hieroglyphics and letter shifting; the [contemporaneous hunt round index](https://web.mit.edu/activities/puzzle/2004/timbuktu/) confirms the puzzle. Its actual puzzle and solution were not successfully retrieved, so the index is only a lead, not evidence of an exact worked operation. The 2002 [Spiral](https://www.devjoe.appspot.com/huntindex/puzzle/mit2002h8greend) is indexed as a spiral crossword, with dual inward/outward clue lists, unlike LP2's single numeric p15 grid without a corresponding clue pair.

## Strongest candidate and rejection

On original LP2 p33–39 (archive images 50–56), the paired decimal readings 1033/3301 reverse each other, and the repeated black emblem could suggest a historical six-block reversal. The full seven-page tape has 1680 runes (multiple of six), but the independent illustrated subsection has 1589 (not a multiple of six). Most importantly the emblem is **four numeral groups encoding (17,13) and (55,1) base60**, not an instruction to count six letters. The six identical reused headers are instances of one graphic, not a six-symbol output alphabet. See the [original pixel/sign audit](lp2-p33-p39-cuneiform-glyph-source-audit.md), [historical seven-column gate](historical-period7-p33-p39-transposition-gate.md), #815 and #2024.

A six-block reversal with an unknown global monoalphabetic mapping is exactly a member of the existing [all-secret substitution + transposition obstruction](pure-transposition-ioc-original-gate.md). The illustrated subsection has IoC 0.03436002155 (unchanged by reversal or bijective monoalphabetic substitution); the already-tested native-GP donor frequency comparisons do not support treating pure permutation as a native-language solution. Reversing the blocks before or after an additional polyalphabetic operation is a different model, but neither the historical puzzle nor LP2 supplies its key, register, or reset. The closest source-cue rival is the established Babylonian positional notation itself plus decorative reuse, with no reversal opcode implied. Thus the historical example provides no new independently selected inverse.

The ADFGVX variants have six visible ciphertext classes and a 6x6 keyed square; LP2's actual rune alphabet has 29 classes, not six. Inferring a different coordinate alphabet, digit mapping, block key, grouping and padding requires additional unselected operations. The repository already contains [rectangular coordinate-mixing all-secret gates](coordinate-fractionation-pair-capacity.md) and historical block/columnar transposition work, although these are not universal exclusions of ADFGVX. The Babylon Stone theme is not a selector for importing a stage key such as STONE.

## Decision

No distinct, fully specified source-selected operation survives this bounded discovery route. No original parameter sweep was run; the candidate's exact equivalence is established mathematically. Do not generate a six-block reversal+unknown-keystream successor merely from reversed numerals or a common ancient-script theme. Further historical discovery needs an external *worked operation* and an LP2 cue selecting its exact receiver, not just prior existence of a cipher. No follow-up requested.
