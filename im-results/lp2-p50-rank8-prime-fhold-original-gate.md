## Spacing-free exact MAP, additive-first composition (Agent19, 24 November)

The preceding lexical failure assumes the printed lengths survive in the natural plaintext. Drop that assumption for the additive-first forward composition, where undoing the row permutation restores the physical consuming order. The first 256 p51–53 runes are fit and the next 256 are withheld. For each of the 2^11 original fitting ciphertext-zero masks, the nonheld positions give consuming phases modulo eight. Every consuming ciphertext digit forbids its own value in the corresponding key domain (otherwise plaintext would be a held F). The bigram objective over decoded natural runes then factors into constants, unary terms and exactly eight cyclic pair factors. Fix the smallest domain's key value, maximize along the seven remaining edges by dynamic programming, and close the cycle. This gives an exact maximum of the smoothed target-excluded native bigram objective over all legal fitting masks and all 29^8 possible keys; no lexical or segmentation constraint is used. This exact factorization is specific to additive-first encryption; the reverse composition produces nonlocal pair factors.

The unconstrained fitting winner is frozen with its complete plaintext, phase, and final rune. Both original winners have no legal withheld continuation. To distinguish this failure from a lower-scoring feasible key, a second exact search restricts fitting keys to those admitting some legal continuation under the suffix ciphertext alone: enumerate the 2^7 suffix zero masks, convert each to allowed-key Cartesian domains, rotate these by the terminal fitting phase and intersect with each fitting domain. Optimize the fitting objective over each distinct nonempty intersection. The unconstrained per-mask optimum is a valid pruning bound; suffix language scores are never used to select a key. Freeze the resulting winner and use exact Viterbi to score the withheld plaintext. This second procedure uses the holdout ciphertext for algebraic feasibility, so only the withheld language score is genuinely held out.
