## Exact event-indexed visible-symbol equivalence of the two iid-key erasure channels (Agent 1, 28 November 2026)

**Mathematical decision:** The independent-deletion GP29 model and hidden-output mod36 model have **identical distributions of the first m successively printed symbols when the observer stops at the m-th print**, not merely approximately equal adjacent-repeat rates, when their encryption/retry key candidates are iid uniform and the GP29 deletion rate is set to exactly **7/36**. This is an exact **embedded-visible-chain** equivalence for the specified models, not equality of the complete finite-horizon observation laws when the source-symbol count is fixed/known; it does **not** claim that the actual author used either model or that historical/periodic keys are iid.

For an alphabet of `v` visible and `h` silent symbols, set `n=v+h`. Uniform independent key draws followed by strict rejection of the immediately preceding *emitted* symbol make each accepted emitted symbol uniform among the other `n−1` states, regardless of the current plaintext. In the hidden-alphabet model, starting from printed rune `a`, the next printed rune `b` is reached immediately, or after first entering any of the `h` invisible states. Once hidden, the first visible hit is uniform among all `v` visible symbols. Therefore

\[
T_{\mathrm{hidden}}(a,a)=\frac{h}{v(n-1)},\qquad
T_{\mathrm{hidden}}(a,b)=\frac{n}{v(n-1)}\quad(b\ne a).
\]

In the alternative `v`-state strict-nonrepeat sender, delete each *accepted* emission independently with probability `d` while still updating the previous-emission state. Let `P=(J-I)/(v-1)` be its uniform-off-diagonal transition matrix. The first-retained-symbol kernel is `(1-d)P(I-dP)^{-1}`, and exact summation of the geometric series gives

\[
T_{\mathrm{delete}}(a,a)=\frac{d}{v-1+d},\qquad
T_{\mathrm{delete}}(a,b)=\frac{1}{v-1+d}\quad(b\ne a).
\]

Substituting **`d=h/(v+h)`** makes the two kernels *entrywise identical*. Both start with a uniform first printed symbol. Thus for a visible rune sequence `w_1,\dots,w_m` observed by stopping immediately after the m-th printed symbol, containing `E` adjacent equalities, both models assign exactly

\[
\Pr(w_1,\dots,w_m)=\frac{1}{v}
\left(\frac{h}{v(n-1)}\right)^E
\left(\frac{n}{v(n-1)}\right)^{m-1-E}.
\]

This event-indexed result holds at every fixed m. No unigram, trigram, **visible-rune** gap, higher-order, or full m-rune value statistic can identify which of these two iid-uniform-key senders produced the printed values, **without access to their input-symbol clock, latent-emission count, or a separately supplied termination condition**. This does not assert that the models have identical joint distributions for a fixed known plaintext/source length. In the LP2 numbers `v=29,h=7,n=36`, the kernel has exact **same-rune probability 7/1015** and probability **36/1015 for each specified different rune**. The corresponding deletion probability is exactly `7/36=.194444…`. The original 12,956-rune tape has 86 adjacent equalities in 12,955 global transitions (no equality on a physical page join), versus an expected 89.3448 under the matched kernel (z≈−0.355). The previously calibrated deletion rate 0.187 gives 0.006634264 same-rune probability versus 0.006896552 for the hidden model; their log-likelihood difference on this sufficient statistic is only **−0.063825**. Both remain compatible with the *marginal repeat count*, without proving either sender.

**Finite source-length discriminator, formally outside the theorem.** For a known input of exactly two plaintext symbols, the mod36 hidden model prints both with probability `(29/36)(28/35)=0.6444444444`; independent GP29 deletion with `d=7/36` prints both with probability `(29/36)^2=0.6489197531`. Neither prints anything with probabilities `(7/36)(6/35)=0.0333333333` versus `(7/36)^2=0.0378086420`, respectively. Their visible/hidden event masks also differ: the mod36 mask has lag-one correlation **−1/35** from the no-repeat rule, while independent deletion has **zero**. For long known input length L, both have the same expected printed count 29L/36 but the hidden mod36 model has asymptotic printed-count variance **34/36** of the independent-deletion model's variance (stationary initialization). Consequently a verified plaintext length, exact latent clock, or retained/deleted-event sidecar *can* separate the two even under iid uniform keys. Original LP2 provides no authenticated hidden-event clock or original plaintext length, and its printed word divisions have not been established as underlying plaintext boundaries. No full-ciphertext identifiability claim is licensed when such auxiliary timing information is present.

**Important limitation / route change:** The homogeneous visible chain's repeat indicators are iid Bernoulli, whereas the original +0.3800 doublet-gap correlation is unusual under the flat-site and several original-source count-conditioned nulls. **Its interpretation is sensitive to conditioning**: the existing exact printed-line event-count null has upper-tail p=0.23194, so the gap statistic is *not* a standalone rejection of either homogeneous sender; any presumed line-scale process still needs an authenticated generator. This anomaly cannot be resolved by choosing between 29+deletion and 36+silence using event-indexed visible values alone. An actual nonuniform/periodic key, position-dependent deletion, punctuation-conditioned sender, or external side information breaks one or more theorem assumptions and could distinguish models; their known-key decoders remain valid distinct tools. The human solve path must supply a specific **key, hidden-state sidecar, or typed operator**, rather than tuning high-order output-only scores to select either channel.

**Reproducer:** [exact model and independently iterated absorbing-chain/retained-event controls](../sims/lp2_round48_channel_observational_equivalence_sft.cjs), standard Node.js 22, no external packages; independent matrix summations agree for `(v,h)=(2,1),(3,2),(5,3),(29,7)`; event-indexed four-visible-symbol word probabilities agree in small exhaustive domains and the first 10,000 fixed full-size cases. The reproducer also asserts the unequal **fixed-L=2** printed-length probabilities above, so the boundary of equivalence is tested rather than assumed. Model theorem is algebraic; numerical controls are implementation checks. Source counts come from the original canonical rune tape; their separate original source audit is [the doublet-boundary report](lp2-doublet-gap-source-boundary-conditional-gate.md). **No original plaintext, source-selected key, or continuation was recovered.**

**Exact source-free singleton inversion and constructive counterexample.** Donor mismatch is not a universal no-erasure theorem. For *any* underlying native GP word-length mixture whatsoever, write q=7/36, p=29/36, and let w_t be the fraction of **surviving nonempty groups** whose original length was t. The exact conditional visible-length probability-generating function for iid deletion is

    H(z) = Σ_(t≥1) w_t [ (q+p z)^t − q^t ] / [ 1−q^t ].

Differentiating at the algebraically distinguished point z=−q/p=−7/29 **annihilates every source word length t≥2** and isolates the original singleton proportion exactly:

    w_1 = H′(−7/29) = Σ_(k≥1) k · P(K=k) · (−7/29)^(k−1) ≥ 0.

The 2,896-group source histogram yields empirical **w1-hat = −0.011020765**, with plug-in independent-group standard error **0.00501317** (nominal −2.20 standard errors below the feasible bound 0). A negative *empirical* derivative is possible due finite sampling; it is a **weak conditional model-mismatch hint**, not mathematical proof of impossible source erasure or a 2.2-sigma multiple-testing-corrected discovery. The solved Cicada source itself contains 28/740=3.784% native one-rune words; conditional erasure would leave ≈3.1% single-rune *source* words among surviving groups, far from the negative empirical inverse estimate.

Crucially, fitting an **entirely arbitrary source native word-length profile** rather than any of the four actual historical/English controls yields a concrete fit. A constrained nonnegative mixture over source lengths 2–14 gives a source mean of **5.537 GP runes**, no one-rune source words, and multinomial deviance only **6.15667** for the printed 14-cell histogram under independent deletion; predicted counts for k=1..6 are **97.2, 418.6, 738.8, 508.8, 317.8, 255.8** versus observed **83, 448, 720, 513, 317, 256**. The 36-state hidden-output exact-DP mixture similarly reaches deviance **4.44511** with source surviving mean **5.546**. These are hypothetical source profiles optimized against the original; they are **counterexamples to a source-free logical exclusion**, *not recovered plaintext* and not grounds to infer the authors constructed 5.5-rune native words. Both fits and the exact derivative appear in the augmented [reproducible source-gate script](../sims/lp2_round48_word_preserving_erasure_length_gate_sft.cjs), executed successfully on the complete original source and all eight independent native-word sender controls in [Windows Node22 run 46107787768](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/46107787768) (job 140864331990, completed 28 November 2026 04:34 UTC). This narrows the next question: an actual original plaintext word-boundary source or separately clued word-length prior, rather than merely checking arbitrary ciphertext statistics.

**Decision:** Reject only the precise conjunction of (i) authentic printed plaintext word boundaries, (ii) a GP-native donor-like word-length distribution, (iii) no dummy inserted visible runes, and (iv) a fresh iid uniform 29-delete or 36-silent C11 sender with deletion/silence 7/36. If printed groups are decoy segmentation or the plaintext is a very-long-word register, this gate is inapplicable. No authentic new key or plaintext was recovered. Do not tune output-only language scorers to rescue this source-preserving model without a separate author-visible boundary rule.

### A literal Latin _Liber Primus_ source route: exact skipped-word obstruction (Agent 1, 28 November 2026)

**New human-clue rival, separately tested.** The authentic manuscript title is Latin. Roman authors' original Latin books often identify their first part as _Liber Primus_; Cicero's _De Officiis_ book I explicitly prints the heading `M. TVLLI CICERONIS DE OFFICIIS LIBER PRIMVS`. If the unknown Liber Primus p0–55 text were an extract of that pre-2014 Latin prose, an author could encrypt a recognizable source with the 29/36 silent/deletion mechanism while preserving original word boundaries. This is a candidate **source lookup hypothesis**, not a proven exact Cicada reference: `LIBER PRIMUS` is common, not unique to Cicero. Independently sampled Latin controls emphasize why an English-only source word-length argument is not generally conclusive. The original Latin _Aeneid_ words (Virgil, PG #227) average 5.632 GP-native symbols and yield mean 4.559 visible symbols after d=7/36; Cicero's **solved-page-calibrated** GP-native Latin mean 5.524 yields **4.490**, nearly identical to the original 4.474. However, averaging alone hides a major word-length histogram mismatch.

**Pinned Latin source and native representation.** From [cltk's public-domain Latin-library text](https://github.com/cltk/lat_text_latin_library/tree/master/cicero), freeze all three _De Officiis_ source books `off1.txt`, `off2.txt`, `off3.txt`, retaining Latin numbered paragraphs from `[1]` through the body end and stripping navigation/header/footer. Normalize Latin words with `QU→CW`, `V→U`, `K/Q→C`, `Z→S` and the authenticated LP1 + p57 English-to-GP29 aliases **`ING→rune21`** and **`IO→rune27`** before longest canonical rune-chart token matching. The resulting fixed source sequence contains **34,199 Latin words / 188,931 solved-chart-calibrated GP29 runes** (mean 5.52446), while the deliberately more permissive naive transliteration **without** the two aliases gives 190,231 runes (mean 5.56247). Treat the calibrated path as primary; the longer naive path is a conservative source-support sensitivity check., compared to the original unknown **2,896 printed groups / 12,956 GP runes**. Source blob SHA per book and exact frozen word lengths: [derived reference](../resources/external/cicero-de-officiis-gp-wordlengths-v1.json). This source is a plausible original Latin *control*, not original Cicada material.

**Exact necessary support test, no key to tune.** Any supposed letter-deleting or silent-output cipher that preserves source plaintext word boundaries with no inserted runes must obey `printed_word_length ≤ original_source_word_length`. It may erase entire source words, producing no printed group; the number, positions and identity of those fully erased words are allowed to vary **arbitrarily**. For every possible starting source word, the [single-file dynamic-programming alignment](../sims/lp2_cicero_liber_primus_wordlength_alignment_sft.cjs) greedily finds the earliest possible original Latin source word long enough for each next printed group, which minimizes total fully-erased source words for that starting offset; scan all **31,304** possible starts. It thus gives a **global exact support minimum** over all source start offsets and all erased-word positions, independent of key, plaintext actual rune values, and choice between the two Round48 channels.

**Original:** Even granting the same-text span to cross Cicero book boundaries, the most generous possible alignment begins at Latin word **9,850**, ends at **16,900** (both zero-based), consumes **7,051** original source words, preserves exactly 2,896 printed groups and necessarily erases **at least 4,155 entire source words**, **58.9278%** of all source words consumed. This is impossible to explain as a typical realization of iid 7/36 deletion or 36-state 7-silent output, even before comparing individual rune values. The original source's 14-cell printed word histogram independently has multinomial deviance ≈247.43 under the calibrated Cicero word-length mixture (observed one-rune words83 vs predicted198.1; three-rune720 vs predicted486.8). The longer/naive word register still requires at least3,730 source-word omissions (56.29%) under the same all-start search, despite near-equal means.

**Positive sender test (not synthetic inverse tautology):** Independently generate exactly 2,896 nonempty printed word lengths from the true contiguous Latin words beginning at index 2,000, with every native rune independently deleted at probability 7/36; this plant fully erases **31** Latin source words, consuming **2,927** Latin words through index4,926. **Conservative stochastic bound.** For each underlying Latin plaintext word of length≥1, in independent deletion the conditional probability of *no* visible rune is at most `7/36 < 0.2`. This bound is deliberately very loose (treats every Latin source word as if length1), hence is a strong rejection **only under the stated original word-boundary and iid uniform erasure model**.

The title _Liber Primus_ does not authenticate Cicero and supplies no exact *key/locator*. Preserve the runnable positive/negative test and derived control. ## Why this is a different route

The [Dukotah Round48 M1 independent pass (27 November)](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/master/liber-primus/analysis/round48/M1-INDEPENDENT-PASS.md) and its [complete experiment](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/master/liber-primus/analysis/round48/M1-leak-census/RESULTS.md) demonstrate that the original 86/12,955 adjacent equalities do **not** identify the fitted 0.813-repeat suppression rule. A strict-repeat filter, followed by either deletion of ≈18.7% of rune emissions, or a 36-symbol alphabet with seven nonprinting outputs, has virtually indistinguishable visible single-step statistics. The external [C11 public challenge](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/master/challenges/c11_alt_constructions.py) reports its older keyskip decoder recovering **0/40** known-key plants for each of the two alternatives. These are different ciphertext *channels*, with different inverse paths, even though their visible one-step statistics can coincide. T7's measured lag-one **doublet-gap** correlation +0.380 is still not explained by these mechanisms; this work does not claim otherwise.

The new decoders model the actual emission/suppression dynamics, not a shuffled candidate English paragraph. For each *visible* input rune they retain the cipher's most recent **emitted** symbol (even if that symbol was invisible), the consumed key-index state, and a rolling GP plaintext register. They enumerate 0–3 intervening invisible events, enumerate any intermediate rejected equal-cipher key draws, and emit only the rune characters genuinely printed in the ciphertext. A bounded Viterbi beam ranks those paths using a source-native **four-symbol** probabilistic model; the model's symbols are GP rune indices 0–28 plus an unprinted plaintext SPACE29 in between original English words. The key is supplied on controls and is never derived from the true plaintext in a test.

### 36-symbol *silent outputs*: exact state transducer

The new [silent-output inverse](../sims/lp2_alt36_silent_exact_latent_viterbi_sft.cjs) uses genuine 36-state ciphertext arithmetic `C=(P+K_j) mod36` with `P∈0..29` (29 native GP runes and plaintext SPACE29) and uniformly sampled `K_j∈0..35`. If the would-be output equals the previous emitted symbol, skip the key and retry with the same plaintext symbol. Outputs 29–35 remain in the cipher's state and consume the key, but print nothing; printed outputs are 0–28. Given a printed output c, its key-index j and previous emitted state, the inverse tests whether all skipped keys would have generated that previous same output. Between printed outputs it explicitly hypothesizes each hidden c∈29..35 and its plaintext p and key advancement, including key-rejection events. The control plants use the same fully specified original C11 channel family; exactness here refers to the *declared generative family*, not identified authorship.

**Fixed held-out test:** train on only the first 57% of each pinned Shakespeare, Gibran and Emerson donor word list (alphabet-normalization QU→CW, V→U, K/Q→C, Z→S, authentic ING/IO spellings). Plant 314-rune/spaced-symbol source windows from the untouched remaining 43%, strict-repeat encipher under a random uniform36 key and hide the symbols 29–35. Decoder beam400, max three hidden emits between prints, and one independently drawn wrong key. Every one of **12/12** fixed-seed controls recovers ≥85% of the retained printed plaintext under normalized longest common subsequence (LCS), with **97.62%–100%** exact recovery and mean **98.62%**. Wrong-key recoveries average **31.63%**; all true-key average per-observation language scores exceed paired wrong-key scores, minimum separation **2.682 nats/printed rune**. Exact true key-clock index recovered in **11/12**; the 12th differs by two consumed indices despite 98.58% rune recovery.

**Cross-author robustness, frozen decoder:** To remove even within-author training transfer, a second 12/12 run trains each model solely on the **other two** authors (Shakespeare withheld when testing Shakespeare, etc.), with the original plants, keys, model order, beam400 and max-hidden3 unchanged. Correct-key recoveries are **95.73%–100%**, mean **97.97%**, with **12/12** passing 85%; random wrong-key controls mean **31.58%**, and all true-key score margins exceed **2.638 nats/printed rune**. The result remains conditional on the correctly supplied key and C11-style in-sample style of GP29 English, but is not just memorization of a particular author's register. Reproduce by calling exported `experiment(raw,{beam:400,plants:12,crossAuthor:true})`.

**Independent external-author bridge:** In a third positive-control run, the receiver trained only on the original three donors and tested on **independent [Dukotah C11 development files](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/tree/master/liber-primus/data/keys/armada19) by John Stuart Mill (*On Liberty*) and Mary Shelley (*Frankenstein*)**, neither represented in model training. The same strict mod36 channel, beam400 and 0–3 invisible events, six fixed-seed ≈240–260-visible-rune plants yield **6/6** ≥85% recoveries; mean **99.19%**, minimum **97.55%**. Independent random wrong-key recoveries remain **31–33%**. This is substantially more out-of-register than the same-author and author-excluded controls, though not the challenge's sealed 40-plant official test. Invoke `experiment(raw,{beam:400,plants:6,external:true})` while passing additional raw strings `mill` and `shelley` fetched from those exact independent development files; the external texts are *test-only*, never incorporated into training.

### 29-symbol strict-repeat output followed by **deletion**
