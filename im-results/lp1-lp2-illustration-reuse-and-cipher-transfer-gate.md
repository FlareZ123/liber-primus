## Duplicated moth as a two-time-pad clue: exact original word-grammar and 25-phase decisions

**Source-selected comparison.** The image-aligned p53/p55 moths are virtually identical rasters after a (+66,+67) translation, but the pictures supply no common clock or explicit reused-key instruction. If the same position-wise GP29 additive key was reused, the unknown key cancels: (C53[i]−C55[i]) mod29 = (P53[i]−P55[i]) mod29. This is a *necessary* plaintext-pair condition independent of all 29^75 possible common keys. Freeze original physical p53 local offsets 0–74 against the actual complete-body p55 runes at page-local 1–75, omitting the p54 NG carry. Neither a freely shifted pad nor a fitted F skip is permitted.

**Broad native-frequency check, suggestive but inconclusive.** The earlier source-exact diagnostic compared p53 against both page-local p55 0–74 and corrected complete-body p55 1–75. Their GP29-unigram difference log-likelihoods were −3.85288467 and −1.25591690; ranks among 50,000 native-LP1 pair controls were 61 and 2,533, respectively. The corrected 0/1 difference histogram is [5,3,1,4,3,4,3,3,1,3,2,2,0,4,2,2,2,0,3,4,1,2,3,2,4,2,2,2,6]; it has five equal symbols, maximum bin 6 and Pearson χ²=20.506667. Under 12,000 *word-length-matched* native-control pairs per source, 1,139 Shakespeare, 1,034 Gibran and 1,024 Emerson controls have χ² no larger than the original. Thus those 75-symbol difference statistics alone do not rule out a wholly unknown nonperiodic shared pad. The [prior 50k original-input diagnostic SFT](../sims/lp2_p53_p55_moth_reused_pad_sft.cjs) remains valid under its narrower native-LP1 model.

**Exact native-word grammar: any secret common 75-symbol additive pad fails by rune 14.** Preserve the original printed boundaries. Physical p53 has 179 GP runes in 40 complete words. The corrected 75-rune p55 body contains 16 complete words of lengths [4,2,2,6,6,2,13,6,4,2,7,7,2,6,3,3]. Build the existing independently fixed **32,081-word distinct GP-native English dictionary** from Shakespeare, Gibran, Emerson and TextBlob/Norvig using historically documented letter-fold and multi-rune spelling rules. Require both hypothetical plaintext streams to comprise actual dictionary words at every original printed word boundary. An exact product-trie dynamic program tests **every** pair of such English passages under **every** common 75-symbol additive key without guessing any pad symbols. On the original ciphertext, four feasible paired-trie nodes survive at rune13; **no node survives at rune14**. The p53 word that extends beyond the 75-symbol comparison is allowed to continue. A 179-rune and 75-rune native-GP positive plant encrypted under a genuine common secret pad passes the same solver to position75; both authentic synthetic plaintext streams decrypt exactly. This is a complete all-key impossibility only for the fixed finite dictionary, unchanged printed word boundaries, native GP spelling and original alignment. Different languages, unseen proper names, word-boundary changes, nonadditive encryption and externally selected state remain outside its scope.

**Every 25-position additive pad is separately strongly disfavoured for ordinary English.** The LP1 p5 Wisdom square presents a source-visible 25-cell object, making a literal 25-period repeating pad with phase zero and no F skip a motivated, distinct shared-key hypothesis. Grant **all 29^25 possible 25-position keys**. Every pair of runes at the same modulo-25 position on p53 and the p55 body must preserve the equality of its corresponding plaintext symbols. The complete original pages give exactly **43 same-phase equal-rune pairs among 1,165 eligible pairs** (rate 0.0369098712). Native GP controls sampled with *the exact original two word-length sequences* and independent empirical word-token frequencies yield means **70.32017 Shakespeare**, **69.66042 Gibran**, and **71.54467 Emerson**. Among 12,000 fixed-seed controls from each source, the counts at or below 43 are **3**, **7** and **1** respectively; native minima are 40, 40 and 42. A planted native-GP pair encrypted with a secret periodic25 pad reproduces all original-native collision counts and decodes exactly. This is a source-native finite-corpus exclusion of a literal original-clock 25-period common additive model, regardless of the 25 unknown key values; it does not exclude nonstationary clocks, missing plaintext symbols or other languages.

**Decision.** The moth's pixel identity is authentic evidence for intentional illustration reuse but does not itself prove pad reuse. The simple 75-position additive reuse hypothesis fails exact dictionary word grammar on original rune14; the literal 25-position pad is extremely incompatible with three independent native-GP English controls. No original unknown plaintext or key has been recovered. Neither result justifies a shifted, unconstrained pad search.
