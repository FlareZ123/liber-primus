# Original LP2 line-endpoint instruction channel (Director #886)

14 November 2026, Agent44. **Conditional negative: no recovered unknown plaintext, key, or cipher.**

A previous exact source test already investigated the 594 **line-initial** runes of unknown LP2 pages under 40 periodic-key hypotheses. The missing narrow human clue test was the **literal line-final** rune, especially near obvious chapter starts and endings. This experiment uses only the real "/" physical line boundaries and "%" source page boundaries in the original transcript, including printed word continuations. The rune-free physical p50 is omitted and the later physical page IDs are offset accordingly.

The four frozen keyless variants are the first printed rune and last printed rune of each line, in forward original page/line order, or reversed **within each published page**. No line resegmentation, inserted runes, arbitrary alphabets, fitted shifts, or chosen starts are allowed. The selected original pages were p0–2 (12+12+9 = 33 runic lines) and p54–55 (11+4 = 15 lines). The authentic solved p56–57 pages supply ten printed lines of independent typography control, and all 55 unknown pages supply a full 594-line sensitivity check.

An independently trained Shakespeare source-native GP29 first-order transition table (first 38,000 source words only) scores each literal readout against its independent-unigram model. Each score is contrasted against 5,000 original-page-stratified exact-inventory permutations, using the identical fixed extraction. An exact contiguous word from the fixed Shakespeare source dictionary must have at least eight GP runes to count as the predeclared literal instruction. Disjoint heldout contiguous Shakespeare passages provide genuine English-positive controls; a length33 positive is +4.24 SD and a length15 positive +3.91 SD above matched shuffles.

The exact p0–2 forward line-start GP29 transliteration is "SOPSAEIAEBOENGEOTAIAMLUPIDMJYSOEDPDECDTHC". The p54–55 forward line-end read is "AWRUXRTYPHYBCMI". In GP transliteration "AE", "IA" etc denote single runes. All eight selected unknown readouts have zero literal eight-rune-or-longer Shakespeare-dictionary words; their null upper-tail p-values range from 0.061 to 0.875 before correcting for eight comparisons. The p0–2 head reverse z +1.57 is a subthreshold coincidence and has no key-selection authority. Whole-book first/last literal line endpoints are also shuffle-like (p≈0.47 and p≈0.22).

## Research decision

Retire this exact **literal printed-line first/last rune acrostic** for p0–2 and p54–55. The original is not identifiable as an English key or original decrypted instruction under these four human-obvious reading conventions. The known solved pages also yield scrambled acrostics, illustrating that normal typesetting itself need not encode an English line message. The fixed test and source details are in sims/lp2_source_endpoint_acrostic_gate_sft.cjs. This excludes no independently sourced reading order, non-English literal instruction, font/color differences, keyed code, or altered word boundaries. A genuinely author-selected endpoint reading grammar would be a separate, higher-priority test; no free permutations are justified by these scores.

The report consolidates the previously tested initial-acrostic family by linking its existing source-exact SFT, sims/line_initial_acrostic_inverse.cjs, without repeating that operation. The full-book authoritative status remains 56 original pages without a reproducible plaintext decipherment.
