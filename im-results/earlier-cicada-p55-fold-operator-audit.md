# Earlier Cicada operations versus the provisional p55 recursive fold

**Status: bounded historical-operation audit for Director Issue #155 (11 November 2026).** No original LP2 unknown plaintext, decryption key, or independently specified recursive fold. This evaluates only **documented earlier Cicada puzzle stages**, not all possible unpublished correspondence or later secondary recollections.

## Original clues and completed predecessor operations

The 2013 Cicada OS `761.MP3` metadata already contains the exact poem later printed as solved LP2 p57 PARABLE. Its words ask the reader to **shed circumferences and find divinity within**. Earlier first-release Liber Primus texts, documented in the 2014 sixth-onion delivery, also discuss the circumference, the voice inside one's head, and discovering truth inside oneself. The first-release passages are explicit philosophical metaphors, without a printed cipher recipe for folding letters, summing their rune indices, choosing a midpoint, or recursing. The poem and those passages establish a genuine **historical semantic recurrence**, not a numerical operator.

Two earlier *computational* operations do exist, with independently recoverable original artifacts:

| Original artifact / dated stage | Attested operation | What it fixes | Transfer to p55 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2014 third/fourth onion page-delivery streams | Reverse an entire residual **byte stream** to expose further JPEG data; extract each JPEG by its EOI boundary | Reverses the order of complete source bytes, no arithmetic, no recursive half-pairing | Reverse order of 17 GP rune symbols preserves their length and membership; cannot generate nine, five, three, two and one symbol layers or specify a subtraction |
| 2014 fourth-onion first-release pages 06–09 | ROT by three indices in a **reversed Gematria Primus alphabet** (Atbash/ROT) | One rune maps to one rune; previously solved original LP1 page prose validates the alphabet operation | A positionwise permutation preserves **17** symbols and never selects a 17-to-9 pair-combine rule |
| 2013 telnet NUMBER command, reported in a later-preserved first-onion reconstruction | Reverse **decimal digits** of a number, then test or factor as specified | Transforms integer arguments, with a source-specific prime/nonprime branch | An integer reversal is not an instruction to pair 17 GP rune values, choose their sign or recurse |
| 2012–2014 source literary instructions and book codes | Explicit line, paragraph, sentence, word, letter addressing | Finite printed-text positions and named book source where a clue is supplied | Does not select an arithmetic fold or even identify the p55 D marks |

Contemporaneous original puzzle material also contains straightforward book-code, Caesar/Vigenère, image-steganography, XOR and MIDI-note-to-letter procedures. Those demonstrate the creator's habit of giving a recoverable operational cue; none instructs recursively folding a rune vector by **left-minus-right modulo 29**.

The exact original 2016 PGP-signed source `resources/historical/cicada2016-4gq25-outguess.asc` and mirrored signed 2012/2014/2017 documents contain no literal fold/split/subtract recipe.

## Audit coverage, p55 control and decision

The stage README source descriptions and embedded quoted original text were inspected for literal *fold*, *folding*, *circumference*, *inside*, *within*, *mirror*, *reflection*, *reverse*, and *boundary* language across **13 archived 2012 stages, 8 archived 2013 stages and 11 archived 2014 stages**. The preserved signed sources were separately checked. This is a **finite archive review** and cannot exclude lost original unpublished material. The 2014 first-release passages and 2013 PARABLE text are positive controls for locating authentic circumference/inside language; the JPEG streams and Atbash-ROT3 instruction are positive controls for finding real nontrivial operators.

The already-completed [p55 D-span exact SFT](../sims/p55_dspan_two_p_original_control_sft.cjs) recovers its genuine 17 runes from physical p55's 43 strict word-interior symbols, and tests the *newly hypothesized* recursive fold left-minus-right, right-minus-left and plus modulo29. Only left-minus-right produces first-level **TWO**, then singleton **P**. The historical transformations above are reversal or one-rune substitution; none reduces vector length or selects subtraction. The historical semantics may motivate stripping word exteriors, but no surviving precedent fixes **GP left-minus-right, mirrored pairing, keeping the center rune, repeated recursion**, or the later **TWO P→86→WE** interpretation.

**Director decision:** Retire the premise that earlier preserved Cicada rounds already supply the precise p55 arithmetic fold operator. Preserve p55's source-real D interval and conditional TWO/P readout in the existing #133 report; require a new independently visible instruction before accepting its sign/recursion/readout or initiating an original-LP2 decrypt. The source evidence warrants considering physical reversal or perimeter stripping as separate hypotheses when an original artifact selects a carrier; it does not warrant replacing the selected fold with yet another tunable arithmetic family.

**No fresh brute-force search or ciphertext-scoring operation was performed for this issue**, since the archive supports an operator classification rather than a key.
