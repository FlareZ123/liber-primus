## A serious competing first-party reading: the quotation predates LP2

The parable is **not an LP2-origin instruction**. Its words already appear in the **2013 Cicada-authored MP3** `761.mp3`, titled *The Instar Emergence*, as an original TXXX metadata tag: “Like the instar, tunneling to the surface … We must shed our own circumferences; Find the divinity within and emerge.” That 2013 artifact was used in a confirmed original puzzle route **as a bytewise XOR operand**: historical Twitter-derived `twitter.bin` XOR the first 64,167 bytes of `761.mp3` from offset zero produced the valid `gematria.jpg`, from which OutGuess retrieved a signed instruction. Exact source paths/blob IDs, reproducibility and the distinction between source-signed text and period solver reconstruction are in [2013 Instar XOR source contract](../resources/external/2013-instar-xor-source.md).

Consequently the same language's appearance on solved LP2 p57 could signal the **older historical transform**, the philosophical metamorphosis theme, or a newly intended literal **word-edge** extraction. The original quote itself does **not uniquely select** “drop the first and last rune of each LP2 word,” a 13-stage clock, a 52-rune key, or a page-55-specific recipient. The 52/34 construction below remains a precise, reproducible **conditional** output *if* the word-edge reading is independently justified. This historical control materially weakens any argument that the word-edge reading is already *creator-selected*. It provides no ready XOR receiver either: the 2013 operation is byte-typed, and LP2 supplies no specified rune→byte serializer or matching second operand.

## A literal operator and a genuine word-boundary conflict

The solved physical p57 PARABLE page explicitly instructs **“WE MUST SHED OUR OWN CIRCUMFERENCES. FIND THE DIVINITY WITHIN AND EMERGE.”** The earlier [literal entire-book circumference test](lp2-p57-literal-word-circumference.md) already established one natural, exact execution: remove the first and last **GP rune of every complete source-printed word**, retaining both streams if the extraction must be reversible. That is a direct, source-compatible **interpretation** of the sentence, conditional on its referring to manuscript word edges rather than its earlier meaning; it is not an authenticated LP2 cipher instruction. It says nothing about GP29 arithmetic, a key, a 43-pair clock, a stream-combining cipher, or whether the words are independent ciphertext blocks.

The original archive `71.jpg`, physical p54, finishes with ten runes:

`G · O · T · TH · X · E · EA · R · G · Y`.

Original `72.jpg`, p55, begins with **`NG` followed by a word delimiter**. Thus the original terminal word is actually **11 runes**; physical page and text-word boundaries differ. P55 then prints 16 complete further words of lengths `[4,2,2,6,6,2,13,6,4,2,7,7,2,6,3,3]`.

This has a decisive implication for *interpreting* the earlier authentic 43+43 count:

| Exact original segment classification | Strict word interior | First/last word exterior |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| P55 treated as seventeen **page-local fragments**, including incoming one-rune `NG` | **43** | **33** |
| All 86 runes as **seventeen complete words**, joining p54's trailing ten with p55's `NG` | **52** | **34** |

The source-authenticated original register `p54 last10 || p55 all76` contains exactly **86** unchanged GP29 runes. P55's page-local first/last extraction gives a 43-rune A stream; appending all ten p54 trailing runes to p55's 33 page-local exteriors gives an exactly balanced **43-rune B stream**. This is a legitimate, lossless *page-chosen grouping* reported earlier in [#2229's original work](lp2-p54-p55-carry10-balanced-hadamard.md).

However, the **literal complete-word reading** of p57 assigns `G` and `NG` as the cross-page word's two shell symbols, and assigns **`O,T,TH,X,E,EA,R,G,Y`**, the other nine p54 runes, to its true word **interior**. These nine characters are the entire discrepancy between **52/34** and the hypothesized equal **43/43**. Moving nine genuine word-interior runes into a shell is a *second operation*, for which the original solved sentence and original word breaks supply no instruction. Therefore the 43+43 balance cannot itself be claimed as the literal execution of p57's instruction on the full 86-rune source.

The source counts are exact, but their causal interpretation matters. A creator might still intentionally choose the page-local 43/43 arrangement as a distinct operation. It requires an **additional author-selected page-boundary override**, along with a pairing direction and a rule changing symbol values, before there is a usable original-cipher inverse.
