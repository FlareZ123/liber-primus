# P55 source GP25 and p50 ranked 5x5: finite digraph/fractionation receiver decision (17 November 2026)

**Decision:** **The entire conventional continuous-digraph Playfair family is key-independently excluded on the original p54–55 ciphertext.** Three adjacent same-rune pairs occur at chapter offsets19–20, 155–156 and 290–291 (with p54/p55 concatenated in source order). The first two start at odd offsets; the third starts at an even offset. Whichever of the two global digraph phases is chosen, one or more identical-symbol pairs occupy a cipher digraph. Conventional Playfair encryption of distinct-letter plaintext digraphs always outputs distinct ciphertext symbols, for every secret 5x5 square. Even resetting the clock after p54's nine-rune red opening leaves the first duplicate at a legal aligned pair, relative offsets10–11. This excludes a standard uninterrupted Playfair ciphertext on this source segment under any 25-symbol selection/key and avoids exhaustive 25! square enumeration. A **separate word-by-word standard Playfair reset is also impossible if ciphertext word breaks are preserved**: the final two complete, independently printed p55 words are DJU and BEI, each exactly three runes (local offsets70–72 and73–75), whereas independently padded Playfair ciphertext words must each have even length. These two key-independent exclusions leave only nonstandard conventions that change printed boundaries, allow incomplete digraph output, or change the underlying symbol order; they do not constrain Bifid or other square mechanisms.

**Separate finite key-derived screen:** The directly source-visible p55 25-symbol support does not, by itself, identify a 5x5 cipher alphabet. A single p50-derived square, converted to a 25-cell rank key without requiring the speculative base59 interpretation, produced no original p55 prose in the frozen Bifid, generalized pair-reversible Playfair-grid, two-square or four-square families below. The separate complete-word Bifid-reset test likewise recovers no text. No authentic p55 plaintext, source-selected inverse or unknown key is recovered.

## Separate two-square/four-square original test

**Upstream source selector and objection.** Conventional two-square and four-square digraphs are distinct from the Playfair family excluded above. They can legally output repeated adjacent symbols, so the key-independent Playfair argument does not eliminate them. Their only proposed key is the same 25-cell ranked p50 square. Both the choice of that square and the mapping of original p55's 25 observed runes into a Latin25 alphabet remain unselected by an authored instruction. This is a bounded, motivated test of otherwise open conventional digraph operators, without adding new keys or p55 alphabet mappings.

[Original-input Node22 SFT](../sims/lp2_p55_source5x5_two_four_square_sft.cjs), commit [3e6b1da](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/commit/3e6b1daaacbd4ec0c8133abbe051b65e7002d994), uses the complete **76-rune original p55** in printed order and the exact existing source cell span (zero-based rows21–25, cols1–5). It fixes two rank directions, eight physical rotations/reflections, and four explicit keyed/unkeyed grid placements. The **64** four-square trials use standard unkeyed plaintext diagonals, plus two ciphertext squares that can independently be the source key or standard Latin25. The **128** two-square trials use those same square placements and the two rectangle axes (row or column exchange). The baseline unkeyed arrangements intentionally duplicate under orientations, so 192 is a count of executions, **not independent cryptographic evidence**. Every original inverse is checked by full forward re-encryption, and every corresponding reversible operation passes an independently planted original-length Latin25 English control.

**Result:** **192/192** original-cipher reconstructions and **192/192** planted controls pass. The strongest two-square output has IoC **0.0473684211**, the strongest four-square has **0.0449122807**; neither is coherent language. The best two-square letter sequence starts 'TXRIILOMTLMDVCZZZGNPDREX', and the best four-square starts 'CVIHTNXLINFKCCQAMWZAWKEM'; both fail to continue as text. Source input, exact key reconstruction, decoder/encoder, explicit trial counts and generated outputs are reproduced by the SFT. This rejects only the direct original p55 Latin25 two-square/four-square family under the one source-ranked candidate square; alternate cipher formats and independently sourced keys remain open.
