# Issue #3041: Smithy Code clue transfer to LP2 p15

## Decision
The 2006 Smithy Code is a solved example of a typographically selected ciphertext and a separately indicated numerical key. LP2 has a superficially analogous combination of red rune spans and a Fibonacci-indexed prime square, but the simplest localized transfer fails. No new plaintext or source-selected key results.

## Historical reconstruction
- [Original 7 April 2006 judgment](https://www.5rb.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/Baigent-v-Random-House-ChD-7-Apr-2006.pdf): unusual italics in the opening paragraphs; paragraph 52 explicitly contrasts HBHG and DVC and refers to a key.
- [Dan Tench's contemporary 28 April account](https://www.theguardian.com/uk/2006/apr/28/books.danbrown): author directed attention to paragraph one, the extracted label SMITHYCODE identified the channel, and an additional Fibonacci hint preceded trial-and-error recovery.
- [Judge's 28 April explanation](https://abcnews.com/WNT/story?id=1902682&page=1): HBHG for typographic extraction, DVC page 255 for Fibonacci numbers 1,1,2,3,5,8,13,21, and cycling. These clues distinguish a selected tape from a key source. They did not uniquely determine every arithmetic detail before the solution.

The published 32-letter payload JAEIEXTOSTGPSACGREAMQWFKADPMQZVZ decodes exactly to JACKIEFISTERWHOAREYOUDREADNOUGHT (FISTER is the historical error) using zero-based alphabet addition with repeating effective shifts [0,0,-2,2,4,7,12,20]. This reconstructs the effective operation from the released solution and ciphertext, including the unusual third shift; it is not a claim that this vector was uniquely inferable in advance.

## Missing clue and next decision
The analogy does not fix a genuine LP2 key. Authentic p56 instead has encrypted red AN END and continuous prime-minus-one shifts into its black body (prefix and first black rune reproduced), while p57 has clear PARABLE. Red is therefore not an independently established extraction channel. See the existing consolidated red-rubric grammar/source-language reports. The historical Smithy label and explicit cross-reference have no authenticated equivalent connecting the p15 grid to a separate red-rune ciphertext or fixing its receiver. Retire this conditional direct cyclic ordinal transfer; do not extend it by tuning phase, signs, exceptions, arbitrary Fibonacci variants, or other color rules absent a new typed clue. Reopen only on independent evidence that specifies a red-only channel and its exact key clock/operation, or another definite LP2 key-selection relation. No broader Fibonacci or color-channel exclusion is claimed.
