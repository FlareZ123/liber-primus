\[
C_i=P_i+\alpha P_{i-1}+K_i+\beta\pmod{29}.
\]

When alpha is nonzero, even the initial plaintext predecessor \(P_{-1}\) is arbitrary; no red-title value is assumed to be known plaintext. The effective extra unknown is \(\gamma=\alpha P_{-1}\). The full secret is therefore 9 LFSR symbols + offset beta + predecessor gamma, in addition to the selected alpha.

For each fixed alpha, the inverse plaintext is **affine in all those unknowns**, because solving for the next plaintext gives \(P_i=C_i-\alpha P_{i-1}-K_i-\beta\).
