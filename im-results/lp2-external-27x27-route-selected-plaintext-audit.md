Public project: [2retooz270703/Liber-Primus-27x27-Map-3-Rune-Nodes-Totient-Decryption](https://github.com/2retooz270703/Liber-Primus-27x27-Map-3-Rune-Nodes-Totient-Decryption), especially its [method](https://github.com/2retooz270703/Liber-Primus-27x27-Map-3-Rune-Nodes-Totient-Decryption/blob/main/Start-reading-here/00-decryption-method.md), [opening worked block](https://github.com/2retooz270703/Liber-Primus-27x27-Map-3-Rune-Nodes-Totient-Decryption/blob/main/plaintext-i-found/01-as-i-go-the.md), [WEATHER worked block](https://github.com/2retooz270703/Liber-Primus-27x27-Map-3-Rune-Nodes-Totient-Decryption/blob/main/plaintext-i-found/02-weather.md) and [statistical/selection discussion](https://github.com/2retooz270703/Liber-Primus-27x27-Map-3-Rune-Nodes-Totient-Decryption/blob/main/Start-reading-here/strongest-plaintext-evidence.md).

The independently pinned physical LP2 pp0–2 have **[262,266,201] = 729 runes** in `resources/liber-primus/lp2-corpus.json`, so a 27×27 row-major matrix is a genuine dimension of original source data. The public method selects a three-rune node, optionally takes the center rune's Euler totient, chooses a cyclic key rotation using a Möbius signature, and subtracts its three-periodic key modulo29 from **selected** ciphertext cells. It permits both center-transformed and untransformed nodes, and changes node, orientation, start cell and block length between independently decoded blocks.

The [read-only, original-source CJS reproducer](../sims/lp2_external_27x27_firstblocks_source_gate.cjs) reconstructs the first two published blocks directly from the canonical original 729 rune values, derives their node keys and phases, validates all 12 positionwise inversions, and exactly re-encrypts them. It was actually executed on the committed CJS source with the unmodified GitHub corpus under a V8 Node built-in adapter; all source/rune/key assertions passed. This is **arithmetic verification of selected examples**, independent of any language interpretation or claim that key/route choices were authored.

A correct forward re-encryption follows algebraically for *every* selected key; it does not authenticate the source of the key.

## Why this does not yet solve p0–2

The candidate's own README states that **selecting an exact next location remains an open problem**. Its [selection discussion](https://github.com/2retooz270703/Liber-Primus-27x27-Map-3-Rune-Nodes-Totient-Decryption/blob/main/Start-reading-here/strongest-plaintext-evidence.md) discloses a saved search catalogue of **600,549 routes and 200,664 strings**, not exhaustive; several numerical selection identities were discovered **after** the target English passage was found, and the report expressly warns that these are *retrospective*, not blind predictions. Its later rules filter properties (key axis, ray deviation, parity, phase) without specifying an exact next key node, ciphertext coordinate, or block length. They are therefore insufficient to produce a unique full original-cipher inverse on withheld original text.

The published selected route contains **38 blocks totaling 115 plaintext runes**, compared with **729** original p0–2 matrix cells. Even supposing every original cell were used at most once, **at least 614 cells** remain without a claimed plaintext. The report itself records duplicate ciphertext use: its first 17 blocks use 61 ciphertext cells but only **54 distinct** coordinates. Therefore the number of undecoded distinct original cells is actually larger than that conservative bound. This is a substantive coverage deficit for a claim that the original 729-rune chapter has been decrypted.

The original first aligned key, when kept continuously after its selected first seven runes, yields the next 13 source indices **[12,15,10,3,1,19,2,26,26,11,14,16,22]**, not a continuing natural phrase. This is **not** a refutation of the author's changing-key model; it demonstrates exactly where an independently recoverable next key/coordinate rule becomes necessary. There is no frozen complete mechanism for all 729 positions, so one cannot run it on an authentic withheld solved chapter/p56 (85 runes) without inventing geometry and selectors not specified in the claim.

## Decision and successor threshold

**Do not promote the two hand-selected original-source English outputs to authenticated decryption.** Their arithmetic is exact and worth retaining as a candidate, but there is no independently author-chosen route selector, no complete page map, and no authentic unknown plaintext verified under a *frozen* procedure. In particular, the postselected prime-sum, coordinate, and English-phrase correspondences are not independent cryptographic checksums unless their selection rules were specified without seeing the proposed reading.

A genuine successor would have to **freeze the next-cell/node/key rule from original visual or signed historical evidence**, then obtain **one contiguous heldout original passage** with no dictionary-chosen coordinates, demonstrate the same algorithm on at least one authentic solved control, and reconstruct the exact ciphertext from that complete key schedule. Until then this candidate is an interesting source-shaped cryptogram interpretation, with no demonstrated decryptor for the remaining **12,956 unknown GP29 runes**. Avoid further prime-relationship searches around already selected words without a new blind prediction.
