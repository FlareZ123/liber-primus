# May 2014 solver forum: an original workpad locator, no p56 destination

**14 November 2026, Agent8.** Bounded period-primary-source investigation for [Director #876](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/876). **Result: the original May 2014 collaborative workpad address is independently fixed by dated participant posts. Its historical contents and any distinct p56 continuation destination have not been recovered.** This advances a narrow archival acquisition locator, not LP2 plaintext, a cryptographic key, a digest preimage or an author-signed instruction.

## Direct contemporaneous witness

[Restored Unfiction forum topic, *CICADA 3301 2014 IS BACK*](https://forums.unfiction.com/forums/viewtopic.php%40p%3D1038407.html) contains dated posts by participant **bambusar**. These are actual participant messages preserved in a modern restoration, rather than later narrative reconstructions.

- **3 May 2014, 05:54 (forum display, timezone unspecified):** the poster announced that a new onion had delivered 58 LP pages, linked the known May 2014 image gallery `http://imgur.com/a/8xnWx#0`, called the shared solutions page `http://titanpad.com/vFCy7T5p0O` and invited participants to Freenode `#cicadasolvers`. This is a dated first-person attribution of the *exact 12-character Titanpad document locator* and the chat channel that could have contained the first p56-solving exchange.
- **18 May 2014, 01:34 (same forum display):** the participant stated that 57 pages remained undeciphered. This is evidence about the forum participant's assessment, not proof that nobody elsewhere had progressed. **Neither visible forum post links a second onion page, a hash preimage or an instruction obtained after decrypting p56.**

The original 2014 distribution URL `ky2khlqdf7qdznac.onion` is the already-known *input delivery host*, not a proposed answer to the `AN END` hash. The live workpad URL was a **solver collaboration service**, also not a candidate p56 hash target. No conflation of these roles is allowed.

## Exact acquisition target and coverage

The historically pinned target is document ID `vFCy7T5p0O` under `titanpad.com`. It is independently repeated in the [Uncovering Cicada community wiki's *Liber Primus Ideas and Suggestions*](https://uncovering-cicada.fandom.com/wiki/Liber_Primus_Ideas_and_Suggestions) and [*What Happened Liber Primus (Post 2014)*](https://uncovering-cicada.fandom.com/wiki/What_Happened_Liber_Primus_%28Post_2014%29); those copies establish repeated modern availability of the reference, **not additional independently dated 2014 workpad bytes**.

The available public search and replay surface in this invocation did **not** return the historical pad text, a dated 2014 snapshot of its revisions, or an indexed document export. Direct attempts to open the original Titanpad path and a dated Wayback replay were **inaccessible from this interface**. This is a retrieval limitation; no conclusion of permanent destruction or of zero archive captures follows. Generic search hits containing the document ID pointed back to contemporary forum/wiki references and modern derivative repositories. No first-party p56 successor address was obtained.

The separately extant [May 2014 `#cicadasolvers` interview attribution](https://therealsamizdat.com/2019/08/30/cicada-files-ghost3301-interview/) reports solver `cluosh` decrypted p56 during that month, but the interview is a **2019 retrospective**; it neither publishes the original channel log nor supplies a target URL. [An undated 1,385-byte `irc_logs.txt` sample](https://github.com/Wulfic/Cicada3301-Liber_Primus/blob/main/reference/community/irc_logs.txt) contains only an anti-repeat conversation and no original timestamps, so it is **not** a qualifying May–August 2014 log. The earlier [#848](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/848) already assessed the 18–20 August 2014 Tor Stack Exchange hash question and the separate January-only IRC transcript; neither is being repeated as a new discovery.

## Research decision

**One original period-dated participant thread is now indexed, and it yields a specific contemporary collaborative-pad locator, but no known first p56 continuation URL.** Do not treat that gap as proof that the original deep-web page never existed. The smallest genuinely different follow-up is an independently dated 2014 archival capture or export of exactly `titanpad.com/vFCy7T5p0O`, or a source-authenticated `#cicadasolvers` log for May–August 2014. Such an artifact would be searched *only then* for the exact solved p56 digest, contemporary URL replies and surrounding time context. An independently source-linked destination would become a focused archived-response acquisition target under the original p56 hash contract; absent it, no speculative onion enumeration or arbitrary digest input search is justified.
