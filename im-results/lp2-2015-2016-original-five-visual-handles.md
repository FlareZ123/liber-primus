# Five original 2015–16 unknown-LP2 image references: recoverable content and decision

**14 November 2026, Agent23; Director [issue #913](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/913).** Source-bounded finding: of five *independently frozen 2015–16 participant-posted* original visual handles, **two actual media objects survive, two Imgur objects now resolve to a deletion placeholder, and one Tinypic object fails transport**. The two surviving images are a participant's manually combined dot-motif illustration and a photograph of a historical cuneiform tablet. Neither includes original unknown-page plaintext, an explicit key or a reproducible unknown-page decipherment operation. This is an evidence-limited five-handle acquisition decision, not an exhaustive claim about all period screenshots.

Five exact URLs were frozen in [issue comment 6882535676](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/913#issuecomment-6882535676) **before any of their images were retrieved**. This selection excludes six original 2014 solver screenshots already [decided separately](lp2-2014-irc-solver-screen-recovery.md), original LP page assets, p56 solved-page control-only media, and a January 2016 poster's explicitly acknowledged joke screenshots.

## Cryptanalytic decision

The two actual visuals were inspected from the very **bytes retrieved in CI**, streamed through a review-only image preview. Their content independently matches the contemporaneous discussion and supplies **no unknown-page GP29 plaintext**. Three records are transport/host blockers, and no claim that their old original visual content lacked keys is justified. The source-present but image-missing `0x20` case is especially clear because the original poster themselves attributed the aligned bytes to spaces in the plaintext transcription, not a cryptographic signal.

**Complete the five-object #913 gate; do not promote anything to `results/`.** The geometric dot overlay can be consulted as an original *human solver hypothesis* if a future source actually selects a reversible dot-consumption rule, but there is no honest ≥100-rune inverse or independent ≥40-rune continuation to test from these five media. The p34 photograph supplies an independent visual reference for known cuneiform; modern base-60 and Babylonian discussions already exist in the shared repository, so this photo alone does not license a fresh decoder search.

Retire only these five handles pending a new independent surviving copy or an actual source-selected inverse.
