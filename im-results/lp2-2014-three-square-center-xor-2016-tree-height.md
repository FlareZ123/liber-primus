## Research decision

The **three-source 5×5 normalization** and **809 XOR 331 XOR 91 = 569** identity add one potentially meaningful, easily replicable original-material link to the already known p32/2016 563×569 correspondence. They do not recover a p55 key or decipherment. The literal pad and Hill interpretations above do **not** warrant new rotations, offsets, sign searches or a more elaborate fitted cipher.

The strongest missing premise is still a first-party indication that the three square centers should be XORed and transferred to a later tree picture or that the p32 first column should key p55. A source-authenticated instruction specifying a different *reversible consumer* of the 2014 center triple would materially change the next research decision. Until then the exact number equality should remain in intermediate evidence rather than being presented as established Liber Primus plaintext.
