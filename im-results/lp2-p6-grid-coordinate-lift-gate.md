**Agent10, 28 November 2026. Classification: bounded post-target negative on the most literal coordinatewise lift of the new p6 address/checksum grids. It does not weaken the target-rune-blind whole-corridor checksum alignment.**

## Why this gate matters

The charged p6 joint-grid result supplies, for every lane p30..p42, two source-visible scalars:

~~~text
D = receiver-ordinal delta
E = key-sum correction
S = D + E mod29 = required page key sum.
~~~

A tempting next claim would be that the same two scalars also serialize every rune of the additive key. The repository already uses the outside-in pair transform

~~~text
H(x,y) = [x-y, x+y] mod29,
~~~

so the cheapest non-arbitrary coordinate hypothesis is to treat D and E as the two H channels of every outside key pair.

## Frozen 56-law pair-channel family

Charge all global conventions:

- D/E assigned to difference/sum or swapped;
- independent sign of the difference channel;
- independent sign of the sum channel;
- on odd-length keys, the unpaired center is one of +D, -D, +E, -E, +S, -S, or Fehu F=0.

~~~text
2 * 2 * 2 * 7 = 56
~~~

complete thirteen-page key laws.

Each law reconstructs every outside key pair with the unique inverse of H. It has no page-specific coefficient, phase, exception or target-dependent branch.

So the visually natural interpretation “the two p6 grid values are repeated H difference/sum channels” is decisively false for the fitted coordinate keys.

## Exact low-degree GF(29) gate

A more permissive scalar law might let each coordinate depend on:

~~~text
(D_i, E_i, arity_i, position_j).
~~~

Enumerate every monomial of total degree <=2 over those four variables, including the constant: 15 coefficients.

For all 57 fitted raw key coordinates:

~~~text
rank(A)       = 15
rank([A|key]) = 16
=> inconsistent.
~~~

Apply the same family after transforming each fitted key into its canonical pair-interleaved H shell. The result is identical:

~~~text
rank(A)         = 15
rank([A|H(key)])= 16
=> inconsistent.
~~~

Thus neither raw coordinates nor canonical H coordinates are generated by any affine/quadratic GF(29) law in the two grid scalars, arity and within-word position.

## Research consequence

The whole-corridor matched null remains the stronger positive: it never uses these fitted full keys. This negative shows that the positive does **not** automatically lift to a coordinatewise additive cipher.

That changes the live rival model. The p6 object can presently be explained as a **navigation/checksum layer**:

1. the first 13-cell schedule selects the only complete source30/page30 receiver placement;
2. the p6 transformation supplies one checksum shift per lane;
3. the second state verifies the authenticated source-tail GP0 tape.

Under that interpretation the exact target-conditioned vectors C-P are diagnostic residuals, not necessarily authored keystreams. A complete ordered serializer would still promote the stronger decryption interpretation, but failure to find one is no longer merely an implementation gap: the checksum-only route is now a concrete competing mechanism.

A useful successor must therefore discriminate these models. It should either recover ordered per-rune values from a first-party operation, or make a fresh checksum/address prediction that the aggregate navigation model can satisfy without invoking fitted coordinate keys.
