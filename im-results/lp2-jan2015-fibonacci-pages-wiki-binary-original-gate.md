## What the source does and does not select

The four numbers **13,21,34,55** were already conspicuous as consecutive Fibonacci terms, and the printed p15 matrix was accessible at the time. These are a plausible *human-noticed address suggestion*, but **no page-local rune index, extraction length, direction, alphabet, byte codec, Base64 string, filename, binary signature or key clock** survives in the quoted message, original fixed wiki article revision, or original three candidate comment collections. Reinterpreting `13,21,34,55` as four physical LP2 page numbers is the commenter's assertion, not a demonstrated cryptographic mapping. Its direct full-page GP29→BASE29 binary reading has already failed in [#910's source-original test](lp2-2015-2016-irc-unknown-plaintext-claim-gate.md) and was deliberately **not rerun** here.

No entire fixed original-cipher decoder can be extracted; hence the required original-passage 100-rune/40-withheld forward replay is **not executable** from these period objects. Fitting an arbitrary Base64 alphabet, cropping a rune substring or guessing a `.bin` payload would manufacture unsupported input.
