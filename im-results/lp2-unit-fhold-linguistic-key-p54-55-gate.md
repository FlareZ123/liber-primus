# Authentic periodic F-hold cipher with an independent cyclic key-language prior

**Decision:** the two actual LP1 ciphertext keys are recovered with no target plaintext crib, and five additional random/linguistic plants recover the entire native plaintext exactly. The same bounded attack on original LP2 p54–55 selects an incoherent 151-rune prefix and its frozen key/phase/context admits **no legal continuation** on the remaining 157 runes. No unknown LP2 plaintext or key. Do not treat the high-ranking key string or exact prefix replay as decryption.

## Constructive attack

Use the authentic LP1 model C=P+K[q] mod29 for nonzero P, then q←q+1 mod L. P=F emits C=0 and holds q; C=0 may alternatively be generated by an ordinary consuming plaintext. L=8 and 13 are the actual historical periods. This transfers the partial-slot completion and word-aware exact dynamic programming from #2544, not its conjectural prime-weighted state.

The independent key-language model is a cyclic GP29 trigram estimated from the repository's Shakespeare public-domain prose control (300,000 characters), word types weighted by log(1+frequency), row pseudocount .1. A key's score is the sum of all cyclic trigram log probabilities, with weight 1 added to the plaintext score. Remove every cyclic spelling of DIUINITY, FIRFUMFERENFE, CIRCUMFERENCE and the reserved linguistic plant keys MOUNTAIN, MISUNDERSTAND, INDEPENDENTLY, NOTEBOOK from the key-model training words and direct finite key dictionary. The plaintext character model and plaintext dictionary exclude the target native section, as in #2544; other sections may naturally contain words semantically related to historical keys. No crib or key is supplied to inference.

From the fitting prefix only, generate shared partial keys across five source-printed eight-word windows, 3,000-state beam, source-length and internal-constraint-count selection. Complete at most 12 final partial states with ≤2 missing slots exhaustively. For partial states with 3–5 missing slots, complete at most 12 by a 128-state key-trigram beam retaining eight completions each. Add all independent dictionary keys of exact period excluding the reserved keys (4,506 at L8; 384 at L13). Canonicalize rotations. A bounded beam streaming prune limits intermediate memory while preserving the top score threshold. Test all initial phases by phase-bitset feasibility, then exact word-aware Viterbi. Ranking uses only prefix plaintext score plus key-language prior. Freeze the selected key, actual starting phase, final phase and two-rune context for the disjoint suffix. The known true key is used only for comparison and oracle diagnostics after candidate search. The actual LP1 encrypted sections come directly from the canonical ciphertext file.

## Original p54–55

The final unknown chapter is 308 runes and starts at the actual source p54 word boundary; preserve the p54→p55 carry. The canonical corpus matches the extracted chapter exactly. Use period13 with 151 fitting runes and 157 untouched suffix runes. Five frozen windows start at printed group indices 11,0,15,7,22. They supply 6,750 distinct cyclic candidates; 384 independent dictionary candidates bring the union to 7,134. 1,360 keys have at least one legal prefix. No candidate selected from suffix data.
