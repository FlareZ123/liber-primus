## Decision

The genuine remaining task is to identify an **original-Cicada-authored selector** fixing the next source node's center transformation, coordinates, the next ciphertext start, path length and reading order **before** examining the next candidate's English. One such selector must apply repeatedly, preferably across a withheld section or an independently solved source. Without it, numeric correspondences and coherent suggested continuations are postselection.

The reproducible finite enumeration makes the exact missing decision explicit and prevents treating one English match as a breakthrough.
