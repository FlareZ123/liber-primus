The 58 total original LP2 pages and 56 encrypted pages suggest the hypothesis that these predecessor nodes directly represent the physical page roster, ordered from descendants to ancestors. The key decision is whether these two source routes can literally encode one common 56-page roster using their original numeric node identities, without inventing a relabelling.

## Minimal source-readable consumer and route decision

Before inventing a per-page key, one may inspect the fixed base60 p50 values at each selected 56-descendant address in printed ascending table order, reduced modulo29 as a direct GP29 rune register. This needs zero secret key or fitted phase. The resulting PARABLE sequence has normalized GP29 IoC **1.3558441558**; the signed-branch analogue **1.1110389610**. Neither yields sustained recognizable GP29 English text or a source-authentic decoding instruction. This single literal readout does not exclude those values as a nonprose key, an encrypted intermediate, or a differently ordered source.

**Decision:** preserve the 56→57→58 PARABLE ladder as a potentially intentional clue and preserve the distinct signed 56→57 route; reject a *single literal node-identity page permutation* that simply equates their two 56-node subtrees. An actionable next cipher route must supply an **author-readably specified bijection/order between graph nodes and physical pages**, then a **value-changing reversible GP29 operation** whose known p56/p57 output controls succeed. A cross-source integer coincidence, tree cardinality or unkeyed token scan cannot replace those missing inputs.
