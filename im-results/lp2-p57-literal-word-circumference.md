# Literal word-circumference extraction on LP2 (Director Issue #343)

**11 November 2026 · Agent 21 · bounded original-source result.** The solved p57 *PARABLE* says "WE MUST SHED OUR OWN CIRCUMFERENCES." Applied literally to each complete source-printed unknown word, remove its first and last rune. Retain the removed endpoints as a separate ordered stream, because the operation otherwise discards information. This operation produces **no independently readable unknown plaintext, authentic key, or destination**. It is not a candidate complete cipher inverse.

## Source and fixed contract

- Canonical original-derived text: resources/liber-primus/lp2-transcription.txt, Git blob SHA-1 43bfeb6bf8dabaf25b0446cc519f8d91d205d8a7.
- Whole words: exactly the characters hyphen, period, ampersand, dollar sign, and section mark end words. Slashes (line breaks) and percent signs (physical-page breaks) do not. This is the existing full-word contract, also used by sims/lp2_historical_exact_wordlength_crib_sft.cjs; do not introduce a boundary at the confirmed p31→32/p54→55 carries. Neither reading order nor GP29 alphabet is changed.
- Unknown physical pp0–55: original rune offsets [0,12956), 2,896 complete words, 12,956 runes, 29 GP symbols. The nine source bodies have rune starts [0,729,1874,3612,5506,6527,7960,8051,9640,12956] and word counts [158,255,385,435,224,326,23,355,735]. Solved pp56–57 are controls only.
- For word r[0]...r[L-1], the interior is r[1]...r[L-2] if L≥3, otherwise empty. The exterior is r[0] for L=1, otherwise r[0]r[L-1]. Preserve original start/end rune offsets and word lengths. A length-one word sheds its one rune exactly once; a length-two word sheds both.

## Frozen solved-page and shuffled-original controls

Exactly the same operation on solved **p57**, with its 20 complete words, yields a visually recognizable endpoint-stripped fragment (native GP-to-Latin transliteration): ARABLICNSTAUNNELURFACUSHEUWIRCUMFERENCEINIUINITITHINMERG. The missing exteriors and word lengths permit recovery of the solved original. In contrast, the opening unknown interior transliterates as HEOGMIAYENGJGJNEWBASOEEOINGAEGIBNBJNGYDIAFXGPTFPUNGYNGX..., with no apparent instruction.

For bounded accidental-overlap calibration, compare the two full unknown streams with the two *identically stripped* p57 streams. Their maximum exact contiguous GP-rune matches (tested up to length 14) are **4 interior** and **3 exterior** runes. In **512** deterministic shuffles of whole unknown words *within each of the nine bodies* (seed 0x34357021), the maximum-match histograms are:

- Interior: 492 trials at length 4, 20 at length 5.
- Exterior: 396 trials at length 3, 111 at length 4, 5 at length 5.

All **512/512** matched or exceeded the original on both metrics. This comparison checks spurious short overlaps with one solved control, not broad lexical plausibility. It does not establish whether later decryption could render one separated stream meaningful. The difference from the solved English control's symbol-frequency IoC also disfavors a *direct* printed-order GP-English readout.

## Decision and research consequence

**Retire only the literal, once-per-complete-word first/last-rune extraction as a direct-plaintext route.** It does not yield a legible instruction or independently recover a cipher key. A lossless external/interior decomposition is trivially reversible and cannot be presented as an original-cipher inverse. This bounded negative does not exclude a different interpretation of "circumferences," hidden instructions on p57, or a clue-selected operation applied to these streams. Avoid unrestricted GP29 shifts, word-phase key searches or p55's already-exhausted 43-interior/33-exterior hypothesis without independent source evidence.
