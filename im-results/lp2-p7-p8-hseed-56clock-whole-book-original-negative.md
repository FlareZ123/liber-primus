## The smallest genuine loophole to the root clock no-go

The exact source objects are p7 final five red GP0 word products **[11,26,8,15,2]** and p8 adjacent printed-order red heading whole-word GP0 products **[4,3]**. The source polynomial fixes irreducible residual **x²−8** over GF29, so take `K=GF29[α]/(α²−8)`, with α of order56. The p8 two real red whole-word products independently define the source-readable element **h=4+3α**, norm2, order168.

The previously proved root-clock no-go assumes an initial page state inside the multiplicative cyclic group generated by α. In this field every α² is the scalar8, so every other root-clock page is a rune relabeling. But **h lies outside that subgroup**, and its entire **56-state coset** `h<α>` has two nonzero coordinates:

- `h α^(2k) = 8^k(4+3α)`;
- `h α^(2k+1) = 8^k(24+4α)`.

Neither can be scalar nor a pure α term. Thus this very small source-typed mixed seed removes the **exact** objection to the bare root clock. It is a defensible next test because both h and α are derived from adjacent original red source objects, without a guessed secret field basis or random key.

However, the manuscript does **not** explicitly authorize a field-pair cipher or identify the receiver/clock; this is a frozen conditional model, not an authenticated historical mechanism.
