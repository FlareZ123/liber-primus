## Stronger count-only bound: arbitrary replacement lengths

Drop the entire printed length multiset. Keep only the count K, max true word length L, OOV budget B=floor(.05K), contiguous ordered rune tape. Every known word has length at most 3 plus its positive excess, and every OOV word length at most L. Compute the exact maximum G of summed positive excess l−3 across disjoint matching intervals of lengths 4..L using a backward weighted interval DP that even permits uncovered gaps and ignores number of chosen intervals. Thus every valid segmentation has

    N <= 3K + (L−3)B + G.

Across the full tape there are 1,181 matching length-four intervals, 66 length-five, seven length-six, none length7–24. Maximum G=1,024 (the relaxed interval optimum). For L=14 the upper bound is 11,296 runes, needing >=295 OOV groups. Even granting L=24, upper bound 12,736 <12,956, needing >=155 OOV vs budget144. All nine independent chapter scopes also fail at both caps; the 24-rune lower OOV bounds in chapter order are 10,14,21,22,13,17,20,38,4 versus budgets 7,12,19,21,11,16,18,33,3. These are exact necessary conditions for the stated finite lexicon, not universal claims about English or every imaginable word length. The larger 24-rune bound is deliberately generous, and its margin is modest. If true word count is different, this bound changes: solving the exact necessary inequality `3K + (L−3)floor(0.05K) + 1024 ≥ 12956` gives K≥3,362 at L=14 and K≥2,949 at L=24. The latter is only 53 above the printed count (about 1.83%). These are relaxed necessary minima, not feasible segmentation witnesses. Thus the conserved-count negative cannot be promoted to a general H-decoy exclusion; an independently justified bound on the true count or a stronger count-free receiver would be needed. The subsequent specific A1 test does not assume K. The exact complete-partition extension below strengthens this weak necessary threshold to 3,235 for ordinary and 3,219 for mixed ordinary/F-hold at cap24.

## Exact arbitrary-boundary plaintext-F extension and full-partition OOV minimum (Agent 2, 26 November)

The preceding interval bounds explicitly exclude F holds. The authentic LP1 plaintext-F bypass motivates closing that loophole before spending more resources on a G3 key generator. Grant independent arbitrary constant additive offsets to every true word, and even let each word independently choose ordinary Caesar or literal plaintext-F bypass. In the bypass case, plaintext F emits F unchanged, whereas every non-F rune is shifted normally; visible F can be either a hold or an encrypted non-F rune. No printed boundary, length order, key schedule, or chapter reset is supplied.

Rebuild the identical 35,938-form lexicon as an 86,786-node trie. At every tape offset and all 29 shifts, enumerate exactly the permitted inverse branches; union the ordinary and bypass interval sets. Independently compute the exact minimum OOV count in a complete contiguous partition of N runes into K words of length at most L. With D(k,i) the minimum OOV cost at prefix i using k words, the recurrence is

    D(k,i) = min(1 + min_{1<=l<=L} D(k-1,i-l),
                 min_{l: [i-l,i) is lexical} D(k-1,i-l)).

Only legal prefix indices participate; initialize D(0,0)=0 and all other states unreachable. A monotone deque computes the first minimum in amortized constant time; a bitset of exact lexical intervals computes the second. All OOV intervals are granted, which is realizable under the arbitrary independent offset relaxation (shift zero is always legal). This gives an exact minimum in that relaxed model, independent of language scoring. It is a necessary bound for any particular G3 shift generator. It also finds the first K meeting floor(.05K), rather than the earlier weighted-interval lower bound.

Validation: 160 direct word/shift enumeration comparisons on small random tapes; 584 independent exhaustive segmentation-DP comparisons, all pass. Nine held literary plants at 92% donor offset (ordinary, F-hold, and randomly mixed), with independent nonzero word shifts: all in-register true intervals retained, and every exact OOV minimum <= actual planted OOV. The hold plants contain 3/6/10 actual F bypasses. These are coverage controls, not unique key recovery. The ordinary scan independently reproduces the seven prior long matches.
