# The 2014 / 2016 / p50 / p55 43-unit bridge: source-selector gate

**Classification:** a source-grounded 43-unit selector candidate has now been found, but no reversible square-to-p55 or p50-to-p55 operation is established. A bounded rank-transposition gate now tests global and per-square numeric sorting, including source-row and spiral tie-breaks; all 16 fixed variants remain nonlinguistic. The three 2014 prior-puzzle squares (5×5, 7×7, 5×5) were explicitly required before the LP2 pages were returned. Their inner cells total 43, their perimeters 56, their rows 17, and all cells 99. This aligns with p55's 43 interior runes, 33 boundary runes, 17 words, and p57's 23-dot mark: 43=43, 33+23=56, 76+23=99, and 17=17. The p57 Parable's instruction to shed circumferences is a plausible literal selector for the square interiors. Direct mod-29, prime-count, spiral, prime-count-difference, and the fixed φ(square-core) pad interpretations fail. A distinct exact prime-hole mask now fills all 76 p55 source runes into the 99 historic square cells and reserves 23 prime-valued positions in the two 1033-sum squares; it supplies a lossless **candidate placement** but no authenticated key or cipher inverse. Its raw-value and totient additive consumers fail on original ciphertext, and a complete 37-pair key-independent original-cipher constraint excludes **arbitrary value-stationary additive GP29 key tables** from decoding this placement into any of 858,983 pinned native-GP control windows; the ninth/tenth-word gate adds stricter printed-word evidence. Earlier p32-compass and 2016/p50 direct-key gates remain negative.

### Ornament-derived 44/193/43 bracket selector

The p55 fold artwork now supplies a separate conditional selector for the same p50 interval. Its ordinary five-position ornaments read **13** and **31**; the left filled cells are exactly positions 2,3,5, while 13 and31 are reversed primes. Their difference **18** reproduces the fold-selected fragment18. The left value13 is GP index P, and fragment18's sole equal folded pair is P/P, whose GP prime is **43**.

The archived 2013 `NUMBER` service would answer either 13 or31 with **`+`**, because each prime reverses to the other prime. Reading that historical response as an addition cue gives **13+31=44**; prime(44)=**193**. Under base60, 193 appears only at p50-global cells **83 and127**, exactly **44** apart, leaving **43** enclosed cells. Exhaustively checking all prime-valued base60 bytes finds this as the only pair with separation equal to the byte's prime ordinal. Under base59, cells83/127 equal190, value193 moves to cells61/234, and there is no such self-indexed prime-distance pair.

This is stronger selector evidence for the physical interval and a conditional discriminator in favor of base60. The unresolved premise is the semantic use of the historical `+` response as an instruction to add the later ornament values; the original server used it as a classification marker. It still does not provide the reversible 43-unit cipher operation.

This is the first *fully specified*, **lossless** 76-rune→99-cell placement drawn from the 2014 square entries: fill the remaining cells consecutively with exactly the original p55 76 runes, and associate the 23 omitted locations with the **count** of p57 terminal dots. It requires no invented rune values for the dots, and 99 positions are preserved exactly. Its causal weakness is substantial: **no authenticated instruction selects primes of the two 1033 squares as hole locations while retaining prime center809 in the 3301 square**. The dots' nine source-visible rows (alternating 3 and 2 dots) are not a known one-to-one geometric layout of the two square masks. Under any single invertible affine transfer, the five source columns remain five parallel straight lines. But the prime-valued cells in the original **7×7 1033 square alone** (17 of the proposed 23 holes) require **at least seven parallel lines in every direction**. The minimum seven is determined exactly by enumerating every primitive normal vector induced by a pair of source points; an invertible affine positive control preserves it. Therefore **a literal scaled, rotated, sheared, or otherwise affine overlay of the entire original 23-dot stencil onto the 2014 square-prime holes is impossible**, regardless of the orientation or placement of the six additional holes in the second 5×5 square. This result is independent of language scoring or any guessed key. The restriction does **not** exclude a non-affine/projective mapping, separately positioned dot clusters, arbitrary hole permutations, or a distinct cipher operator, none of which is specified by a historical instruction. All three historical numerical squares are centrosymmetric, and the two latter prime-hole patterns have **11 diametric pairs and one central hole (value 331)**, just like the original 23-dot centrosymmetric graphic has 11 pairs and its middle dot. This identifies a shared symmetry class but leaves **11! source-unselected orbit correspondences**. A count and symmetry match alone do not establish the route.

The prime-hole proposal is **incompatible with simultaneously requiring all 43 p55 strict-word interiors to occupy all 43 square interior cells**: the 23 holes include **11 square-interior cells** (nine in the 7×7, two in the last 5×5) and **12 perimeter cells**. It does not exclude out-of-lexicon words, changed printed boundaries, stateful/key-skip operations or another placement. The corresponding signed words are **Hello** (initial **H**) and **Its** (initial **I**). A stationary map would require **f(M)=H=I**, impossible in GP29. Across the whole 43-position source alignment, the ciphertext has **21** distinct rune symbols; **30 of 31** same-cipher position pairs request different signed initials. Even allowing an arbitrary 29-to-29 function selected after viewing the proposed plaintext, the optimal stationary assignment must misread at least **21 of 43 positions**. The result is independent of additive keys and of p50 radix59/radix60.

The appended exact test in [the existing p32/p55 43-symbol SFT](../sims/p32_p55_43symbol_additive_bridge.cjs) derives the signed initials from the original clear-signed source, recomputes the p55 interior with physical-word boundaries, and asserts the witness, collision counts, and minimum contradictions. The only first-letter spelling normalization needed here is **V→U** for Verify; the explicit H/I collision does not depend on it.

**Decision:** retire only the one-initial-to-one-interior *stationary GP29 substitution* reading. The signed message does not prescribe those initials as p55 plaintext; it could be a navigation instruction or a key for a separate stateful method. This counterexample supplies no general exclusion of nonstationary, polyalphabetic, or multi-symbol ciphers.
