## External-source rank and decision

1. **Exact image identity, independent of textual interpretation:** LP1→LP2 moth, figure and insect families. These supply reproducible historical inter-page parentage and at most previously tested source-topic/key candidates. Positive controls are the original 2D registrations and wrong-orientation/other-art negatives above.
2. **Named pre-2014 sources with independently identifiable text/figure:** 1977 Thieme mayfly specimen5 and Evans Fig12 cicada forewing. The former caption-key model has a completed original-input negative. **Evans Fig12 was retrieved in full and its exact artwork-parent hypothesis is excluded** by native-image registration. The original vein labels do not select a face ordering; no new external caption suggests a reusable cipher.
3. **Symbol-family explanations without exact dated plate:** Orthodox crosses, rooted stalk, blurred p32 tree, generic cadaver iconography. No source caption selecting an LP2 text, edition, coordinate unit or reversible decoding rule.

**Research decision for #2116, updated 22 November:** No exact external LP2 illustration parent with an independently selected GP29 decoding instruction has been established. The previously outstanding Evans Fig12 pixel-comparison now excludes its exact plate: 2 original-image inliers on the correct p41 versus a prespecified ≥30, with successful native p27 plant. **Retire that exact parent and avoid generic additional motif sweeps.** A new image source needs an independently identifiable, dateable parent and an associated decoding instruction. For a true plaintext breakthrough, the prerequisite remains a creator-authored key or operation with a 40+ original-rune prediction.
