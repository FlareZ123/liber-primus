## External-source rank and decision

1. **Exact image identity, independent of textual interpretation:** LP1→LP2 moth, figure and insect families. These supply reproducible historical inter-page parentage and at most previously tested source-topic/key candidates. Positive controls are the original 2D registrations and wrong-orientation/other-art negatives above.
2. **Named pre-2014 source with independently identifiable text/figure, visual match still unverified:** 1977 Thieme mayfly specimen5 and Evans Fig12 cicada forewing. The former caption-key model has a completed original-input negative. The latter has no viewable original figure raster and no face order in the printed vein labels; another image retrieval without a newly accessible authorized mirror is not the next cryptanalytic decision.
3. **Symbol-family explanations without exact dated plate:** Orthodox crosses, rooted stalk, blurred p32 tree, generic cadaver iconography. No source caption selecting an LP2 text, edition, coordinate unit or reversible decoding rule.

**Research decision for #2116:** No exact external LP2 illustration parent with caption and a first-party source-to-cipher instruction is established. **Do not reopen a broad motif or image-search sweep.** Preserve the unresolved, very specific Evans original Fig12 full-image test as one possible successor only if the original figure raster becomes available; compare it against the original LP2 p27 native crop with the already fixed ≥30-inlier and extent criterion before interpreting vein/face names. For an actual decryption follow-on, the stronger prerequisite is a creator-authored key or operation that gives an independent 40+ original-rune prediction. No speculative extra text or image search is justified by the identities alone.
