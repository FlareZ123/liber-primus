# 2019 “Chart the Stars” material: extra-canonical provenance gate

**Decision (16 November 2026, Agent35):** Do not treat the 2019 “Chart the Stars” graphics as an original Liber Primus instruction, key, or selector. The artwork is reported by its own designer as work assigned within a Schoenberger-branded group beginning in October 2017. The separate “third key” attributed to Z 3301 remains an unverified report of email correspondence, not an authenticated Cicada instruction. No LP2 plaintext or key is recovered.

## Source record

1. **2019 article:** [Estéban Trujillo, “Cicada Files: Z 3301 et al”](https://therealsamizdat.com/2019/09/12/cicada-files-schoenberger-1/), especially “Remember the Puzzle?” The article reports that Z 3301 allegedly sent a “complete hint” on 13 September 2019, said “There’s a third key as well,” and later said “Chart the stars is actually the hint.” It separately says Arturo Tafoyovsky/Lestat shared “Chart the Stars” graphics and associated star-map/Aquila illustrations on 18 September 2019. The article does not reproduce the alleged Z email as a verifiable signed message. It also acknowledges the distinction between the original PGP-signed puzzle period and later extra-canonical activity.

2. **First-person attribution (2026):** [Arturo Tafoya, *Network of Interest*](https://noi.enzenada.cloud/), Rev. 5, in the author’s own timeline and “Why I’m the one telling you” sections. Tafoya says he was formally invited into Schoenberger’s Cicada-branded group on **19 October 2017**, was immediately tasked with visual assets including “Follow the White Rabbit” and **“Chart the Stars”**, and identifies himself as the artist/graphics lead. The page includes a PGP-signed statement of authorship for the dossier; the specific historical claim is presented as first-person testimony on the author’s own site, not as a separately signed 2014 Cicada message.

3. **Canonical authentication rule:** The existing 2016 signed-message record in [the 2016 signed-message analysis](lp2-2016-signed-message-fold-square.md) records the instruction **“Beware false paths. Verify OpenPGP 7A35090F.”** A 2019 email claim and artwork produced for a 2017 group do not meet that authentication standard.

## Assessment

- The “Chart the Stars” artwork belongs to the post-2016 Schoenberger-branded/extra-canonical context on the artist’s own account. It cannot by itself be evidence of a source-authored LP2 operation.
- The alleged Z 3301 “third key” is a distinct claim. It remains **unverified**: the cited 2019 article reports the emails but does not supply a Cicada-signed message or an independently authenticated key artifact.
- The article’s star-map, Aquila, mirrored-map and celestial-logo images were not recovered in this investigation. Their absence does not justify inventing a mapping from them to LP2 pages.
- The 2019 gHOST constellation statements are also retrospective testimony, not an original PGP-authenticated LP2 instruction. Do not conflate them with the artist’s later artwork.
- The 2016 “Beware false paths” warning and the absence of a source-authenticated LP2 consumer make this a poor research branch. Do not spend further effort retrieving the 2019 images unless an independent pre-2016 LP2 feature selects a specific operation and yields a blind prediction on original ciphertext.

## Reopening condition

Reconsider only if a pre-2016 artifact authenticated under the original Cicada key explicitly selects the star-map operation, or if an original LP2 feature independently fixes a unique mapping that predicts previously unused ciphertext. A recovered 2019 image alone would establish its contents, not its canonicity.

**Classification:** source/historical exclusion; no cryptanalytic plaintext, key, or destination.
