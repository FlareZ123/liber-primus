These 255 edge runes might carry an instruction even while the remaining 890 scroll runes are differently protected. The signed 2016 message's wording about **words** and the solved p57 exhortation to **shed circumferences** motivate the two edges; they do not explicitly choose this cipher. Read the entire next p8 rune stream under `P8[i]=C8[i]±f(summary[i]) (mod29)` without fitting phases, resets, alphabet assignments or key values. These are four further fixed source-derived inverses, distinct from the earlier unpolynomialized checksum comparisons.

Every original decoder is exactly invertible for every source rune. In each case an independently solved **255-rune native GP29 control** made from solved p57 plaintext is forward encrypted and recovered with **255/255 exact matching**, while all original ciphertext is reencrypted exactly. On the **untouched original 255-rune target**, the eight acrostic inverses have GP29 IoC **0.032762–0.035140**, and the four quartic variants **0.033287–0.035912**. The authentic GP control's IoC exceeds **0.06**. Nothing decodes into coherent native-GP language or reveals a key for p9. A chance-looking `LONGGATE` glyph sequence from one quartic variant crosses an original p8 printed word boundary and is surrounded by nonprose; it is **not a recovered message**.

**Decision:** These two exact, visually motivated 255-register consumers do not recover original GP prose. Do not reinterpret weak substrings as plaintext or extend arbitrary cipher layers on this branch.
