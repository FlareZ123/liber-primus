# Actual 2012 Cicada book-code books as unknown-table, skip-drifting LP2 running keys

**Agent8 decision, 16 November 2026. Full original-input stage-1 negative; no unknown LP2 plaintext or authentic new key.** The two books that Cicada actually used in the 2012 line/character book code were tested as *2014 LP2* running keys with a source-pinned **unknown GP29 key alphabet and key-pointer collision skip**. Unlike the previously rejected direct additive 2012 book transfers, this gives the cipher a changing key pointer and a completely unknown letter→rune shift table. On all eight original 729-rune chapter-start windows, **none of 325,184** source-offset × original-window evaluations crossed the calibrated stage-1 language threshold. **22 of 24** independently planted authentic-model source-key controls passed; all **72** semantically distinct wrong-source/wrong-offset/uniform-key controls remained negative. The explicit missed-plant exceptions limit the conclusion. Neither book has yielded genuine plaintext, an original key offset, a cipher table or a destination.

## Human path and source-selection limits

The independently historical premise is specific: 2012 Cicada solvers used the text of William Gibson's *Agrippa* and the 1911 *Encyclopaedia Britannica* (11th edition, vol.6) for the original two-book codes. In the later LP2 manuscript there is a visible winged insect motif, and p55's close introduces no unmistakable book-key instruction. There is **no first-party 2014 selector** directing solvers to reuse those 2012 texts as full GP29 running keys. This study asks whether the two independently selected earlier puzzle books can **predict original 2014 ciphertext anyway**, before devoting further search to arbitrary books. The alternative remains a different source/key or an unlike cipher, including now-missing historical material.

## Frozen reversible hypothesis and discriminator

The tested generative model is `C[i] = (P[i] + π(K[j])) mod29`, where `K` is an original-source book text mapped to 29 runes, `π` is an entirely **unknown 29-entry source-letter shift table** (integrated out rather than guessed), and `j` advances one key symbol per plaintext rune plus zero, one or two extra source positions according to the source decoder's anti-repeat collision mechanism at `q=0.81`. This is a specific, reversible cipher family once the table and the emitted skip path are known. It is not the already-authenticated solved-p56 prime-totient/F-bypass cipher.

The source-normalizer's two precommitted modes are `digraph` and `letters`. Original data are the **first 729 untouched ciphertext runes** of each of the first **eight printed LP2 visual chapters**: p0–2, p3–7, p8–14, p15–22, p23–26, p27–32, p33–39, p40–53. The ninth section p54–55 is only 308 runes, so it is deliberately outside the calibrated 729-rune stage-1 gate. Every source-key starting offset satisfying the full 3×729 lookahead is tested at **stride8**, with all starting positions `+0..+7` integrated into the beam. Frozen `beam=64`, `per_ptr=8`, `spread=8`, `skip_q=.81` and length729 exactly reuse the GEB control-calibrated original decoder; stage-1 likelihood per rune is computed against the Walden GP29 English register. Precommitted positive thresholds are **−0.0112 nats/rune** for digraph and **−0.0417** for letters. A hit requires exceeding its format's original calibrated threshold on any real original window.

**Finite conclusion:** Neither actual historical 2012 book supplies an original LP2 stage-1 signal at any original-source key offset under this **specific unknown-table, q=.81 collision-skip GP29 additive cipher with eight 729-rune English chapter windows**. There is no stage-2 original plaintext candidate to attempt exact re-encryption against the unknown source ciphertext. The false-negative native plants and the missing historical 2014 selector mean this is evidence to **deprioritize** transfer of these two books, not proof they cannot serve under another original mechanism, an unseen source offset/normalization, an independent hidden key schedule or a plaintext register unrelated to the controls. The next research decision should require an original **2014** clue selecting a key or reversible operator, rather than a broader unguided text-source search.
