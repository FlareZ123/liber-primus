## A new, independent geometric property: the right is a mirror of the left

The left and right decoded RGB bytes are different, but this does not mean that their underlying artwork differs. Read the original archive **59.jpg** (physical p42) without cropping beyond the fixed 500-pixel side strips. Convert each to grayscale and make an ink mask using a predetermined threshold **pixel intensity less than 180**. Compare the left mask to the right under only the source-visible bilateral reflection and three exact geometric controls, with no rotation/scaling/registration/threshold fitting.

There are 125,798 left and 126,591 right dark pixels in the two native 500×3600 strips. The horizontal pairing recovers **125,046 matching dark pixels**, over 99% of the smaller ink mask. Since these original strips remain byte-for-byte unchanged on all sixteen pages, the mirror property generalizes to every page in this block. The tiny difference is compatible with independently JPEG-compressed edge pixels and dots, without establishing the original drawing/vector pipeline. ## Historical five-bit Pentagon receiver (#3312, Agent37, 28 November 2026)

**Decision: reject the stationary five-bit XOR/permutation interpretation of the two actual dot masks.** This is a distinct historical convention, not another botanical source census or a new LP2 plaintext claim. Frode Weierud's [Sturgeon study](https://museum.cs.auckland.ac.nz/rutherfordjournal/article010106.html), [dated December 2005 by its author](https://www.cryptocellar.org/pubs/pubs.html), identifies the Bletchley Park T52c Pentagon as five vertices and ten connecting edges, controlling a five-element XOR subtractor and five-element permutor. Its equations (1)–(2) specify XOR followed by bit permutation and the inverse order on reception. T52c subtractors have even parity; the modified T52ca links subtractor parity to permutor parity. These are genuine period-available cryptographic rules. No Cicada text connects them to the floral drawing.

Actual archive 57.jpg (p40), 58.jpg (p41), 73.jpg (p56) and 74.jpg (p57) were visually inspected from the maintainer ZIP. The first two retain the same left hollow/filled and right filled five-position motifs; solved p56 has a separate five-dot group without a paired left/right floral frame, and p57 lacks the paired motif. The historical Pentagon is a connected graph with numbered vertices, not two unlabelled decorative clusters. A direct transfer would treat one printed mask as the stationary subtractor and the other as the permutor. The missing wiring means testing a superset is more decisive than inventing a mapping from the second mask to swaps: permit every one of the 120 permutations of five bit positions, with no fitted clocks.

The observed left pattern in top-clockwise order is 01101; right is 11111. Allow either cluster to be subtractor, both bit polarities, and all five cyclic start points and either direction. Deduplicate the resulting subtractor set: **0,5,9,10,11,13,18,20,21,22,26,31**. Use both conventional GP numeric encodings (offset o=0 or 1). Frozen decoding is P = permInverse(C+o) XOR mask, minus o. Forward encryption is perm((P+o) XOR mask), minus o. No modulo29 reduction of illegal outputs; that would destroy the tested inverse. This is not a complete simulation of the T52's changing wheel generators.

[Single-file reproducible CJS](../sims/lp2_floral_t52_pentagon_static_gp29_sft.cjs) uses canonical ciphertext blob 054f2a0f780b7d46901acbe3d9bf4f0ac1801165 and native control blob 1b064ef6d85819b7d96adaff31a82d1ec551598f. The same algorithm core executed in the connector JS runtime using actual fetched content (no standalone Node or CI execution claimed). Full original outputs of all legal candidates are emitted by the SFT. All 2,880 fixed configurations exactly round-trip both pages as five-bit tapes; legality is a separate test. Both original pages contain all 29 GP symbols, so any one fixed inverse must preserve the entire GP29 set. No nonzero mask is legal, even before considering the actual T52c parity restrictions. Minimum invalid counts over all nonzero masks/permutations: 3 on p40, 5 on p41. All 36 surviving configurations (35 distinct maps) have mask zero: 12 zero-based and 24 one-based. Two nonidentity zero-mask plants on the 184-rune WARNING source each recover 184/184 and remain entirely in the GP domain. These are positive inverse checks, not independent recovery trials.

The algebra explains the zero count. For zero-based indices, the three forbidden values are 29,30,31. Their differing coordinates are exactly the low two bits; the remaining three coordinates are all fixed to one. A bit permutation preserving the forbidden triple must preserve the set of two differing coordinates. A nonzero XOR preserving that triple can flip only those low two bits; odd-weight masks also flip parity and cannot preserve a set with unequal even/odd counts. The observed left hollow sites are nonadjacent on the five-position cycle, so no dihedral ordering yields the adjacent two-bit mask 3. For one-based indices, the forbidden triple 0,30,31 has all three pairwise Hamming distances distinct (4,5,1); an isometry preserving the triple must fix each of its points, including zero, hence its XOR mask must be zero.

The zero-mask survivors are stationary bijections; their IoC remains exactly p40 **0.0333521551** (231 runes) and held p41 **0.0331286361** (273 runes), below every matched within-section native window (1,449 windows of length231, minimum0.0542819499; 1,281 windows of length273, minimum0.0555106658). These are finite, overlapping reference windows, not an independent statistical sample of all English. A native bigram model fitted excluding p56/p57 selects the highest p40 mean log score among the valid configurations: offset1, mask0, bit-source permutation [0,3,2,1,4], −4.160145 nats/transition; frozen p41 −4.318634. Independently withheld p56/p57 native scores are −2.670663/−2.606622. This is only a diagnostic, not a new universal static-substitution exclusion. No credible plaintext or operand emerges.

**Specific missing role:** the historical mechanism requires the changing subtractor/permutor wheel sequences, a wiring assignment, and the starting wheel state. The fixed artwork provides no independent clock, wheel patterns, graph edges or mapping to the adjacent 29-symbol text. Its two fixed masks cannot serve as a nonzero stationary XOR operand in the directly corresponding GP register. A full historical moving-key simulation, alternate 32-symbol carrier, arbitrary non-dihedral bit numbering, or an outer code is not excluded. Do not invent such components to rescue the static interpretation. The ordinary decorative-frame rival remains viable; no independent source selects T52 or any other concrete cipher operation. This closes the narrow source-to-receiver decision in #3312 without a new arbitrary sweep.
