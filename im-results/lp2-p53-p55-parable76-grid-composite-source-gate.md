# Shared-moth terminal pages, PARABLE edge shedding, and the 76/180 table: original-source inverse

**Status, 17 November 2026, Agent 4:** One new proposed PARABLE edge-shed tape, two previously established source-size correspondences, and negative original-cipher results. **No unknown LP2 plaintext, encryption key, or author-selected transform recovered.** This is exploratory supporting evidence, not an established decryption or a reason to expand random parameter searches.

## Observable input and proposed human path

The 2014 LP2 original images place the same moth emblem on physical **p53** (70.jpg) and **p55** (72.jpg). The marked rune corpus and original-red-span annotation give p53 **179 GP runes**, with **128 black and 51 red**, and p55 **76 GP runes**. Each page independently ends with its own 13-dot punctuation mark. The sum of the printed rune lengths is **179 + 76 = 255**. Counting **one** of the two terminal 13-dot marks as an additional event gives **256**, matching the **32×8 = 256** printed two-character grid cells on physical p49–p51. Among the 55 rune-bearing unknown pages, p53+p55 are the sole unordered pair of complete pages summing to 255. The other pair in the full physical range p0–p55 is p8 (255 runes) plus rune-free p50.

**Prior-work correction:** The p53+p55 = 255 pair, its unique-pair census, the 256-byte grid comparison, and a stronger source-supplied completion were already documented in [the original tree/terminal-page study](tree-outline-blur-crosspage.md#source-defined-completion-of-the-missing-position-agent-20). The intervening p54 page visibly enlarges one red A, and using **that actual rune** supplies the better-motivated missing 256th position; extensive direct-lookup and byte-pad gates on this 256-rune frame already failed. Counting one of the two terminal punctuation marks is a *weaker alternative*, not a novel discovery of the 256 frame.

The source-visible p50 table has a **76-cell perimeter** and **180 interior cells**. These match p55's **76 runes**, and p53's **179 runes + one 13-dot mark**, respectively. Its orientation and the role of that mark are hypotheses. Importantly, p55 *also* ends in a 13-dot mark, so the count does **not** uniquely specify how to serialize the source elements. This is an equal-capacity hypothesis only.

The solved p57 **PARABLE** comprises **20 printed words**, beginning with a seven-rune title, followed by **19 body words** containing **88 GP runes**. The literal instruction “shed our own circumferences” could motivate dropping the **first** or **last** GP rune from each of the 19 body words, retaining the title. Either choice produces **7 + 88 − 19 = 76 GP runes**, matching both p55 and the table's outer 76 cells. Keeping the title and choosing one edge are extra decisions the original does not explicitly command. A previously examined different reading sheds *both* endpoints from all 20 words and yields 55 word-interior runes; this test is distinct from that Josephus/p50 route.

## Complete source-fixed reversible tests

[Single-file Windows-compatible Node.js 22/CommonJS source test](../sims/lp2_p53_p55_parable76_grid_gate.cjs) reads the original full rune corpus, LP2 transcription, ink marks, and the 256-cell base60 grid. Its table bytes have SHA-256 **3b9b07d9a26e6d55c432d94d2661fdff3c2b348daed06821f2bdb23184a4b290**. No plaintext crib, fitted offset, secret substitution, or key-search parameter enters these tests.

1. **76-element GP29 receiver:** Make the key \(K_i = \pm S_i \pm (B_i\bmod29)\), where \(S\) is one of the two source-exact 76-rune PARABLE edge-shed tapes, and \(B\) is the complete visible table rim in clockwise or reversed direction. All **16** sign/edge/direction cases are tested against the **unchanged p55 76-rune ciphertext**, with full-consumption GP29 addition and with the solved-p56 plaintext-F transparent clock. The no-skip cases recipher all 76 original symbols exactly, but the maximum original IoC is **0.04245614035**, versus **0.05964912281** for the independently clear p57 first 76 GP runes. **Only four complete F-transparent inverse paths across all sixteen models** satisfy the exact F rule; their IoCs are **0.03263–0.03579**, and their full outputs are not readable GP-native prose. One 76-rune source-native planted control recovers **76/76** under the same F-transparent inversion.
2. **Historical three-input XOR byte receiver:** For the same two shed tapes and two rim orientations, independently convert both rune sequences (p55 and PARABLE) to either original GP indices or original GP primes and XOR them with the rim bytes. All **eight** exact 76-byte outputs have **24–32 printable ASCII bytes**, with nontext, high-bit-dominated first 24-byte prefixes; no offset-zero human-readable instruction is recovered. The 2014 three-share XOR is historically authentic, but its use on these later artifacts is speculative.
3. **256-element moth-page receiver:** Construct 256-value tapes from the complete p53 and p55 rune streams plus **one** 13-dot mark, choosing either plausible terminal-mark placement. Test ordinal/prime rune values, direct byte XOR and subtraction against the 256 table bytes in printed row-major order. Separately map p55 to the visible 76-cell clockwise rim and p53+one mark to the 180-cell interior in row-major order, using the same two rune encodings and operators. All **12** 256-byte outputs have only **85–103 printable characters** and longest printable runs of **4–6 characters**, with nonlinguistic first 24-byte prefixes. They do not yield an immediately readable plaintext or recognizable payload signature.

The SFT asserts source lengths, red-span offsets, terminal punctuation, row geometry, byte hash, exact GP29 re-encryption, and native-source F-transparent planted recovery. It passed on **Windows GitHub Actions, Node.js 22**, [manual run 44003004632](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/44003004632). The script prints all model output digests and source-count checks.

## Decision and existing related work

This **does not authenticate an author-designed four-part key or any plaintext**. In particular, no historical source chooses which p57 word edge to shed, which p53/p55 13-dot mark to retain, a clockwise reading, or the tested key operation. The 76 perimeter/p55 relationship and its direct **608** single-pad alternatives were already tested in the [prior PARABLE/p50 analysis](lp2-parable55-totient-josephus-p50-original-route.md); the earlier p50 polynomial double-root quotient and **51 p53 red + 76 p55 = 127** direct stationary pair model were tested in the [source-number and polynomial analysis](grid-bigint-factor-page53.md). The distinct experiment here is **keeping the seven-rune PARABLE title and stripping exactly one edge rune from each of its 19 body words**, giving a 76-rune third register whose direct combined consumers fail. The p53+one-mark mapping is a weaker, expressly tested alternative to the pre-existing p54-illuminated-A frame; it adds no established mechanism.

Future work on the three-register route should seek a **first-party operand/operation selector** or a discriminating original-cipher prediction independent of the observed matching lengths. More additive-pad variants or arbitrary byte permutations from the same coincidence are not justified by these results.
