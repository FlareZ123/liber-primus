# Superseded: mistaken red8/marker8/black5 original-image interpretation

**RETRACTED, 16 November 2026.** The original claim in this report was wrong. The original p0 first line has red word1 SHEOGMIAF (8 runes), an ordinary small red word divider, then red word2 SYENGC (5 runes), then the large red 13-dot marker **after rune13**. The black body text begins THJGAE-FJOE, at original rune offset13.

The earlier red/black pixel census was correct. Its interpretation counted twelve smaller red heading runes as seven and misidentified the first seven black body runes as the five-rune title word. All former key-switch arguments based on a marker at offset8 are unsupported by source ink.
