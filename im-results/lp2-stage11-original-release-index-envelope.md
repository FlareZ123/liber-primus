## May 2014 cleartext whitespace primes: absent 19 is insufficient as an LP2 key

The independently preserved Cicada-signed **2 May 2014** Stage11 redirect (`resources/historical/cicada2014-stage11-message.asc`) has visual blank-run groups of sizes **2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 23, 29, 31, 37** around its text and onion address: notably the prime **19** is absent between 17 and 23. The *internal* five-space indentation preceding the onion address appears within signed content. **The prime lengths consisting of trailing spaces are outside OpenPGP cleartext signature authentication:** RFC 4880 section 7 explicitly removes trailing whitespace before signature generation, and RFC 9580 section 7.1 reiterates the same verification behavior. Thus a valid historical PGP signature authenticates the textual redirect but **cannot by itself authenticate that the space-prime pattern, or omission of 19, was creator-chosen**. Original byte-preserving participant evidence is relevant; the creator-authenticated W-key interpretation is unproved.

A literal test of the most constrained GP29 interpretation sets **missing prime19 → GP rune W (index7)** as a ciphertext W pass-through and applies the already solved p56 `P=C−φ(prime_i) mod29` stream, with the prime key starting at 2 and resetting at the nine original-image visual chapter starts. On untouched original pp0–55 the **nine** decoded chapter scores are **−7.40429 to −7.64656** mean log10 probability per four-letter window in the frozen English quadgram metric (raw originals **−7.33601 to −7.50181**); no grammatical original passage or stronger key selector appears. The independently authentic p56 phi cipher with its **position-specific** F at offset56 skipped scores **−4.46165**, while applying the global W-skip conjecture to p56 gives **−7.49151**. A further structural obstruction arises from the provisional global skip rule: on the 12,956 source runes, **406** non-W ciphertext sites decrypt to plaintext W while the **401** actual ciphertext-W sites are taken literally; a naive plaintext-W literal re-encryption cannot distinguish these event types. It mismatches the original at **11,964** rune positions when replayed with the false skip semantics. An actual reversible W-interrupter would need additional source-specified side information or a different 29-symbol output encoding.

This retires the **single fixed 2014-missing-19 → literal W + solved-p56 phi key** operation for all nine physical chapters, without excluding a historically explicit non-Phi use of 19. Its original-input, authentic-p56 and planted-180 control is in [the standalone CJS experiment](../sims/lp2_historical_missing_primes_terminal_sft.cjs), successfully run in [manual Actions](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43262221041).
