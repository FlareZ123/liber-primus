## Distinct premise and receiver

The five red words beside five dots on original p7 motivated an earlier fixed quartic polynomial convolution on the immediately following p8 black body of 243=3^5 runes. See [fixed-source gate](lp2-p7-p8-complete-cube-orbit-receiver.md) and [five-axis fixed operators](lp2-p7-p8-fiveaxis-ternary-difference-gate.md). This is a different search from the existing order-3 zero-state and order-2 arbitrary-state plaintext FIR gates: the support is five consecutive taps and the state closes *cyclically*, not at a chosen zero or free start. It also differs from five-tap ciphertext feedback. No first-party evidence establishes that the unknown coefficients or wrap are authored.

Let X_i=(P_i-b)/a, with a nonzero in GF29, and define C_i=X_i−r0 X_(i−4)−r1 X_(i−3)−r2 X_(i−2)−r3 X_(i−1), subscripts mod243. Every r in GF29^4 is allowed. A nonzero leading coefficient of a general quartic can be absorbed into a; a constant bias can be absorbed into b whenever the filter is invertible. If the leading coefficient is zero, a cyclic plaintext rotation and shorter filter represent it; the concentration maximum is invariant to rotation. Singular filters are deliberately excluded because this family requires a uniquely invertible 243-symbol operator. No F hold, transposition, intermediate alphabet, or per-word clock is granted. No printed word boundaries used.

Use the chronological four-history companion T. Compute x^243 modulo its characteristic polynomial and generate the four rows of T^243 by multiplication by x. For zero-start response y, exact cyclic initial state is s=(I−T^243)^−1 y_final. If I−T^243 is singular the filter is not bijective on the cyclic tape. Forward and inverse then use the same recurrence. All keys scored by unordered equal-rune pairs (affine-output invariant). Top32 per tape rescored over all 812 affine maps by smoothed bigram model trained on disjoint LP1 passages and solved p57.
