## Frozen exact inverse and result

For numbered span i, concatenate its GP29 indices in original order, preserving all runes and the p37→p38 carry of item 4. Interpret them as the single nonnegative integer N = Σ c_j 29^(L-1-j). Convert N to ordinary decimal, with no padding, offsets or alternate bases. Decode consecutive triples using the authentic table, permitting 611 between proof lines. A direct axiom representation must equal the corresponding exact axiom number. A direct proof representation must at least be made exclusively of legal complete triplets; a proof beginning at the corresponding axiom also requires the exact known decimal prefix. Failure of that necessary alphabet condition is sufficient without checking provability or optimizing a language model.
