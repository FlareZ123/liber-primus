# German 2014–15 independent LP2 forum: source-specific decipherment gate

**Decision, 15 November 2026.** The 9 May 2014 German-language thread focused specifically on the newly published 58-page Liber Primus contains a date-stamped positive explanation of the **already-solved LP2 p56** on 22 May 2014. The visible comments through September 2015 contain **no executable decipherment, page-specific original unknown-key, or >=70-rune claimed plaintext for LP2 p0–55**. There is no source-frozen unknown-page decoder to test; ciphertext reencryption and an unknown-page holdout are inapplicable. This is a finite source decision, not a claim that all German-language solvers failed.
