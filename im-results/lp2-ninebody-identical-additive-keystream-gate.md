# Nine titled LP2 units: fixed and arbitrary-relative-phase GP29 key reuse

**Decision (11 November 2026):** Two original-input gates find no support for a single reused additive GP29 keystream among the nine source-titled bodies under the solved-Cicada GP-English letter register. The original source-start alignment has 36 negative pairwise differences. An independently held-out extension scans all 62,083 overlap-valid relative alignments across 28 sufficiently long section pairs; none of the 28 selected original alignments has a positive native-English difference-model log-likelihood on its held-out suffix. A deliberately shifted common-pad positive recovers 28/28 true relative offsets and gives 28/28 positive held-out likelihoods. These are scoped tests of *one identical-symbol additive pad* with unknown per-body offset, rather than recovery of unknown LP2 plaintext. Changing keys, changing symbol alphabets, unknown binary registers, nonlinear mixing and stateful exclusion remain outside this result.

## Source and frozen experiment

The original p32/p33 two-title evidence establishes nine units with lengths **[729, 1145, 1738, 1894, 1021, 1433, 91, 1589, 3316]**, totaling 12,956 runes. Their global starts are **[0, 729, 1874, 3612, 5506, 6527, 7960, 8051, 9640]**. The corresponding printed word counts are **[158, 255, 385, 435, 224, 326, 23, 355, 735]**, verified against `resources/lp2-transcription.txt`. The 91-rune unit is the standalone p33 first-title interlude. Source basis: [typographic boundary evidence](../workplace/lp2-section-clock-ambiguity.md) and [canonical GP29 dataset](../resources/liber-primus/lp2-corpus.json).

For each 1-based unit pair (i,j), 1 ≤ i < j ≤ 9, subtract `C_j[t]` from `C_i[t]` modulo 29 at the *same local offset* `t=0..min(length_i,length_j)-1`. **No shift is chosen for decipherment.** The hypothesis `C_i[t]=P_i[t]+K[t] (mod 29)` with an exactly restarted common stream implies `C_i[t]-C_j[t]=P_i[t]-P_j[t]`. Each pair's difference-symbol histogram and exact-rune match count are recorded by the SFT.

The natural-language alternative is frozen to chapter translations **1, 3, 6, 9–15 and 57** of `resources/liber-primus/lp1-plaintext-reference.txt`. Words containing Latin Q/V/K/Z are *omitted* rather than given speculative spellings, and valid letter/digraph tokens are greedily converted using the canonical GP29 names. This yields **2,491 authentic solved-message-derived English GP symbols**. Under the independent-position approximation, its predicted symbol equality is **0.062302974**, and its full difference distribution is the 29-bin circular autocorrelation of that source distribution. This approximation is a language model, not a proven exact law for all LP2 text.

## Original-input results

- **36/36 pairwise GP difference log-likelihood ratios** `log P(English difference model) / P(uniform difference)` are negative; sum **−929.459** across the 36 dependent pair comparisons. The sum is descriptive, **not** an independent-sample p-value.
- **1,126 / 33,551 aligned positions match**, or **0.033560848**. Independently drawn English-GP control symbols would match approximately **0.062303**; this deficit is incompatible with treating the entire set as standard identical-pad English streams.
- Highest observed raw pair match rates: units **4–5: 50/1,021 = 0.048972**, **1–6: 34/729 = 0.046639**, **2–4: 53/1,145 = 0.046288**, **5–9: 46/1,021 = 0.045054**, and **6–8: 62/1,433 = 0.043266**. These were selected *after* inspecting all 36 and cannot be interpreted as isolated discoveries.
- Exact **full-rune circular-shift ranks**, rotating only the second original unit while retaining its complete rune order and length: best observed p = **0.00881489** (4–5; 9 or fewer shifts among 1,021 give at least 50 matches). Correcting the 36 pair search by Bonferroni gives **p ≥ 0.31734**.
- Exact **printed-word-boundary circular-shift ranks**, only rotating the second original unit by an original local word-start offset so no original word is split at the rotation seam: best observed p = **0.01339286** (4–5; 3 of 224 printed starts attain at least the observed match count). Bonferroni gives **p ≥ 0.48214**. Both nulls retain the full source-unit lengths, the second preserves its word order and all complete words. They do not reconstitute arbitrary new cipher clocks and do not keep page break *coordinates* fixed.
- **Positive control:** nine synthetic plaintext streams are sampled deterministically from the solved-message GP control, enciphered with **one identical restarted GP29 additive stream** generated from a fixed seed `0x33013301`. Their 36 start-aligned pairs give **2,150 / 33,551 = 0.064081547** matches. The planted inverse reconstructs every symbol and exact forward reencryption reproduces all planted ciphertext, demonstrating both mechanism and detector sensitivity under the stated natural-text model.

## Arbitrary relative-phase extension: original input and shifted positive

**Independent premise and limitation.** A solver might infer that successive titled bodies restart a single source-generated pad at different, unknown offsets. Under the literal GP29 additive model `C_s[i] = P_s[i] + K[i + offset_s] mod 29`, aligned ciphertext differences cancel the identical pad regardless of its actual values. This permits a key-independent original-input test of all pairwise relative offsets. The original titles supply the nine section boundaries, while **no original instruction authenticates a shared pad or relative offsets**. This extension is a negative family test, not a license to infer a key from alignment noise.

**Frozen detector.** For each of the 28 section pairs whose shorter body contains at least 500 runes, scan every integer relative shift with at least 500 aligned original runes. Exactly **62,083** pair-and-shift candidates meet this condition. Construct the 29-symbol difference distribution from the same **2,491** solved-Cicada GP symbols described above; its zero-difference probability is **0.062302973966**, versus the uniform GP29 null **1/29**. At every candidate shift, the first half of its aligned overlap supplies a log-likelihood ratio for this native-GP difference model against uniform. Freeze only the *highest training likelihood* offset for each pair. Score the **disjoint second half** at that same offset, with no extra optimization or per-section Caesar rotation.

**Original result.** The strongest uncorrected *full-overlap equal-rune* excess in any scanned alignment is units 6 and 9 (zero-based indices 5/8), shift −2,806, **36/510 equal runes**, z = **4.46866** against a simple uniform-index reference. A search over 62,083 alignments can yield such peaks by chance; this candidate was not a key. On the preregistered language-difference decision, **0/28 original training-selected alignments** have a positive held-out likelihood ratio. The descriptive, dependent sum of their held-out log-likelihood ratios is **−299.504758**. In particular, the strongest training winner (units 4 and 5, zero-based 3/4, shift +72, overlap 1,021) scores +8.39522 on its first half and **−15.07816** on its second. This is consistent with a wrong relative phase rather than detectable shared GP-English pad cancellation.

**Shifted-key positive.** The same code draws symbol samples from the exact solved-Cicada GP29 unigram source, enciphers the nine streams with one deterministic xorshift32-derived pad (seed `0x34ab224f`), and assigns hidden section offsets `[317,0,13,111,271,98,177,39,409]`. The generator is a detector plant; the historical cipher does not select xorshift32. Its key-informed inverse and forward reencryption recover all planted source symbols exactly. Without giving the offset values to the training search, the original detector identifies the correct relative offset in **all 28/28** eligible pairs, and **28/28** independently held-out likelihood ratios are positive, descriptive sum **+394.512049**. The planted source retains native GP29 frequencies, although it samples symbols independently and does not preserve an actual English word sequence.

**Execution and decision.** [Passing original-source manual CI 43121699305](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43121699305) executed the complete attack and archived the machine-readable per-pair results. Single-file SFT: [`sims/lp2_ninebody_anyoffset_keystream_sft.cjs`](../sims/lp2_ninebody_anyoffset_keystream_sft.cjs). This is a new bounded negative against *identical additive GP29 key values at any single relative phase per sufficiently long pair* for the stated native-English source register. It leaves periodic/relabelled/nonadditive/feedback keystreams and other representations open; do not rescue one common additive pad by freely varying offsets plus another cipher layer without an independent original instruction.

## Consequence and next gate

The original page divisions alone do not warrant a reused identical-phase additive GP29 pad. The strongest single pair does not withstand the predeclared 36-pair familywise test, and no original plaintext, key stream, or independently withheld rune prediction is recovered. **Retire the identical GP29 additive-pad hypothesis at natural section starts and the stated sufficiently long arbitrary relative phases for source-compatible English-like plaintext.** The source-visible nine-unit segmentation remains correct as a layout observation and may matter for a different, independently keyed or nonadditive mechanism. Reopen only if a primary-source clue fixes a genuinely different repeated pad/key operation and yields an independent cross-unit plaintext prediction. The unconstrained offsets and this detector have already been tested.

**Reproducer:** [`sims/lp2_ninebody_samephase_keystream_sft.cjs`](../sims/lp2_ninebody_samephase_keystream_sft.cjs), Node.js CommonJS, reads only the three named repository resources, asserts all section/word counts and planted forward inverse, emits all 36 pair scores and null ranks. Run directly with Node from the repository. The result includes full pairwise measures and the control-source model, allowing independent variation only by explicitly changing the hypothesis.
