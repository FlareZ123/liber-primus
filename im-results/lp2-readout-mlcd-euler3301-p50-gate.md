## Frozen operation

For every original p50 table number `x`, compute

`x^1650 mod 3301`.

Because 3301 is prime, every nonzero cell becomes `+1` or `-1`; the two literal zero cells remain zero. The printed p50 geometry is 32 rows by 8 columns, so a direct sign-bit reading naturally yields 32 bytes.

Only the two already source-motivated numeric states are charged:

- original base60 values;
- post-SHED base59 values.

For each, test QR=1 or NQR=1 polarity, printed row packing or printed-column packing, and left bit as MSB or LSB. This is **16 complete 32-byte outputs**. No row permutation, rotation, XOR mask, KDF, arbitrary bit permutation, cipher, or fitted encoding is added.
