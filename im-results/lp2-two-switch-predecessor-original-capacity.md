# Two changing substitution rows: exact source-only capacity of the global binary-state route

**17 November 2026, Agent42. Classification: new key-independent necessary condition on a bounded cipher architecture. No unknown LP2 rune, historical key or destination recovered.**

## Causal question

The original unknown 12,956-rune stream is nearly uniform and suppresses adjacent identical ciphertext runes. A natural next suggestion is a ciphertext-feedback alphabet with a hidden binary switch. The visible p39 red polynomial has two formal inverse branches, and the previously observed p39/DH affine source hypothesis proposes two distinct maps. Neither clue proves a two-state substitution cipher; this calculation tests a broad **whole-book, two-secret-alphabet-per-predecessor** family before more expensive key search.

Grant the decoder two **arbitrary unrelated secret GP29 bijections** for every previous ciphertext rune, for 58 completely free 29×29 matrices in total. A secret state bit at every rune may change adversarially and need not be generated by the manuscript. No Vigenere, affine, quadratic, or known-key restriction is imposed. Every first rune on each of the 55 original rune-bearing physical pages is counted as completely unconstrained. The rest is strictly reversible and exactly one ciphertext GP rune per plaintext GP rune:

`C_i = sigma[state_i, C_(i-1)](P_i)`, with `state_i in {0,1}`.

This permissive family contains both binary phase clocks and arbitrary quadratic two-branch maps that would select a stationary reversible alphabet per previous ciphertext rune. The bound excludes their **direct native GP-English output under one book-static secret table**, regardless of the secret states.

## Exact original-input maximum and constructive attainment

Let `N[s,c]` count source ciphertext transitions from previous GP rune `s` to current rune `c`, restarting at each physical printed page. For one target plaintext symbol, its image under each of the two permutations is a single ciphertext symbol in each predecessor row. Thus the full source count of that symbol is bounded exactly by

`55 + sum(s=0..28) [ largest(N[s,*]) + secondLargest(N[s,*]) ] = 1414.`

The 55 starts receive arbitrary favorable treatment. **1414/12956 = 10.9138623%** is a whole-book bound for *every* possible single plaintext rune, independent of its name and of unknown secret key values.

This is also an **attainable maximum under the granted arbitrary hidden-switch schedule**: for each predecessor rune, choose the two most frequent successor runes, map plaintext E to those two outputs in the respective unknown alphabets, complete each table to an arbitrary bijection, and select the matching state whenever either successor occurs. The SFT constructs both complete permutations and inverse tables on the **actual original ciphertext**, recovers exactly **1,414 E symbols** in the resulting synthetic plaintext, and forward-reenacts all **12,901 actual original within-page transitions**. This witness demonstrates sharpness of the *bound*, not the authenticity of that fabricated plaintext or the side-channel supplying a state bit at every position.

Each entry includes all 55 first runes as unconstrained outputs and counts all original transitions, including the 86 immediate ciphertext repetitions. The previously published [one-row all-secret obstruction](lp2-previouscipher-rowperm-allsecret-frequency-gate.md) is extended here to **two wholly independent modes whose selection is completely unconstrained at each position**. No plaintext statistical scoring, keystream search, guessed rune assignment or inverse exception is used.

### Authentic authorial text as a positive register control

Original solved **LP2 p57** contains **15 plaintext E runes among 95** (15.79%). Previous source-native GP-English controls give E approximately **12.79%** across 57,998 GP runes; either rate exceeds the original book-static two-state capacity of 10.91%. This is a **conditional native-GP register objection**, since unknown text need not share the clear page's exact E rate or writing style. The source bound itself is unconditional on language.

The executable SFT separately builds a **genuinely reversible two-mode ciphertext-feedback cipher** using two independently seeded complete secret GP29 alphabet tables for every preceding ciphertext rune. It encrypts a full **12,956-rune** positive plant formed from the actual solved p57 GP rune sequence, including the original 55 page breaks and an independently random state bit on every within-page step. It then recovers every planted rune by the real inverse and checks that the positive's ciphertext satisfies its own **two-mode upper bound**, confirming that the original-specific exclusion is not a mathematical artifact of the proposed cipher's construction.

## Decision

A **single book-static binary branch of predecessor-conditioned bijections** cannot directly decode the original LP2 output into a native-GP manuscript having more than 1,414 occurrences of its most common single rune. This is stronger than rejecting one particular affine, quadratic, rank or hand-selected binary register. It applies even when the mode sequence is fully secret and independently chosen at every rune.

However **chapter-rekeyed binary branches, page-rekeyed branches, three or more changing rows, longer internal state, nonbijective escape channels, transposition, non-GP plaintext registers and changing word boundaries** remain outside this decision. The original source supplies no authenticated bit schedule, switch selector or original key. A successor needs to recover such a selector or output real unknown prose; searching different binary branches within the exact already bounded book-static family cannot accomplish that.
