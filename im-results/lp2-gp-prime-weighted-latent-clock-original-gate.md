# Period-13 GP-prime-weighted plaintext-state clock: constructive recovery and original p3–7 gate

**Decision:** seven distinct random keys on four solved-native source sections are recovered with exact phase, exact plaintext and exact replay (3,983 runes total, 2,009 withheld). The frozen five-window lexical attack produces 9,521 distinct cyclic keys from original LP2 physical p3–7, and **none has even one legal path through the 569-rune fitting prefix**, before applying any language scoring. The 576-rune suffix is untouched by key selection. Retire this bounded native-printed-word transfer; it is not an all-key exclusion or a solution. No new unknown LP2 plaintext.

## Mechanism and source premise

For plaintext GP index P≠0, output C=(P+K[q]) mod29 and set q=(q+GPprime[P]) mod13. Plaintext F (P=0) emits C=0 and does not advance. Ciphertext F can also be generated by a nonzero plaintext. A consuming plaintext C (GP index5, prime13) has zero phase advance, a distinction from a transparent F. The 29 prime values and F hold are authentic historical ingredients; using plaintext prime weights as the clock is conjectural. Prior ciphertext-prime rank28 clocks, fixed periodic F holds, affine-prime index and Latin-length clocks are different. Fixed L=13 is motivated by the solved second-KOAN key length; no period, sign or source chapter scan.

## Recovery algorithm

Use the repository's TextBlob frequency lexicon plus solved-native GP words from every section except the target. Normalize Latin K/Q→C, QU→CW, V→U, Z→S and the declared greedy GP digraphs; dictionary one-rune entries restricted to A/I/O except additional native training forms. Score the native unigram, bigram and trigram with pseudocounts .25/.1/.1.

For each printed ciphertext group, enumerate dictionary words of the same GP length. The candidate plaintext word determines all relative phases within the word, so its observed ciphertext assigns individual K slots. Inconsistent assignments within a word reject it. Combine adjacent words under a shared partial 13-slot key and continuous phase, rejecting conflicting assignments. Choose up to five eight-word windows from the fitting half with ≥27 runes, using smallest log-product of locally compatible word counts per rune; starts separated by at least four words. Keep at most 3,000 partial states per word (score sum log(count+1)), deduplicate by partial key and phase. Save complete keys; after a window stops, complete at most 12 highest-ranked partial states with at most two unassigned slots (exhaustively 29 or 841 completions each). Canonicalize cyclic rotations. This is a bounded beam, not exhaustive keyspace coverage.

Rank keys by exact latent-state Viterbi over the *entire fitting prefix*, including all 13 possible initial phases. A cheap phase-bitset feasibility pass eliminates keys with no legal complete prefix. At each word enumerate all legal F/non-F transitions; add native trigram score and a lexical bonus of 4+.15 log(count+1) for in-vocabulary words. Unknown words retain their native character score. The best prefix key, phase and last-two context are frozen; decode the suffix without refitting or alternate prefix paths. Every reported path is independently forward-replayed. A key's cyclic canonical rotation is compared to the planted key; initial phase is also checked by exact recovered prefix/replay.

## Native positive controls

The target section is excluded from the native language model and native lexicon during each test. Original source word boundaries are retained, but the synthetic cipher and random keys are generated by the experiment, not historical. Initial development used three keys with seed base 12345 (xorshift32, per-section seed base+101×section). Earlier narrower search with no partial-key completions and no word bonus recovered only 2/3 keys; one withheld interval had 28 errors and the third key was absent. The revised search was then frozen and independently tested with seed base 67890, including an additional native section not used during tuning.

## Preselected original chapter

Physical LP2 p3–7 (the second dollar-delimited section), 1,145 source runes. Fix the continuous chapter and printed-group framing before scoring. Fitting prefix: 569 runes; heldout suffix: 576. Source windows begin at zero-based printed group indices 4,8,82,0,38. Their candidate counts are 2,079,1,19,230,7,192, with overlap removed to yield 9,521 distinct cyclic keys. **0/9,521** survives the prefix feasibility pass over all initial phases and legal F branches. Consequently there is no meaningful runner-up, full-prefix plaintext, or suffix prediction. There are 26 ciphertext F symbols over the chapter. The finite key set is generated by native lexical restrictions; the entire 29^13 model space is not excluded. Printed-group grammar is independently known to differ from native prose; a different source-motivated boundary representation could evade this negative.
