# Sparse shared additive keystream overlap: hidden-offset recovery and original gate (#2580)

**24 November 2026 — four of four hidden overlap alignments recovered; 251/256 key runes correct; no original candidate on the selected 4,142-window scan.** The strongest alternative is independent pads, a different cipher, or printed groups that do not preserve native word boundaries. Do not infer that the whole LP2 manuscript lacks shared pad fragments.

## Exact model and attack

A shared consuming pad fragment gives (D_i=C^{(1)}_i-C^{(2)}_i=P^{(1)}_i-P^{(2)}_i\pmod{29}). Authentic transparent plaintext F emits 0 and does not consume, whereas ciphertext 0 can also be an encrypted non-F. A physical interval containing no ciphertext zeros in either tape therefore contains no plaintext F and every rune consumes. The test restricts both sides to such intervals; it makes no claim about overlaps crossing F events. Freeze span length 64 and allow all alignments whose start is a printed-word boundary on at least one tape. The original source is the complete-group transcription (all 2,896 groups and 12,956 runes reconciled); use full chapters p15–22 and p40–53.

Assign each observed fragment of a printed word a prior over same-length native GP words, marginalizing unobserved word prefixes/suffixes, restricting the observed portion to non-F and normalizing. The 12,267-type original vocabulary combines the three pinned Gibran/Shakespeare/Emerson donors and native solved records. The two target sections are excluded from each plant's training vocabulary and character model. A fragment string is a unique observed plaintext candidate; duplicate full-word completions outside the window are summed, not counted as distinct fragment assignments.

The two word partitions induce a bipartite interval-overlap forest: every maximal intersection of a word from each side imposes the exact vector difference. Process the end of the earliest finishing word, pass its probability distribution over the required intersecting slice to the other word, and eliminate it. At a common boundary the connected component completes. This is exact sum-product because at most one word of each tape remains active; each eliminated word interacts with the remaining graph through one overlapping neighbor. Normalize after each message and accumulate log normalizers. Max-product with backpointers recovers the highest-probability pair; count messages track distinct feasible observed pairs (capped at 10^15). This is exact under the fixed independent length-conditioned word model, not exact English-language inference.

The first screen tests up to four most restrictive intersection slices (at most five runes each) for necessary support. A safe packed base-64 lane operation adds residues modulo29 without digit carry. Every screen rejection has empty support for an actual required overlapping slice, so screening cannot remove a feasible full dictionary pair. The score for a feasible window is (\log\Pr(D\mid\text{word lengths, non-F})+64\log29), the log-likelihood ratio for the observed ciphertext under a common uniform pad vs independent uniform pads conditional on the non-F plaintext model. Selection of zero-free windows is charged by matched controls; the score is not by itself a familywise p-value. At a nominated window recover (K_i=C^{(1)}_i-P^{(1)}_i); require replay of both ciphertexts, and separate independent original continuation/third-edge evidence before claiming decipherment.

## Original blind scan and matched null

The original full chapter lengths are 1,903 and 3,008 runes, with 103 and 106 individual zero-free 64-rune windows (10,918 possible cross-products), of which 4,142 begin at a printed group boundary on either tape. No window was discarded for empty length/fragment vocabulary. Original 131 windows passed the four necessary-support screens; **none** admitted a full pair of lexical plaintexts.

All 99 deterministic matched permutations independently shuffle nonzero symbols within each original chapter while preserving both chapter lengths, each zero mask, printed group geometry and per-rune counts. Each repeats the complete same 4,142-alignment search (410,058 null windows in total); all 99 have zero feasible pairs. The per-permutation screen survival counts range 87–193. Original and all null maxima are -infinity, hence conservative whole-search empirical p=1. This null does not preserve the original adjacent equality deficit or other higher-order structure; there is no positive statistic here requiring a stronger matched null. No overlap was nominated, so no third-edge/continuation scoring was invoked.

**Decision:** retire only 64 consecutive zero-free consuming pad reuse between p15–22 and p40–53, starting at a boundary on either tape, under the fixed native lexical register. Do not expand alignment/crib scans merely after this negative. Shared fragments shorter than 64, entirely interior starts, latent F interruptions, other chapter combinations and OOV/changed printed segmentation remain open. The exact overlap-forest message-passing method is a reusable constructive two-message attack when independent source evidence selects a reuse span. No unknown original LP2 plaintext/key recovered.
