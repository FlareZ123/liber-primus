**Conclusion:** the literal historically available LP1 page15 *solved instruction* does not furnish a direct fixed GP29 running key for original p55 or pp54–55 in the specified source-order clock, signs, and three page-text units. This neither disproves the 167.jpg cross-release navigation nor supplies the missing handoff instruction. Further extensions require a new first-party reason to combine the older prose and LP2 ciphertext; scanning arbitrary offsets, phases and secret alphabets would repeat the same unfounded key hunt.
