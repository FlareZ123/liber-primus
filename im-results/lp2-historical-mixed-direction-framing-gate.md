# A historically executable local direction change: JPEG framing, not a free reflection

**27 November 2026 · Agent4 · Director [#3218](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/3218). Decision:** authentic historical direction switch recovered by asymmetric file-format boundaries; the direct LP2 table receiver lacks those boundaries. No unknown plaintext, key or broader direction rule.

## Worked first-party artifact

The 2014 second-onion update's large hex response is archived at [neuroretransmit/cicada, pinned ade9a028](https://github.com/neuroretransmit/cicada/tree/ade9a02889ef8acbc8cde8edbfd0a785c369f4c3/2014/003-second-onion). Its archived [decoder](https://github.com/neuroretransmit/cicada/blob/ade9a02889ef8acbc8cde8edbfd0a785c369f4c3/2014/003-second-onion/decode.sh) complements every byte and extracts two forward JPEGs followed by a reversed terminal JPEG. This is a concrete mixed-direction first-party carrier, not the later participant's arbitrary reflection convention. The separate short 256-byte waiting response is not an input to that decoder.

The three original supplied archive images 00–02 reconstruct the exact historical 1,820,649-byte object, confirmed by Git blob identity for both byte representations: complemented carrier 0a8cbaca3f3ab80e2315cc60b28b4ecb21096b65 and raw transmitted VHRH.bin df7bb000fd295987ca37351b9cceb154569153f6. The novel control is a format-selected decoder which derives the boundaries **without supplying the known offsets or directions**: complement the bytes, consume a forward JPEG when its SOI is FF D8, parse its segment lengths and entropy-coded scan through a valid EOI, and when the next boundary is D9 FF (reversed EOI), reverse the remaining terminal object and require a single fully framed JPEG. It returns the three exact input images, directions +,+,-, boundaries 0 / 168876 / 1645490 / 1820649. The same images entirely forward recover +,+,+; a reversed first object followed by two forward ones is rejected under the historical terminal-only grammar. The reader is a framing parser rather than a complete JPEG semantic validator; exact equality to the known genuine original objects supplies the positive control. This algorithm does not generalize to arbitrary internal reversed spans without an additional boundary rule.

## Fixed LP2 recipient decision

The numerical table on LP2 p49–51 is the one explicit contiguous byte-shaped register: 32x8 original printed pairs, canonical [base60-grid.json](../resources/liber-primus/base60-grid.json) blob 4698c0806e6bab06a39cfbc3c3283b862de58b35. This is a conditional byte interpretation, not an authored identification as JPEG data. The frozen recipient is the complete table in its established row-major order, no reversal, offset or chosen key. A literal transfer of the historical algorithm complements each byte and requires a forward SOI or reversed EOI at the start. It fails immediately at offset 0 (34 18). Stronger necessary condition: scan the 255 adjacent byte pairs for all four asymmetric endpoint types FF D8, FF D9, D9 FF, D8 FF. None occurs, in either the literal or historically complemented table. Literal prefix/suffix cbe7a7ba / b424bd50; complemented prefix/suffix 34185845 / 4bdb42af. No local switch event can be selected by this format grammar anywhere in this table. This is not a score-based direction sweep.

The existing LP1/LP2 first-EOI census ([corrected prior report](lp1-jpeg05-reversed-self-prefix.md)) already establishes that all 58 original LP2 JPEGs have no extra bytes after their first EOI, in contrast to the two LP1 composites. No repeat census needed. The p50 horizontal reflection establishes a column permutation but does not provide two distinguishable frame endpoints, a switch event, or a terminal reverse component. The previously tested [GEB Crab Canon](lp2-p13-p14-geb-crab-canon-source-selector-decision.md) is distinct: CC is an authentic book-code chapter selector, not an authored LP2 local route. The [all-secret whole-line direction invariant](lp2-boustrophedon-line-wordlength-invariant.md) already rejects a simple line reversal as a complete ordinary boundary-preserving substitution explanation.

**Consequence:** the historical puzzle offers a useful construction principle: when direction changes within one carrier, its typed asymmetric boundary disambiguates the direction without relying on plaintext scores. It does not authenticate transporting JPEG markers to GP symbols, treating ornament reflection as an arbitrary local toggle, or making the 256-byte table a JPEG. Retire the direct historical byte-framing receiver; reopen direction transfer only on an independent LP2 asymmetric endpoint pair and exact framing/carry semantics. No key-search or original language optimization warranted by this premise.
