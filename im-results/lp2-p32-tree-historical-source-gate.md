## A specific independently found source lead, tested as a rival

The CicadaSolvers [Liber Primus Structural Overview](https://www.cicadasolvers.com/wiki/liber-primus/liber-primus-structural-overview/) states that some Cicada art derives from existing images, including an **oak tree** and mayfly, without thereby identifying the p32 central image. Its oak-tree reference belongs to the **2016 Cicada message image**, documented in the contemporaneous [scream314 2016 archive](https://github.com/scream314/cicada3301/blob/master/2016.md). The [Uncovering Cicada 2016 image notes](https://uncovering-cicada.fandom.com/wiki/2016_Message) explicitly identify a “dead oak tree clipart” at `http://www.clipartbest.com/cliparts/abc/y97/abcy97qTL.jpeg`. This attribution is **2016**, later than the 2014 original LP2 release, though the clipart itself may be older.

The previously visually dismissed MonokumaBear originalTreeImage.jpg (blob 90015384624ee17c24c948c0968b17e03167122f) is now byte-matched to the ClipartBest candidate, and independent held-out registrations support a common source. See [27 November common-stock correction](lp2-tree-common-stock-source-reopening.md). The earlier visual assessment does not establish independent lineage.

The repository author speculates that the 2016 source image may undergo convolution to produce the LP trees; the author reports no exact kernel. Neither the historic source image nor that speculative transform provides the original branches, a key schedule, a GP29 operation or a pre-2014 authored traversal.

## Additional bounded source search

Targeted public source/image catalog queries covered pre-2014 leafless-oak and bare-tree art, symbolic “tree of life” illustrations, botanical and engraving keywords, and specific Liber Primus source-image attributions. The inspected returns were modern stock graphics, generic line-art silhouettes, contemporary puzzle discussion, or the separate 2016 oak. No candidate had sufficiently distinctive matching branch junctions/trunk/foot to justify measuring a transformed exact registration. No external title, annotated plate, numbering, reading orientation or instruction was obtained.

**Negative scope:** the named indexed source collections and direct 2016 source-image control only. This is not a claim that the image must be original, that no older artwork existed, or that all physical books/archives were searched. No new heavy image optimization is warranted from generic silhouettes.

## Director decision

Retain the p32/p55 relationship and reopen the signed-2016 common-stock candidate. The remaining missing input is a source-defined discrete transform or traversal and typed receiver. Existing bounded negative consumers remain negative.
