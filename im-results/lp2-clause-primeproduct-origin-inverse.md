- Consuming non-F plaintext P emits C=(P+p[s+j]-1) mod29, incrementing j (starting at 0). Literal plaintext F emits C=0 without incrementing j; a visible ciphertext zero may instead consume and encode non-F.
- At the next image-corrected full stop set s'=s*sum(g(P)) mod3301, counting the GP weight of literal F as well. The next clause starts at its new origin; prime indexing is ordinary increasing within each clause, without modulo wrapping.
- Reset at the nine leading black-body openings defined by original page/ink layout, with independent unknown initial origins. Only the contiguous leading rubric is omitted; later red passages are included in the physical full-stop tape. No word-boundary or dictionary constraints enter the exact gate.

The product is real source evidence; interpreting it as a state update, selecting modulus 3301, and selecting the clause receiver are **not** first-party instructions. Competing explanations include an entirely unrelated use of the parable's numerical checksum and a different nonstationary key mechanism. This exact model is distinguishable without a speculative key-word search.

## Research decision

Reject this fixed cumulative prime-product origin rule manuscript-wide: seven bodies have no legal path independent of language; the two remaining bodies require at least 31 and 18 EO/OE exceptions respectively and the best exact candidate texts are nonprose. Do not search alternative moduli, ad hoc internal resets, re-key operations or products of different quantities merely to rescue this model. A new first-party source instruction selecting a different prime-product consumer would be needed. The source evidence that the 2013 tag equals the solved 2014 parable's product remains unchanged; the absence of this receiver does not establish what that numerical link was intended to do.
