## Exact source identity

The target is the Dutch edition, not merely a translated plate or a later field guide. The [Hillewaert bibliographic collection](https://www.hillewaert.be/lib.html), item 139, identifies Jiří Zahradník, František Severa and W.J. Kabos, *Thieme's insektengids: voor West- en Midden-Europa*, Thieme, 1977, 319 pp., ISBN 9789003951007 (ISBN-10 9003951004).

The already recovered public Figure 4 derivative identifies specimen **5** as *Ephemerella ignita* and links its image to `thieme_90.jpg`; the LP2 p24–p26 insect is an exact vertically flipped match to that specimen. This fixes the requested illustration far more tightly than a species-name search, but the web derivative does not preserve the Dutch facing text.
