# LP2 fitted 42.5%-of-words null insertion: original length evidence and missing inverse

**16 November 2026, Agent27, Director [#1188](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/1188).** This is a bounded test of the *specific* one-internal-null-per-selected-multi-rune-word hypothesis. It recovers **no new LP2 plaintext, key, insertion positions, or source-selected cipher operation**. Reproducer: [read-only Node22 SFT](../sims/lp2_midword_null_length_grammar_sft.cjs). [Manual CI invocation](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43793407354) was queued at this report's initial commit; independently executed source-identical calculations and results appear below. Original-source material is unchanged.

## What the published 42% statistic does, and does not, predict

[External Phase20](https://github.com/pnotisdev/cicada3301/blob/main/work/log/phase20_segmentation_plan.md) reports that LP2's printed word-length histogram differs sharply from ordinary English encoded in Gematria Primus. [Its source code](https://github.com/pnotisdev/cicada3301/blob/main/work/wordlen.py) fits a one-added-rune mixture at **42% of multi-rune source words**, with no insertion-position prediction. [Its Phase24 exact-likelihood implementation](https://github.com/pnotisdev/cicada3301/blob/main/work/log/phase24_word_edge_channels.md) models *word-edge* nulls, and explicitly does not model nulls inside words. The [local inversion contract](../workplace/integrated-original-inversion-challenge.md) also has no authenticated unknown-page plaintext. Different source spelling, resegmentation, genre or genuine ciphertext expansion remain competing accounts.

We independently reimplemented the external histogram calculation using its frozen `work/ref/lp_verified.txt`, reference Emerson `work/ref/emerson_essays1.txt` (after SELF-RELIANCE), greedy GP29 map with **ING** and **IO→IA**, and true page-spanning word parsing. Its original unsolved segment selection is `stats.UNSOLVED = range(7,16)`. The original source yields **2,896 complete printed words and 12,956 runes**, with length bins 1–11 and 12+:
```text
LP2:      83 448 720 513 317 256 216 156 78 52 32 25
Emerson: 2257 20286 16328 10851 7336 6073 4266 2831 2172 1245 676 559
```
These measurements include words carried across each of the **32 physical page boundaries with continued words**; a line or page turn by itself never terminates a word. The printed 2-rune share is 448/2,896 = 15.47%, compared with Emerson's ordinary GP 27.09%. The G-test against the unchanged English GP reference is **242.3825562** (11 degrees of freedom); with English counted as separate Latin letters rather than GP digraphs it is **60.4322925**. The fitted extra-rune mixture, minimizing over r=0..0.8 by .005, is **r=0.425**, **G=16.5409217** (10 degrees of freedom). Thus the published rounded **42%** is an *estimated parameter of a histogram fit*, and the alternative letter-only spelling already explains much of the length shift without inserting anything. The recovered fit is conditional on its English reference, word-boundary retention, and the allowed mixture.

For reference proportions p(L), the external model for L≥2 has `q(L) = (1-r) p(L) + r p(L-1)` (with p(1) exempt from insertion); r=.425. More exactly q(2)=.575 p(2), and the 12+ terminal bin accumulates overflow. The reverse conditional `Pr(inserted | observed L) = r p(L-1) / q(L)` gives **zero** at L=1,2 and approximately **0.479, 0.527, 0.522, 0.472, 0.513, 0.527, 0.491, 0.563, 0.576, 0.620** for L=3..12+ respectively (the terminal probability includes overflow). These are mostly near 50% per observed word. A printed two-rune word cannot contain an *internal* extra rune if source words must be multi-rune.

**Missing source-to-inverse input:** Of the 2,896 observed words, **2,365** are length≥3 and eligible for a single true interior insertion, yet neither the length model nor LP2 source marks distinguish which eligible words were lengthened, where within an eligible word a rune was inserted, or whether inserted values update keystream/cipher predecessor state. In the independent latent-event model, the posterior expects **~1,196** inserted words. The resulting approximate independent-word conditional uncertainty is **~2,359 bits for which words** and an additional **≥1,549 bits for interior positions if each eligible location is uniform** (12+ treated conservatively as length 12). These are **model-conditional ambiguity diagnostics**, not an entropy lower bound on an authored deterministic rule. One can always obtain an apparent inverse by selecting favorable deletions and storing deleted values/positions. That is a side channel, not recovery of a source-specified reversible encryption architecture. No original-position oracle or historically authenticated operation was found in the examined instructions.

## Decisive no-plaintext gate: ordinary prose length order survives one-null mixing

The length *histogram* fit cannot rescue an encoder if its output must also preserve ordinary prose's ordered word relationships. The pre-existing independent original source [length-grammar study](lp2-original-wordlength-grammar-mi.md) measures within-chapter adjacent-length mutual information (MI). It found that LP2 retains virtually no ordinary-prose word-length adjacency. Its earlier doubled-rune escape has only ~216–287 lengthened words, so it does **not** already test a ~42.5% insertion mixture.

We therefore froze the **published r=.425** and planted one extra rune-length slot independently with that probability in every GP29 source word of length≥2. Its location inside the word cannot alter its length, so this checks the full class of interior placements under the same random choice of lengthened words. No ciphertext was scored; no key, word ordering, null values, or insertion sites were fitted. We used the [LP1 solved-source-attested ING/IO orthography](../results/lp1-source-orthography.md) for all 15 independently *disjoint* 2,896-word English control windows. Word offsets are **1,000, 3,896, 6,792, 9,688, 12,584**, five each of pinned Gibran, Shakespeare, Emerson. All windows keep the **same nine original LP2 chapter word-count partitions** `[158,255,383,437,224,326,378,669,66]`. Xorshift32-insert decisions use seed `20261116 + 23*(windowIndex+1) + sourceId` with source IDs 1,2,3. Each is tested against **160** original-chapter-length-preserving Fisher–Yates random shuffles; original LP2 receives **360** independent shuffles. `sims/lp2_midword_null_length_grammar_sft.cjs` is the exact native Windows Node22 source-checked test.

All **15/15** independent source-native normal-English windows retain detectable adjacent-word-length grammar after 42.5% one-extra-rune insertion; LP2 itself remains near its same-inventory shuffled baseline. The finite control set is a calibrated failure for **ordinary ordered English plaintext + independently chosen 42.5%-of-multi-rune-words one-internal-null + retained word boundaries** as a complete account of LP2. It is **not** a theorem against dependent/keyed insertion, non-English or unordered plaintext, altered segment boundaries, length-dependent insertion probabilities different from the published model, or insertion within a larger cipher.

## Research decision for #1188

**Retire the standalone source-unselected mid-word-null interpretation now.** Histogram matching selects an approximate event *frequency*, not a reversible event grammar, a null-symbol value, a source-attested word/site locator, or a key. Running a latent-position beam against original LP2 without such an independent selector would inevitably fit the same ciphertext used to choose the model. Random word-internal insertion also does not erase ordinary prose's length grammar in the independent frozen controls. A new original-image or historical instruction that actually fixes flags, locations, and the cipher key could reopen a *distinct bounded* mid-word insertion model; at that point require a source-native positive, exact reconstruction including null values and keystream state, ≥150 contiguous original plaintext runes, and disjoint blind original text.

The substantive open problem remains recovery of authentic cipher operation/key or LP2 plaintext; this negative neither supplies nor rules out that missing material.
