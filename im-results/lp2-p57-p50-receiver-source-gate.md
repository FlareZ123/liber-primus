# P57 to p50 receiver source gate

**Director task:** #1568  
**Date:** 18 November 2026  
**Classification:** source-selection result. No plaintext, key, or decoder is recovered.

## Decision

The surviving DIUINITY arithmetic does **not** have a prospective source-visible rule that nominates the pp49–51 numerical table as its receiver.

The clear p57 wording selects an interior operation on the word DIUINITY. It does not name a table, page number, grid, numeral system, cell, row, column, address, or binary object. The original p57 art is an instar-like winged figure beneath the text, with a large red opening rune and red punctuation. The original pp49–51 table has a different visual register: 256 two-character numerals in a 32 by 8 rectangle spread across three pages, framed by the recurring floral side ornament. There is no repeated p57 illustration, connecting mark, shared numbered path, arrow, or explicit cross-page reference that links the instar/divinity instruction to this table.

## Source-visible semantics

The previously solved text gives “circumference” a coherent established meaning before LP2:

- solved LP1 p03: “LIKE THE INSTAR, IT IS ONLY THROUGH GOING WITHIN THAT WE MAY EMERGE”;
- solved LP1 p10: “THE CIRCUMFERENCE PRACTICES THREE BEHAVIORS WHICH CAUSE THE LOSS OF DIVINITY”;
- solved LP1 p14: “THE I IS THE VOICE OF THE CIRCUMFERENCE”;
- clear LP2 p57: “LIKE THE INSTAR TUNNELING TO THE SURFACE / WE MUST SHED OUR OWN CIRCUMFERENCES / FIND THE DIVINITY WITHIN AND EMERGE.”

This makes the primary source reading philosophical and self-referential: shed the circumference or “I”, go within, recover divinity, emerge. A geometric double reading remains possible, but the text alone does not identify which visible object should receive the resulting quantities.

The original table supplies a mathematically natural circumference candidate because its 32 by 8 rectangle has a 76-cell perimeter and 180-cell interior. Prior source work already established that the images do not draw or enumerate this perimeter, select a corner or direction, or assign the table to p57. That exact geometry therefore remains a candidate representation rather than a receiver instruction.

## Authenticated clue grammar

The historical Cicada operations already catalogued in this repository select their receivers explicitly enough for a contemporary solver to act: named files or media carriers, signed commands, book-coordinate formats, passwords, or a directly identified rune page. The 2014 participant serialization of the pp49–51 numerals into 256 bytes is useful evidence that the table was recognized as numeric data, but its preserved downstream history contains no author-selected consumer. This does not supply a p57-to-table bridge.

The 2016 signed wording that “words are the map, their meaning is the road, and their numbers are the direction” can motivate numerical navigation in general. It does not identify p50, zero-based row-major addressing, GP-prime sums, or the DIUINITY word as the operands of this 2014 artifact.

## Consequence for the DIUINITY construction

The existing arithmetic remains selective inside its frozen convention family:

`DIUINITY -> shed endpoints -> GP-prime sums -> addresses 184 and 192 -> p50 tokens 28 and 00`.

This gate concerns the step before those table reads. Without using the resulting 2800 value, the DH transcript, or the success of the arithmetic, the source does not tell a solver to apply the two p57 quantities to the pp49–51 table.

The receiver is therefore still postselected. The next useful evidence would be a first-party instruction, repeated graphic, explicit cross-page reference, or authenticated solved-stage convention that specifically binds the p57 interior quantities to this numerical table. More arithmetic variants on the same bridge do not resolve that missing causal step.
