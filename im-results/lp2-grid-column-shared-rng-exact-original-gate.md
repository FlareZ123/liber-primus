## Decision

Retire the specified table-column bijective shared-generator models. Individual edge feasibility or exact replay alone is not evidence of a key, particularly under no-hold rejection ambiguity. This does not exclude other seed constructions, resets, group boundaries, independent coin sources, non-MT mechanisms, atypical language, or no-hold thresholds outside the frozen domain. No source-supported reason for arbitrary parameter expansion. The exact interval Viterbi is reusable if an independently authenticated source provides a credible seed or deterministic key/coin stream.
