# p23–26 five-dot body: all affine Hill-5 inverses with literal-F holds

The ordinary consuming Hill-5 family was already investigated in #541. This extension permits latent literal-F holds: a physical zero can be a literal plaintext F bypassing the block or an ordinary consuming ciphertext zero; consuming plaintext symbols must be nonzero. The p23 red heading is 16 runes; the remainder of p23–26 is the source-selected 1,005-rune body (sum 14,023; positional checksum 7,096,046; 37 physical zeros).

For each of (29^5−1)/28 = 732,541 projective row directions, enumerate all 28 nonzero scales and 29 offsets (594,823,292 affine rows). The row-wise exact DAG permits all legal literal holds and five-symbol consumption blocks, including holds inside a block. It also permits arbitrary start fringe of 0–4 physical runes and unconstrained tail with at most four nonzero ciphertext symbols. A held F contributes its rounded-up unigram log probability once; a consumed block contributes five times the row's decoded symbol weight. Fringe uses the largest weight. Independent per-row hold paths and phases relax the real shared path. Greedy maximum-weight independent projective basis gives the exact relaxed maximum for five linearly independent rows; dividing by five and tape length gives an upper bound on full plaintext mean log likelihood. No language-model beam, key sampling or fixed hold mask.

* Original maximum −3.26099665671642 nats/rune; 1,153 consuming edges.
* 1,125 exact native training runes from warning, welcome, wisdom, jpg229, p56, p57. All 850 length-matched contiguous 1,005-rune windows of the separate 1,854-rune native donor (koan, loss of divinity, jpg107–167) score −3.008381592039801 to −2.9746523383084575.
* Independent invertible affine 5×5 plant, 1,600 native runes, 23 true literal Fs and 75 ciphertext zeros. On 1,000 fit runes the five highest-weight independent rows include one impostor; true missing row ranks sixth. Six five-row subsets and all lane permutations were evaluated using shared-path bigram Viterbi; only one subset is feasible on fit. Frozen correct matrix/order gives 1,000/1,000 fit and 600/600 heldout exact. The subset repair is control-informed, not a predeclared blind method. It does not affect the original all-key upper bound.

Conclusion: all shared affine Hill-5 literal-F keys under these conventions are bounded below even the least concentrated matched native window in this unigram metric. This is a strong conditional exclusion, not a proof against every possible plaintext, a different matrix per page, or an additional transformation.
