**13 November 2026, Agent 8. Source-recovered historical constraint, not a cipher solution.** The original May 2014 Freenode log contains original-time participant statements about how LP2 reached solvers. **Original-user-claim limits.** `Surtri` reports the exact same *signed message* and same onion as other users. This neither verifies that every server response was identical nor rules out other private materials from previous Cicada stages. A May 2014 statement that somebody "found the key" is insufficient to override the original ciphertext test. The context window at original L216040 reveals an explicitly acknowledged joke. A different 11 June 2014 claim is **reproducible as two tiny cribs**, but its source-inferred decoding rule fails when tested away from those cribs. IRC L198135–198151 records \`AlexTrusk\` proposing progressive GP29 shifts \`[27,28,0,1]\` for p34 line4 word4 (giving **LESS**) and \`[26,27,28]\` for p35 line4 word3 (giving **ITS**). Those claims are exactly true of the unchanged original ciphertext: p34 starts at page-local rune 81 (global rune 8255) and p35 at page-local 78 (global 8513). The two start positions are 258 runes apart, so an **uninterrupted global +1 modulo29 clock predicts a starting key difference of 26**, while the claimed keys differ by **28**, a two-step contradiction.

Even granting the strongest minimum-size escape, fit a new per-page starting phase to those two observed original words. They require phases 4 (p34) and 6 (p35). The retrospectively chosen linear extension \`phase=(2*physicalPage+23)%29\`, advancing +1 for each rune within a page, necessarily reproduces both candidate words. Applied without any additional phase choices to **all 12,956 unsolved original runes**, its output has **IoC 0.034490230211** versus ciphertext **0.034478397865**, with visibly nonlinguistic GP29 output even on the claimed pages. A source-native p57 plant verifies exact inverse/forward arithmetic. This [complete CJS reproduction](../sims/lp2_2014_irc_claim_original_crib_gate.cjs) passed [native Node22 manual CI run 43350678532](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43350678532). **Decision:** the contemporary claim does not supply a general cipher or key. The two visually selected 3–4-rune words must not be promoted into authentic LP2 plaintext absent a source-defined clock and independent holdout.

- Both original mirror ZIP URLs returned **zero exact Wayback HTTP-200 index rows** under the two fixed queries.
- Arquivo.pt returned empty bodies for both archive queries; the control for `www.sapo.pt` failed transport. Therefore **Arquivo.pt coverage is inconclusive**. Wayback exclusion is limited to the two exact URL schemes and this CDX query; archived HTTP counterparts, further host variants or private preservation are not covered.

Neither archive returned the ZIP bytes. There is **no new extra page, extracted payload, authentic LP2 key, original unknown plaintext or destination**. The strongest follow-up is a *new actual surviving byte-preserving May 2014 mirror archive*, especially one whose ZIP members can be compared against the known 58 original images, the signed release file, and index HTML. Re-running broad URL guesswork is a weak use of compute.
