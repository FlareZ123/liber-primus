## Source-selected transport decision

The original LP2 opening p0 (archive 17.jpg) has 13 red runes in two groups and a 13-dot boundary, while solved terminal p57 (archive 74.jpg) has a 23-dot terminal mark. Both original images were inspected locally. These counts motivate asking whether the historical numeral sequence and red heading are parallel labels, but unlike the 2012 stage there is no visible aligned second sequence or explicit continuation of the heading into a 23-character key. The p57 mark provides a count, not ten missing digit labels.

There is an exact negative even before asking for a source-selected alphabet. Canonical p0 heading indices are `[15,8,18,3,6,19,27,0,15,26,18,21,5]`. Positions 0 and 8 both carry S; positions 2 and 10 both carry E. The historical prefix assigns different values to both repeated pairs (10 vs7;14 vs17). For a deterministic symbolwise map f, identical source glyphs must give identical output values. This necessity does not assume an injective mapping, GP primes, ordinary GP order, English, or a cipher family.

Grant all 13 phases in both directions of the 13-number ring, and all 23 phases in both directions of the complete 23-number ring, matching 13 consecutive digits to the p0 heading. The necessary test fails 26/26 and 46/46, respectively. No static symbol-label map can equate this heading to a cyclic contiguous historical key segment. This is not an exclusion of a new independently supplied numeric stream or context-dependent algorithm. In particular the counts alone cannot distinguish a deliberate reference to 2012 from ordinary punctuation reuse.

The cuneiform 1033/3301 and p49–51 fixed-width numeric panel are not the paired 13-symbol banner/23-character continuation object. #2332 already rejects a literal visual reuse in the irregular p23 five-dot mark. #3200 and #2235 address distinct number fields. Do not rerun those investigations or invent a new label assignment.

As a separate explicitly conditional low-cost check, apply the historical full 23-value key verbatim to p0 black body, mapping the additive receiver from Z26 to Z29, skipping separators, starting at the first black rune and restarting at every printed line, no F hold (none in the historical ASCII sender). The first body word renders NG-N-NG-E, then I-C-R, and the subsequent physical lines are similarly nonprose. All 249 original runes replay forward (implementation invariant only); no language threshold or familywise claim, no alternate phase/sign/mask fitted. The exact raw output is emitted by the SFT. This conditional trial is not evidence that LP2 author selected this key.
