# Signed-2016 PRNG line:character candidate: historical index gate and original OutGuess inverse

**Status, 11 November 2026:** The signed-source PRNG mnemonic remains conditional. Three bounded direct uses of the historical OutGuess ARC4 have been tested against original unsolved LP2: positionwise GP29 addition, whole-word Fisher–Yates permutation, and collision-rejected GP29 Viterbi stream inversion. Each was tested with a planted positive control; none yields authenticated unknown plaintext, a cipher key, or a destination. The line:character address grammar and the proposed 13-token password remain speculative. Reproducers: [original marker and direct stream](../sims/2016_prng_linechar_source_gate.cjs), [whole-word permutation](../sims/lp2_2016_outguess_word_shuffle_sft.cjs), and [collision-skip inverse](../sims/lp2_2016_outguess_keyskip_viterbi_sft.cjs).

## Original clue and smallest proposed human route

The authenticated 2016 OutGuess-signed message says: “Its words are the map, their meaning is the road, and their numbers are the direction.” Its **16** words are `ITS WORDS ARE THE MAP THEIR MEANING IS THE ROAD AND THEIR NUMBERS ARE THE DIRECTION`. An independent Agent21 original-transcription observation prompted this fixed test: convert each word to GP runes, add its GP prime weights `S`, divide `S=29q+r`, and address **line r+1, rune q+1** in the original LP2 printed lines from physical p0 onward. Ignore printed punctuation/word separators for the **rune position**. The complete 16 output runes, rendered in GP Latin, are **`L C B P P R NG NG P A R R TH B P C`**. The contiguous instruction words `MAP THEIR MEANING` map to original physical coordinates **9:8, 28:4, 1:12**, producing `P R NG` = **PRNG**.

These three values are fixed source arithmetic:
- `MAP` GP sum **211 = 29×7+8**, addresses line9 rune8, **P**.
- `THEIR` GP sum **114 = 29×3+27**, addresses line28 rune4, **R**.
- `MEANING` GP sum **319 = 29×11+0**, addresses line1 rune12, **NG**.

The thirteen remaining original-output runes are **`LCBPNGPARRTHBPC`**. Only under the further, unselected GP base29-integer reading do they form **7144649572585460758** (`0x6326e4eeaa9a5416`, a 63-bit unsigned integer). Its size is consistent with a 64-bit seed, but the signed text gives no integer encoding, seed length, bit order or PRNG family.

## What authentic historical book codes select

Earlier Cicada book-code usage gives a **plausible general *class*** of operations: the 2012 Mabinogion/Agrippa clues used explicitly printed line:character coordinates with one-based positions (and included blank lines, spaces and punctuation in a source-specific edition); the 2013 signed *Liber AL vel Legis* riddle explicitly printed `chapter:verse:character` coordinates, ignoring spaces but retaining some punctuation; the 2014 Emerson *Self-Reliance* clue explicitly printed four-field `paragraph:sentence:word:letter` coordinates. Sources: [original 2012 reconstruction](https://cicadanotebook.com/sessions/12.html), [2013 original signed-book-code transcript](https://uncovering-cicada.fandom.com/wiki/What_Happened_Part_1_%282013%29), [2014 original-puzzle contemporary account](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2014/jan/10/cicada-3301-i-tried-the-hardest-puzzle-on-the-internet-and-failed-spectacularly). Those examples do **not** tell a 2016 solver to synthesize coordinates by dividing each word's GP-prime sum by **29**, to interpret the remainder as line and quotient as character, or where to begin counting LP2 lines.

The fixed candidate has one genuine internal gate: if the more ordinary `line=q+1, rune=r+1` order is substituted, **4 of 16 positions are out of bounds** (ITS, both THEIR occurrences, IS). The selected reverse orientation is at least valid for all 16. One-based positions are natural because `MEANING` has remainder 0. Still, being within bounds is a necessary plausibility check and does not authenticate the grammar.

The **exact character-counting convention matters**. Preserving original visible separators and punctuation, as in the 2012 Agrippa rule, changes the three addressed characters to **X R E**, destroying PRNG. Ignoring word separators as in the 2013 convention leaves **P R NG**. The full printed-character diagnostic is **`L U W P X R E - P B OE R F W P -`**, where hyphens denote a printed word separator. The 2016 source chooses neither historical variant. The complete rune-only 43-word signed-message reading documented by the Director produces a largely nonlinguistic stream, with no clear second instruction. Its odd single mnemonic is therefore promising to examine, but cannot authorize arbitrary key/seed searches.

## One source-anchored PRNG, frozen bounded cipher test

The 2016 signed-message carrier **actually used OutGuess**, whose published source `arc.c` uses an ARC4 random/stream engine initialized by `MD5(type || password)` with a further 256-state mixing pass. The **`Encryption`** domain string produces the encrypted carrier data; the **`Seeding`** domain string is used for the image-position iterator. [OutGuess original code](https://github.com/resurrecting-open-source-projects/outguess/blob/master/src/arc.c) and [iterator](https://github.com/resurrecting-open-source-projects/outguess/blob/master/src/iterator.c). This is a historically grounded **candidate generator**, absent from the earlier candidate's direct RSA/RC4/MT guesses in exactly this KDF/domain form.

The reproducer fixes two direct representations of the thirteen remaining rune tokens as the potential password, the **literal GP-Latin string** and the **single base29 decimal integer**. For each, the genuine source domain `Encryption`, the explicitly marked wrong-domain control `Seeding`, and both direct additive GP29 signs are run once against all **12,956 original unknown runes** with a fixed global start. There are **eight** clearly bounded treatments total, with no offset, guessed pad clock, post-selected segment or tuned seed. The full-book decrypted IoCs lie between **0.03446001 and 0.03454304** (uniform GP29 is 1/29≈0.03448276); no candidate reveals native-GP prose. Even the best full-book value remains near chance. This rejects only those direct original-source PRNG-additive transfers and says nothing about OutGuess's complex JPEG embedding semantics or other cipher constructions.

## OutGuess ARC4 as a complete-word permutation (Agent 40, 11 November 2026)

The signed wording says **“Its words are the map”**, and the conditional three-token readout spells **PRNG**. One distinct reversible interpretation is that a PRNG changes the order of **entire printed rune words**, rather than serving as a positionwise additive pad. This is motivated by the manuscript's original **2,896 complete rune words / 12,956 runes**, whose adjacent short (1–2), medium (3–5), and long (6+) word-length categories have unusually little dependence: the unchanged original Pearson χ² is **3.94944609** for a 3×3 transition table. Native GP-English word-length sequences generally show a stronger association. The cipher conjecture and the thirteen-token seed assignment remain unconfirmed.

The independently documented 2016 OutGuess carrier provides an authentic **ARC4 PRNG implementation** with two MD5 role strings, `Encryption` and `Seeding`. Freeze its two candidate passwords from the source extraction above: literal `LCBPNGPARRTHBPC` and base29-decimal `7144649572585460758`. For each of the resulting four source-key/role generators, consume four ARC4 bytes per Fisher–Yates index in either big- or little-endian 32-bit order. Invert or apply the resulting permutation either to all 2,896 original words continuously, within each of 55 rune-bearing physical pages while continuing the same PRNG, or within each page with a fresh PRNG instance. The original complete-word boundaries are preserved across printed line and page wraps. This gives exactly **4 × 2 × 2 × 3 = 48** finite candidate original-input inverses, with **no fitted seed, free phase or plaintext-derived sorting order**.

All 48 outputs remain word-length-disordered. The best candidate is `Seeding` / decimal password / book-global / big-endian / forward, χ² **10.3639043421**; the unchanged original is χ² **3.9494460884**. This is far weaker than the source-native GP-English control below. The result excludes this declared literal full-word Fisher–Yates interpretation; a byte-valued key stream, encrypted intermediary, changing word boundaries, or a different stateful traversal remains possible.

The complete planted control uses **2,896 actual Shakespeare words rendered into GP rune lengths** under a fixed transliteration, disjoint from original LP2. It has χ² **19.7239514150**. Encrypting by the source-prescribed `Encryption` / literal password / global / big-endian shuffle reduces χ² to **4.5529707207**, matching the weakly dependent appearance of the original. Applying the exact inverse recovers **2,896/2,896** GP word lengths and restores χ² **19.7239514150**. This demonstrates that the model is capable of obscuring and restoring a real native-word ordering. The original input does not recover the analogous signal in the tested family.

**Reproduction:** [CommonJS original-source SFT](../sims/lp2_2016_outguess_word_shuffle_sft.cjs); [successful opt-in Node22 CI run](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43000741166). The script rederives all sixteen source line:character runes from the signed message, asserts the thirteen-rune seed, processes original whole-word boundaries, runs all 48 configurations and verifies the positive planted inverse. Its byte-order and Fisher–Yates choices are explicit hypotheses, and do not imply Cicada specified them.

## OutGuess ARC4 with ciphertext-collision key rejection (Agent 40, 11 November 2026)

The observed LP2 ciphertext has **86 adjacent repeat runes across 12,956 unknown runes**, much fewer than an ordinary iid GP29 stream. A particular hypothesized generator could suppress repeats by discarding one drawn key symbol whenever its candidate enciphered rune equals the preceding published ciphertext rune. The existing exact collision-aware GP29 Viterbi model provides a reversible language-scored inverse, previously used with historical byte sources. Testing it with the *conditional 2016 thirteen-token password* is a distinct operation from the earlier direct fixed-position stream or 2,896-word Fisher–Yates experiment. The source still provides no instruction to discard draws or to use GP29 addition.

Freeze two candidate source passwords, `LCBPNGPARRTHBPC` and `7144649572585460758`, the authentic OutGuess ARC4 MD5 roles `Encryption`/`Seeding`, two direct mappings of each 8-bit drawn byte into a GP symbol (`byte%29` and `floor(29*byte/256)`), and both additive signs. These define **16** source-key models. Extract the same **eight 300-rune original section-body prefixes** that the existing collision model uses. For each source key and sign, run the exact Viterbi inverse with at most one rejected key per output, up to 34 per 300-rune section, collision admissibility enforced against the original predecessor rune, and a fixed skip-penalty 3.5. A smoothed GP trigram prior is independently trained on the first 200,000 characters of the retained Shakespeare source. Select models only on four even-numbered sections; freeze the winner on the other four.

**Original result:** The top train-selected model is the `Encryption` role, literal thirteen-token password, scaled-byte GP29 reduction, and positive sign. Its average GP trigrams are **−5.73498380 nats/rune on train** and **−5.80889594 nats/rune on untouched heldout**. This is extremely far from source-correct native GP-English recovery, and the recovered rune sequences are incoherent. None of the sixteen hypotheses produces recognizable unknown prose. The exact number of rejected draws inferred by the model on eight original section prefixes is [5,3,8,6,5,4,2,4]; those fitted rejection choices are model-consistent guesses, not independently authenticated cipher events.

**Independent positive control:** Encrypt **2,400 genuine GP Shakespeare runes** from a disjoint later source excerpt using the identical historically derived `Encryption`/literal/`byte%29`/-1 key model, with 81% probability of rejecting the first candidate key draw whenever it repeats the previous ciphertext rune. On the same eight 300-rune section windows, the exact Viterbi inverse recovers **2,398/2,400 plaintext symbols**, with a native GP-English mean trigram score **−2.50621707 nats/rune**. Seven of eight planted windows recover all 300, with the remaining window 298/300. The planted known-key calibration demonstrates that the inverse handles genuine key-consumption ambiguity. The original heldout score remains drastically weaker than the planted example.

**Decision:** The finite, authentic OutGuess algorithm with the proposed 2016 source passwords gives no credible GP-English plaintext under direct addition with at most one collision-conditioned key discard. Retire this scope rather than tuning seeds, skip penalties, key offsets or arbitrary extra rewrite rules without an independent author-visible instruction. It does not exclude a different password, a non-GP intermediate, unlimited skips, page- or word-dependent generator resets, an evolving outer alphabet, or another genuine PRNG.

**Reproduction:** [Source SFT](../sims/lp2_2016_outguess_keyskip_viterbi_sft.cjs), [opt-in successful Node22 GitHub Actions run 43001777057](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43001777057), and [workflow](../.github/workflows/lp2-2016-outguess-keyskip-viterbi.yml). The original and planted windows, passwords, ARC4 initialization, markov model, 16 configurations, exact collision rejection and both original/positive outputs are all preserved.

## Decision for the Director

A plausible original-solver narrative exists: Cicada used book codes, its signed text refers to word-number directions, one reversible coordinate orientation survives bounds, and the exact resulting three-symbol `PRNG` acronym might name the class of next operation. The weakest premise remains **unselected word-sum division into coordinates**, with more than one historically attested character-counting convention; no primary artifact supplies the seed representation or a uniquely identified generator/state. The signed 2016 message's *actual* OutGuess ARC4 is a natural independent source-control family, and its simplest eight direct inverses fail on the original. Close this fixed readout as **conditional** until a new source instruction defines an exact coordinate grammar *and* the next reversible cipher action. The substring cannot be promoted to decrypted LP2 content. The tested whole-word shuffle also fails to recover native-GP word-length ordering, while the new collision-rejection Viterbi extension fails on heldout original sections despite recovering 2,398/2,400 controlled GP English runes. Do not repeat either finite family without a source-selected generator, address schedule, or key.
