## Frozen operation

Preserve every complete printed group, including the word carried from p54 into p55. Position i of length L belongs to depth d=min(i,L−1−i). Each depth has an independent zero-based prime origin 0–3301, advanced continuously in physical order. At each non-F plaintext symbol P emit C=(P+s*(prime[cursor]−1)) mod29 and advance the corresponding depth cursor; literal F emits F and holds. Both signs s=±1 are granted, common across depths. This differs from a single peeled sequence, fixed shell-derived pads, and per-word resets. The physical nine chapter ranges are [0,3,8,15,23,27,33,40,54,56). The image-corrected transcription is checked rune-for-rune against the corpus: 2,941 groups / 13,136 runes including solved pages; unknown 2,896 groups / 12,956 runes; terminal exactly 66 groups / 308 runes at offsets [12648,12956).

## Exact terminal lexical relaxation

The same 35,944-form GP register as the prior anagram SFT is built from literary training prefixes, selected solved native sections, and both native spelling variants of the pinned word-frequency list. The terminal depth-0 and depth-1 independent candidate-origin domains contain 5×115 and 3×156 pairs. For each pair, enumerate all local hold/consume branches across each complete word. Test its full length and outer one- or two-layer projected glyph signature against the lexicon; arbitrary deeper glyphs are granted. Dynamic programming merges equal cursor-pair states by minimum OOV count. Thus each score is a lower bound on actual whole-word OOV under this finite register, regardless of deeper clock values.
