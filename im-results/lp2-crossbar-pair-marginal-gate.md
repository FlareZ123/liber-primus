## Exact inference
For uniform independent key symbols K and sharing, X=A+K and Y=B+K modulo 29. D=X-Y=A-B. Train normalized order-3 Markov rune models on complete separate Shakespeare and Emerson sources (217106 and 321327 encoded symbols) with hierarchical smoothing. Hold out complete distinct native solved LP sections and Gibran. No literal donor restriction. For each possible A prefix, B is fixed by D; last-three-symbol state permits exact sum-product with normalized forward/backward vectors, summing all 29^44 pairs rather than selecting the highest-scoring path. Compare log P(D|share)+44 log 29 against independent uniform pads. Marginal MAP per coordinate minimizes expected key-symbol Hamming loss under this model; per-coordinate MAP need not be the joint MAP path. Re-encryption is exact by construction and is not evidence of correctness. The independent-pad model is idealized. Posterior probabilities depend on the training language model, not cryptographic certainty.
