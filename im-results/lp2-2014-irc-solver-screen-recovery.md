## Cryptanalytic decision

All **six original 2014-posted links currently yield actual images**; the four Lightshot pages additionally expose image addresses in their returned HTML (the fifth Lightshot page is the fifth PNG; in total five wrappers). Direct picture inspection found **zero** complete period-stated unknown-page keys, decoder commands, long coherent original unknown-page plaintext passages, or independent key-selection rules. No proposed operation is sufficiently frozen to justify a ≥100-rune original-input inverse or independent ≥40-rune continuation; adding a language-tuned key would violate the source gate. A strict future exception requires new first-party selection evidence or a different historically named medium, not re-running these images with guessed ciphers.

A valuable source-specific distinction is that `3ilh97` / `3ix2fd` demonstrate the contemporary *human* hypothesis of detecting repeating-key length from guessed plaintext and interruptions, while `3fugdf` records early failed crib work. These are participant hypotheses rather than evidence of the creator's intended operation. Retain the files as historical controls; focus subsequent research on source-derived keys, executable steps, or actual original unknown-page plaintext.
