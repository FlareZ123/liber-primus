## Decision

This finite result closes these three exact transport variants at the source-proposed offset. It does **not** rule out a different specifically instructed pad, a compressed or encrypted binary with no independently selected header, or wholly different GP29 cipher architecture. The original 2013 dataset is genuine and its historic XOR use is established; its **LP2 source selection, derived offset, and byte framing are still hypotheses**. Do not expand into guessed neighboring file offsets, arbitrary bit alignments, reversal combinations or imagined next-stage ciphers merely to rescue high-entropy outputs.

A genuinely productive successor needs a new original clue which fixes a **distinct** reversible GP29-to-data operation or first-stage key, then a complete untouched plaintext or typed message validated independently.
