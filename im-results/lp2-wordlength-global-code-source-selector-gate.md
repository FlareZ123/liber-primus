# LP2 global word-length exception channel: source-selector gate

**Decision: close Director Issue #1463 as source-selector absent (18 November 2026).** No original unknown-page plaintext, key, complete cipher inverse, or destination was recovered. This is a bounded rejection of the proposed *2016-word-length global exception-channel reading as an actionable decoder*, not a proof that every conceivable global side channel is impossible.

## Question

Could the PGP-signed 2016 instruction that Liber Primus words form a map, their meaning a road, and their numbers a direction select one globally invertible code in which the printed word-length register carries the information lost by a 29-to-28 anti-repeat emitter? Such a decoder would have to recover both the original plaintext word lengths and every exception position, including the two disjoint doublets inside original p27 word 1476, without an unprinted per-word key table.

## Source-selector decision

The preserved 2016 signed object is [resources/historical/cicada2016-4gq25-outguess.asc](../resources/historical/cicada2016-4gq25-outguess.asc). It identifies words, meaning, and numbers as relevant concepts, but it does **not** specify word length as the numeric register, a permutation or ordering of the word-length sequence, a length-to-state map, an exception code, a state update, a starting position, page/chapter reset semantics, or a receiver that reconstructs the original word lengths. “Numbers” is not uniquely distinguishable here from the Gematria Primus prime values, word prime sums, rune positions, or word lengths.

The earlier signed 2012 source is a useful human-solve-path comparison: it uses path/road language but also explicitly says “Let the Chorus be your guide”; the original MIDI note-and-time ordering supplies the executable selector. See [the preserved signed message](../resources/historical/cicada2012-midi-email.asc). The 2014 GEB route likewise used explicit title/exchange/word/letter coordinates. The source audit in [Issue #1304](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/1304) found no first-party semantic-to-index codebook or start/stop rule in the 2016 clue. None of these historical precedents selects a global code over LP2 word lengths.

A competing interpretation of “numbers are the direction” is currently being explored from the first-onion participant `count` examples: GP word-prime sums such as PARABLE=449 and FIND=151 are paired with `*`/`+` suffixes, while p57 clause sums are emirps. That source is explicitly an unsigned participant reconstruction, and the proposed mapping from suffixes to cipher-pointer direction remains conjectural; it points to word numbers, not a defined word-length permutation. Preserve it as a separate lead, not as proof of this issue's premise ([Agent4 handoff](../communications/broadcast/20261118T103654388Z_agent4_p57-count-emirp-direction.md)).

## Source-derived 2013 NEXT word lengths: exact crib rejected

A second historically sourced candidate is the **PGP-signed March 2013 NEXT payload**, preserved as a derived 406-entry GP word-length fingerprint in [2013-signed-next-gp-wordlengths.json](../resources/derived/2013-signed-next-gp-wordlengths.json). This is authentic signed text, but the authenticated TCP protocol defines `NEXT` as client-supplied text storage; it does not identify the stored payload's word lengths as a key or instruction for LP2. The signed origin of the text must not be confused with an authenticated LP2 key role. The body is an ordinary membership invitation and explicitly says there will be no hidden codes, secret messages, or physical treasure hunts. It has 406 alphabetic words across 11 nonempty prose paragraphs plus a terminal numeric 3301 line; the factorization 406=14×29 does not correspond to a 14×29 paragraph or word layout. This is direct source evidence against treating the length vector as a hidden key.

The frozen exact test [lp2_2013_signed_next_wordlength_sft.cjs](../sims/lp2_2013_signed_next_wordlength_sft.cjs) compares the complete **406-word** GP-length sequence with all **2,896** original LP2 word lengths. Its longest exact contiguous match is **8 words**, at original word 410 / source word 308. No six-word-seeded extension allowing at most two length errors reaches 11 words; the planted 40-word/two-error positive control is recovered, and 128 shuffled-source controls give exact maxima 6–10 and fuzzy maxima 0–13. Thus the source sequence is not an exact plaintext word-length crib and supplies no complete or withheld plaintext prediction. This does not test arbitrary transforms of the 406 values, but no source selects a permutation, repetition period, state clock, or inverse over them.

A separate direct-key interpretation of the same payload also fails: the tempting 1+1,680 split requires deleting all 29 ordinary F letters, treats a page span containing a separate 91-rune p33 prelude as one source unit, and produces no native-language plaintext under its 16 frozen inverses ([2013 NEXT chain-key gate](2013-next-chain-p33-39-gate.md)). These tests are useful exclusions, not proof that every possible use of the 2013 text is impossible.

## Original-input constraints already measured

The canonical original LP2 register contains **12,956 runes, 2,896 printed words, and 32 physical-page word carries**. The 86 adjacent-equality events comprise **63 internal** and **23 word-boundary** events. Per word, the exception-count distribution is **2,812 with zero, 82 with one, and two with two**; the p27 stress word is index 1476, length 12, with two disjoint internal doublets.

Agent3's original-source one-step model (E_i=f(L_{i+1}-L_i)) is not a lossless exception decoder. The measured forward mutual information is **56.154 bits total**, leaving **505.543 conditional bits**; the reverse alignment is weaker. A centered three-word length tuple has **276.120 empirical bits**, but fails its within-chapter shuffle control (**p=0.14495**) and still leaves **285.193 residual bits**. See the full [word-length conditional-independence and exception report](lp2-printed-wordlength-conditional-independence-original-gate.md) and [Issue #1456](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/1456).

The complete word-length multiset has **8,791.876 bits of theoretical ordering capacity**. This establishes only that many possible orderings exist; it does not identify which ordering is intended, encode the 86 exception events, or let a decoder recover the plaintext length sequence. Capacity is not a code. A globally chosen permutation would need an independently sourced rule and an exact inverse.

The obvious source-visible global orders have already been tested separately: p15's 16-cell Fibonacci order against the 2,896=16×181 word layout; p50's eight-column order against 2,896=8×362; and all standard unkeyed ragged rectangular word transpositions at widths 2–256. These fixed receivers do not restore calibrated native-GP English word-length grammar. Their scoped results are consolidated in the [word-length report](lp2-printed-wordlength-conditional-independence-original-gate.md). They do not exclude every possible global or stateful code, but they provide no selector for this one.

## Direct whole-original word-length parity as five-bit GP29 payload (Agent5, 21 November)

The signed 2016 `ITS WORDS ARE THE MAP... THEIR NUMBERS ARE THE DIRECTION` wording motivates a distinct human-readable **formatting-channel** test: odd/even rune length of each authentic printed word as a bit. The 2,896-word full original source includes 32 cross-page words, which must be joined before splitting only on printed word delimiters. Full source counts are independently checked: **2,896 printed words, 12,956 original ciphertext runes, 83 singleton words**.

An exact prospective reader was [frozen before output](../workplace/agent5-whole-lp2-wordlength-bacon-5bit-precommit.md) (`1814457`): original printed word order p0–55 (p50 rune-free), **odd=1/even=0**, group five bits most-significant-first from original first word, and map integers0..28 to canonical GP29; values29,30,31 are invalid. This yields **579 complete 5-bit codes plus one unused bit**. A genuine unescaped GP29 plaintext must have **zero** invalid groups.

The actual original full stream contains **53 invalid 5-bit groups** (first at output positions21,38,52,63,64,85...) and 526 valid codes with empirical GP29 IoC **0.03445229**, rather than English plaintext. This rejects the entire exact unescaped length-parity GP29 decoder independently of linguistic judgement. A positive control reproduces all **95/95 clear solved-p57 original GP29 runes** when encoded into even/odd synthetic word lengths with exactly the same five-bit decoder.

[Reproducer](../sims/lp2_whole_wordlength_parity_bacon_gp29_sft.cjs) `3be776e` reads the full original line/page-preserving transcription and complete canonical rune alphabet, includes the authentic cross-page word joins, freezes the bit reader and checks the positive plant. This does **not** disprove other unselected binary codes, escaped encodings, punctuation channels, or a separately evidenced source-specific word-length scheme; it means Bacon-style parity is not the missing original GP29 decoder under the frozen historic-readable convention.

## Historical 2013 COUNT on every original LP2 word (Agent5, 21 November)

An independent original-source readout applies the historically participant-visible Cicada 2013 `COUNT [phrase]` operation to all **2,896 authentic printed words** p0–55 (12,956 GP29 runes; p50 has no rune text), rather than using word lengths or guessing a key. For each original word, sum **all actual Gematria Primus prime values**, reduce the sum mod29, and render one canonical GP29 glyph. The signed 2016 “words/numbers/direction” clue motivates this computation but does **not** authenticate mod29 as a decoder or prescribe a GP29 output register.

The direct 2,896-glyph original stream has **index of coincidence 0.03443806716**, uniform-like symbol frequencies, and no coherent 150-symbol English message in its original order. A planted positive re-encodes all **95** original clear solved-p57 GP symbols into synthetic one- or two-rune words with exactly the required GP-prime COUNT modulo29, then recovers the original 95/95 symbols under the same consumer. This rejects the **one** canonical signed `COUNT mod29` whole-word plaintext operation; it does not exclude other separately selected word-code operations.

Exact inputs, word parsing, output and success criteria were [frozen before the readout](../workplace/agent5-original-word-primesum-count-stream-precommit.md) `dc67157`. [Read-only Node SFT](../sims/lp2_original_word_gp_prime_count_stream_sft.cjs) `46e57c7` checks all source totals, provides the native positive control and returns the fixed output. Avoid rescuing this result by trying unrelated indices, offsets or moduli without independent author evidence.

## Decision and reopening condition

No first-party artifact selects a global permutation/order or finite-state distribution code over printed word lengths, specifies how the original plaintext lengths are recovered, or defines a clock that survives the 32 page carries and p27's double-doublet word. Constructing a higher-order automaton, choosing a permutation from the large theoretical space, or adding exception-specific state now would manufacture the missing instruction rather than test one. No new synthetic SFT is justified until the source fixes that operation.

Close #1463 at **source selector absent**. Reopen only if an independently authenticated source supplies the length register, global order/state transition, start/reset and inverse, or if an independently constrained mechanism produces a substantial original plaintext passage and a correct withheld-page prediction. Do not reopen the retired memoryless ΔL family or the already-tested p15/p50/all-width unkeyed word-order variants under new names.
