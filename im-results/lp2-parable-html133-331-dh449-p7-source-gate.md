2. The [PGP-verified 2013 TCP challenge](../resources/external/2013-tcp-server-signed-source.md) teaches a **participant/server interactive `DH` operation** with explicit role-typed parameters. It does not instruct carrying the DH operation into the following year's LP2 manuscript, interpreting HTML attributes as public shares, or deriving protocol values by reducing GP-prime sums.
3. Original solved p57 PARABLE gives GP-prime sums `[449,1259,1031,1229]`. Taking heading449 as modulus produces body remainders `[361,133,331]`. Given the two HTML labels, `p=449,g=133,A=361,B=331` is a mathematically legitimate *retrospective assignment*: `133^330 mod449 =361`, `133^208 mod449=331`, and the two shared-secret paths give `273`. The original 208-rune p7 is the sole physical LP2 page of that length. These exact equalities and a source-native positive-control implementation are preserved in [`sims/lp2_p57_parable_dh85_source_sft.cjs`](../sims/lp2_p57_parable_dh85_source_sft.cjs).

It supplies no unambiguous *receiver type* for the decimal 208 or the DH shared273: no key-to-GP29 alphabet serialization, cipher family, character clock, bit-to-image instruction, or continuation pointer follows from the p7 illustration.

## Competing interpretations and disposition

The 133 and 331 are genuine HTML fields and reverse-digit companions. Their match to PARABLE modulo449 increases source coherence, but **an HTML title and an element ID are not standard DH operand fields**. The hypothetical roles `p,g,A,B` arise only by combining a later publication with a different year's interactive protocol. A separate *equally standard* DH grammar from the same four p57 sums has `p=1259,g=1031,A/B=1229/449`, shared352 and exponent85 matching the adjacent solved p56 length. The authentic TCP protocol does not choose between them. Under the second HTML grammar, replacing the absent consumer with `x_{n+1}=133x_n mod449`, then `k_n=x_n mod29`, gives the already performed original p7/full-book nonprose outputs [reported in the primary study](lp2-parable-dh85-source-packet.md). Retesting that pad or choosing a new numeral-to-key transform would add parameters after observing ciphertext and would violate this issue's source-selection gate.

**Outcome:** the first-party records substantiate the source arithmetic, the creator's historical use of DH in a *different task*, and p7's length. They do not specify a single reversible LP2 operation on p7. Consequently the historical-source branch stops at **missing first-party rule mapping the p57/html packet to a p7 operator**, without initiating an unconstrained p7 cipher sweep. Reopen only upon a newly found authenticated cross-round instruction, a source-typed extra operand, or a demonstrable original p7 decryption that independently predicts another page.

**Evidence scope:** original HTML and signed 2013 challenge were read directly; the p57/P7 numerical statements and already-executed p7 orbit gate were checked against the repository's source-native reproducer and report. No novel original-input plaintext recovery is claimed.
