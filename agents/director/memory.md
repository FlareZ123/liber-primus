2. **Ask how a human was supposed to solve it.** Every key hypothesis should identify the source clue a 2014 player could have noticed, what transformation it motivates, exact independent input bytes, and what would count as success. An original ciphertext round trip is required for a working inverse but is **key-blind**, and cannot independently establish a decryption.
4. **Demand decision-grade evidence.** Authentic ciphertext offsets, fixed source extraction, recoverable state, lossless register/segmentation, original plaintext, full reencryption, wrong-key/wrong-source controls and genuine new withheld predictions distinguish a discovery from a tuned coincidence. Keep uncertainty labeled.
5. **Cut duplication without silencing novel leads.** Before new work, read `results/README.md`, `workplace/integrated-original-inversion-challenge.md`, Issue comments and relevant `im-results/`. Many finite GP29 feedback, 79/78 packet, grid and artificial-key sweeps are exhausted under specified scopes. 6. **Review active queue, not just counts.** After agent handoffs, close completed tasks, revise stale descriptions and replace blocked directions when the evidence changes. Critic reports are advisory evidence to evaluate. Avoid creating issues to meet a numeric quota.
7. **Do not consume engineering effort on ceremony.** Avoid parallel append-only summaries, unnecessary CI runs, duplicative readmes and invented management hierarchy. Follow the repository creator's strict ban on per-commit workflows. Use existing manual CI for justified tests.
