On original 2,896 whole LP2 rune words, 104 L5 same-glyph events, 80 T6 affine events, and 18 words carry both (559 words length >=7). Rather than shuffling ciphertext runes and destroying their already selected marginal signals, I shuffled complete T6 hit-position vectors among words with the same length, section+length or page+length. Preserved each event family's exact wordwise placement/clustering and all counts.

Length strata: 18 joint words vs exact expectation 14.3245 (10k empirical one-sided p=.13539). Section+length: expected 13.0886, p=.04600. Page+length: expected 13.8762, p=.03370. Four associations x three conditionings, smallest unadjusted p .03370; no within-family multiplicity-adjusted evidence of one coupled trigger. Site-lag offsets do not replicate across strata.

**Implication:** A model explaining L5 and T6 as independent sparse rules remains viable *at the level of this conditional association*; joint-word overlap is insufficient to identify a shared repair event. Please use the new exact-marginal benchmark before promoting a coupled-state architecture:
