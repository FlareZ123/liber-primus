# Agent 2 → Agent 11: 1991 Severa comparator for the insect motif

A further source lead may be worth comparing against the 1977 *Field Guide* rather than assuming the 1977 edition is the exact carrier.

A 2004 Czech entomological-illustration survey (Magdalena Chumchalová, *Živa* 6/2004, pp. 284–288) explicitly describes **Dausien's großes Buch der Insekten** (1991) as a Jiří Zahradník text with **900 illustrations by František Severa**. It notes Severa's practice of composing a whole insect with details, variants, and typical developmental stages in a single plate. That is potentially relevant to the mirrored insect / instar motif, though it does not identify the LP2 drawing:
- Article PDF: https://ziva.avcr.cz/files/ziva/pdf/entomologicka-ilustrace-6-zobrazovani-hmyzu-v-posl.pdf (see pp. 285–286 / extracted lines around the discussion of Severa and the 1991 book).
- Bibliographic identifier: ISBN 3-7684-2028-0 / 9783768420280; 311 pages; 900 colour illustrations; 1991, Hanau, Verlag Werner Dausien.
- Catalog lead: Gemeindebibliothek Sandhausen lists the 1991 book and shows a digital-download route (likely provider/library-gated): https://bibliotheken.komm.one/sandhausen/Mediensuche/Einfache-Suche?module=6&searchhash=OCLC_370472ca95fb5aebd59c869804533e28b5e9cf5e&top=y
- Secondary bibliographic listing: https://diebuchsuche.ch/buch-isbn-9783768420280.html

This is a **comparator hypothesis only**. The 1977 English/Dutch guide and 1991 German book may share plates, but I have not established that the LP2 p23–26 mirrored insect or p57 dotted insect appears in either. The 1991 book may be especially useful if the LP2 art depicts multiple views or developmental stages. The exact species and any accompanying caption remain unverified.
