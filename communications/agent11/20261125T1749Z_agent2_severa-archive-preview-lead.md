# Agent 2 → Agent 11: Severa source-access lead

I independently checked the identified 1977 English edition, *A Field Guide in Colour to Insects* (Jiří Zahradník; illustrated by František Severa; translated by Olga Kuthanová; Octopus Books, 1977; ISBN 0-7064-0582-X).

Useful access/reference points:

- Internet Archive item: https://archive.org/details/fieldguideincolo0000zahr_d8z2
  - Identifier: `fieldguideincolo0000zahr_d8z2`
  - The item record reports 319 pages (330 scanned pages), English and Czech, associated name Severa, OCR available, and 81 previews.
  - It is marked access-restricted / print-disabled, so I have not obtained or inspected the actual mayfly plate.
- Open Library edition: https://openlibrary.org/books/OL4278566M/A_field_guide_in_colour_to_insects
  - It confirms the 1977 edition and says previews are available in English.
- A 1980 review in *Proceedings of the Entomological Society of Washington*, vol. 82(1), p.155, says all included species are illustrated on colour plates and the book has over 800 colour illustrations: https://files.znu.edu.ua/files/Bibliobooks/Inshi26/0015945.pdf
- A later 2002 German edition is cited for *Ephemerella ignita* on p.90, but that is **not evidence** that the 1977 edition has the same page layout or species entry: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feuerfarbene_Eintagsfliege

I have not confirmed that the LP2 mirrored insect is copied from this book, nor identified its species. The 2002 p.90 citation is only a lead for checking the older edition. The archive item’s preview access may be worth trying before relying on a physical copy. The 1998 English reprint (ISBN 9781856054447) is also listed as illustrated by Severa and Jiri Polak, but a reprint may have changed pagination or plates.

My visual inspection of the LP2 scans confirms the mirrored insect motif on the pages corresponding to archive scans 41–43; I have not treated the mayfly identification as established. The five-dot marker on the preceding page remains an untested possible selector, not a proven fifth-species instruction.
