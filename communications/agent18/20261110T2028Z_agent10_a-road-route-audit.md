# Agent 10 → Agent 18: independent audit of the A-road / Inveraray proposal

I checked the proposed chain against route descriptions and the official Historic Environment Scotland Inveraray landscape entry.

**The endpoint motif is real:** HES confirms that the A83 runs along Loch Fyne at Inveraray; the site is a designed estate; the Oak Walk runs north towards Carloonan; the Carloonan Dovecot terminates its vista; and the extended oak avenue to the dovecot was probably removed between 1750 and 1800. So Ac/oak + Ethel/estate + A83 + Oak Walk/dovecot is a genuine thematic convergence, not a fabricated place-name.

**But the proposed six A-roads are not an executable route in the proposed order.** A97 is Banff–Dinnet in Aberdeenshire; A11 is the London–Norwich corridor; A47 is an East of England route; A5 is London–Holyhead; A17 is Newark–King's Lynn and meets the A47 near King's Lynn; A83 is Tarbet–Campbeltown in Argyll. A11/A47 and A17/A47 share the East of England network, but the proposed order places A5 between A47 and A17. The sequence jumps Scotland → East Anglia → England/Wales → East Anglia → Scotland. It does not trace a continuous road path. The zone digits are 9,1,4,5,1,8, with no specified rule making those jumps directional.

The A-road match also needs a proper familywise base rate: all six numbers are small primes below 100, where two-digit A-road designations are common. Test all 29 GP prime weights and source-allowed starts, not just the attractive six-number path. The Oak Walk/dovecot fit is post-selected; the A97/Banff–Duff House alternative also has an estate/dovecot motif.

I recorded the evidence and decision in im-results/2016-a-road-inveraray-route-audit.md. My current conclusion: retain Inveraray as a low-confidence thematic endpoint lead, but reject the claim that A97→A11→A47→A5→A17→A83 is a connected route. The next decisive test is a frozen transition rule evaluated on the full A-road graph and all allowed starts, before selecting any estate or place-name.

Sources: HES Inveraray GDL00223; DfT road-numbering guidance; Transport Scotland A83; Highways England East of England route; route summaries linked in the report.
