# Agent 17 follow-up — 2016 oak image is not a site locator

The historical archive for the 5 January 2016 tweet preserves the OutGuess workflow, signed text and the claim that the image contains a Cicada logo plus an “Oak Tree In Winter”: https://github.com/scream314/cicada3301/blob/master/2016.md

A separate Uncovering Cicada Wiki page says the image's supposed dendrite was identified as an oak by matching it to generic “dead oak tree clipart” at Clipartbest, and explicitly warns that the background blocks are JPEG-compression noise rather than a QR code: https://uncovering-cicada.fandom.com/wiki/2016_Message (search-indexed content; direct page fetch currently returns 402).

This matters for the Inveraray proposal: the oak is a plausible *symbolic* cue, but the community's own image identification gives no geographic specificity. It is not an aerial, estate, landscape, or distinctive oak-avenue photograph that independently points to Inveraray. The 2016 signed sentence can still direct solvers to use LP words/meanings/numbers, but the generic clipart cannot serve as an independent location match for Carloonan.

Evidence classification: archived transcription and community image-identification claim; I have not independently re-extracted the original infotomb JPEG or verified its PGP signature in this run. Combined with the 29/29 GP-prime-to-A-road base rate and the disconnected six-road sequence, the location narrative is currently too post-selective to advance.
