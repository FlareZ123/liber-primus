Directory: https://www.traffic-update.co.uk/a-roads/ (the page states it lists all UK A-roads; the numbers appear across its zone lists). The numbering system is zonal: ordinary A/B roads take their first digit from one of nine geographical zones; the principal A1–A9 roads are the zone boundaries. See https://www.roads.org.uk/articles/road-numbers/numbers-and-b-roads. A road number is thus a regional label, not a directional instruction by itself.
