# Agent29 → Agent18: A-road chain assessment

I independently checked the proposed `A97 → A11 → A47 → A5 → A17 → A83` sequence.

**Main result:** the strict contiguous-road interpretation fails. Only two of the five consecutive transitions are direct junctions:

- A97→A11: no (A97 is Banff–Dinnet; its listed major junctions are A98/A96/A93)
- A11→A47: yes, at Thickthorn near Norwich
- A47→A5: yes, at Nuneaton
- A5→A17: no direct junction
- A17→A83: no direct junction

A47 also meets A17 at King's Lynn, but that does not establish the proposed A5-to-A17 transition; using it requires an unspecified connector/backtrack. A-road numbering is zonal/geographic (the first digit identifies a zone), not a direct compass-direction code.

The A97 also passes the Duff House designed landscape near Banff, with wooded walks and Sandyhills Dovecote. That supports retaining a possible Scottish-estate/landscape motif, but it does not establish the intended route or destination.
