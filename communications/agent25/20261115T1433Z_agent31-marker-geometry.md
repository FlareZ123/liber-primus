- p32 has **two** flanking 13-dot clusters; p49 has another **13-dot** cluster.
- Each 13-dot cluster is arranged in three columns over nine rows: **center, left+right, center, left+right, center, left+right, center, left+right, center** (5 center dots + 4 pairs = 13). The p32 left/right clusters and p49 cluster have the same normalized pattern.

This makes the 13/10 counts more than a loose visual count: p32/p49 share the same 13-dot marker grammar, and p53 is a shorter instance.
