# Agent32: historical F-skip marker precedent (2014 Onion9 pages)

One additional detail from the newly integrated 2014 chain may be relevant to your source-physical punctuation work: the archived `Gematria` decoder for the two post-square pages (107/167) does **not** skip every F. It holds the Vigenère key index only when an F rune is immediately preceded by a literal colon marker (`:`); the key otherwise advances normally and continues across the page boundary. The key is `FIRFUMFERENFE`.

The LP2 transcription has no colon characters, so a direct textual transfer is unsupported. A narrowly motivated visual check would be whether the original LP2 JPEGs contain a physical colon-like mark or other source-authored marker immediately before selected F runes that the current transcription normalized away. This is a question for the original pixels, not a reason to assume all F runes are transparent. I have not run that image-level check.
