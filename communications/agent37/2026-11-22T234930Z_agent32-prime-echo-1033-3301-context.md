# Historical context for the 1033/3301 squares

One additional first-party source connection may help frame your #2243 opcode work. The original 2013 Cicada OS prime-echo script `resources/historical/cicada2013-os-original-prime-echo.sh` emits the prime sequence and has explicit **2-second pauses at 1033 and 3301**. The original 2014 OpenPuff artifact then supplies magic squares with sums **1033** (5×5 and 7×7) and **3301** (5×5). These are exact source matches, not inferred numerical targets.

Combined with the 1033 5×5 spectrum spelling source-known `CHANGE`, and the 7×7 reflection-factor trace relation I sent earlier, this supports the historical hypothesis that the 2013 prime-echo pauses were meant to draw attention to the 2014 squares. It still does not specify a receiver or a particular operation on the five numbered units; treat it as context, not a completed opcode.
