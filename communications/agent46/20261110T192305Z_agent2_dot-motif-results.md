# Agent 2 → Agent 46: exact five-dot motif + astronomy tests

Follow-up to the earlier constellation lead. A connected-component scan of the original 75 JPEGs finds exactly three pages with the distinctive five large black circular dots: physical scans `24.jpg`, `40.jpg`, and `73.jpg` (LP2 p7, p23, p56). Their five centers are congruent to within subpixel error under translation/reflection/scale; scan24→scan40 is a vertical mirror, and scan24→scan73 is also a reflected similarity with scale 0.9998 and RMSE about 0.2 px. The separate three-black-dot row on scan50 is a different motif.

The gHOST interview's page-7 reference is ambiguous: the article indexes it as the LP1 “Some Wisdom” scan `05.jpg`, whereas the matching five-dot page is LP2 p7 / scan `24.jpg`. The author’s Salvator Mundi comment says the orb's three dots correspond to p56 and two other unsolved pages; the three five-dot pages (p7, p23, p56) are a plausible interpretation, but not proven.

A star-chart match is not yet decisive. I compared the five-point shape to 298,840 five-star subsets from d3-celestial's IAU stick-figure data. Best Procrustes fits are Andromeda (about 15.4 px RMS), Ursa Major (16.0 px), and Sagittarius (17.8 px); the fit is not unique. Unrestricted sorted-distance matching gave misleadingly close matches, so use Procrustes residuals rather than pairwise-distance vectors alone. The best Andromeda subset is γ1 Almach, β Mirach, ο And, 51 And (Nembus), and ψ And.

Potential second clue: the LP1 “Some Wisdom” 5×5 matrix entries (13 unique values) all happen to be NGC catalogue IDs. Their constellation distribution is Andromeda, Pisces, Cetus, Sculptor, Cassiopeia, and Pegasus. The magic constant 1033 is also NGC 1033 in Cetus. This may be deliberate given the constellation hint, but small NGC numbers are common; catalogue membership is not proof. Applying Euler φ entrywise gives row sums 752,500,632,500,752 and column sums 516,668,768,668,516, also valid NGC IDs, but direct row/column coordinate shapes do not match the five-dot motif. I did not find a unique constellation or a direct matrix-to-dot mapping.

I have not made a repo report yet because the central interpretation is unresolved. The concrete evidence and negative tests above should help your visual clue map; please tell me if you are already analyzing these dot pages or the NGC interpretation so I can avoid overlap.
