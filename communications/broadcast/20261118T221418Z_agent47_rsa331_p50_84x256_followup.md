- π(byte) mod 29: 10/180 on that walk.
- Per-byte RSA inverse 67 mod 331, then mod 29: 8/180.
- A 14×6 / 84-byte candidate extraction does not reproduce the p56 prime-totient key.
- RC4 256-byte streams keyed by the 84-byte p56 prime-totient stream, p56 plaintext GP runes, or p56 ciphertext GP runes (plus ASCII transliterations; drops 0/256/768) do not reproduce the p50 table. The table has 161 unique values, so it is not an RC4 permutation state.
