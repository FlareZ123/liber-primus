# Agent 13: January–March 2015 Tor crawl as a preimage-recovery source

**Classification: unqueried original-era archival *host/link-index and normalized-text* candidate. No page, key, hash preimage, or plaintext recovered.** This is a separate and earlier research corpus from the May–June 2016 EURECOM crawl and the May–July 2016 DUTA material identified by Agent 1.

Daniel Moore and Thomas Rid's *Cryptopolitik and the Darknet* (Survival 58(1), 2016, DOI [10.1080/00396338.2016.1142085](https://doi.org/10.1080/00396338.2016.1142085)) describes a **January–March 2015** Tor hidden-service website crawl. Its initial address list combined **5,615 distinct .onion URLs** from Ahmia and onion.city. The crawler followed hidden-service links to **five hops** and up to **100 pages per site**, extracting textual content rather than media. The article reports **5,205 responding websites**. These collection dates precede the 2016 academic crawls and follow the 2014 LP2 release by less than a year, which makes the dataset a potentially higher-value historical witness if usable files survive.

Primary open article: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00396338.2016.1142085 ; author research page: https://ridt.co/cryptopolitik/ . The authors' 2016 Tor-hosted technical annex is itself an historical endpoint, not a currently authenticated source of the 2015 corpus. No public raw-HTML download, surviving index, capture of a Cicada domain, or permission for access has been located in this invocation. The independently recovered [original 2 February 2016 technical annex](https://ridt.co/d/moore-rid-technical-annex.pdf) resolves the storage constraint. The crawler stripped HTML, invisible text, JavaScript and media, translated non-English content, then **hashed and stored normalized text in MongoDB**. Binary files including `.bin` were excluded outright. Accordingly **the available described corpus cannot support a byte-exact p56 hash-preimage gate**; its remaining plausible use is a 2015 original-era .onion URL/link-index match or a source-specific textual hint. Original raw HTTP response preservation has no support in this methodology. The public PDF is a contemporaneous capture of the original onion technical annex.
