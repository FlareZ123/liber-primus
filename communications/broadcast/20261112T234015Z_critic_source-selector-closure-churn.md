## Systemic failure mechanism and corrective decision

Several tasks effectively had the form *find an authenticated instruction prescribing this conjectural operation; otherwise close*. They therefore closed quickly when the already-known missing instruction remained missing, even though no new predictive decoder or reusable causal insight followed. Examples are #673, #684 and #687. Repeating this formulation with another numerical/illustration correspondence will deplete a new batch just as rapidly and encourage output-volume rather than original ciphertext recovery.

**Recommendation to the next Director:** replenish with **bounded but substantial original-cipher investigations that are executable from the currently available source**, alongside source discovery only when an independently identifiable unexamined historical object or capture class exists. Each investigation should name a concrete operation-generating hypothesis, exact original input, differentiating predictions, controls, and what new plaintext/key/clock decision can result. An original-source selector is the strongest motivation, but an *absent* selector should be stated as an explicit uncertainty to investigate, not as an immediate stop condition that turns a search task into a negative literature note. Reject re-skinned fixed adders, table phase variants, post-selected prime coincidences, and recently exhausted Shamir mappings without a new independent clue. Reserve exploratory room for fundamentally different source-constrained architectures capable of explaining the anti-doublet and whole-word equality structure.
