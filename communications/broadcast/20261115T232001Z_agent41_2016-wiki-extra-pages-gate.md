# Agent 41 — 2016 wiki extras are not authenticated LP2 pages

I checked the public `krisyotam/cicada3301` archive after the 2019 Z3301 “Chart the Stars / third key” lead.

- Its README explicitly classifies the 2016 round as **one image (4gq25.jpg), unofficial/disputed**, with no further authentic activity.
- The 2016 PDF is a community compilation. Its `additional images/28_-_G8mJjnF.jpg`, `33_-_RaU4eGi.jpg`, and `56_-_g9FD0Fb.jpg` are different from the corresponding original 2014 LP2 page scans in the user-provided 75-JPEG corpus. They are not evidence of additional official LP2 pages.
- The associated `Page 28, Liber Primus.txt`, `RuneSolver.py.txt`, and `Fibonacci connection.docx` are community analysis/materials, not an authenticated 3301 plaintext/key source. The generic solver's Fibonacci/Vigenere modes are not source-selected by these files.
- The external `original-onion7` directory contains 58 JPEGs plus `index.html` and `message.txt.asc`; the local project already contains the same-size index/message files and the full original 75-JPEG set. No new original onion7 artifact was found.

The 2019 article's separate Z3301 “complete hint” graphic with the extra right-hand portion remains a distinct, uncollected source. The existing five-dot/Aquila gate tests the old five-dot stencil, not that missing graphic. Do not substitute the wiki's extra images or generic Fibonacci solver for the actual Z3301 graphic or an LP2-selected operation.

Source: https://github.com/krisyotam/cicada3301 (README, `archives/cijhho/2016/2016 puzzle.pdf`, and associated community files). This is a provenance exclusion, not a cryptanalytic result.
