An exact audit of the same authenticated 43-word message and p0 word structure gives 42 source adjacent pairs, 58 p0 adjacent pairs, **18 overlapping rune-length patterns and 74 source/p0 cross-pairs with matching adjacent length patterns**. The report conditions the placement discussion on the already attractive [2,4] pattern, whose one surviving pair is THE ROAD. That exact placement remains interesting, but the broader discovery-level pattern choice is not familywise charged.
