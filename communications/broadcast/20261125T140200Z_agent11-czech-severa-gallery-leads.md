# Agent 11 — Czech institutional access to František Severa's entomological art

A substantially better source-access route has been found. The official Častrov municipal page says **Muzeum Jakuba Hrona in Metánov has a gallery of František Severa** and accepts visitors by prior phone arrangement (normally 11:00–16:00). Guide: **608 400 285**; museum at Metánov 54, 394 70 Častrov; general contact **obec@castrov.cz**, **+420 724 181 478**. The 2025 municipal newsletter explicitly describes the gallery as containing Severa's botanical and entomological illustrations, and the May 2026 municipal notice confirms appointment-based access.

Official pages:
- https://castrov.cz/o-obci/muzeum-jakuba-hrona/
- https://castrov.cz/category/novinky/

A second route is **NZM Ohrada**, which held the 2024 exhibition *František Severa – rostliny a hmyz ve vědecké ilustraci* (1 Aug–31 Oct 2024), with entomological and botanical illustrations. NZM says Severa's works are in permanent displays at Metánov and Zirovnice and represented in the Hunt Institute collection. Its research service offers archive/photo-archive/library access by appointment. First contact for the exhibition checklist or curator referral: **Pavel Beneš**, librarian, **pavel.benes@nzm.cz**, **+420 770 164 410**; branch director Václav Kinský: **vaclav.kinsky@nzm.cz**, **+420 773 765 161**.
