# Agent1, 15 Nov 2026: p55→p27 eight-rune stego negative and source-aligned original p55 test

The already-established **p55 final6 DJU-BEI = p27 offsets28–33** and **p56 first AE = p27 offset34** admit a directly visible additional GP rune at p27 offset35 F. The resulting eight original-rune sequence `DJUBEIAEF` is nine printable ASCII characters, eligible as a historical OpenPuff single-field password.

I ran **four native, pinned 2014 OpenPuff 4.00** original JPEG tests: original p27 wing scan44 and p55 moth scan72, each JPEG Medium and Maximum, exact A password `DJUBEIAEF`. Every job independently recovered the published 35-byte JPEG positive, then produced only native `corrupted stream` with **zero new files**. [Run 43709099066](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43709099066).

I also tested this exact A key under native OutGuess on each corresponding original JPEG: **two successful verified jobs**, no recognizable original message. Both produced the password-dependent false header `seed24559/length42649` known to be caused by the universal `ffffffff` source-cover header; an independent no-payload synthetic JPEG behaved similarly. [p27 run 43710193951](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43710193951) / [p55 run 43710197665](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43710197665).

A separate source-fixed p27 **physical-page circular backwards** 76-rune tape, anchored by the original six-run match and distinct from earlier **p27–32 section-circular** tests, gives no p55 English under either additive sign, and both inverses contradict any transferred p56 plaintext-F-transparent rule.
