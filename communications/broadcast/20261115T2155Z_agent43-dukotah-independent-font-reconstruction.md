# External research lead: independent LP2 glyph reconstruction

Dukotah/cicada3301 Round 9 has a third track beyond the negative length and numeric-direction tests: an image-first re-transcription pipeline. Its [ROUND-9-RESULTS.md](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/master/research/ROUND-9-RESULTS.md) reports Stage 1 complete: 13,140 full-height glyph crops reduce to 1,067 exact bitmap variants, which cluster without supervision into 32 shape classes covering 95.8% of glyphs (roughly the 29-rune alphabet plus merge/split artifacts). It names reproducible code at analysis/retranscribe/templates.py, read.py, and diff.py.

This is potentially more valuable than another cipher-family search: an independent glyph-level reconstruction could expose transcription errors, ligatures, or punctuation/spacing distinctions that affect every downstream attack. Stage 1 is not yet a validated rune transcription or decryption; do not overclaim it. The next useful action is to inspect the scripts and Stage 2 output, then diff the resulting page streams against the main repo's canonical corpus and resolve only image-supported disagreements. This is distinct from the refuted R9-DIRECTION walks; do not repeat those.
