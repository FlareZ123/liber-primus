## Assessment

The current research record is scientifically better calibrated than a raw count of experiments would suggest. `results/README.md` and `workplace/integrated-original-inversion-challenge.md` consistently distinguish source facts, finite conditional exclusions, synthetic positive controls, and actual plaintext/key recovery. They explicitly state that no unknown LP2 plaintext, authenticated unknown-page key, complete inverse, or continuation destination has been recovered. I found no current contradiction in the authoritative top-level status that warrants an immediate correction.

Recent closures generally respected their stop gates:

- **#748 (F5 detector):** the new Q92 detector failed its preregistered held-out detection threshold (1/50 L, 0/50 RGB); the 26 unscored LP2 originals were explicitly withheld. Correctly does not claim F5 is absent or justify screening/tuning those originals.
- **#754 (p57 GIMP opacity):** the exact SECRA 31-parameter master was unavailable. The surrogate was explicitly labelled non-equivalent and not a payload; closing the exact reconstruction gate as blocked is appropriate.
- **#752 (native orthography):** the ING/IO spelling correction was applied to the controls, prior overbroad capacity exclusions were narrowed, and the remaining finite deterministic-input contradiction was separately tested. The synthesis was updated.
- **#753 (F-clock):** the post-closure word-boundary lexical Viterbi recovered all 710 known-key control runes across three independently keyed solved texts, including the authentic F bypasses. This is a useful reusable known-key inverse, not unknown-key recovery. The related ciphertext-F count error was correctly retracted in the main synthesis.
- **#764 (Archive Team):** enumerated item metadata and direct index-byte requests establish access denial (401/403), not absence of a target capture. The closure preserves that distinction.
- **#756 (830-image wiki corpus):** the accessible 829-item snapshot was assessed, but the purported October 830th row remains unresolved. The report correctly limits its conclusion to the accessible 829-item corpus.

These examples indicate that bounded negative decisions and corrections are being preserved rather than inflated into solutions. Continue to avoid promoting a synthetic plant, a key-blind round trip, a source resemblance, or a postselected numerical relation into plaintext evidence.
