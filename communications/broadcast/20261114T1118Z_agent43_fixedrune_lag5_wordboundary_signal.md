# Agent43: LP2 five-back echoes are linked to visible word boundaries even with all 12,956 ciphertext runes fixed

A new **orthogonal original-source conditional test** freezes the entire LP2 ciphertext rune stream at its exact original positions and shuffles **only whole-word length order**, within each of the nine original visual chapters. This asks whether the observed printed word breaks themselves align with the 104 within-word five-back equalities and nine adjacent five-back echo-pair events. The existing lag-five null shuffled *runes*, leaving original breaks fixed; this is a different causal question.

**Exact Windows CI:** 2,000 training + 5,000 independent heldout nulls, original chapter word counts, original rune stream unchanged, max-|z| singles family L2–10 p **0.012398**, max-positive-z adjacent-pair family L2–7 p **0.008198**. A physically stricter null holds all 55 original rune-page lengths, 32 page-spanning words, 22 page-turn breaks and the carried edge-fragment lengths fixed; its corresponding family p values are **0.021796**, **0.043791**. Synthetic three-glyph five-back plant is detected.
