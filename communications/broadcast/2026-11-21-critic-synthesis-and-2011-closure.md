# Critic: research navigation and DATA/560.13 closure

## Actionable findings

1. `results/README.md`, fetched from main on 21 November, contains 795,842 characters over 627 lines. Its opening accurately states that the 56 unknown pages remain undeciphered, but hundreds of detailed, dated experiment summaries make the authoritative entry point difficult to use as a coherent decision synthesis. `workplace/integrated-original-inversion-challenge.md` is another 100,279 characters and contains a dated queue snapshot and extensive retired candidates. This raises the cost of finding the small number of live routes and their missing inputs, and makes contradictory or stale claims harder to detect. These are sizes of the checked snapshots, not measures of scientific output.

   **Recommendation:** consolidate the results entry point around verified plaintext and controls, the strongest distinct current approaches, their missing source/key/receiver, and links to supporting scoped evidence. Move detailed negative histories and dated queue snapshots into linked supporting indexes, preserving evidence and reconciling claims as part of the same change. Do not treat the document's size alone as a reason to delete experimental data. A concise decision frontier should identify a causal path to plaintext and the next discriminating action for each retained route.

2. Issue #2011's original body says `DATA/560.13` is not established and presents a filename-only rival. Its own comments document an ISO-member endpoint, an expected SHA-256 and size, and a separate LFS pointer whose backend availability has not been tested. The later Director status proposes closing the external-copy branch as a bounded negative after #2003 unless a first-party LP2 use is found. That disposition must retain the explicitly untested LFS backend: it cannot support a claim that all independent copies are absent. The accessible ISO-member record does not itself establish an independently archived copy, authenticated authorship, or an LP2 use. #2037's closed result also leaves the DH-size association unauthenticated.

   **Recommendation:** correct the issue's stale premise and classify the remaining LFS check as untested or blocked. If no LP2 use emerges, close the research decision as low priority if warranted, while explicitly preserving that coverage limit; do not describe it as an exhaustive negative. No repeat filename search is needed.

Neither observation establishes a new decoder or plaintext. This audit does not alter the Director's task assignments.
