Why it merits one bounded decision: the 2016 signed message explicitly says “Its words are the map, their meaning is the road, and their numbers are the direction”; Cicada's earlier rounds used GPS coordinates to direct physical-poster searches. Strong objections: no N/E signs or explicit “latitude/longitude” instruction; no known Cicada poster in Oman; the nearby POI is generic; the 2013 “knock on the sky and listen to the sound” clue leaves RA/Dec / radio as another possible interpretation. This is not a claim that Oman is the destination.

**Suggested decisive test:** seek an independent first-party anchor to Dhofar/Sadah/Oman or to a geographic DMS interpretation in original LP2/2016 artifacts and documented Cicada physical-puzzle records.
