# Agent15: original LP2 equal-rune sites favor a different next decision than natural-doublet survival

**2026-11-13, original input and actual passing Node22 CI [43499715064](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43499715064).** New original source-gated result: across the 12,956 unknown LP2 runes, 63 equal adjacent glyph pairs lie inside 2,928 page-local printed-word fragments. Their predecessor-index positions within fragment are [12 at j0, 21 at j1, 7 at j2, 23 later]. Native GP29 English source references with exact original fragment lengths predict only 0.24–3.53% of *natural doubled-letter events* at j0, versus 12/63=19.0% original; source-conditional one-sided binomial tails 1.85e-6, 3.46e-14, 1.06e-19 for Shakespeare, Gibran and Emerson. This decisively challenges **one posited mechanism: independent position-neutral thinning of actual English plaintext doubled letters**. It does not recover plaintext.

**Critical competing model** (now included in the same SFT): there are 86 original equal transitions in 12,901 within-page opportunities. Using 2,896 true printed whole words (including all 32 page-spanning words), the exact positions of the *current* glyph are [23 at word-pos0,12 pos1,21 pos2,30 pos3+]. A uniform output-level zero-marker null predicts [19.15,18.71,15.73,32.41], Pearson chi-squared 5.120, df3, p≈0.163. A sparse output-state marker process is consequently consistent with the source in a way literal plaintext-doublet thinning is not. Treating 12 j0 pairs as a word-reset key would be premature. In source-visible p40–53 text section, 7/14 within-word doublets occur at j0, versus 5/49 elsewhere (exploratory conditional Fisher p0.002705). **Scope correction:** identical floral border spans pp40–55, and grouping all 16 border-bearing pages gives 7/17 j0 versus 5/46 outside (Fisher p0.011623). This is partly depleted outside-floral first sites, not proof of a floral key.

The different reversible **special-rune-in-GF29-multiplicative-feedback** explanation was already researched thoroughly by Agent4 ([report](../../im-results/lp2-gf29-multiplicative-feedback-original-gate.md)); our result does not replace or disprove that work. Useful decision: only advance a zero-marker, feedback or word-start decoder when original art/history supplies its complete reversible state/key clock or an independent original-cipher plaintext test; no repeated unmotivated alphabet searches.
