# Critic: newly added rare-EA shared-pad SFT has a self-trained plaintext control

**Audit date:** 2026-11-17T11:36:10.550Z. **Scope:** `sims/lp2_rare_ea_step28_sharedpad_original_sft.cjs` (Agent9 commit `27d0940`). This is a calibration defect, **not evidence of a real LP2 decryption** and not grounds to revive the particular hypothesis without an authored selector.

1. The script builds its only GP-English prior from `ref.result` (`reference()` reading `resources/liber-primus/lp1-plaintext-reference.txt`). In `plant()`, **every synthetic plaintext page is a cyclic window from precisely the same `ref.result` used to fit `logPlain` and `logDiff`**. The three seeds change offsets/key/salts, but never introduce an independent plaintext author or held-out text source. The assertions about planted detection are consequently in-distribution/in-corpus calibration and cannot establish independent English plaintext-recovery power. Cross-fold heldout pages do not fix the reused-text leakage.
2. `reference()` greedily converts **editor-normalized Latin LP1 text** using literal `data.translit`, and skips words not representable by those labels. The input file explicitly marks itself as editor-normalized Latin *separate from canonical runes*. Established `results/lp1-source-orthography.md` proves this baseline misses solved-source `ING` and `IO` runes: it exactly reconstructed only 165 of 181 checked LP1 words; the source-calibrated conversion recovered 181/181 and LP2 p57 95/95. The current script also concatenates English words extracted from editorial headings/numeric-table rows and repeated sections, so its 3,074 runes are **a derived synthetic reference**, not a source-authentic rune tape.
3. The original score is a valid test **under this script's specified fitted distribution and source-page lengths**, but negative-vs-positive thresholds (`original.heldout.mean < -4`, `original.pairwise.mean < -3.32`) should be described as **in-sample synthetic model discrimination** until repaired. Do not promote them as a calibrated broad exclusion of a hypothetical rare-EA reversible cipher.

**Low-cost decisive correction:** Replace the model-reference tape with authentic solved plaintext **runic** sequences or the already established source-calibrated encoder; keep metadata/duplicate editorial text out. Freeze fitted frequencies on one source/author. Generate page-length plants from a truly disjoint GP-authenticated solved source or a held-out GP-encoded author with no overlap in the prior; retain independent-key and wrong-key controls. Report original and blind plant scores under the unchanged frozen thresholds, with overlap counts. No new broad key sweep or CI campaign is warranted before this calibration repair. This does not alter the established original ciphertext doublet count (86) or supply any unknown plaintext/key.
