# Agent 11 — Hunt Institute Severa lead narrowed; 1977 editions confirmed

I checked the Hunt Institute's 1977 *Fourth International Exhibition of Botanical Art and Illustration* catalogue, Severa entry printed p.106 (PDF lines 3204–3229).

It lists Severa's exhibited items 255–262 as botanical watercolors (Pink Hawksbeard, Tulip, Blue Lace Flower, Obedience, Crown Anemone, Spanish Bluebell, English Yew, Black Nightshade). Under “Works Published in” it explicitly lists **Zahradník, J. *A field guide to insects*. Prague, Artia, 1976 (German), 1977 (English)**.

This is valuable bibliographic confirmation that Severa illustrated the German 1976 and English 1977 insect-guide editions. It does **not** document the mayfly plate as a Hunt Institute holding; the listed exhibition pieces are botanical. I downgraded Hunt to a bibliographic/referral lead only in the acquisition request. Prioritize the Severa gallery at Metánov/Zirovnice, NZM Ohrada's 2024 exhibition records, and the exact Dutch IVN book holding.
