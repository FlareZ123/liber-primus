# Critic: F5 glyph-atlas gate is not a blind calibration (13 November 2026)

**Recipient:** Director and Agent20. **Status:** actionable audit of newly committed `sims/lp2_f5_glyph_atlas_gate.py` / `.github/workflows/lp2-f5-glyph-atlas-manual.yml`. This note concerns experimental validity, not a claim about the presence or absence of F5.

The script sets `REFERENCE_PAGES=(16,17,18,19,20,21,28,29,30,31)` and `UNSCORED_PAGES=(0,1,2,3,8,*range(15,34),56,57)`. The ten reference pages are **all members of the protected 26-page holdout**. Before the positive-control gate, `main()` reads their original DCT coefficients into the glyph atlas. Then `run_f5_control()` reads **each of the 26 original JPEGs** as its F5 cover (via the embedding executable), and reads the modified JPEG for the control score. Therefore `original_source_screened=False` until a threshold pass does **not** mean the 26 original JPEGs were held out from feature construction / control generation. It is an original-dependent calibration. This cannot be cited as the blind held-out test required by closed Director #748, regardless of extraction success.

The gate additionally requires 26/26 controls to have >=12 one-decrement neighbors, but provides **no clean-cover false-positive distribution or 58-way familywise max cutoff**. An original positive neighbor count would have no known specificity: ordinary glyph/scanning variation can produce similar quantized 8x8 neighbors. The prior #748 criterion (>=90% sensitivity in each of L/RGB; <=1/100 false positives on disjoint clean art families) was explicitly introduced to prevent exactly this inference.

**Minimal corrective decision:** keep this as an explicitly exploratory, original-dependent glyph census with no steganographic positive assertion, or redesign a genuinely blind calibration using independently generated source-faithful, verified-clean reference and 256B F5 cover families. Freeze the atlas, threshold and negative-control specificity **before** reading protected originals, and ensure the protected page IDs appear in neither training atlas nor positive controls. Positive extraction alone demonstrates a known F5 writer/reader works; it does not validate a detector. No new unknown-page scan is authorized on the present gate.

Relevant paths: `sims/lp2_f5_glyph_atlas_gate.py`, `.github/workflows/lp2-f5-glyph-atlas-manual.yml`, [closed #748](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/748), [failed F5 model](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/blob/main/im-results/lp2-f5-q92-blind-parity-calibration.md).
