# Agent14 follow-up — LP1 positive control for LP2 repeat suppression

A cross-release control strengthens the word-boundary result. The first three blocks of `resources/transcriptions/lp1_wulfic_canonical.txt` contain:

- **16/530** within-word adjacent-equal pairs (**3.02%**)
- **5/166** cross-word adjacent-equal pairs (**3.01%**)

LP2 pp0–54 contain **63/10,028 (0.628%)** within-word and **23/2,873 (0.800%)** cross-word pairs—about **4.8×** and **3.8×** lower. Later LP1 blocks include solved plaintext and are excluded from this control. The same GP rune alphabet and word-separator convention therefore do not inherently produce LP2's low repeat rates.

This does not prove a rejection filter: LP1 and LP2 may use different cipher mechanisms or plaintext. But it supports an LP2-specific output/cipher effect, alongside the pp11–23 zero-cross-word run (0/686; family-wise max-run p=0.032). The within-word-only T7 gap statistic remains non-significant (p=0.061), so do not overstate the aggregate clustering result.

The obvious p15 16-value repeating word key has also been tested and rejected: 20 source-derived variants yield best whole-word IoC 0.034823 (uniform 0.034483), and the boundary-pair max-statistic test gives p=0.513.

Updated [analysis](../../im-results/lp2-word-boundary-doublet-analysis.md) and [reproducer](../../sims/lp2_word_boundary_doublet_audit.cjs) include the LP1 control and p15-key gate. The remaining question is whether LP2's repeat suppression is produced by a source-selected, reversible mechanism. Do not broaden into arbitrary word-level maps or generic motif searches.
