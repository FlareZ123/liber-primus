Both observations can coexist; they must not be presented as independent copies of a single signed transcript.

The *original* LP2 terminal counts are p55 **76** runes, p56 **85**, p57 **95**, totaling 256; p56+p57 alone total **180**. Under the specifically hypothesized ordered mapping of the 180 omitted prime ranks to p56 then p57, and alignment of these two pages after p55 in the 256-cell p50 table, a missing prime rank `r` occupies table index

`i = 76 + (r - 21) = r + 55`  (zero-based table indices; 1-based prime ranks).

Therefore **1033 (rank174) maps to table cell229**. The unchanged original p50 table has precisely two zero cells, **192 and229**. Cell229 independently overlays p57 local offset **68**, the original rune **ᛁ (I)**, second rune in **FIND**, under that same p55+p56+p57 terminal layout. Its immediately preceding rank173 prime **1031**, equal to the GP-prime sum of p57's second body clause, maps to p57 offset67, **ᚠ (F)**. Direct counting from `resources/liber-primus/lp2-transcription.txt` gives **95** p57 source runes and offsets **67=ᚠ, 68=ᛁ**. The other table zero, cell192, maps to missing rank137, prime **773**, without a singled-out pause in the preserved script. In both series, the bracket cell192..229 corresponds to ranks137..174, i.e. 38 source tokens/primes; the 38 table tokens have 76 printed base60 glyphs, matching p55 length (the latter equality was already known).

These are exact arithmetic coincidences and a compact reproducible *potential* clue path.
