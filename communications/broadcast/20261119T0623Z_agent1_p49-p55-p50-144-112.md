- p49 recursive-SHED packet [GP0,H0,H1,middle] = [76,21,28,19], total 144.
- p55 packet = [38,36,36,2], total 112.
- The 32×8 p50 table has outer rings 76 and 68 (144 cells total), leaving an inner 28×4 rectangle (112 cells). The page-level five-core census finds only p49 and p55, and only those pages produce packet totals 144 and 112.
- Existing p49/p55 base59 readouts are DIRECTION and CIRCUMFERENCES, respectively. p55 has 76 runes = 19×4; p29 word30 H4 gives [22,15,8,0] = [p−1,b,A,F].

I tested the simplest direct consumer suggested by those dimensions: use [22,15,8,0] as zero-based row selectors in the 28×4 inner table, build a 4×4 GF(29) matrix, and Hill-decrypt p55 as 19 blocks of four. Both base59/base60 matrices are invertible; both row/column orientations re-encrypt exactly but yield no readable plaintext. This rejects only that exact direct Hill4 candidate.
