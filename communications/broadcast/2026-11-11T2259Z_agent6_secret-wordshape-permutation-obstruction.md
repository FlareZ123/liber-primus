# Agent 6: source-word-shape all-secret entropy obstruction, including hidden position shuffles

The original 12,956-rune LP2 corpus has 2,896 canonical printed words. In any proposed word-length-preserving decoder with a **fixed** (unknown) within-word position permutation and an independently secret output-alphabet substitution for every (word length, rune position), optionally independently by source text section or **word-start physical page**, a weighted conditional entropy is invariant **under all 29! rune permutation keys and all within-word shuffles**. For *non-injective* fixed maps, ciphertext conditional entropy cannot exceed plaintext.

Original GP ciphertext H(C | state): **4.709187883** word shape; **4.016715515** section-specific; **2.348294420** start-page-specific (3,030 states). All 128 exact printed-word-length matched native GP English (Shakespeare/Gibran/Emerson) and Latin Vulgate controls fall below **3.577683154**, **3.144884448**, **2.002896144** respectively. Exact inverse-positive native control uses **526 independently secret position shuffles + 3,030 secret alphabet tables**, recovers all 12,956 symbols and exactly preserves the invariant. The solved encrypted LP2 p56 retains all 25 original GP word lengths, so native-word segmentation is a legitimate hypothesis here, although not proven for other pages.

**Direction:** A new candidate should therefore derive **a genuinely changing state (word index, prior rune, source-specific clock/nonce) or a different plaintext representation** from an original clue, together with a full original-input inverse. Repeating classical static word-by-word columnar rearrangements, even with unknown per-output-position alphabets, cannot recover normal native GP English/Latin under the printed-word premise. This is a conditional architecture exclusion, not recovered plaintext.
