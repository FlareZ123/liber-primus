# Agent20: two original-source constraints that change model selection (17 Nov 2026)

**Original-JPEG image carriers:** Quantized Y-DCT coefficients in the shared floral side bands of original LP2 pp40–54 (\`57.jpg\`–\`71.jpg\`) are **exactly equal at all 4,320,000 positions per page**, including 67,500 full 8×8 blocks per image. Those bands contain 185,779 eligible |AC|≥2 coefficients, which are 30.68–47.73% of the per-page eligible luma pool. An independent post-template, image-wide coefficient-changing stego scheme must explain why all those coefficients remained identical on 15 distinct finished pages. This is **not** a negative against a common pre-rendered payload, central-field-only or chroma stego, other channels or keyed local placement. Reproducer and exact scopes: [C11 original JPEG SFT](../../sims/lp2_floral_border_dct_sft.c) and [source report](../../im-results/lp2-floral-border-original-jpeg-dct-stegocover-gate.md). Do not repeat undirected JPEG password scans on these same images without a source-selected key/receiver.

**Original low-doublet cipher:** The unmodified 12,956 unknown LP2 runes include **86** adjacent equals, of which **23 begin** a printed word and **19 end** a printed word (zero in a singleton word). A *lossless* GP29 anti-repeat coder can put one fixed plaintext escape rune at every equal ciphertext position, but the original \`[86,23,19]\` site triple does **not** match any of 29 GP native-English rune occurrence profiles under two independent 700-sample original-word-shape controls. After fitting the **best** single escape, Shakespeare's largest standardized discrepancy is 8.57 and Gibran's is 7.76. Conversely, a 10,000-trial null that keeps original per-page repeat counts and places residual repeats uniformly among page-internal candidate sites is **compatible** with the observed word positions (joint descriptive tail 0.6161). This favors investigating source-licensed ciphertext/key-state repeat suppression over a speculative *single fixed plaintext rune* escape, but it does **not** authenticate H2, a key, unknown-page plaintext, or any specific filter.
