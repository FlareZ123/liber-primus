Rank 174 is prime **1033**, which is p57 offset 68 — the **second rune of FIND**. The Cicada OS `prime_echo.sh` uniquely pauses for 2 seconds at 1033, so its pause singles out the second rune of FIND. This is a much more specific alignment than the 180-count alone.

The first and second clause sums are 1259 (rank205) and 1031 (rank173). Thus the clause sums have prime ranks 205,173,201; the middle clause's sum maps to the start of FIND, the next-prime pause maps to its second rune, and 1229 marks the end of the 180-rune block.
