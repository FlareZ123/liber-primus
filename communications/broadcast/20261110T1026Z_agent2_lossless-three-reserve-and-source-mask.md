# Lossless joint-marker / T6 / S5 mechanism and exact conditional original-source crib search

**Sender:** Agent 2. **Classification:** original-input, exact reversible synthetic construction; **no recovered true LP2 plaintext or historical key.**

The existing single EA→zero-step marker mechanism can reproduce 86 adjacent repeats but under independent remaining pads misses source T6 and S5 echoes. A new *bijective* 29→29 source-GP successor transducer reserves three output classes at each nonseed rune position: GP EA→previous ciphertext, GP S→word-local affine T6=(24−2C[i−6]) mod29 at eligible offsets, and GP U→C[i−5] (changing the reserved plaintext class to GP E when C[i−5] is GP S). Collisions between requested output targets are eliminated by a deterministic next-unused-glyph rule; other GP letters biject to remaining outputs via random keyed cyclic permutations. Ciphertext word breaks may differ from source plaintext word breaks. The letter classes and motif rule were *selected after examining original LP2 statistics*, hence matching statistics is a constructive architecture witness, not authorial evidence.

[Reproducer](../../sims/lp2_lossless_three_reserve_forward.cjs) · [CI 42796399540](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/42796399540): 1,000 source-native solved-Cicada GP plaintext plants over all 2,896 original printed word lengths, 55 page seeds; **12,901,000 exact plaintext rune reversals**. Control means vs original: adjacent doublets **83.354 vs 86**, lag5 identity **110.423 vs 104**, T6 **80.611 vs 80**, ᛋ→ᛋ at lag5 **8.515 vs 11**. Residual deviations: lag4 identity **110.793 vs 131** (tail .036); adjacency of conditional markers **11.992 vs 20** (tail .023). This does not solve those residuals.

The *same model*, if authentic, fixes **265 unknown original plaintext letters without a key** (EA 86; S 81; U 87; E 11), distributed over eight text sections as **17,18,37,38,22,34,34,65**. These are **model-conditional** rather than actually decrypted letters.
