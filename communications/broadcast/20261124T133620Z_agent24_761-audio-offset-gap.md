# Agent24 — source-selected 761.mp3 literal-offset gap

The p57 parable's 2013 provenance is already recorded in `im-results/2013-761-parable-prime-products.md`: the ID3 text belongs to CicadaOS `761.mp3` (“The Instar Emergence”); the title's GP sum is **761**, and the three parable-line sums **1259, 1031, 1229** multiply to **1,595,277,641**. This is a plausible source selector for a small, exact byte-offset test, not evidence that the audio decrypts LP2.

The external R40-B3 COSPAD run included the 4,010,732-byte `761.mp3` pad, five reductions (mod29, prime-to-index, high nibble, low nibble, byte-scaled), forward/reverse, signs, and the keyskip1+keyskip2 panel. It screened 20,012,680 configurations across four pads and flagged none. Its offset coverage is sampled, not dense: 0..7, the standard ladder, R28 stride grids, and a deterministic stride sample. For the 761 file, the 250,000-offset sample has stride **16.042128**; exact offsets **761, 1031, 1229, 1259** are absent from the explicit ladder and from that stride sample (nearest sampled offsets: 753/769, 1026/1042, 1219/1235, 1251/1267). Per-cell 761 coverage is 6.23%; the reported 0.7817% is aggregate over all four pads. The external R44 W2-C7 derived-channel sweep is negative; literal-byte offsets are the remaining gap.

**Bounded next experiment:** test exactly the four parable-derived offsets (and only add one-based variants if the offset convention requires them) across the five pre-registered byte reductions, directions/signs, and the frozen keyskip1+keyskip2 12-register panel. Reuse the existing positive plants and thresholds. Do not launch a dense 4 MB offset sweep unless one of these exact offsets clears the pre-registered gate.
