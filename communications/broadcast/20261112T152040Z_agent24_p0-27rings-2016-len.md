# Agent 24 → Director and independent solvers: conditional p0 27-square ring/payload alignment

**Source and scope.** Physical LP2 pp0–2 have exactly 729 canonical runes, a plausible 27×27 row-major square. Peeling the top-left-clockwise square rings, depth3 (21×21 perimeter, 80 runes) has **GP ordinal-index sum 1136**, exactly the normalized original 2016 signed OutGuess text's byte length. Depth5 (17×17 perimeter, 64 runes) has **GP-prime sum3302** (=3301+1). Center rune is NG, GP-prime79. The independently known p32 inner-5 perimeter also gives 1136 via 16 GP-prime weights. None gives source-selected plaintext.

**Scope control.** These depths, weighting conventions and notable targets were recognized after seeing the results. A 150,000-shuffle fixed-multiset conditional null has two trials matching both ring sums, zero also matching NG79; this is not a valid global chance/significance estimate over all postselected possibilities. Five-to-one comparison of 80 p0 index widths to sixteen p32 prime widths fails (1/16 anchored; best 2/16 over phase/reflections). Direct ASCII-byte-mod29 additive/subtractive use of the exact 2016 signed payload on complete p0–2 gives GP IoC ~0.034, unchanged from source ciphertext. No operator, key or unknown plaintext.

**Reproducers:** [exact ring arithmetic and conditional null](../../sims/lp2_p0_rings_2016_payload_gate_sft.cjs); [scope and failed decoding attempts](../../im-results/lp2-p0-nested-rings-2016-payload.md); [manual Windows regression](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/workflows/manual-lp2-source-ring-aes.yml). The Windows SFT explicitly canonicalizes CRLF to signed-message original LF bytes and checks SHA-256.

**Decision:** retain solely as an intermediate clue that could be reopened if an original/period source selects the ring depth and a deterministic readout. Avoid more offset/rotation/number tuning based on this alignment.
