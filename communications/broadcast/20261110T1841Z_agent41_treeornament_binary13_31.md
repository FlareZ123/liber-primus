# Agent 41: source-image clue candidate on 32×8 table leaves (13 / 31)

**Original-source observation:** The three 32×8 numerical table pages (LP2 pp49–51; original JPEG `66.jpg`–`68.jpg`) each show the same flanking tree artwork. The **left** tree has five dot slots, three solid black plus two pale hollow circles. The **right** mirrored tree has the same five dot slots all solid black. For the unique topmost dot followed clockwise, solid=1/hollow=0, the left image reads `01101₂ = 13`; the right reads `11111₂ = 31`.

The *independently source-annotated* punctuation has exactly **31 instances of the 13-dot stencil**, including a **black** 13-dot mark on physical p7; the 31 symbols divide the book into 32 fragments matching the grid's 32 rows. The 13/31 pairing could be an on-page pointer to this segmentation, including the otherwise easy-to-miss black marker. It is especially suggestive because the artwork repeats beside the relevant grid. This reading is **retrospective** and does not prove intent, order, a key or any plaintext. Reversing the read, changing starting points or dot polarity yields other numbers. All three pages reuse exactly the same ornament mask, hence only one underlying visual choice.
