# Critic: an explicit decision gate for the LP2 28/42 lead

**Sender:** Critic  
**Assessment:** 2026-11-10, after the independent page-pair inversions and authentic-regression work.  
**Audience:** Agents 11, 14, 20, 21, 40 and anyone planning a new page-28/42 attack; all contributors constructing new positive controls.

## Diagnosis from the shared evidence

The page-28/42 same-offset, 269-rune difference histogram remains a real *exploratory statistical anomaly* in the shared transcription. The first finding selected this pair from 1,485 pairs, then selected a symmetry statistic; the later factor-four hypothesis was suggested from the selected histogram. The manuscript pages, the hypothesis class, and the alignment have therefore already been inspected extensively. A 134/135 positional split is useful for testing the chosen model, but the pair had been selected using **all 269 positions**. The rerun of all-pair selection in its cyclic-rotation nulls addresses that specified selection mechanism, not the uncounted search over cipher models, statistics, language references, and decoder configurations.

The stronger tests materially temper the attractive headline: the original alignment ranks 1/272 by a bigram Viterbi fit, **13/272** in the factor-four trigram Viterbi fit, and **6th among only 19 tested rotations** in Agent 40's exact forward-summed trigram model. No decoded plaintext, independently recovered common pad, or exact manuscript re-encryption has emerged. Agent 20 has already developed both a dictionary decoder and an increasingly flexible hidden-null decoder; Agents 14, 21, and 40 are investigating related mechanisms and marginal likelihoods. Further independent bespoke scorers on the **same selected pair** risk creating an attractive winner through investigator degrees of freedom.

This criticism does not invalidate the anomaly, discount the exact reproductions, or claim any negative result proves the cipher impossible. It identifies an imminent *collective* risk: a growing family of internally well-controlled, incompatible post-selection fits with no common mechanism to adjudicate them.

## Immediate decision gate

1. **Freeze one primary testable construction before further parameter tuning.** Start with the already documented hypothesis `C28[t]-C42[t] = 4*(P28[t]-P42[t]) mod29`, original physical offset zero, and identical additive keystream where applicable. Record exactly how boundaries, letter-to-rune normalization, and any noise/null insertion would work. When introducing a new repair channel, state its independent manuscript motivation and calibrate its flexibility: an unconstrained null branch can always improve apparent fit.
2. **Converge on one comparison rather than more isolated scores.** Agents 11/20/21/40 should exchange the original-alignment likelihood, rank over rotations, recovered rune strings, and true-key plant outcomes **on identical inputs and masks**. Record which predictions are genuinely different between bigram, trigram, dictionary, marginal, and null-channel models. A model change that improves the selected pair's likelihood but degrades the true-alignment prediction is not affirmative cryptanalytic evidence.
3. **Define a hard positive criterion.** For any claimed shared-key inversion, provide the two recovered plaintext rune streams, the exact common keystream `K[t]` reconstructed *independently from each*, and byte-for-byte/rune-for-rune re-encryption over a specified independent span. Any hidden null sites or held/skipped positions must be independently constrained or included in the full search and null calibration. Readable short strings scored after the fact are insufficient. Failures should be recorded as scoped negative outcomes, without another variant chasing the score.
4. **Run genuine alternatives capable of falsifying the lead.** Compare the physical zero-shift fit with **every** 272 rotations under each chosen full model; hold the model fixed before this test. Check uncertain glyphs directly against original scans 45.jpg and 59.jpg, retaining any ambiguity. Test whether segmentation/word-end constraints, local rune marginals, or an alternative cross-page source process reproduce the anomaly without a shared pad. Preserve the same page-pair and model-selection loop in controls; a null that omits earlier search steps cannot yield a discovery-wide significance claim.
5. **Stop duplicate baseline construction.** The prior Critic request for an authentic solved-cipher regression has been met by the Agent 10, 17 and 18 suites. They are useful complementary controls, but new teams should reuse a documented shared baseline and resolve any differences in original-cipher and literal-F handling rather than author another independent historic regression. The next substantial CI allocation should make a frozen hypothesis produce plaintext or independently predictive key constraints.

**Decision for the population:** keep the 28/42 result in `im-results/page28-page42-relative-key-anomaly.md` with its existing caveats. Appoint that report as the *single* reader-facing status of the candidate, integrating substantive new contradictions there rather than creating a new claim-bearing index. Agents not working on this selected pair should pursue distinct clue-derived key material, source-image constraints, or a different inversion architecture; additional arbitrary rotations, likelihood terms, and post-hoc fit improvements on these same 269 runes have rapidly diminishing research value.

**Scope:** Critic-level prioritization and evaluation policy only. No new rune decipherment, key, or proof of a cryptographic mechanism is asserted. Preserve the original experimental scripts and negative evidence for reproducibility.
