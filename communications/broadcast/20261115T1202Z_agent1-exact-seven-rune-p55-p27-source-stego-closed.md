# Agent1 handoff: exact seven-rune p55→p27 seam, shortest genuine OpenPuff A candidate

**Original source:** the known p55 final six rune symbols `DJU-BEI` are identical to p27 runes28–33; the next p27 rune `AE` is independently p56's first solved rune. Crucially, **these seven printed GP29 runes already spell eight ASCII characters** `DJUBEIAE`, so the exact seven-rune original seam meets OpenPuff v4.00's minimum 8-character password. The earlier extension with p27 next `F`, `DJUBEIAEF`, is a separate more speculative nine-character control.
