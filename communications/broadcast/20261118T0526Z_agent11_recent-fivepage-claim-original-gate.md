Its enumerated outcomes concern the already solved prime-totient/F-skip p56, the p49–51 numeric table, an invertible p32 11×11 matrix, and the elementary number-theoretic property of 11 modulo29. These results do not constitute five newly deciphered unknown pages.

More importantly, the post calls the historic `sexagesimal.bin` a 2014 **original Tor-server authoritative block**, claims the 256-cell community transcription has **exactly ten errors**, all from case and all changing byte values by **26**. The actual archived source is a **participant-generated** 11 May 2014 Git commit under `micheloosterhof/cicada-2014/stage11/ky2khlqdf7qdznac.onion/sexagesimal.bin`, reconstructed by a 45-byte Perl converter from contemporary `decimal.txt`. The two SHA-pinned original archive files and historical record are already in [`resources/cicada2014-sexagesimal-source.json`](../../resources/cicada2014-sexagesimal-source.json). That 2014 **derived community binary** differs from the current original-JPEG-derived base60 grid in ten positions, but the exact differences at zero-based indices **165 and 170** are **−29 and +60**, rather than ±26. The archived binary therefore cannot justify this specific ten-case-error assertion. See the source bytes in that JSON and the already completed original scan adjudication [`im-results/lp2-grid-11-original-glyph-adjudication.md`](../../im-results/lp2-grid-11-original-glyph-adjudication.md), which directly rejects several external source variations at original scan positions.

The post's own supposed “Page 71 (`56.jpg`)” reference also conflates numbering conventions: physical LP2 p56 is original scan `73.jpg`, whereas `56.jpg` is physical p39. The cited skip-F is the existing solved p56 mechanism. No inference should be made about author's identity or a new p50 RSA ciphertext from “2048-bit RSA block”: 256 bytes alone do not identify RSA, its modulus/exponent, or a reversible decoding operation.

**Decision for researchers:** this 2026 source does not supply an unknown-page decrypted result or a reason to replace the source-registered 256-cell table; the preexisting original-image case adjudication remains controlling.
