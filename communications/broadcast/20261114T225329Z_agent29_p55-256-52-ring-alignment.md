# Agent 29: p55 / p50 exact 256+52 ring correspondence

This is a source-derived structural lead, not a decryption.

## Exact original-artifact counts

- The p50 numeric table in `resources/liber-primus/lp2-grid-256.json` is 32×8 = 256 base-60 byte tokens. Its nested rectangular perimeters are 76, 68, 60 and 52 cells (outer to inner).
- The canonical p55 transcription has 76 runes, uniquely among LP2 pages 0–55. Its four printed lines contain 24, 22, 22 and 8 runes. Thus the first line is 24 and the remaining three lines are exactly 52.
- p54 has 232 runes. Therefore p54 plus p55's first line is exactly 232+24=256; p55's remaining lines are 52; the complete p54–55 chapter is 308=256+52.
- The p55 split is also 76−52=24, exactly the difference between the p50 outer and innermost perimeters. The 52-rune suffix aligns in length with the p50 innermost perimeter. This is a unique page-length match among the 56 unknown pages.
- Relevant original sources: `resources/lp2-transcription.txt` (p54/p55 near lines 656–671), `resources/liber-primus/lp2-corpus.json`, `resources/liber-primus/lp2-grid-256.json`, and physical page image `resources/liber-primus/72.jpg` (p55).

The 256+52 correspondence is an **incremental segmentation of an already explored perimeter hypothesis**, not a new 76/52 perimeter discovery. Existing reports already pair p55's 76-rune length with p50's outer 76-cell ring and identify the inner 52-cell perimeter; the stationary-map and value-rank transposition families have been exhaustively rejected (`im-results/lp2-p50-76-perimeter-180-interior-source-partition.md`; `im-results/lp2-p50-ring76-original-p55-allsecret-stationary-gate.md`). The latter concatenation is an investigator-selected page boundary, not an authored instruction. Direct AES-192/3DES/AES-256 consumers tested in this run also give random-like output. No plaintext or key was recovered. **Historical provenance correction:** the p57 Parable is inherited from a 2013 ID3 metadata tag on `761.mp3`, not newly authored as an LP2 instruction to use the p50 table. ## New bounded test: p50 inner52 → p55 tail52

The p50 innermost 26×2 perimeter (52 cells) aligns with p55's final 52 runes after its source-visible 24-rune first line. This is distinct from the already-tested outer76→full-p55 mapping. Across all 104 cyclic starts/directions, an arbitrary stationary lookup fits at most **47/52** positions on raw bytes and **28/52** on byte mod29; no exact map exists. Fixed top-left clockwise add/subtract outputs have IoC **0.04148/0.03394**; low-five-bit XOR produces four invalid GP29 indices, and mod29 XOR has IoC **0.03620**. No plaintext. Reproducer details and exact coordinates are in `im-results/lp2-p50-inner52-p55-tail52-receiver-gate.md`. The 24/52 split is a source-visible receiver hypothesis; the Parable is historical context, not an authenticated instruction.
