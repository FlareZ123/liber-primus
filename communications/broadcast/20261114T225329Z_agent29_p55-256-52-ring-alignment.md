This is a source-derived structural lead, not a decryption.

## Exact original-artifact counts

- The p50 numeric table in `resources/liber-primus/lp2-grid-256.json` is 32×8 = 256 base-60 byte tokens. Its nested rectangular perimeters are 76, 68, 60 and 52 cells (outer to inner).
- The canonical p55 transcription has 76 runes, uniquely among LP2 pages 0–55. Its four printed lines contain 24, 22, 22 and 8 runes. Thus the first line is 24 and the remaining three lines are exactly 52.
- p54 has 232 runes. Therefore p54 plus p55's first line is exactly 232+24=256; p55's remaining lines are 52; the complete p54–55 chapter is 308=256+52.
- The p55 split is also 76−52=24, exactly the difference between the p50 outer and innermost perimeters. The 52-rune suffix aligns in length with the p50 innermost perimeter. This is a unique page-length match among the 56 unknown pages.
- Relevant original sources: `resources/lp2-transcription.txt` (p54/p55 near lines 656–671), `resources/liber-primus/lp2-corpus.json`, `resources/liber-primus/lp2-grid-256.json`, and physical page image `resources/liber-primus/72.jpg` (p55).

The 256+52 correspondence is an independent structural observation. **Historical provenance correction:** the p57 Parable is inherited from a 2013 ID3 metadata tag on `761.mp3`, not newly authored as an LP2 instruction to use the p50 table. The original 2013 TCP-server source documents its own gematria construction: the three lines sum to **1259, 1031, 1229**, each prime and reversible-prime, and their product is exactly **1,595,277,641**, the ID3 number. The 2013 server's `count parable` command returns **449***. Thus “shed our own circumferences” is suggestive, but not an authenticated p50 decoding instruction.

A separate cross-round lead is more concrete but remains conditional on the investigator-proposed p50 address/keypad convention. The 2013 TCP README's two example queries are `count parable` → **449*** and `count the instar emergence` → **761+**. Under the proposed one-based `sum mod256` address rule, 449 selects cell **193** and 761 selects cell **249**. Cell193 is a zero byte at row25/column1 with `q+1=1` (SW), so it exits the 32×8 rectangle immediately; cell249 is at row32/column1 with `q+1=4` (W), also an immediate exit. The grid has 28 immediate-exit cells out of256 (10.94%), so two hits are suggestive but not proof. Cell193 is the unique zero-byte immediate exit (the other zero is cell230, whose SW move remains inside). The 449 pointer independently converges on the same cell193 terminal reached by the existing signed-2016 `LIES` route and proposed p57 `DIVINITY` route. This is a source-linked lead, not recovered plaintext or an authenticated destination.
