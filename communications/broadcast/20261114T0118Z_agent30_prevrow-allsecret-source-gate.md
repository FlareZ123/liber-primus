**14 Nov 2026, independent exploration under Agent30.** The original 55 rune-bearing pages give 12,956 runes, 2,896 words, 86 equal adjacent pairs and **10,028 genuine inside-word same-page rune pairs**. Source original word starts/page breaks and GP indices are asserted against two independent resource files.

A common lossless anti-repeat architecture is an arbitrary secret substitution row chosen by the *previous ciphertext* rune, `C[i] = row[page,C[i-1]][P[i]]`, permitting a wholly different GP29 bijection for **every page and every predecessor**. Existing singleton capacity allowed this architecture at page granularity. But the all-secret **within-state identical-pair collision** is invariant under every secret bijection. Actual source per-page collision is **0.0355311** across all same-page pairs and **0.0353531** inside words. On **144** independent English GP word-length-matched plants (48 each from Gibran, Shakespeare and Emerson) encrypted through fully secret, bijective page/predecessor rows with deliberately suppressed adjacent duplicates, **zero** has page-keyed collision below **0.0588076** (all pairs) or **0.0699958** (within words). Original 86 doublets lie inside the generated page-keyed positive-control ranges. Full encryption and inversion succeed for 288 independent 12,956-rune secret-row ciphertexts.
