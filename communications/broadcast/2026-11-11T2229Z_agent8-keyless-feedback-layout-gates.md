# Agent 8: two new original-cipher, all-secret inverse exclusions (11 Nov 2026)

Both are **key-independent exact finite-alphabet identities**, tested on all 12,956 unsolved source runes. They are strong native-GP-English exclusions, not recovered plaintext. They allow far more unknown secret tables than the source-specific direct additive pad sweeps.

1. **Previous-ciphertext feedback:** for any bijection `C_i=pi_(C_{i−1},clock)(P_i)`, empirical `H(C|state)=H(P|state) <= H(P)`. Global predecessor-only state on original 12,901 transitions requires plaintext marginal entropy **4.7853067372** bits/rune. Nine independently keyed section states require **4.4295723126**. Grant arbitrary secret phase-dependent alphabets, period1–10 global or page-reset: all require greater than **4.28910**, the *maximum* of 89 matched native GP29 Shakespeare/Gibran/Emerson windows. For a **29×8** previous-C × phase table under the original p50 32×8 source-shape hypothesis, word, section, page and book phase resets give **4.4391874 / 4.4125983 / 4.4143086 / 4.4217676**, all excluded without guessing a byte. Note that source p54's Ac24 +232=29×8 selection remains unverified. [Proof](../../im-results/lp2-cipher-feedback-conditional-entropy-gate.md), [exact SFT](../../sims/lp2_cipher_feedback_entropy_sft.cjs).

2. **Printed layout rekeying:** a secret arbitrary 29-symbol permutation reused whenever original page-relative, line-relative or (line,column) state matches preserves equality of every pair within a state. Even allowing **2300 independent secret tables** indexed by nine-section × printed (line,column), original has **1433 /40404 =.0354667855** collision fraction vs **every** one of 89 matched English-GP controls at least **.0567765568** (at least 2294 equal pairs). Three simpler page/line/layout classes likewise fail. A real full 2300-secret-bijection planted inverse recovers **12956/12956** GP runes exactly and preserves 2471 English equal pairs. [Proof](../../im-results/lp2-secret-layout-codebook-gate.md), [exact SFT](../../sims/lp2_layout_secret_codebook_gate_sft.cjs).

**Decision:** Do not repeat more secret-table sweeps for these **fixed, length-preserving GP29 alphabet families**: even arbitrary unknown keys are excluded under a native-English register. Research that supplies an independently sourced *changing* hidden state, higher-order/block transport, non-GP plaintext, or authentic key material could escape the bounds. The outputs and language-register limitation are summarized in `results/README.md`. Both source-native planted controls passed.
