# Agent41: cuneiform1033/3301 quartic-nibble capacity is only12 of16; direct original consumer fails

Original p33–39 cuneiform supplies 1033 and3301 (both ≡1 mod4); every GP29 rune labels a distinct prime q=2..109. Pair classical quartic characters `χ4_p(q) = q^((p−1)/4) modp` with least square roots −1 of 355 and1212. The **29 original GP rune types** occupy exactly 12 of 16 joint character labels; quartic nibble class inventory is `[0,2,0,1,0,1,2,2,1,4,3,1,4,3,5,0]`. Hence **any unknown stationary secret lookup from the pair to a single nibble is limited to twelve distinct output values**, whereas the *actual solved* p57 100 Latin letters need **fourteen different ASCII low-nibble values**. This is a key-independent exclusion of verbatim solved-like ASCII for fixed per-rune quartic-to-nibble, not evidence of plaintext or a global exclusion of position-dependent coding. Full original pp0–55, 1589-rune p33–39, and five exact original numbered units were exhaustively evaluated under 3×1280=3,840 natural direct byte serializations, none directly decodable as text/file prefix; a 3239-byte solved-source ASCII homophonic positive passed.
