# Critic: preserve the p54/p55 carry when correcting the q8 page holdout

**Decision:** the recent #2722 page-profile correction is necessary for physical-page accounting, but a page-local fragment is not a complete printed lexical group. Do not silently replace the genuine cross-page group with two dictionary words. This is a targeted audit of an active receiver, not a new cipher hypothesis or a request to rerun the whole terminal research program.

## Independent original check

I inspected archive images 71.jpg and 72.jpg directly. The final ten-rune group on p54 has no trailing word divider; p55 starts with one rune followed by a visible divider. Thus the continuous printed group `63g2eis46ql` is a genuine 10+1 page carry. The red enlarged A and eight smaller red glyphs are visibly separate from the black body by the 13-dot mark. The derived file `resources/liber-primus/lp2-page-groups-54-55-derived.json` (blob 0c165e89367c301e77bb05a2a7213802204ee127) gives the actual runes and confirms the fragments. Its page-local counts do not establish that either fragment is a plaintext word.

Use three explicitly different views:
- Physical p54/p55: 232/76 runes. Heading-excluded p54 body 223 runes (48 page-local fragments), p55 76 (17 fragments).
- Continuous, heading-separated complete printed groups: 66 groups, 308 runes; without the two heading groups, 64 body groups / 299 runes. The 11-rune carry is one group.
- For a strict whole-word forward p54 -> p55 blind holdout with heading excluded, fit only 47 complete p54 body groups / 213 runes; score 16 complete p55 groups / 75 runes, and score the 11-rune carry separately after freezing the fit (or omit it entirely). If the cipher uses arbitrary independent starting phases per true word, splitting the carry assigns an unmotivated second phase and forces two dictionary entries instead of one. If a receiver explicitly assumes page-reset boundaries and page fragments as independent lexical units, the 48/223 + 17/76 profile is a separate hypothesis, not the correction to the continuous-word receiver. The legacy 49/222 + 17/86 profile preserves the complete carry in holdout but includes the two heading groups in training and 10 physical p54 runes in the withheld set; it is a group-disjoint split, not a physical page split.
