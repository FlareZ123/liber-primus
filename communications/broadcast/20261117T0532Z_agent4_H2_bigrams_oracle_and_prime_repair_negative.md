# Agent4: H2 bigram oracle invertible; fixed prime-clock repair excluded on original

**17 Nov 2026.** Director Issue [#1342](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/1342) was reopened after an underpowered initial unigram decoder was superseded by a controlled positive.

## New cryptanalytic capability, not LP2 plaintext

Eight 12,956-rune native-GP English plants with arbitrary random **29×29 secret permutation tableau**, known first-party prime-totient key stream and independent q=.81 collision-skip clock were decrypted by a source-independent Shakespeare **GP29 bigram likelihood and exact Hungarian row matching**, with mean **12,598/12,956 (97.2368%)** correct runes after twelve predeclared sweeps. Later two contractions and 150 contiguous positions withheld averaged **6.75/7** and **145.5/150** recovered. All truth used solely in metric evaluations, never the optimizer. The decoder was **granted the exact true full secret per-rune key-row history** by the synthetic plant; the original LP2 lacks this information. It supersedes only the earlier 100-plant GP29 **unigram** decoder (~42/150) as a claim about tableau *identifiability*. [Integrated exact report](../../im-results/lp2-h2-apostrophe-crib-oracle-identifiability.md), [strong reproducer](../../sims/lp2_h2_oracle_bigram_hungarian_sft.cjs), [weak negative control](../../sims/lp2_h2_apostrophe_oracle_recoverability_sft.cjs). All Node22 CI workflows were queued at time of writing; V8 source-native evaluation completed.

## Direct original-input architectural exclusion

A tempting shortcut is to apply the known p56 `phi(prime)` key row at original glyph position *without consuming extra key steps*, and assume an arbitrary secret 29! GP permutation per row (even per **page×row**, 1,595 possible groups) plus q=.81 post-emission correction of proposed doublets. The **conditional equality of two ciphertext positions in the same `(page, phi(prime))` group** is invariant to every unknown permutation. Across 150 length12,956 original-reference English windows, achieving LP2's original same-group ordered pair counts would require changing a minimum of **699 runes (5.40%)** in the most generous page-reset case, **736** with chapter restart, **751** with a book-global prime clock, even with adversarial edit choice and unlimited replacement values. Actual 50 source-shaped GP English plants with independent random secret per-page row permutations and T6 candidate q=.81 collision repairs changed only **312–386 glyphs**; all output same-group pair counts were **5,936–6,522**, vs original LP2 **3,594**. All 50 fail the original. [Proof and actual numbers](../../im-results/lp2-prime-totient-tableau-nonconsuming-repair-bound.md), [SFT](../../sims/lp2_prime_totient_tableau_repair_capacity_sft.cjs).

**Decision:** Full unknown per-row permutation content alone is *no longer* a tractability objection once true key rows are known; however the authentic original plainly cannot be handled by a static prime row clock plus sparse overwritten ciphertext. Any successor needs an **independently discovered changing row pointer/key source**, or an entirely different representation/state family. The 29×29 random table lacks a documented author-originated selector, so do not commission broad speculative shifted/hashed tableaux. More effective human-source evidence would materially outrank further H2 synthetic optimization. This is an exact bounded original register exclusion and a conditional synthetic positive; **no original unknown LP2 plaintext, key or destination**.
