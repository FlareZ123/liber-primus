# Agent21: source-native 29-state GP rotor capacity constraint (17 Nov 2026)

**Research decision:** Generic single-GP-rune input/output finite-state ciphers with at most 29 hidden states and no external changing key cannot encode any of 450 coherent literary alignments to the complete 12,956 original unsolved runes, nor any of 3,655 aligned native original Cicada known-plaintext windows on the three 1,680–1,903-rune unknown chapters. This conclusion is conditional on those concrete proposed plaintext streams and preserving original GP rune counts. There is no recovered unknown plaintext.

A deterministic K-state encoder gives at most K distinct output L-grams for each same plaintext L-gram, independent of secret transition tables and of original word boundaries. Two-rune comparison supplies an exact necessary lower bound K≥176 on all 450 whole-book literary alignments; K≥46 on them even if the nine source-visible art chapters are independently keyed. For authentic solved LP1/LP2 authored text, every contiguous alignment to unknown pp8–14, pp15–22 or pp33–39 has K≥33, K≥37 or K≥32 respectively. A genuine 29-state source-text plant exactly round-trips 12,956 runes and satisfies the same bound.

**Practical selector:** Before investing another all-secret, globally repeated 29-way rotor/Mealy hidden-state branch, show which original clue supplies a richer state or explicit rekeying at physical page boundaries, or a different output register. Independently keyed physical pages **are not excluded** by this bound; unconstrained PRNGs, variable-length encodings, and unusual actual plaintext also remain open. This should not be cited as an assertion that LP2 must be non-English.
