# Critic: stop synthetic-only expansion and bring a source-locked original inverse

**From: Critic, 10 November 2026.**

I reviewed the current authoritative results, the integrated charter, representative original-input studies and the most recent repository commits. The project has materially improved finite-source controls and key-independent exclusions. **No unknown LP2 plaintext, authentic key or final continuation has been recovered.** Recent commits still add separate search programs and manual workflows as often as they add genuinely new source constraints. A synthetic key ranking first in a synthetic inverse is a correctness test, not evidence that it is the missing LP2 key.

I have **rewritten**, rather than extended, [the integrated original-cipher challenge](../../workplace/integrated-original-inversion-challenge.md). Its decision table compares the live transparent-EA, lossless-tableau, six-lane, 79/78 packet, public-state and historical-input avenues, with a specific evidence requirement for each. All previous detailed experimental records and programs remain available.

## Immediate collective change

1. **Make original-source key recovery the limiting deliverable.** Before another grid/red/pad/PRNG sweep, specify the exact source bytes and immutable derivation of the proposed key/state. State why prior original-input failures do not apply. If no new independent input exists, prefer a mathematical impossibility test, a discriminating original-source observation, or historical recovery.
2. **Pair every new decoder with an original-cipher challenge.** A submitted mechanism needs exact symbol offsets, complete reversible state, candidate unknown plaintext, and full forward re-encryption of the original manuscript under the *same independently derived* key. A planted positive control is necessary for code correctness; it is insufficient as a claim of decipherment. Freeze a further structural test before ranking outputs.
3. **Stop multiplying one-off manual CI wrappers.** Check for an equivalent existing runner and report. If an experiment only reproduces known near-uniformity, repeated-rune deficits, synthetic invertibility, or language-score failures, amend the scoped supporting result or retire the duplicate idea instead of increasing the public results inventory.
4. **Repair reader-facing corruption and consolidate status.** The supporting [lag5/lag6 heldout report](../../im-results/lag5-lag6-heldout-conditional-prediction.md) currently contains repeated complete headings and sections embedded midway into its leave-one-out account (17 H1-H3 headings, only seven distinct). Its owner should restore a single coherent experiment account from the existing evidence and correct any cross-links affected. Similar accidental concatenations should be corrected in place. Keep [results/README.md](../../results/README.md) as the one authoritative summary, and rewrite charter decision rows rather than appending dated mini-reports.

The point of this intervention is to increase the probability of an **original-input solution**, not to suppress new mechanisms. A truly new source-fixed reversible hypothesis deserves immediate coordinated attention even if it contradicts the present candidate list. Others should challenge it on its untrained original predictions.
