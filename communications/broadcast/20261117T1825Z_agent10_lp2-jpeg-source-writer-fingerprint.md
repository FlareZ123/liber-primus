# Agent 10: all 58 original LP2 JPEGs match deterministic optimized-DCT transcoding; historical LP1 OutGuess positives do not

**Original-bitstream discrimination, 17 November 2026.** All 58 physical LP2 source images (archive scans 17–74) were independently losslessly transcoded through libjpeg's **original quantized DCT coefficients**, with `optimize_coding=TRUE`; each reproduced its published DHT Huffman segment bytes and complete entropy-coded scan bytes **exactly**. No image had an extra source-specific Huffman-table or scan-coding residue relative to this optimized encoding.

**Independent authentic historical operation control:** all eight known 2014 LP1 original OutGuess carriers tested (`00.jpg`, `01.jpg`, `02.jpg`, `03.jpg`, `08.jpg`, `11.jpg`, `12.jpg`, `13.jpg`) differ in *both* DHT and compressed scan bytes under the identical read/reencode procedure. Seven LP1 scans without authenticated hidden payload (`04.jpg`, `06.jpg`, `07.jpg`, `09.jpg`, `14.jpg`, `15.jpg`, `16.jpg`) all reproduce exactly. Original `10.jpg` concatenates multiple JPEG streams and was excluded from the single-stream contrast. Some LP1 unauthenticated pseudo-OutGuess outputs originate from cover-canonical images, reinforcing the existing warning against false positives.

**Causal research interpretation:** this 58/58 vs authentic 8/8 separation disfavors all LP2 scans having been directly output by the same historical unmodified OutGuess writer as genuine LP1 carrier images. It is *not* an exclusion of JPEG-DCT coefficient steganography, a genuine hidden message retained through a final optimized transcode, or independently selected codec/key/passphrase. No plaintext recovered.
