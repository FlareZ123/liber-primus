# Critic: an LP1 running-key F-skip surrogate does not exclude the authentic F clock

**16 November 2026, Critic audit.** Original-cipher status is unchanged: no unknown LP2 plaintext, creator key or full inverse has been recovered. This is a correction to how a completed source-key test is interpreted, not a new proposal to sweep key space.

The [LP1 source running-key experiment](../../im-results/lp2-lp1-solved-plaintext-direct-runningkey-original-gate.md) tested six specified author-era source registers/encodings, both GP29 additive signs, every contiguous offset and, optionally, a shortcut that treats **every ciphertext F** as a plaintext F with zero key consumption. The [actual decoder](../../sims/lp2_lp1_plaintext_direct_runningkey_original_sft.cjs) makes this explicit at its `bypassCipherF` branch. Its synthetic F control was constructed to prevent a non-F plaintext rune from encrypting to F.

Historical solved p56 and LP1 instead specify **plaintext**-F transparency: a plaintext F emits F without consuming the key, while a non-F plaintext rune may also encrypt to ciphertext F and **must** consume a key symbol. Thus the shortcut is a useful, deliberately restricted surrogate; its negatives cannot exclude the authentic plaintext-F-transparent LP1-key model. The unconditional direct additive negatives remain finite evidence, and the nine-rune `WHOYOUARE` crib's zero-hit scan remains valid for the particular contiguous LP1-source key family because the crib has no plaintext F and all starting key offsets were allowed. There is no independent LP2 instruction selecting LP1 as the unknown-page key, so this is **not** a reason to launch another high-cost source-offset expansion.

The report, its source comments and the [authoritative results synthesis](../../results/README.md) were corrected together in [commit b9917896](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/commit/b9917896b0d17f76b9fa8f443836995f042f3a3d). An additional [source-target correction](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/commit/5daa9a00e7835f7798c215374d9203761438da21) fixes the lingering incorrect **66-rune** "complete table instruction" in the synthesis to the source-exact **138-rune** p48→p49 sentence, with the **170-rune** p51→p52 continuation held out.
