# Critic — #2683 anchor and holdout audit

**Decision:** retain the finite original abstention and the six positive-control key recoveries. Do not treat the result as a new separation of H-shuffle/H-decoy on LP2 or an exhaustive q8 key exclusion. Two small implementation/interpretation corrections matter for reuse.

1. **Repeated-anchor count:** `propose` in [the #2683 SFT](../../sims/lp2_q8_shuffled_decoy_joint_inverse_sft.cjs) scans starts in order, but keeps the first accepted interval's end, ignoring shorter overlapping intervals. For example [0,20), [1,10), [10,19) yield one vote instead of two. For exact interval scheduling at each rotation class, replace the original `else{if(i>=z.last){z.votes++;z.last=i+w.length;}` with `else{if(i>=z.last){z.votes++;z.last=i+w.length;}else z.last=Math.min(z.last,i+w.length);`. This maintains the earliest possible end for the current count, since starts arrive monotonically. I independently executed the corrected proposal stage against the seven repository inputs named in the SFT. Original p33–39 still has **34,391 distinct canonical proposals and zero with two disjoint anchor hits** (1088-rune prefix). The corrected fresh-control multi counts in order fresh1-shuffle/decoy, fresh2-shuffle/decoy, fresh3-shuffle/decoy are 1,19,1,35,1,1, identical to the preserved outputs. This validates the particular original count despite the generic counting defect. It does not prove all 34,391 classes are infeasible: only 256 classes × eight orientations passed to the original exact phase filter. The report's finite-search limitation is correct.

2. **Decoy segmentation:** `wordPass` ends at N and returns only a phase posterior. The subsequent suffix invocation starts a fresh word; consequently it marginalizes all segmentations **conditional on a word break at the printed fit/suffix split**. It is not a complete semi-Markov marginal that includes words straddling the split. Independent inspection of the regenerated controls gives true-boundary status at the split: fresh1-decoy yes (1083), fresh2-decoy no (1090), fresh3-decoy no (1091); all three shuffled controls yes. The two non-boundary decoy controls still recover their full withheld rune sequences, so the observed predictive calibration remains useful. Their likelihoods and boundary F1 are those of the cut-conditioned receiver. The discriminator's common-key free/fixed suffix comparison is legitimate at this imposed cut, not an unconditional whole-tape model comparison. The original never reached this stage, so the correction cannot reverse its abstention.

3. **Forward replay:** separate MAP prefix and posterior-initialized suffix traces need not select the same join phase. For the six saved fresh cases, I independently compared all 18 method traces: every prefix's end phase, computed as (fitPhase + number of non-F fit runes) mod 8, equals its selected suffix initial phase. Thus there is no observed discontinuity here. Future inverse acceptance should assert concatenated exact forward replay from the chapter start rather than infer it from two separate replays.

The appropriate next decision remains to wait for an independent source-selected key/operation or genuinely distinct receiver. No repeat of the q8 sweep is warranted just to address a reporting qualification. If reusing the decoy receiver for an actual promising key, retain partial-word state across a fit cut or explicitly label the statistic as cut-conditioned. Apply the same distinction to other semi-Markov studies. No new unknown plaintext or key; no claim of independent untouched original holdout.
