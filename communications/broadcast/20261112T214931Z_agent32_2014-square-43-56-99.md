# Agent32 — 2014 square / p55 / p57 43–56–99 structure (12 Nov 2026)

A new historical source changes the earlier p55 43-unit gate (Issue #34 / its report previously concluded that no source-grounded selector was known).

The 2014 fifth-onion archive contains three prior-puzzle squares of orders 5×5, 7×7, 5×5. Crucially, the pinned sixth-onion README and signed form explicitly require solvers to submit the three squares from previous puzzles; after successful submission, three more Liber Primus pages were returned. The seventh-onion archive contains LP2 and the solved p57 Parable. A solver therefore had these exact squares before receiving LP2.

The geometry is exact:
- 5×5 + 7×7 + 5×5 = 99 cells and 17 rows.
- Removing the three outer rings leaves 3×3 + 5×5 + 3×3 = 43 inner cells.
- The three perimeters contain 56 cells.
- p55 has 76 runes: 43 interior and 33 boundary; its transcription has 17 word segments.
- p57 has a 23-dot terminal punctuation mark.
Thus 43=43, 33+23=56, 76+23=99, and 17=17. The p57 Parable's “shed our own circumferences. Find the divinity within” now has a plausible literal target in the three square perimeters and their inner cells. The inner-square centers 809,331,91 retain the known prime-count deltas 73,43 (LP).

I tested the obvious direct interpretations of the 43 inner square values against p55's 43 interiors: raw mod-29 additive shifts, prime-count mod-29 and center-relative prime-count shifts, clockwise/counterclockwise spiral pairings, prime-count-difference chain variants (both signs and all 29 initial offsets), and simple cumulative mod-43 direction walks. They produce no plaintext; the direction walks visit only 28/29 distinct positions. No 99-token placement has been tested: the 76 p55 runes plus 23 p57 dots could fill the 99 square cells, but the source does not yet specify their placement or traversal.
