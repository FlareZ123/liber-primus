**16 November 2026, 13:22 UTC.** Audit of the newly closed #1253–#1263 tasks against their Issue contracts, posted evidence and the existing authoritative synthesis. **No new unknown LP2 plaintext, source-authenticated key or full original inverse was found.** The completed negative experiments are largely scoped correctly. This note concerns task selection, not a demand to rerun any test.

1. **#1258 was based on an already contradicted four-stanza source premise.** The original p36–p38 red numbers are **1–5**, with 553 runes split 139/91/98/96/129. The five-part enclosure and five-state tests were already documented in `im-results/lp2-cuneiform-five-numbered-wisdom-inversion.md` and `results/README.md` *before the task was opened*. Existing period book/source attribution studies and the source images supplied no independently named new work. 3. **#1257's systematic six-parity placement could never be uniquely selected by the stated six GF29 checks alone.** The source coefficient polynomial already obeys those six checks, and a degree-six generator with invertible constant term determines *any* consecutive six coefficients from the remaining 250. Agent11 demonstrated all **251** placements reconstruct the same original table, so the experiment was valuable as an identifiability proof, but no source-independent checksum test could have preferred first-six to last-six parity. Any successor needs an *independent page-layout or author-supplied placement/serialization cue*, not more parity-window enumeration.

#1253 (shared apostrophe/lag-five family), #1255 (three p54 title cribs/Hill2), #1259 (2015 delta plus historical transposition/phi), #1261 (p55 two-stream), and #1262 (word-local two-tap emitter) were appropriately reported as failures of their **frozen families**, with original inputs and planted controls. **Recommendation to the Director:** before opening a task, inspect the *known input inventory* and ask whether the proposed discriminating result could differ among alternatives under the stated equations. A missing independent clue or a provably nondiscriminating test should be a short prerequisite decision, not another expensive implementation assignment. The queue belongs to the Director; this audit neither reallocates agent identities nor opens replacement tasks.
