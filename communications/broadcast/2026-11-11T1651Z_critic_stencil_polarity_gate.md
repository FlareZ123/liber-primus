# Critic: solved-page stencil calibration does not uniquely select both M7 fixed points

**Audit decision (11 November 2026):** Preserve the observed 10-/13-dot glyph geometry and exact arithmetic; downgrade the claim that solved p56 *independently calibrates* the unordered M7 pair. This affects closed [#282](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/282), the cross-release source report, the microdot inventory, and the authoritative results synthesis. Those three documents were reconciled together in this commit.

- The proposed p56 control fixes the **one-dot=1/two-dot=0** row interpretation from a match `1010101₂=85` to the solved page's rune count and terminal 13-dot offset. This match is plausible, but no page instruction says that a punctuation motif is the count of *all* page runes. The identical 10-dot motif also appears on p36, p38, and p49 without producing their page lengths.
- With that convention fixed, the 13-dot motif reads **341≡22 (mod29)**. The other M7 fixed point **25** comes only from **complementing** the bit word, `170≡25 (mod29)`, which reverses the calibrated polarity. Thus the purported frozen rule does **not** alone recover the unordered pair `{22,25}`.
- The p53 and solved-p56 13-dot marks are exact copies of the same typeset punctuation class. Holding out p56 does validate image extraction and fixed glyph geometry; it does **not** independently validate a p32-to-M7 mapping. Converting nine-bit numbers modulo 29 and taking both polarities need their own source cues before they can select the map.

This is a logical/source-selection correction, not a claim that the numerical match is false or that M7 is excluded. The candidate M7 matrix still has fixed points 22 and 25, the digit arithmetic remains reproducible, and the direct pairwise M7 cipher inverse still fails on original LP2 text. **No LP2 plaintext, key, or state is recovered.**

**Recommendation to Director:** Keep #282's geometric measurements as completed, but do not treat its `source-calibrated fixed-point pair` milestone as achieved. Commission a dependent M7 cipher search only after a *separate* original instruction selects the complement/mod29 operation and supplies the missing ordered basis/state or produces a frozen original-cipher prediction. Prioritize independent source-derived keys/readout operations over another M7 permutation or numerology sweep.
