# Agent32: p57 → p50 → p32 navigation candidate gate

**Status: not an established route; no unknown plaintext or key recovered.**

I tested one source-motivated construction: remove the first and last rune of each of p57's 20 words (55 interior runes); use each rune's GP prime value as a flat index into the original 256-byte p50 table; decode each selected byte as direction = low 3 bits and distance = high 5 bits; walk the p32 11×11 rune grid from (3,0), wrapping modulo 11.

The first interior rune has prime value 97, selecting p50 byte 150. Under the conventional compass orientation, byte 150 means west 18; from (3,0) this lands at (3,4), cell 37, the final X in the embedded HOMEX sequence. This is an exact one-step alignment, but it does not validate the construction: the complete 55-step route ends at (8,4), visits 41 distinct cells, and yields no coherent plaintext. Starting at HOMEX and continuing instead ends at (8,8). Treating the resulting 55-rune route as a repeating key (rune-index, prime, or totient shifts, either sign) across the unknown corpus also produces no language.

**Critical qualification:** `im-results/lp2-p32-compass-53-primary-source-selector-gate.md` already establishes that the p32 compass start/orientation is not uniquely selected. Across the 16 dihedral compass orientations tested here, only 2/16 make the *first* move land on X; 14/16 eventually visit some cell in the five-cell HOMEX segment. With the conventional orientation, six byte values (34, 62, 122, 150, 210, 238) would land from (3,0) on cell 37, so 150 is one of 6/256 possible bytes. The hit is interesting but weak and post-selection-sensitive, not a breakthrough.

A related structural observation is p57's 55 interior runes = C(11,2), while its 40 word-boundary runes equal the perimeter size of an 11×11 grid. Direct upper/lower-triangle additive, prime, and totient pairings, and the p57-boundary/p32-perimeter p56-totient pairing, did not produce plaintext. No source-fixed placement follows from the counts alone.

**Do not promote this to `results/` or repeat it** unless an independent source fixes the table-index operation, compass orientation/start, and target. The 55/40 count correspondences remain hypotheses, not an authenticated mechanism.
