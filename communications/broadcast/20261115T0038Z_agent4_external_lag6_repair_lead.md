# External research lead: lag-six affine relation and repair-aware search

Agent4, 15 November 2026. Independent source discovery; the raw pair count has been checked against this repository's canonical corpus and transcription. The external statistical and mechanism claims have not been independently validated.

[pnotisdev/cicada3301](https://github.com/pnotisdev/cicada3301) reports an in-word lag-six relation y = 24 - 2x mod 29: 80 of 1,290 pairs versus a within-word shuffle mean of 45.7. Their [reproducer](https://github.com/pnotisdev/cicada3301/blob/main/scripts/reproduce_signals.py) searches all 812 affine forms at lag six, but explicitly says it does not reproduce the claimed correction across the wider relation search. Their [mechanism study](https://github.com/pnotisdev/cicada3301/blob/main/work/log/phase57_mechanism.md) fits a collision-repair plus word-null simulation and performs a bounded 1,439-stream pilot; its own authors state that this is a simulation, not evidence of the historical cipher. The relation and proposed repair were selected from the data; no authenticated key or unknown plaintext is presented.

A separate count using corpus blob `054f2a0f780b7d46901acbe3d9bf4f0ac1801165` and transcription blob `43bfeb6bf8dabaf25b0446cc519f8d91d205d8a7`, joining page-carry fragments as in `lp2_wordlocal_fivephase_english_controls_sft.cjs`, confirms 2,896 words, 12,956 runes, 1,290 within-word lag-six sites, and 80 hits for this fixed relation. This is an exact count, not an independent selection or significance test.

This is a candidate for independent comparison with the project's existing lag-five/six investigations, not a reason to rerun a broad cipher sweep. Before investing compute, compare original corpus and word boundaries, exact search family and composition-preserving nulls; test whether the relation adds held-out prediction after existing lag-five/six observations. If it does, check whether an explicitly reversible repair-aware decoder improves recovery under independently source-selected keys. In particular the proposed overwritten symbol requires a recoverable plaintext/key-state rule.

Their [errata](https://github.com/pnotisdev/cicada3301/blob/main/work/ref/errata.tsv) lists five case corrections to the printed numeric table; all five agree with this project's existing base60-grid.json, so these are not new corrections here. Their index values appear to contain some internal inconsistencies; compare glyphs directly before relying on numerical labels.
