# Critic: retire unselected 2015 PGP timestamp as an LP2 decoder waypoint

**Scope:** The source-derived arithmetic in [p57/p50 prime-rank date study](../../im-results/lp2-p57-parable-p50-prime-rank-date-hypothesis.md) is reproducible. It is valuable as a **retrospective clue candidate**, with no recovered unknown LP2 plaintext or key. The strongest issue is **temporal and causal selection**.

The p50 original table was released in **2014**, and the p57 PARABLE was present in the **2013** audio. The apparent timestamp target is the **2015-07-28T05:12:39Z** signed denial, whose first-party body begins "Some news organisations have recently claimed..." and explains a reaction to contemporary Planned Parenthood-related allegations. There is presently **no pre-2015 instruction selecting that specific future communication**, or explaining why an LP2 cipher route should consume its clock fields. The separately matched 2016 LP signature is also later than the book.

The exact table values at proposed offsets **[205,173,201,135]** are **[76,117,74,171]** under the conditional base60 reading. Reversing the first three produces mixed-case ASCII **JuL** and GP29-remainder **TUE**; the fourth byte is « in Latin-1. This is an interesting exact arithmetic coincidence. The table contains 1, 2, 2 and 3 occurrences respectively of bytes 74, 76, 117, 171 in its 256 cells. Under a *purely illustrative fixed-index random-table-reordering model*, the probability of that exact ordered four-byte outcome is about **2.86×10⁻⁹**. It is **not** a discovery significance or Bayes factor: index-origin choice, reversed reading, mixed-case ASCII, two encodings, moduli, multiple sources, metadata targets and all earlier hypothesis searches were selected adaptively. Neither that null nor the small 56-signature corpus corrects for these choices.

The same study explicitly finds that ordinary prime-rank indexing (rank-minus-one), natural column-major and full Möbius/boustrophedon layouts fail to produce the readout, and the matched 2015 denial supplies **no p56 digest preimage** (33 checked combinations) or readable continuation. The hour fields remain unfixed. Promoting this as a **source-authenticated PGP waypoint** or using it to decide the table's radix is therefore premature.

**Decision for Director:** Preserve the precise byte-level anomaly and reproducer. Park dependent date/PGP/cryptanalytic branches unless an independently dated source or locked, previously unselected original-cipher prediction fixes the missing index, direction, year, timestamp grammar and a reversible consumer. Do not run additional flexible clock-field or hash sweeps to repair this route. A single-authored rule tested on new original material would materially change its standing.
