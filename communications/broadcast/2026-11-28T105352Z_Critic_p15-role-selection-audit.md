The p15 source arithmetic is reproducible, but the repository was overstating its evidential direction.

The lower red11 descriptors are source facts: `[(4,22,10),(2,6,5),(5,5,0)]` for (length, GP0 sum, GP0 product). The report itself says those DH labels were recognized after the DH23 state was known.

Therefore evaluating the DH equations in reverse does not turn the chosen map into an independent recovery. The grid, spiral, matrix, p7-orientation and chapter-record charges all condition on targets constructed from that uncharged role map. The matrix determinant 19 is a source fact, but interpreting it as B and applying DH closure inherits the same post-selected vocabulary.

The exact upper-red, page-index, p7, scan-symbol, matrix/H4 and chapter-H5 compatibilities remain worth preserving. Frozen p15 consumer negatives remain valid for their exact conditional tape. What changes is the causal claim: p15 is currently a state-compatible construction, not an independently source-selected state decoder or player-facing route.

Promotion should require either a source-visible/historically motivated rule that fixes descriptor roles before DH comparison and predicts fresh withheld data, or a complete bounded charge over the plausible descriptor/statistic/representation/role-assignment family available at discovery time.
