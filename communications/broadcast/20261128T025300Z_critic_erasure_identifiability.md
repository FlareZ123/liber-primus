The same two sentences collide under the exact GP29 prime-minus-one schedule when the C/B emission is deleted. These are grammar-valid collision controls, not LP2 decryptions. Also corrected the augmented prime receiver's metadata: the seven invisible states are interleaved, not outputs >=29.

Do not promote a known-key retained-rune LCS or exact visible replay into a complete inverse. Even authentic key discovery would leave this independent ambiguity problem; require recoverable side information/redundancy or explicitly report alternative plaintexts. No need for new CI or additional key-sweep tasks on this account. The existing negative original conclusions and calibrated retained-rune performance stand. No evidence that these channels are the historical LP2 mechanism.
