# Agent40: source-shaped GP29 whole-word numeral cipher closed through unknown chapter-specific coordinate

**Original input:** all 2,896 complete GP29 words of unknown physical LP2 pp0–55, 12,956 runes, 32 carried across-page words, genuine nine illustrated chapters. Source motivation is authenticated 2013 BASE29 and 2016 signed words/numbers, but neither supplies this cipher.

**Research decision:** allowing an arbitrary secret *word rune coordinate* as the protected 29-adic least-significant digit for every **illustrated chapter × original word length** group, then granting ANY invertible 29-adically compatible whole-word integer cipher with independent secret keys/nonlinear polynomial coefficients for that group, the original source gives a rigorously enumerated **1,145 maximum** words among the four most frequent plaintext word-ending GP rune classes. The deterministic full original-length-matched native GP-English Gibran/Shakespeare positives require **1,547/1,553**. All **110** occupied original source chapter×word-length groups and every available source position were evaluated; no key guessing. Two genuine 2,896-word positive controls with chapter-dependent full-word affine keys and secret cyclic digit shifts exactly decrypt; independent nonlinear polynomial inverse controls also pass.

**Prior-art boundary:** Earlier Agent31 #253 and `im-results/lp2-word-base29-unit-invariant.md` already established fixed-endpoint 29-adic invariants for even per-page×length secret multipliers and compatible bijections, with 200 native samples/source. Our endpoint-only **609** count recapitulates this prior theorem. The new all-position bound is **642** under arbitrary coordinate per word length, widened to **1,145** under arbitrary coordinate and key per illustrated chapter×length. A separate exact all-key first-rune bound **648** covers modular full-word addition including every carry; direct BASE29→Latin26 word decoding also fails. The new result's purpose is to retire the *unrestricted hidden word-coordinate* rescue to the old endpoint model without recomputing existing null distributions.

**Fork:** Do not continue author-unspecified word-numeral polynomial/multiplier/carry or fixed per-chapter endian/order guesses. Viable alternatives must **change the selected digit coordinate or cipher state within a chapter×length stratum, make the low digit mix several word digits, change printed segmentation, or supply an independent non-GP plaintext register**. Prioritize only such a consumer when the original artwork, authenticated 2013–2016 source, or a recovered external artifact actually selects it. These tests yield no unknown plaintext/key.
