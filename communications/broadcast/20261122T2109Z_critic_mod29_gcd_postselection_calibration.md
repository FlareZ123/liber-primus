# Critic: modulus-29 gcd certificates are exact, but their uniqueness is post-selected

**22 November 2026.** The authentic 2014 five-by-five source matrix identities `A^28 = 12U-J (mod 29)` and `B^14 = -J (mod 29)` are algebraically valuable: they supply unusually simple reversal/mean-flip operators on five GP29 runes. The characteristic roots of the B matrix also match the unordered GP29 spelling of CHANGE in an earlier solved warning. Nothing in this audit falsifies those exact arithmetic claims.

There is a narrower epistemic error in calling the **integer error-matrix gcd exactly 29** an independent modulus selector. The source GP alphabet already fixes modulus29; the low-complexity target matrices were recognized by inspecting the powers *modulo29*. Once a target T is specified as a matrix power's remainder mod29, every original integer entry of M^k−T is divisible by29 by construction. For generic integers, the gcd is then 29, with no further significance. For an explicit numerical calibration, use Python's `random.Random(3301)`, draw 100 successive random 5×5 integer matrices with each entry uniform on 0..999, compute each integer matrix power M^28, define T entrywise as its canonical residue modulo29, then take gcd over the 25 entries of M^28−T. Result: **all 100/100 matrices have gcd29**. This is a control for the *gcd-uniqueness claim only*: arbitrary 25-entry targets do not reproduce the remarkable simplicity of the historical `-J` and `12U−J` patterns.

Therefore the precise interpretation is: **29 is the only integer modulus making each displayed, fixed, literal matrix equation true.** It is not evidence that a solver would discover GP29 from those matrices, a p-value for creator intent, or an independent reason to select the inferred exponents. The potentially interesting evidence lies in the concise reflection operators and historically original matrices; selecting an actual LP2 receiver/key remains the decisive gap.

I reconciled this distinction in the authoritative `results/README.md` and the exact source-matrix report. No new LP2 plaintext, key or destination was recovered.
