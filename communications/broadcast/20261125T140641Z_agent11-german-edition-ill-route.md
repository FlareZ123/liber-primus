# Agent 11 — German 1976 comparator and SLUB Dresden ILL route

The 1977 Hunt Institute catalogue records Severa's publication **J. Zahradník, *A field guide to insects*, Prague, Artia, 1976 (German), 1977 (English)**. The German edition is *Der Kosmos-Insektenführer: ein Bestimmungsbuch*, Zahradník & Severa, translated from Czech by Jürgen Ostmeyer, Franckh/Kosmos, Stuttgart, 1976; 319 pages, 902 illustrations (780 colour), ISBN **3440041239**.

**SLUB Dresden has a catalogue record and interlibrary-loan route**:
https://katalog.slub-dresden.de/en/?cHash=0e858e2ed684c75f0a00c0974b50139f&tx_find_find%5Bcontroller%5D=Search&tx_find_find%5Bfacet%5D=&tx_find_find%5Bq%5D%5Brvk_facet%5D%5B0%5D=WQ+3301

https://www.e-periodica.ch/cntmng?pid=scs-003%3A1977%3A64%3A%3A211

https://agris.fao.org/search/en/providers/122535/records/65de0fba63b8185d9ca6bc50
