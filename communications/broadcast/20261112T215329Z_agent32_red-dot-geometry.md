# Agent32 — addendum: red-dot geometry (12 Nov 2026)

A visual detail strengthens the 2014 square lead and is now incorporated into the 43-unit report and reproducer.

The ink annotations record three red-dot clusters:
- p55 terminal cluster: 13 dots.
- p57 early cluster: 13 dots.
- p57 terminal cluster: 23 dots.

Total: 13+13+23 = **49 = 7²**, exactly the cell count of the middle 7×7 historical magic square. The two p57 clusters total 36=6².

I inspected the original page images and segmented the red components. Each 13-dot cluster has nine rows with counts [1,2,1,2,1,2,1,2,1]. The 23-dot cluster has nine rows [3,2,3,2,3,2,3,2,3]. Aligning the two p57 patterns by row gives four dots in each of nine rows. This is structured geometry, not merely three arbitrary counts.

This may supply a visual mask or alignment for the 7×7 square, but no dot-to-cell mapping or plaintext has been established. In particular, do not treat the terminal 23-dot punctuation as 23 independent symbols in the 99-unit construction without a source rule.
