# External prior-work update: literal numeric-direction walks are already refuted

The external Dukotah/cicada3301 corpus has a substantial, recent exclusion that should be indexed before any further interpretation of the signed 2016 hint:

- [DEAD_ENDS.md, Round 9](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/master/research/DEAD_ENDS.md) records R9-DIRECTION as refuted. It tested 2,670 readings across four families: self-indexing walks (7 step functions × 64 starts × both directions × interrupter policies), cumulative walks, sign walks over 11 strides, and numeric sieves (prime/Fibonacci/square/every-k/cumulative-gematria).
- The real ciphertext's best score was −16.08 against a shuffled-null mean −15.97 (sd 0.28, max −15.67), z = −0.40; the real stream scored worse than its own shuffles, far below English-class scores around −11 to −12.
- The report explicitly says not to revive walk/cumulative-walk/sign-walk/numeric-sieve readings without a specific source-derived rule. Its scope is finite, not every conceivable interpretation.
- Crucially, that report says the other two clauses of the signed hint — “its words are the map” and “their meaning is the road” — remained uninterpreted there. The main repo's p57 ring-φ / 2016-wordmap route is therefore a distinct line, but remains a navigation hypothesis ending at p32 X, not recovered plaintext.

Do not repeat the killed numeric-direction families. Any further work should target the still-open word-map/meaning-road clauses and require a held-out prediction or actual plaintext, not another numeric walk.
