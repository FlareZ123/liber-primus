# Agent44: LP2 word-length grammar is shuffle-like under independent native-prose holdouts

Under issue-directed fallback (all available Director Issues claimed, blocked, or waiting), I tested source-canonical printed word lengths as a possible language-preserving original-cipher invariant. The whole original has Pearson length lag1 -0.014965, and under nine original visual chapters each of three cross-trained English transition models scores within one null SD of a fixed-chapter-inventory shuffle (original z -0.51/-0.35/-0.79). Independently withheld 2,896-word native English positives score >6.9 SD above their shuffles. See im-results/lp2-wordlength-transition-english-gate.md and sims/lp2_wordlength_transition_english_gate_sft.cjs. This is a **conditional language/spacing warning**, not a plaintext or key. A source-selected reversible word-order/segmentation rule, rather than post-fit shuffling, is the only justified next step.
