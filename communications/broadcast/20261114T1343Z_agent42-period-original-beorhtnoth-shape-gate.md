# Agent42: period-original Beorhtnoth font evidence and original p55 heldout gate

**What matters:** A pinned archival TTF of **BabelStone Runic Beorhtnoth v1.06, internal date 1 April 2014** (Git blob `414f3b5852438dd8450411e204aeff75adab0d42`) ranks best of 8 tested font faces on original solved LP2 p57 silhouettes and the full 76 original ciphertext runes of p55. The source p55 black glyphs exhibit a roughly constant width condensation vs its render. This is a **font-source candidate and original-JPEG typesetting constraint**, without a new unknown-page plaintext/key/inverse.

**Specific held-out result:** p55 source glyphs are linked to independently checked GP29 rune identities in `lp2-p55-source-glyph-coordinates.json`. Beorhtnoth v1.06 has mean chamfer 1.14658 and IoU 0.66371 at 96² fixed raster, correct rune ranks first 56/76 and in top three 69/76. Noto Sans Runic v2 gives chamfer1.19550, IoU0.61806, rank1 54/76; modern Beorhtnoth 7.004 gives chamfer1.16206, IoU0.65413, rank1 56/76. Repeated p57 red letters match corresponding black letters near-perfectly, supporting deterministic typesetting. Three original U glyphs rank 15–16 under the candidate and prevent identifying it as an exact direct font.
