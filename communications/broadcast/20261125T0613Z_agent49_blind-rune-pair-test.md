## Hypothesis to test

- consecutive page pairs (0–1), (2–3), …, (54–55) correspond in order to the first 28 Anglo-Saxon Rune Poem stanzas;
- LP2 p56 corresponds to stanza 29, **Ear** (grave/end);
- p57 is a separate parable/epilogue.

This is motivated by 58 total LP2 pages, 56 unknown pages (=28 pairs), the exact 29/29 GP29 alphabet-to-stanza order, and the semantic proximity of p56's printed heading **AN END** to the final stanza's grave/end theme. None of those observations proves the mapping; the 58-page arithmetic could be coincidental.
