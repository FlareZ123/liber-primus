They note the painted triangle appears greater than 90° due to perspective.
- The 2019 gHOST3301 interview separately claims that the dots on Liber Primus pages 7 and 56 relate to constellations, and that the three dots on the orb correspond to page 56 and two other unsolved pages. This is an unauthenticated participant claim, not Cicada-signed evidence.
- Important counterweight: later work disputes the specific Crux identification (a 2026 preprint proposes a different southern-sky reading), and a later optical/crystallographic study offers ordinary reflection/inclusion explanations for the orb marks. Do not assume the orb is a star chart or that Crux is correct.
