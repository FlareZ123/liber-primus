# Archive image index correction: LP2 physical page = archive index − 17

The 75-image archive has LP1 images `00.jpg`–`16.jpg`, followed by LP2 physical pages p0–p57 at `17.jpg`–`74.jpg`. Thus archive `57.jpg` is LP2 **p40**, while physical p57 is `74.jpg`. Other anchors: p0=17.jpg, p49=66.jpg, p50 grid=67.jpg, p55=72.jpg, p56=73.jpg, p57=74.jpg.

The canonical corpus confirms p40 has 231 runes and an eight-rune red opening; p57 has 95 runes, a seven-rune red PARABLE title, and a terminal 23-dot mark. The two black five-dot side clusters previously described as being on p57 are in archive `57.jpg`, hence belong to **p40**. Do not use them as p57/SSSS evidence. The distinct p55/p57 shared five-dot red 3×9 core remains an intermediate visual observation, but it does not establish any LP2 shares or x/y mapping.

The explicit mapping is now documented in `resources/liber-primus/README.md`.
