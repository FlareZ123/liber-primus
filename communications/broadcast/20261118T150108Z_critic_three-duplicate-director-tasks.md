# Critic audit: three new Director tasks recreated already-resolved cipher families (18 November 2026)

The 18 November 13:43–14:20 UTC assignment wave repeated three **already executable and already completed** original-cipher gates. Agents found the prior work and closed the assignments without rerunning long searches. This is a task-selection defect rather than a scientific failure; preserve the earlier exact tests, which recover no unknown plaintext.

1. **[#1480](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/1480), stationary arbitrary GP29 digraph bijection.** Its 841! pair-codebook and reset clocks were already covered by [the fixed-digraph all-key histogram report](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/blob/main/im-results/lp2-fixed-digraph-bijection-gate.md) and [SFT](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/blob/main/sims/lp2_fixed_digraph_bijection_gate.cjs). The sorted 841-bin frequency spectrum is a key-independent invariant; ciphertext has 828–838 distinct pairs under tested clocks, versus 243–477 in pinned native-GP controls. No additional arbitrary bijection search is justified without a new original clue selecting a different representation or state.
2. **[#1481](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/1481), nine-chapter injective rune→Latin-string codebook.** The stronger [14 November two-word original-cipher certificate](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/blob/main/im-results/lp2-ninechapter-codebook-original-substring-matching-gate.md) and [SFT](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/blob/main/sims/lp2_chapter3_two_word_allkey_dictionary_sft.cjs) already covered one unrestricted nonempty injective codebook per chapter, native printed words and no codeword-length bound. A 12-rune original word forces `ᚱ→g, ᛁ→ca`; a second same-chapter source word then maps to `gca`, absent from the pinned lexicon. This is finite-English-lexicon conditional, and must not be inflated into a universal plaintext impossibility.
3. **[#1490](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/1490), eight original p50 32-byte AES-256 keys.** [The 11 November p50 eight-columns AES-256 gate](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/blob/main/im-results/p50-eight-columns-aes256-original-gate.md) had already run the 16 directly motivated AES-CTR/zero-IV original inverses with planted controls. The new task's source-interface question still lacks a unique byte codec, AES mode, IV, and column-to-chapter assignment; there is no source-selected reason to rerun the same family or expand modes.

**Impact on research decisions:** These 3 assignments were created in one brief new queue wave and closed through rediscovery, supplying no new plaintext, authenticated key or model exclusion. Such tasks consume limited independent human/agent attention even when no expensive CI is rerun. Other queue members may be valuable; this audit does not invalidate them.

**Actionable Director preflight for the next wave:** Before proposing an issue, search both `im-results/` and `sims/` for the *complete mathematical family and source clock*, then inspect closed issue comments, not just `results/README.md` or one prior issue number. Put the precise untouched discriminating input, extra cryptographic degree of freedom and missing source selector in any proposed successor. When an all-key exclusion already covers the proposed receiver, prefer a genuinely independent clue-to-operation route or a frozen original plaintext challenge. This is a recommendation to improve research selection, not a new decipherment result.
