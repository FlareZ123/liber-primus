Subject: Bounded source check on cuneiform DMS destination

I checked the proposed 17°13′N, 55°01′E reading against the authenticated 2016 text and the surviving physical-puzzle record.

**What supports trying it:** the signed 2016 message really says “Its words are the map, their meaning is the road, and their numbers are the direction.” Cicada demonstrably used GPS coordinates for physical posters in 2012 and 2013. Reading the two base-60 pairs (17,13) and (55,1) as latitude/longitude degrees and minutes is a coherent human hypothesis.

**What does not select it:** the signed text never says DMS, latitude/longitude, N/E, or Oman. The 2012 source list's 14 locations and the documented 2013 poster list use decimal-degree coordinates, and neither includes Dhofar/Sadah/Oman. I computed the proposed point's distance to those 22 published 2012/2013 poster coordinates: its nearest is Moscow at about 4,536 km. The mapped attraction near the coordinate is generic POI coincidence, not a Cicada link. The exact authenticated 2016 source record also warns “Beware false paths.”

There is a close prior-art caution: the separate p32 ring/DMS hypothesis was already exhaustively enumerated (24 word orders; 2,208 signed ordered route pairs) and retired because no source selected DMS, the order, or hemispheres. That does not mathematically disprove the much simpler cuneiform pair reading, but it shows that the 2016 map sentence plus an attractive coordinate is not enough by itself.

The stronger historical context for these exact numbers remains the 2013 CicadaOS `prime_echo` source, which pauses at 1033 and 3301, plus the documented 2014 reuse of 33011033. The DMS decomposition is an additional interpretation, not an independently authenticated operation. The RA/Dec alternative (17h13m, +55°01′) also lacks a named source or first-party selector.

**Decision:** retain this only as an unverified destination hypothesis; do not query nearby landmarks, sweep signs/orders, or promote Oman as the destination. Reopen only on an independent signed/period source that names the region or explicitly selects geographic DMS. Otherwise retire it as unselected.
