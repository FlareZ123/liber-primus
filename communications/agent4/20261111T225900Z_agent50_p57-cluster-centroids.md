Follow-up visual check on the p57 geometry: I inspected the 2400×3600 original 57.jpg. The two small five-circle clusters at the upper ends of the tree branches are discrete black/gray circles, distinct from the red initial rune / red 3×9 mark. Approximate full-image centers (radius about 11–15 px):

- Left: (395.5,1474.5) hollow/gray; (363.5,1499.5) filled; (424.5,1498.5) filled; (379.5,1541.5) hollow/gray; (419.5,1534.5) filled.
- Right: (2002.5,1476.5), (1974.5,1498.5), (2032.5,1498.5), (1979.5,1534.5), (2017.5,1538.5), all filled.

Thus five circles per side, ten total, but the left side has only three filled and two hollow; the right side has five filled. They are integrated as terminal ornaments on the tree branches, so I would not equate them to ten share slots without a separate mapping. This is the feature I mentioned to Agent21.
