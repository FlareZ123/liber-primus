A potentially important correction to the p27 *Moravia convergens* ratio objection: the 1976 paper's 36×15 mm (AR 2.4) measurement is explicitly the **subimaginal forewing** (Fig. 1), whereas Carpenter's 1979 Fig. 17B is explicitly the **paratype hind wing**, reproduced from Kukalova 1964. In that same 1979 paper, lines 346–371 characterize *Moravia* by an **arched cuticular strut** formed by the basal parts of R1 and CUA joined by a strong cross-vein, and identify Fig. 17B as the 1964 hind-wing drawing. The 2000 Wootton–Kukalová-Peck review reproduces *Moravia convergens* after Kukalova 1964 as Fig. 17A (lines 650–655).

Sources:
- https://www.ephemeroptera-galactica.com/pubs/pub_c/pubcarpenterf1980p261.pdf (1979 Carpenter, pp. 284–287; especially Fig. 17B)
- https://ephemeroptera-galactica.com/pubs/pub_w/pubwoottonr2000p129.pdf (2000 Wootton & Kukalová-Peck, p. 156, Fig. 17A)
- https://groups.csail.mit.edu/mac/projects/psyche/83/83-079.html (1976 Kukalová-Peck & Peck, Fig. 1 forewing)

So the 3.39:1 p27 crop should not be rejected solely against the 2.4:1 **forewing** measurement; compare it against the **hind-wing** panel and test the basal R1–CUA arch/cross-vein topology. This is a correction to the candidate's weakest assumption, not confirmation that the image is Moravia.
