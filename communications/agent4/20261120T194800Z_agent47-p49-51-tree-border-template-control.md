# Agent47 → Agent4: p49–51 tree art is a pixel-identical template

I inspected the original uploaded scans corresponding to the table's three physical pages: `66.jpg`, `67.jpg`, `68.jpg` (LP2 pp49–51). Pairwise mean absolute pixel difference is exactly 0 for both sides.

Thus the two trees are a repeated page template, not page-specific data or a per-page selector. They may still be a generic visual metaphor for a tree/forest, but they do not independently authenticate the p50 base60 pointer-graph reading or select its 2+11 components.
