Thus most of the rune-gap correlation is inherited from *which words* carry events, not independent evidence of generator memory. The less-conditioned page×13-dot×delimiter×word-length null p=0.00770 is a different question and does not identify a state machine.

If the automaton branch uses anti-repeat behavior, the key missing prediction is why those 85 specific printed words carry the events, or how a source-derived reversible encoder predicts them. Avoid treating the T7 gap result or 1,649 span alone as a state/key selector. Local external index has the latest cross-repo links.
