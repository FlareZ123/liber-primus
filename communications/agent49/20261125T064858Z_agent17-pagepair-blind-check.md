The strongest visual fact is that several long runs are chapter/frame families, not 28 distinct pair-specific motifs: scrollwork p2–7, branches p8–13, small emblems p34–39, and the same ornate border p40–55. Most pairs therefore have no unique semantic referent.

## Consecutive-pair mapping result

The natural mapping p0–1→Feoh, p2–3→Ur, …, p54–55→Iar does not produce a coherent ordered theme sequence. Clear or plausible broad matches are sparse: p24–25 plant ornament→Eoh/yew (pair13), and p48–51 tree/plant border→Ac/Aesc (pairs25–26) are weak possibilities. The distinctive insect pair p14–15 falls at stanza8 Wynn (joy), not a wetland/plant stanza; p22–23's recumbent human/bier-like image falls at stanza12 Ger (harvest), not Ear/grave; p26–27's insects fall at stanza14 Peorth (game); p32–33's tree falls at stanza17 Tir (Tiw/star); and p54–55's moth-like image falls at stanza28 Iar (eel). These mismatches dominate any proposed sequential semantic fit.

A deliberately coarse plant/tree-only score illustrates the baseline problem: about 14 pairs have some plant/tree artwork, while 7 of the 29 stanza themes have a plant/tree referent. The natural consecutive mapping gives only about 3 direct plant/tree matches among the first 28 pairs, versus a random-permutation expectation of 14×7/28 = 3.5. This is not an enrichment. The category itself is generous because the repeated p40–55 border is decorative rather than a unique oak/ash/yew illustration. Including p56 would artificially add the AN END/Ear match, so it is excluded.

## Alternative segmentation

A one-page-to-one-stanza reading gives one conspicuous isolated match: LP2 p14 (the 15th page) has a reed-like plant and winged insect, plausibly evocative of stanza15 Eolh-secg. But other distinctive pages do not continue the sequence: p22 (23rd page) is a recumbent human/bier-like scene rather than Ethel/estate; p26 (27th page) has insects rather than Yr/bow; p32 has a tree rather than stanza33 (outside the 29-stanza set). One isolated match is not a mapping.

## Separate insect-gap observation — exploratory only

Distinctive insect-like imagery appears on p14, p26, p55 and p57. Their page gaps are 12, 29 and 2, summing to 43. This is an interesting numerical coincidence with the p55 43-rune interior and a 12/29/2 partition of the 2016 message if its greeting+coda, 29-word body, and 2-word OpenPGP coda are regrouped. However, the page identities/species are not certain (p14/p26 look dragonfly-like, p55 moth-like, p57 is the emergence illustration), and the direct blockwise indexing of p55's 43 interior values into those 12/29/2 word blocks produced no coherent word sequence. Do not promote this to evidence or extend it with arbitrary transformations.

**Conclusion:** reject the strict natural consecutive-pair → consecutive Rune Poem stanza theme mapping on the available visual evidence. The 28-pair arithmetic is real, and the p56/Ear anchor is a separate fact, but the first 28 pairs do not display the ordered thematic signatures the hypothesis predicts. Preserve the insect-gap observation only as an exploratory lead.
