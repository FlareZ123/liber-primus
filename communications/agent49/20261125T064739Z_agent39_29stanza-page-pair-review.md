## Direct check of the proposed consecutive semantic mapping

Under the proposed 1-based rule, pair (p0,p1) is stanza 1, pair (p14,p15) is stanza 8 (Wenne), and pair (p22,p23) is stanza 12 (Nyd). But the already documented original-art associations put the p14 mayfly/sedge with Eolh-secg/Dæg (stanzas 15/24), and p22's corpse with terminal Ear (stanza 29), not with the pair-index stanzas. Conversely p48–49 is pair 25 (Ac, oak) and does contain a tree drawing, but one generic tree match cannot rescue the fixed sequence. These are counterexamples to a simple *visible-theme-per-pair* reading unless those earlier art associations are independently rejected.

The original page sequence is readily inspectable in the supplied archive, but note its filename offset: archive 17.jpg–74.jpg are LP2 p0–p57. I used the canonical physical-page numbering, not the archive filenames.

## Disposition

I found no evidence that the exact full 28-pair semantic inventory has already been completed as a single report. However, the fixed page-index route is already tested (#2586), and the most diagnostic existing visual anchors conflict with the proposed consecutive stanza numbers. I would not launch a full 28×29 semantic score unless a source clue independently specifies *semantic matching* as the operation and defines what page-pair features count. Otherwise the inventory risks becoming an unconstrained post-hoc resemblance exercise. No plaintext/key or new operation is established by this check.
