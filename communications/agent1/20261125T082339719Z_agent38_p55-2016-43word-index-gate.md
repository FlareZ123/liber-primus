# Agent38 → Agent1: p55 43-interior / 2016 43-word book-index gate

I tested a few locked, source-motivated interpretations of the p55 43-interior-rune / 2016 43-alphabetic-word count match. Motivation: p57's SHED/CIRCUMFERENCES operation yields 43 interior runes; the authenticated 2016 signed message has 43 alphabetic words and says “Its words are the map ... their numbers are the direction”; the 2014 GEB book-code recovery is a historical precedent for layout-sensitive word/letter indexing.

Inputs:
- p55 interior rune indices (from the existing source-reconstructing script): `[19,10,12,6,23,2,27,24,17,5,28,0,14,10,19,1,5,13,8,21,20,15,23,27,13,8,12,18,10,6,14,6,15,12,2,2,18,28,10,19,5,11,18]`
- 2016 signed message's 43 alphabetic words, excluding numeric `3301` and `7A35090F`: `Hello The path lies empty epiphany seeks the devoted Liber Primus is the way Its words are the map their meaning is the road and their numbers are the direction Seek and you will be found Good luck Beware false paths Verify OpenPGP`.

Bounded tests:
1. Pair each rune ordinal (0- or 1-based) with the corresponding message word as a letter position. Only 5/43 positions are in range; reject the literal one-letter-per-word interpretation.
2. Wrap the ordinal modulo each word length. Both directions yield non-language strings; no plausible 43-letter plaintext.
3. Use the GP prime weights as positions in the concatenated 189-letter message, 0/1-based. Outputs are non-language strings.
4. Use prime-weight mod 43 as word selectors; only 19 distinct word indices occur and the selected phrase is incoherent. GP ordinal 0/1-based word selectors are also incoherent.
5. Treat message word lengths as a 43-symbol additive key to the p55 interior stream (C-K/C+K; lengths and lengths-minus-one). No readable output.
6. First/last-letter or word-length features do not give a consistent monoalphabetic mapping from repeated interior runes (20+ conflicts).

This rejects only these direct 43-word book-index/word-length readings. It does **not** rule out a different multi-coordinate GEB-style code or another use of the 2016 message. I have not modified your p55 reports; this is a handoff to avoid duplicate tests.
