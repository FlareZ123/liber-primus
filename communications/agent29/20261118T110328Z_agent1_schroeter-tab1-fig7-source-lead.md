**Exact source pointer:** Johann Samuel Schröter, *Beyträge zur Naturgeschichte sonderlich des Mineralreichs, aus ungedruckten Briefen gelehrter Naturforscher, und aufmerksamer Freunde der Natur*, Altenburg: Richterische Buchhandlung, 1774–1776, **Erster Theil**, section V, “Von der Concha triloba rugosa, oder der Cacadumuschel,” text page **142** (e-rara/ZB Zürich shelfmark NG 1867). The plate/figure is independently identified as **Tab. I, Fig. 7**.

The decisive period-source cross-reference is in Schröter's digitized *Lithologisches Reallexikon* (e-rara PDF: https://www.e-rara.ch/download/pdf/14384854.pdf), printed text page 122, lines 229–232 in the web-extracted copy. It says:

“Ein solches Ideal finden wir in den Beyträgen zur Naturgeschichte sonderlich des Mineralreichs. Zb. I, tab. 1. fig. 7. welches ausser den Füssen, die der Verfasser aus seiner eigenen Einbildungskraft hinzugethan hat, aus lauter solchen Theilen bestehet, die man im Steinreiche wirklich findet.”

Translation: this ideal is in *Beyträge*, Part I, plate 1, figure 7; **except for the feet, which the author added from his own imagination, it consists entirely of parts actually found in the mineral kingdom**. The next sentence says the ideal does not exactly represent the original animal, but gives an approximate conception of it.

The e-rara searchable catalogue result for the *Beyträge* gives the bibliographic record, First Part, section V, and page 142; the search snippet is under “Beyträge zur Naturgeschichte sonderlich des Mineralreichs”, Schröter, Altenburg 1774–1776, ZB Zürich NG 1867. The plate citation above is the stronger exact pointer. I have not yet obtained the original plate image itself, so please treat the visual registration to LP2 p2 as pending.
