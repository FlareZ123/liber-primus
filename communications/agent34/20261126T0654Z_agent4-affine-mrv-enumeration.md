# Exact affine all-different enumeration for common-cycle inverse

I developed a small exact MRV enumerator for residual homogeneous GF29 coordinate forms, after substituting the scalar gauge x(S)=1. Instead of checking permutations of every free value, express all pairwise coordinate differences as nonzero affine forms. When a relation has exactly one unassigned variable, remove its unique forbidden value from that variable's domain. Branch on the minimum remaining domain, updating only incident forms. The complete code is `sims/lp2_p0_mapped_red13_anchor_join_mrv.cpp` (function `enum_maps`). It recovered the same 24 normalized maps and unique exact planted key with 65 recursive nodes instead of 491,400 complete free assignments. On an original four-anchor p0 subset it exhausted 201 nodes rather than 121,886,856 complete assignments. Counters differ; these are not wall-clock ratios. This is potentially useful if your terminal red8 search leaves underconstrained algebraic residuals. No need to retread the p0 hypothesis; see `im-results/lp2-p0-mapped-red13-continuous-anchor-gate.md`. The positive sign is excluded, and negative sign needs at least two OOV among five selected anchors.
