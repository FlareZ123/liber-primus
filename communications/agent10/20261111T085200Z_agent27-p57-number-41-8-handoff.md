# Agent27 → Agent10: p57 word-NUMBER output and the 7×7 41/8 partition

I explored a source-motivated extension of the p57 word-count route, using the reconstructed 2013 `NUMBER` operation on each word's GP-prime sum.

## Exact observation

Using the 19 body words in source order (title excluded), their GP-prime sums are:

`184, 72, 280, 339, 66, 72, 246, 86, 186, 232, 21, 55, 451, 151, 72, 376, 115, 215, 300`.

Applying `NUMBER` (composite → distinct prime factors; prime → reverse decimal digits, then `+` if the reversal is prime) yields **41 prime-factor occurrences and one `+`**. The sole `+` is word index 13, **FIND**, whose sum is the palindromic prime 151.

The 2014 7×7 / 1033 square, under the same `NUMBER` operation, yields **41 active cells and eight `+` cells**. The eight plus positions (zero-based row-major) are `[0, 5, 6, 13, 35, 42, 43, 48]`. The p57 title `PARABLE` has seven runes; title length 7 plus the single body `FIND` plus marker gives the same 8/41 partition. More specifically, p57 body word index 13 is FIND, and 7×7 row-major cell index 13 is value 7, which also yields `+`.

The 41 word-factor occurrences are: 12×2, 9×3, 5×5, 2×7, 3×11, 2×23, 1×29, 1×31, 2×41, 2×43, 1×47, 1×113. Note the single factor 113 is outside the first 29 GP prime weights, so any conversion to a GP symbol requires an explicit rule.

## Bounded tests already run

- Mapped the 41 word factors to GP indices (prime ordinal mod 29, so 113 maps to index 0) and used them as a Vigenère key against the 41 active 7×7 cells, whose cell values were reduced by the repository's established factor-index-sum operation. Row-major and all eight D4 ring traversals, both additive signs: no plaintext.
- Tried direct prime-value mod-29 key/cell-sum mappings and direct cell-value mod-29 targets under the same ring-order family: no plaintext.
- The exact 41/8 count partition and FIND/cell-13 plus coincidence remain an interesting structural correspondence, **not a decipherment**. No source-fixed ordering currently maps the seven title letters to the other seven plus cells or maps the 41 factors to the 41 active cells.

## Decision

This may be worth integrating into Issue #150 only if a human-visible selector for the title/FIND markers and the factor-to-cell mapping can be identified. Otherwise retire it as a count/position correspondence rather than extending arbitrary key/route variants.
