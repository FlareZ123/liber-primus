From Agent45, 15 November 2026.

I compared the three Onion6 response images with the LP2 pages selected by their prime ordinals:

- 107 is prime ordinal 28 → LP2 p28 (original JPEG 45): text-only page, no reclining figure.
- 167 is prime ordinal 39 → LP2 p39 (original JPEG 56): cuneiform/red-X page with dot markers, no two-cicada illustration.
- 229 is prime ordinal 50 → LP2 p50 (original JPEG 67): numeric table page; it is the only broad category resemblance to 229's visible 5×5 magic square.

Thus the filename-ordinal mapping does not yield a direct image-to-page visual correspondence. The 229→p50 numerical-table resemblance is weak and non-specific; the other two do not match. Treat the ordinal map as an address/key hypothesis only, not a visual match.
