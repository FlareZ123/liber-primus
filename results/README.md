# Liber Primus: established state and research frontier

**Current status (13 November 2026).** All 56 unresolved LP2 physical pages p0–p55 remain undeciphered. The 55 rune-bearing pages contain **12,956 original ciphertext runes** across **2,896 printed words**; p50 contains no runes. No authenticated unknown-page plaintext, recovered creator key, complete original-cipher inverse, or verified final Cicada destination has been found. An exact numerical fit, decoded synthetic control, or self-consistent round trip is not a decipherment.

This is the **authoritative, decision-focused research entry point**. It consolidates the strongest findings and their limits, rather than reprinting each past experiment. The prior full-length synthesis is preserved as a [dated research archive](../im-results/archive-results-readme-20261113-before-critic-consolidation.md); its older claims must be read through the corrected conclusions here. Exact tests and raw evidence remain in linked reports, source files and [the active inversion challenge](../workplace/integrated-original-inversion-challenge.md).

## 1. Authenticated plaintext and known methods

- **Solved controls:** LP2 p56 is genuinely solved under the historical prime-totient cipher; p57 is 95 clear GP runes. The authentic cipher has a **transparent plaintext-F skip**: plaintext F emits F without consuming a key symbol, while a ciphertext F may also be produced by an encrypted non-F rune. A known-key lexical Viterbi recovers 710/710 positions, including 14 literal-F bypasses, on original LP1 WELCOME, LP2 p56 and a separate-key LP1 koan. This is a known-key decoder and **does not find an unknown LP2 key**. [Method, SFT and scope](../im-results/lp2-ftransparent-word-lexical-viterbi.md).
- **Original language spelling matters:** Solved LP1 and p57 source orthography includes single GP runes for ING and IO. Comparisons built from naïve letter-to-rune transcription can produce invalid exclusions; use the authenticated source-calibrated GP29 encoder and distinguish it from inventory-label encodings. [Original source result](lp1-source-orthography.md).
- **Historical continuation:** The seventh 2014 onion, *ky2khlqdf7qdznac.onion*, distributed the Liber Primus images. It is **not known to be** the deep-web page described in p56. That solved page prints the 128-hex target **36367763ab73783c7af284446c59466b4cd653239a311cb7116d4618dee09a8425893dc7500b464fdaf1672d7bef5e891c6e2274568926a49fb4f45132c2a8b4**. A period-original exact-byte page match could supply missing instructions or key material; the preimage and hash algorithm remain unresolved. [Original-source/archive assessment](../im-results/archive-hash-preimage.md).
- **Historical 2013 source material:** Cicada's own 761.MP3 ID3 text carrier contains the complete later-solved p57 PARABLE. Its clause prime sums multiply to the associated 1,595,277,641 field value. This is a verified cross-round text correspondence, **not** a recovered cipher seed. Cicada's 2013 protocol explicitly used BASE29, and earlier stages used compressed payloads. Neither fact by itself instructs a BASE29/GZIP LP2 decipherment. [Source-register benchmark](../im-results/source-register-benchmark.md) and [historical protocol](../resources/external/2013-tcp-server-signed-source.md).

## 2. Cryptanalytic constraints that affect the next attack

**The ciphertext is strikingly unlike ordinary GP-native prose.** Its 12,956 rune values are nearly uniform; only **86 of 12,901 same-physical-page adjacent positions** repeat the preceding rune. Original-source inspection has now localized all **86 adjacent doublets** and all **83 one-rune printed words** with no image-supported transcription corrections. This anti-repeat pattern spans word boundaries, although neither a no-repeat encoder nor a plaintext language is authenticated. [Source-pixel audit](../im-results/lp2-original-ink-critical-site-audit.md) · [boundary analysis](../im-results/lp2-word-boundary-doublet-analysis.md).

**Several broad families have genuinely strong, but conditional, exclusions.** In each case, an unknown bijective alphabet preserves a specified equality or frequency statistic regardless of its secret key, and the untouched LP2 source falls outside a finite, source-calibrated reference set. The strongest decisions include:

- Reused page-local and chapter-local **same-offset secret alphabet** maps, including fixed page-reset additive pads, fail cross-page/chapter equality tests on thousands of length-matched GP English windows. This says nothing about independently keyed pages, other clocks or transposition. [Page-offset gate](../im-results/lp2-pageoffset-allsecret-reuse-gate.md) · [chapter-offset gate](../im-results/lp2-chapteroffset-allsecret-reuse-gate.md).
- Every independent, **position-preserving per-word secret alphabet** fails a finite native-English word-pattern test on 39 of the 102 original 10–12-rune words. Within-word transposition or altered printed-word boundaries can remove this necessary-condition obstruction, so it is not a proof against all linguistic plaintexts. [Original word-pattern test](../im-results/lp2-perword-alphabet-pattern-allsecret-gate.md).
- Fixed secret **four- or five-rune block substitution** under tested page-local clocks has invariant repeated-block patterns inconsistent with source-native reference windows. Independently, the p15-adjacent p16–22 section defeats the bounded **input- and output-feedback Hill4** families, even when the 4×4 key is unknown under the specified source-native reference register. Those results do not exclude changing-state ciphers, a different register or alternative block boundaries. [Block test](../im-results/lp2-p15-fourblock-allsecret-collision-gate.md) · [CBC test](../im-results/lp2-p15-cbc4-source-difference-gate.md).
- Explicit word-position cumulative-difference encoders, arbitrary phase alphabets and fixed or shared alphabet maps have further source-native entropy/equality exclusions. **Only their exact state clocks, conditioning and reference-language assumptions are excluded.** [Differential-map gates](../im-results/lp2-wordposition-delta-allsecret-page-gate.md) · [period16 source gate](../im-results/lp2-p15-period16-allsecret-source-gate.md).

**The historical plaintext-F exception is a much stronger local word constraint than a total-count constraint.** A ciphertext F need not be plaintext F. The 458 unknown ciphertext Fs permit the overall F counts of all 562,060 tested GP-English windows, correcting an earlier erroneous exclusion. But with the authentic solved-page plaintext-F bypass and unchanged printed word segmentation, **only ten of the original 448 two-rune ciphertext words end in F**. Every hypothetical native-GP plaintext OF or IF word must occupy one of these ten slots; word-length-matched Shakespeare, Gibran and Emerson reference registers contain approximately **47–76** F-ending two-rune words per 448, making this a strong **reference-conditional constraint on transferring p56's cipher to unsolved pages**. The single actual solved p56 OF is correctly preserved in its ciphertext and re-encodes exactly. A separate conditional probability calculation also disfavors independent uniform per-rune keys with authentic F bypass. Neither calculation excludes a different unknown cipher, modified plaintext-word boundaries, or unusual language. [Corrected total F report](../im-results/lp2-literal-f-fullbook-native-capacity.md) · [new two-rune original-source gate](../im-results/lp2-ftransparent-two-rune-word-end-f-capacity.md).

**These are reference-class tests.** Zero of N windows matching an invariant is not a theorem about arbitrary unknown English passages or arbitrary transformed plaintext. A 12,956-symbol synthetic positive plant demonstrates implementation power under its planted model; it cannot choose a historical model. For any true bijective cipher, wrong-key decode followed by the matching encoder exactly reproduces the ciphertext. Independent, source-derived key/state constraints and actual plaintext are mandatory.

## 3. Source clues with specific possible cryptographic uses

**P15 number square and p39 red triad.** Physical p15 prints a 4×4 numerical square. Its sixteen magnitudes are reconstructed exactly by a Fibonacci-indexed prime spiral using 3301; this is reproducible mathematical source structure. Restoring the *two unprinted negative signs* inferred from that arithmetic produces a GF(29) matrix whose characteristic polynomial is *(x−8)(x−17)(x−24)^2*. The independently printed p39 red triad gives *15(x−24)^2*, sharing the double root. Only one of 120 possible two-sign placements produces that double root, and a row-preserving null also makes the overlap uncommon **conditional on a retrospectively chosen operation**. No original instruction specifies sign restoration, characteristic-polynomial matching, a key clock or decryption operand. The marked red-cell matrix-power stream has period 28 rather than the full matrix order 812, and its frozen direct original inverse fails. Direct 4-rune CBC, four-page Hill, stationary 16-rune cyclic Hill and changing matrix-power candidates have also failed on their specific original intervals. Further arbitrary phase/coordinate sweeps are low-value without a newly authored selector. [P15 construction](lp2-p15-fibonacci-prime-spiral.md) · [P15/p39 algebra and limits](../im-results/lp2-p15-p39-signed-matrix-eigen-crosslink.md) · [changing-matrix negative](../im-results/lp2-p15-p39-matrix-power-original-inverse.md) · [cyclic Hill16 negative](../im-results/lp2-p15-circulant16-source-inverse.md).

**P3/p39 red119 address observation and retired paired-pad interpretation.** Both original red three-rune spans begin at local rune offset 119. Their GP-prime sums are **137 = the 33rd prime** and **173 = the 40th prime**. Subtracting their respective one-based physical page numbers, 4 and 40, gives 133, also found as a historical HTML title. The arithmetic and proposed page pointers were recognized retrospectively; **no authored operation, decoded word or key follows**. The completed source-paired test of a shared GP29 additive pad at the four frozen zero/red119 alignments **p3↔p33 and p39↔p40** yields negative source-native English evidence on all four original differences, with passing same-pad native positive controls and unremarkable original cyclic-shift ranks. **Retire this specific same-clock two-time-pad interpretation**, while leaving the observed red glyph arithmetic unassigned; other clocks, registers and clue uses were not tested. [Original-input negative and controls](../im-results/lp2-red119-sourcepointed-two-timepad-original-gate.md) · [closed #785](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/785).

**PP49–51 numeric grid and terminal section.** The 256 two-character numeral cells occupy physical pp49–51 (80+104+72). Separately p55 has 76 unknown runes, followed by 85 solved p56 and 95 clear p57 runes, giving **256**. The table's 32×8 geometry, ring sizes 76/68/60/52 and p57's “shed our own circumferences” prose offer a visible layout conjecture. Many direct ring/table, polynomial, finite-field and 180-known-rune inverse consumers fail, even with secret parameters in their precisely tested families. **No source-chosen reversible map for all 76 p55 unknown runes exists yet**; blind validation of that page remains waiting. [Terminal source-input challenges](../im-results/lp2-terminal256-knowninterior-ring-gate.md) · [all-secret inner gates](../im-results/lp2-terminal256-original-inner-separable-gates.md) · [waiting decoder task #41](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/41).

**Visual segmentation is source-dependent.** Original image red-heading boundaries yield nine chapters with rune lengths **729, 1145, 1729, 1903, 1021, 1433, 1680, 3008, 308**. These cuts differ from derived titled-text units and are not proven key resets. The direct GP29 big-integer-to-file reading of the whole book and nine original visual chapters has no expected GZIP/BZIP2/XZ/PNG/ZIP/PDF signatures; a more elaborate encoded container is not ruled out. [Original visual map](../im-results/lp2-global-original-visual-section-map.md) · [direct file gate](../im-results/lp2-original-chapter-base29-direct-file-gate.md).

## 4. Decisions with the shortest causal path to plaintext

1. **Recover original external continuation bytes or historical key material.** The p56 deep-web reference is a genuine instruction, so a dated original HTTP body matching its exact 128-hex digest is higher-value than another free-key sweep. The 2014 distributor host is not established as the hash target. The [July–August 2014 Common Crawl WARC/Tor2web test #786](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/786) remains an active public-byte route. The [KAIST 2017 study](../workplace/kaist-2017-tor-crawl-hash-target-recovery.md) has no identified *public* byte-preserving corpus; the [CNR 2017 public supplement](../im-results/2017-tor-warc-source-supplement-host-index.md) contains graph metadata without original WARC bodies. Neither outcome excludes an inaccessible original archive. The separately closed [SRI LIGHTS public-interface test #791](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/791) found only public software/documentation and tokenized index formats, without authenticated original response bytes; [EURECOM's May–June 2016 public-deposit test #793](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/793) likewise found no qualifying public response. EURECOM's documented PhantomJS delayed-page capture and script processing additionally mean that a future stored HTML page is **not automatically an on-wire response-body hash input**. These are bounded public-access findings, not negative corpus-coverage or digest results. Historical raw-body custody inquiries, including DWM/CFLW and the potential KAIST/S2W continuity lead, require [owner authorization #784](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/784). A textual excerpt, modern host list or normalized HTML cannot establish an exact-byte hash match. [Archive assessment](../im-results/archive-hash-preimage.md).
2. **Find a genuinely authored operation for a visible mathematical object.** The p15/p39 matrix coincidence and the 256-cell terminal grid constrain possible inputs but do **not** instruct a cipher. Progress requires an independent extraction coordinate, key/state, IV and punctuation/word clocks before scoring the unknown text. Prefer an original image clue or historically authenticated instruction over another unconstrained polynomial/phase search. [P15/p39 evidence](../im-results/lp2-p15-p39-signed-matrix-eigen-crosslink.md) · [integrated challenge](../workplace/integrated-original-inversion-challenge.md).
3. **Test a distinct lossless architecture only when its proposed encoder explains the source anomalies.** A new model must account for globally suppressed adjacent equals, its handling of all 29 runes, printed word and page transitions, and the 56-page source geometry. Document original-input heldout predictions; mere source-native reference-class difference is not original plaintext.

**Promotion rule.** A substantive solution claim requires exact original page/rune offsets, a source-supported key/operation and state clock, authentic GP or historically supported alternative orthography, coherent unknown plaintext on a meaningful contiguous interval, independent previously unselected original predictions, and exact forward re-encryption.
