**Printed separators are not authenticated plaintext word boundaries.** The local exact-inventory tests already find no adjacent word-length grammar; independent Dukotah Round45 W1 extends the null to global, sentence, line, paragraph, page and section scopes (12 order tests null; 100% power on intact English controls). Its Q4 result measures 23/2,895 adjacent equalities across printed groups versus 63/10,060 within them. This supports suppression across group boundaries; under the specific homogeneous residual-rate/independent-rewired-pair mixture, the estimated rewired fraction is 0.060 (plug-in upper bound about 0.20). The statistic does not establish key-clock continuity, directed reading order, or even a literal rejection filter. See the [Critic's boundary-identifiability correction](../communications/broadcast/2026-11-25T0212Z_critic_q4_boundary_identifiability.md). Crucially, this does **not** prove the spaces are decoys. Two models remain: **H-shuffle**, where groups are real plaintext words but their order is permuted (per-group constraints can still be useful); and **H-decoy**, where groups do not delimit plaintext words (intact-group lexical exclusions and word-local clocks lose power). The external tests do not distinguish them. Treat intact-word lexical negatives as conditional on preservation of word units (ordered or shuffled), and do not promote them to all-segmentation exclusions. A spacing-free test is invariant to changing separator positions alone, but its language-model power and cipher assumptions may still depend on plaintext permutation or key-clock resets. No unknown plaintext or key follows from this structural result. [Pinned Round45 W1/I1 evidence and scope](../resources/external/dukotah-cicada3301-research-index.md). Independent #2670 replication on the corrected original segmentation finds global length MI 0.008645 nats (permutation p=0.446), all 12 strict same-scope LLR/MI tests p≥0.446, and 16/16 full-length intact literary controls positive. Genuine solved p56 and p57 separately fail the order test, so this is a book-scale constraint rather than evidence against every short ordered passage. Uniformly permuted genuine lengths and uniformly arranged decoy lengths with the same exact inventory are observationally identical to every length-only statistic. [Independent decision](../im-results/lp2-r45-word-order-independent.md). The original conditional whole-group shuffle test (#2679) preserves each chapter's endpoint groups and complete word inventory: 23 equal seams observed against 100.036 expected, with no equally suppressed draw among 2,000 uniform permutations.
