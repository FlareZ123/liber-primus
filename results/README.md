## 2. Strongest cryptanalytic constraints

**The central anomaly is the low equality rate.** Across 12,956 runes, the original has 86 adjacent equalities (63 within printed groups, 23 across group boundaries) and near-uniform marginal frequencies. Distinguish the 12,901 within-physical-page adjacent opportunities from the 12,955 continuous-tape opportunities; the ordinary transition subset is 85/12,781. The T7 gap lag-one correlation is +0.380019689 (fixed-count hard-core null p=0.000500). Round45 analysis of the same rune-identical transcription reports that T7 survives chapter, page, page×13-dot, word/line-class and exact-word-length conditioning; the line-occupancy null p≈0.23 has power only 0.04, while the event-bearing-word-identity null p≈0.26 has unmeasured power. These controls do not explain away T7, but it remains a generation-process anomaly rather than a recovered cipher. The 1,649-rune figure is 400+712+537, not one gap; the longest single gap is 712. The p15 page-axis familywise p=0.07943 still does not select p15 as a switch location.

The p50 leading-zero mask places 33/86 events on the candidate predecessor phase. The exact within-group conditional tail is 0.000624, and exact circular shifts give fixed-predecessor p=0.00247 **if preselected**; however max-phase p=0.46820 and max 17-mask×256-phase family p=0.50918 show that the peak is ordinary after phase/predicate selection. The p0 body physically begins at offset13, but no first-party instruction links that layout boundary to a p50 clock or fixes the predecessor-cell convention, so this remains a conditional candidate, not a selector. The p0 13-rune red header and p49 13-dot delimiter motivated one diagnostic offset-13 test (phase242): prefix0 gives 29/86, but the five-prefix family at that phase is p=0.14881. The original-image adjudication establishes that p0's 13-dot mark is a layout delimiter, not a cipher initial state; 13-dot marks recur internally, terminally and at page start. Thus this does not select a p50 clock origin. Printed word-length order has no natural-language grammar (MI z=+0.429, p=0.3025, versus strong solved-prose controls); printed spaces/groups must not be assumed to be plaintext word boundaries.
