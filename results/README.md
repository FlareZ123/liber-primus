## 5. Research decisions with a path to actual decryption

1. **A source-constrained, reversible original-cipher inverse.** Freeze an independently justified key/state derivation from the original ink, page layout, 256-entry table or historical source material. Specify page/word resets and the treatment of all observed repeated outputs. A credible result must recover previously unfitted LP2 runes and **exactly re-encrypt** them. Use existing authentic solved-Cicada regressions and the [contextual conditional inverse](../sims/lag6_repair_contextual_inverse.cjs) as tools, with its architecture explicitly treated as provisional.

2. **Architecture discrimination before another large key sweep.** A proposed forward model should reproduce the *same heldout manuscript intervals* at the word-boundary, page-boundary, lag-five-cluster, lag-six, word-equality and printed-line levels. Report which features motivated its construction. A bounded original-JPEG test of inter-word dot geometry and vertical line placement across LP2 pp0–2, 16–21 and 40–48, with solved pp56–57 controls, found no independent spacing alphabet: 37 repeated exact rune-pair boundaries vary by at most 1 px within each pair, and 192/201 measured line intervals follow a 188–189 px grid; the remaining nine are enlarged paragraph intervals.
