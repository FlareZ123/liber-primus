# Liber Primus: current research state

**10 November 2026. The second release remains unsolved.** LP2 has 56 unresolved physical pages (0–55), including one rune-free numerical/table page (p50). The other 55 pages contain **12,956 ciphertext runes** arranged in **2,896 canonical words**. Pages 56–57 are solved historical controls. This repository has recovered **no unknown LP2 plaintext, authentic cipher key, complete cipher mechanism, or final destination**. An exact arithmetic relationship or a strong ciphertext anomaly is not a decipherment.

This entry point separates source facts, measured ciphertext properties, conditional exclusions, and prospective tests. Full methods, distributions, scripts, and failure cases remain in the linked supporting files. The original scans, [normalized ciphertext](../resources/liber-primus/lp2-corpus.json), [word/page parsing](../sims/lp2_word_pages.cjs), and [two-ink source annotation](../resources/liber-primus/lp2-ink-annotations.json) provide the input contract. Physical page numbers must not be confused with text-section indices.

## 2. Strongest manuscript-wide ciphertext constraints

**Boundary dependence.** The cipher has **86/12,901** equal adjacent rune pairs within pages, including **63/10,060** inside complete words and **23/2,887** between adjacent words within sections. The observed **23** cross-word equalities are far below the **99.806** expectation when original words are shuffled within sections. The deficit survives more constrained rearrangements. A mechanism must account for both within-word and across-word behavior, including [physical line breaks](../im-results/physical-linebreak-continuity.md). [Word-boundary experiment](../im-results/cross-word-anti-repeat-constraint.md).

**Page-turn ordinal-modulus signal (exploratory, source-width-sensitive).** The signed GP-index difference at consecutive physical rune-page endpoints falls in residues **12–17 mod 29** at **23/54** page turns. The original early/late page-disjoint window search selected 12–17 and scored **12/26** later hits (whole-page-order refit p≈**0.00467**; original-neighborhood local-displacement p≈**0.00298**). A newer original-JPEG width/section/word-status matched null reveals an important limitation: reselecting all 377 cyclic arcs on the matched early pages now picks **16–17**, yielding only **2** heldout hits, crossfit p≈**0.71549** over 15,000 matched nulls (width alone p≈**0.52103**). Holding the previously selected 12–17 arc fixed gives a weaker exact matched tail **0.03833**, with source selection uncorrected. The nulls answer different conditional questions; this is not an established page-key rule. [Integrated layout and countercheck](../im-results/physical-linebreak-continuity.md) · [Source-exact conditional crossfit](../sims/page_turn_width_word_matched_crossfit.cjs).

**Word-internal structure.** Among **1,536 complete words of length 4–9**, only **526 nonadjacent identical-rune pairs** occur. Matched solved-LP1/ordinary-English controls usually contain substantially more. Exact consequences depend on whether source words, spelling, segmentation, and transformations are preserved. A simple position-independent deterministic rune substitution per word cannot decrease within-word equality counts. The finding is a conditional exclusion of that plaintext model. [Invariant and source controls](../im-results/wordwise-equality-invariant.md). Adjacent *word-length* dependence is also unusually small; original spaces should not automatically be treated as plaintext spaces. [Word-length study](../im-results/wordlength-order.md).

**Two-register zero-step source gate (conditional).** An exactly reversible F29 chain can assign different *unknown* source-plaintext symbols to repeated ciphertext at word beginnings and elsewhere. Searching all **841 GP marker pairs** against length-matched native-English and Latin reference words shows how source pooling can mislead: the pooled three-role Latin Vulgate assignment **G/X** looks ordinary (deviance **1.64463**, maximal 3,000-bootstrap empirical tail **0.68244**), but retaining original **word lengths 1–7/8–12** makes even the best refitted Latin **L/AE** deviance **91.20436** and gives **zero of 3,000** equally extreme source-matched nulls for **every** candidate true pair (worst plus-one tail **1/3,001**). A competing source-independent residual-repeat model, conditioned on the same 86 events across 21 word-role/length cells, fits normally (**G² 21.6042**, 100,000-null upper tail **0.4756**). A finite 128-mode authentic source-derived AES128/field28 inverse passes a full Latin-GP native control but yields no readable original plaintext; 9/12 wrong-key controls score better heldout. These are exclusions under specified plaintext-source, segmentation and key families, not a discovered Cicada cipher or a rejection of shifted plaintext boundaries. [Unified source-conditioned report and three executable controls](../im-results/dual-marker-wordrole-source-gate.md).

**Singleton-word key capacity.** The **83** original one-rune ciphertext words contain **28** distinct glyphs. Even allowing any unknown bijective 29-rune alphabet independently on each physical page, an exact all-key bound forces **13/83** to violate native A/I singleton plaintext (book-fixed: **73/83**; source-section-fixed: **57/83**). A publicly word-ordinal-periodic alphabet requires at least period **130** if book-global, **28** if section-local, or **6** if page-local to escape this *singleton-only* necessary condition; those periods are not cipher discoveries. The independently tested **previous-ciphertext-keyed** model, also allowing an arbitrary hidden GP29 permutation per observed predecessor, forces **31/82** eligible A/I singleton violations under a book-global table and **2/82** under independently section-global tables; independently page-local feedback rows escape that singleton-only bound. These exact restrictions require more state, changed segmentation or a different plaintext register under the stated same-length models. [Exact bound, controls, and scope](../im-results/word-start-reset-and-periodicity.md#exact-one-rune-word-capacity-for-arbitrary-secret-alphabets).

**Word-local echoes.** Two post-selected patterns remain measurable: **80/1,290** eligible positions satisfy the lag-six affine rule `cᵢ = 24 − 2cᵢ₋₆ (mod 29)`, and **11** lag-five positions match ᛋ to ᛋ. The manuscript also has **nine contiguous two-hit lag-five events**. An eight-section crossfit of already-selected features gains **23.272631 nats** against its constrained word-interior shuffle baseline. These properties are reproducible, though their feature discovery used the manuscript, and the gain has no discovery-wide p-value. A **lossy M3 surrogate** is not rejected by the aggregate predictor (model-matched p ≈ **0.159**); its separate clustered and S-specific counts are inadequate. Neither surrogate is a recovered reversible cipher. **Bookend phrase and exact internal endpoint.** The exact adjacent 3+3-rune word pair **ᛞᛄᚢ ᛒᛖᛁ** occurs at global rune offsets **6,555–6,560** (ending at **6,561 = 3⁸ = 81²**) and the final **12,950–12,955**. It is the only repeated adjacent two-word type in the canonical unsolved text. The first three physical LP2 pages contain **729 = 3⁶** runes, and the suffix after 6,561 has **6,395 = 80²−5** runes. These retrospectively observed square counts propose a *hypothetical* 81×81/80×80 framing; they do not establish a cipher reset. Original-source Fourier constraints rule against arbitrary separable GP-English row/column additive pads in these layouts. A further exact **all-secret-alphabet cross-square bound** permits each of the source square, second-block ciphertext and decoded output to have its own unrestricted 29-symbol alphabet permutation: over all 256 crop/orientation pairings, the highest possible count of any one resulting GP rune is **395/6,395**. All 169 matched-length Shakespeare/Gibran GP-English source windows contain at least **657** E runes; thus such a direct combined-source cipher needs at least 262 additional symbol alterations before it could resemble even the weakest of these native-GP controls. The 6,395-rune synthetic planted key is exactly recovered. This is a source-original conditional exclusion, with no authenticated square boundary or historical key. A separate **reversible Fibonacci bilinear rank-one cipher**, allowing each square column an unrestricted secret key, also fails: Fibonacci modulo29 has exact period14, forcing five or more *unmasked* GP plaintext rows for every key and phase. Its decoder recovers both blinded original-length Shakespeare controls exactly (12,956/12,956 GP runes) but cannot produce source-like heldout original plaintext. More decisively, **all 16-row-periodic, column-specific secret 29-symbol permutation ciphers** preserve every rune-equality pair 16 square rows apart, independent of all 1,296/1,280 arbitrary hidden alphabet keys. Authentic LP2 has only **151/5,265** such row-pair equalities in the 81² block and **178/5,115** in the terminal block; independent GP-literary windows have minima **265 and 267** under the same geometric tests. These are conditional exclusions of two candidate cipher families, not recoveries or proof of the retrospective square layout. A proposed **6,395-rune synchronized key recurrence** also failed. A new [source-original nonlinear stencil bound](../im-results/lp2-repeated-wordpair-structure.md) exhausts 8 orientations, 15 local 2×2 corner masks and every placement of a contiguous five-cell omission: even permitting two secret lookup tables and 86 arbitrary changes, any stationary transform depending on at most three source corners requires **at least 378 edits**. A separate **all-gap GF29 polynomial rank witness** excludes every full-four-corner source transform of total degree≤3 even with **165** alterations and completely independent secret polynomial keys per 36-rune block. A fully unrestricted four-corner map with arbitrary non-collision changes remains open. A separate **exact, key-independent predecessor-collision intersection gate** rejects *all* stationary arbitrary four-corner tables that redraw only when their first tentative output equals the preceding ciphertext rune: at least **10 contradictory source tuples** survive every isometry and five-cell gap, even granting independent secret tables on either side. The square framing remains hypothetical. These exclusions concern specific assumed square layouts and plaintext registers, not a discovered 6,561 cipher reset. ## 3. Clue-linked candidates: genuinely open, with bounded direct failures

**Historical Parable-prime source: all-secret-state period restriction.** A source predating LP2, Cicada's 2013 `AUDIO/761.MP3`, contains the exact number **1,595,277,641 = 1,259 × 1,031 × 1,229**, subsequently matching the solved *PARABLE* clause prime sums. This historical relationship does not establish a cipher. If the full number were used as a modular-squaring keystream modulus, a CRT group-order theorem forces **every possible secret starting state** to become periodic within **1,224 square steps** after at most two initial steps; an eight-parity-bit-per-rune stream would have a 153-rune period. Original unsolved LP2 has **416/12,801 = 0.03250** same-rune matches at lag153 after excluding the two transient positions, versus **0.06038** in independently sampled English GP control. Transparent-EA field-step ranks also lack the English equality signal. A complete **5,201,280-candidate** two-prime start search, an additional **2,600,640-candidate** hidden keyskip screen, and **29,459,892** direct historical decoded-waveform sample-offset tests all failed to recover unknown text while passing source-native concealed-key English recovery tests. The exact theorem restricts **only direct synchronized additive/field-step native-GP output**. Hidden state advance, non-English intermediates, distinct cipher families and external continuations remain open. ## 5. Research decisions with a path to actual decryption

1. **A source-constrained, reversible original-cipher inverse.** Freeze an independently justified key/state derivation from the original ink, page layout, 256-entry table or historical source material. Specify page/word resets and the treatment of all observed repeated outputs. A credible result must recover previously unfitted LP2 runes and **exactly re-encrypt** them. Use existing authentic solved-Cicada regressions and the [contextual conditional inverse](../sims/lag6_repair_contextual_inverse.cjs) as tools, with its architecture explicitly treated as provisional.

2. **Architecture discrimination before another large key sweep.** A proposed forward model should reproduce the *same heldout manuscript intervals* at the word-boundary, page-boundary, lag-five-cluster, lag-six, word-equality and printed-line levels. Report which features motivated its construction. The current lossy repair surrogates can match some selected summaries without yielding a decryptable inverse. Require a recoverable hidden state or a mathematically lossless encoding.

3. **Recoverable missing historical input.** The solved end text points to an unresolved 64-byte hash target. Bounded searches of archived Cicada-stage assets and documented candidate encodings are preferable to unconstrained guessed URLs. A verified digest match needs exact bytes and a specified algorithm before anyone infers an LP2 key. [Source-aware historic candidate search](../im-results/archive-hash-preimage.md).

4. **Alternative source language or segmentation only with a predictive decoder.** Low word-order dependence, word-internal repeat suppression, and genuine author-vocabulary mismatch make the plaintext register an open variable. Specify a reversible symbol/word-boundary map and test it on untouched original material rather than maximizing a dictionary score on the same source-selected patterns.

**Promotion rule:** a new unknown plaintext claim needs original ciphertext offsets, an explicit alphabet, an independently justified algorithm and key/state, a complete round-trip encoder/decoder, untrained original-page predictions, and documented failure controls. Preserve negative results under `im-results/` with their exact finite scope. Promote robust general findings here only when they materially change the cryptanalytic frontier.
