# Liber Primus: established state and research frontier

**Current as of 22 November 2026, following closure of Director issues #2162 and #2166–#2175.** All **56 unknown LP2 physical pages p0–p55 remain undeciphered**. No authenticated new-page plaintext, creator-selected key, complete unknown-page encryption mechanism, or confirmed final Cicada destination has been recovered. Short English outputs and elaborate numerical correspondences remain conditional candidates unless a source-constrained decoder predicts additional original ciphertext. A bijective cipher can perfectly reencrypt wrong-key gibberish.

This is the **authoritative short synthesis**. The full pre-consolidation research account, including the numerous restricted negative tests, links and historical interpretations through the 05:17Z Director checkpoint, is preserved verbatim in [the 22 November research archive](../im-results/archive-results-readme-20261122-before-critic-closure-consolidation.md). The [integrated inversion challenge](../workplace/integrated-original-inversion-challenge.md) tracks candidate architectures and source links. Detailed experiments remain under [im-results](../im-results/) with reusable programs under [sims](../sims/). Those materials preserve hypotheses and failed tests; their presence is not proof that any unknown text was deciphered.

## 2. Observed cryptanalytic constraints

**Unusual repetition and preserved word structure.** The original rune-bearing pages have **86 within-page adjacent-equal events** across 12,956 runes; within-word lag-five equalities are enriched under some conditioned baselines. These observations are legitimate prompts to investigate feedback, constrained encoding, or multiple symbol classes, but they are **not evidence by themselves of a forbidden-adjacency cipher, a period-five key, or a particular plaintext language**. Source-native solved-text controls and boundary-preserving nulls are necessary to distinguish these explanations. See [cross-page exact-repeat SFT](../sims/lp2_crosspage_exact_repeat_external_claim_sft.cjs), [word-pair null](../im-results/lp2-repeated-wordpair-structure.md), and the source-led ongoing [issue #2083](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/2083).

**Source markers encode a visible reading grammar.** The current original-image census identifies **31 thirteen-dot marks**, **four ten-dot marks**, and the sole terminal 23-dot mark at p57. Of the thirteen-dot marks, 22 meet a red-rune boundary, eight sit beyond the final page rune, and one p15 mark separates adjacent red spans. The ten-dot marks occur at p36:99, p38:186, p49:66 and solved p56:45; p49's mark precedes the p50 table. On solved p56, the verified keystream traverses the internal marks without extra symbol consumption or reset. Therefore placement and red-ink relationships explain much of the morphology; a **new cipher-state switch remains unselected**. [Director #2174](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/2174); [solved punctuation clock](../im-results/lp2-punctuation-solved-clock.md).

**Arbitrary secret states are weak evidence.** For any invertible GP29 family, choosing a key and decoding the original ciphertext followed by reencoding with that same key necessarily round-trips, even if the decoded runes are random. A synthetic positive validates the implementation's intended branch but does not identify the author's algorithm. Language scores can serve as rejection diagnostics only when the prior model, candidate multiplicity, source language and withheld material are stated. High-dimensional fitted keys require independently sourced constraints and meaningful long-span predictions.

## 3. Strongest still-conditional original-source routes

- **Opening and p28 navigation.** The literal initial DH runes on p28, 2016 signed word/direction guidance, and solved p57 lead to nontrivial numeric and receiver coincidences involving DH23, the p50 square, p0 and the local candidate **READ OUT**. The inferred conversion from historical word ordinals to LP2 page/word addresses and the local H/cube key grammar are not fully source-selected; direct whole-text transfers are nonprose. Preserve the finite observations as navigation hypotheses rather than unknown plaintext. [Integrated route and alternative paths](../workplace/integrated-original-inversion-challenge.md), [p0 reciprocal transfer gate](../im-results/lp2-readout-p0-center-reciprocal-transfer-gate.md).
- **Other isolated plaintext-like strings.** P0 **WILL** has a finite receiver/key correspondence but its prospective successor fails. P0 **DEVOTED** is a source-conditioned candidate with at least 29⁴ residual key freedoms and lexical rivals. P31 **AND** and p33 **YOU** use a constrained layout and p56-serializer construction, but the page/receiver assumptions and local three-rune key are not a validated long decoder. Do not promote their exact letters as recovered unknown plaintext. [WILL and related audits in archive](../im-results/archive-results-readme-20261122-before-critic-closure-consolidation.md), [DEVOTED counterexamples](../im-results/lp2-p0-devoted-adjacent-gp0-decoder.md), [p31/p33 route](../im-results/lp2-p31-and-p33-you-rise-will.md).
- **P50 as a possible state or instruction carrier.** The 256 cell values, 32×8 geometry and 76 perimeter cells are authentic, as are reproducible algebraic coincidences involving GF257 roots, DH parameters and ASCII-like outputs. However, no contemporaneous instruction specifies a numeral radix, address convention, GP29 serialization, state update, or key schedule that decrypts both original runic flanks or an untouched chapter. P50's strongest unresolved role is **what operation its visibly unusual insertion instructs**, if any. [Table and adjacent-flank gate](../im-results/lp2-p50-wordclock-eighttap-flank-original-gate.md).
- **P56 and the terminal continuation.** The known SHA-512 is a validator of candidate outside bytes, not a reversible hash preimage or automatically a keystream. Multiple proposed p55 43/33 tree, ring, hash-digit and 76-symbol transformations do reencrypt their own decoded output, but original results fail the native-language/source-operator gates. [Terminal source decision #2173](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/2173), [waiting external verifier #41](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/41).

The creator's intended human solve-path remains unknown. Missing or unarchived historical materials could supply an essential instruction or key; the absence of such material does not establish that the manuscript is random or unbreakable. Pursue a specific first-party artifact, uniquely motivated read operation, or complete original-text result instead of proliferating loosely related arithmetic.

## 5. Practical path to an actual solution

The next meaningful advance must supply at least one of these: **(a)** an authenticated creator-selected operand, key or period-readable instruction; **(b)** a complete reversible mapping that produces native-language plaintext over a substantial contiguous original interval and predicts original material withheld from its selection; **(c)** original external bytes that match p56's target digest and reveal a real continuation. Once found, perform exact original-input decoding and forward replay, publish the key and clock, and test a second independently selected page or chapter.

Use original images and the canonical page/rune mapping as immutable inputs. Retire model-specific negatives only within their exact scope. Preserve genuine surprises even when no existing task predicted them. Do not mistake a large number of closed issues, workflow executions, back-fitted short words, or perfect synthetic reencryption for progress.
