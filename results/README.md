# Liber Primus: established state and research frontier

**Current status (14 November 2026).** All 56 unresolved LP2 physical pages p0–p55 remain undeciphered. The 55 rune-bearing pages contain **12,956 original ciphertext runes** across **2,896 authentic printed words**, of which **32** continue across physical page boundaries; p50 contains no runes. No authenticated unknown-page plaintext, recovered creator key, complete original-cipher inverse, or verified final Cicada destination has been found. An exact numerical fit, decoded synthetic control, or self-consistent round trip is not a decipherment.

This is the **authoritative, decision-focused research entry point**. It consolidates the strongest findings and their limits, rather than reprinting each past experiment. The prior full-length synthesis is preserved as a [dated research archive](../im-results/archive-results-readme-20261113-before-critic-consolidation.md); its older claims must be read through the corrected conclusions here. Exact tests and raw evidence remain in linked reports, source files and [the active inversion challenge](../workplace/integrated-original-inversion-challenge.md).

## 1. Authenticated plaintext and known methods

- **Solved controls:** LP2 p56 is genuinely solved under the historical prime-totient cipher; p57 is 95 clear GP runes. The authentic cipher has a **transparent plaintext-F skip**: plaintext F emits F without consuming a key symbol, while a ciphertext F may also be produced by an encrypted non-F rune. A known-key lexical Viterbi recovers 710/710 positions, including 14 literal-F bypasses, on original LP1 WELCOME, LP2 p56 and a separate-key LP1 koan. This is a known-key decoder and **does not find an unknown LP2 key**. [Method, SFT and scope](../im-results/lp2-ftransparent-word-lexical-viterbi.md).
- **Original language spelling matters:** Solved LP1 and p57 source orthography includes single GP runes for ING and IO. Comparisons built from naïve letter-to-rune transcription can produce invalid exclusions; use the authenticated source-calibrated GP29 encoder and distinguish it from inventory-label encodings. [Original source result](lp1-source-orthography.md).
- **Historical continuation:** The seventh 2014 onion, *ky2khlqdf7qdznac.onion*, distributed the Liber Primus images. It is **not known to be** the deep-web page described in p56. That solved page prints the 128-hex target **36367763ab73783c7af284446c59466b4cd653239a311cb7116d4618dee09a8425893dc7500b464fdaf1672d7bef5e891c6e2274568926a49fb4f45132c2a8b4**. A period-original exact-byte page match could supply missing instructions or key material; the preimage and hash algorithm remain unresolved. [Original-source/archive assessment](../im-results/archive-hash-preimage.md).
- **Historical 2013 source material:** Cicada's own 761.MP3 ID3 text carrier contains the complete later-solved p57 PARABLE. ## 2. Cryptanalytic constraints that affect the next attack

**The ciphertext is strikingly unlike ordinary GP-native prose.** Its 12,956 rune values are nearly uniform; only **86 of 12,901 same-physical-page adjacent positions** repeat the preceding rune. Original-source inspection has now localized all **86 adjacent doublets** and all **83 one-rune printed words** with no image-supported transcription corrections. This anti-repeat pattern spans word boundaries, although neither a no-repeat encoder nor a plaintext language is authenticated. A separate **original-source-conditioned** analysis refines an independently reported doublet-placement anomaly: the Pearson lag-one correlation of the 85 doublet-site gaps is **+0.3800**. Holding the nine visible chapter counts fixed leaves the original in the exploratory upper **0.000075** of source-valid random placements, while holding exact per-page counts raises the null mean to **+0.2300** but leaves an upper tail near **0.0050**. Physical-page heterogeneity explains much of the effect, and separately preserving the printed **upper/lower page-half** counts raises the null mean to **+0.3119** (upper tail **0.0383**). Conditioning on the event counts on each actual printed line raises it to **+0.3730**, with unexceptional original upper-tail **0.232**; that line-conditioned model is highly saturated (594 strata, 86 events), so it locates the pattern in *line occupancy* without proving a line-state cipher. Fixed page×printed-word-boundary counts leave the same residual as page counts alone. This remains an exploratory generator-model constraint without a source-selected key or decrypted rune. [Source-pixel audit](../im-results/lp2-original-ink-critical-site-audit.md) · [boundary analysis](../im-results/lp2-word-boundary-doublet-analysis.md) · [source-conditioned T7 test](../im-results/lp2-doublet-gap-original-source-conditioned.md).

**Several broad families have genuinely strong, but conditional, exclusions.** In each case, an unknown bijective alphabet preserves a specified equality or frequency statistic regardless of its secret key, and the untouched LP2 source falls outside a finite, source-calibrated reference set. The strongest decisions include:

- Reused page-local and chapter-local **same-offset secret alphabet** maps, including fixed page-reset additive pads, fail cross-page/chapter equality tests on thousands of length-matched GP English windows. This says nothing about independently keyed pages, other clocks or transposition. [Page-offset gate](../im-results/lp2-pageoffset-allsecret-reuse-gate.md) · [chapter-offset gate](../im-results/lp2-chapteroffset-allsecret-reuse-gate.md).
- **Deterministic stationary whole-word encoders**, including a codebook rekeyed once per original page, cannot explain ordinary repeated-word GP-native prose under the original printed segmentation: all **1,645 original length≥4 words are distinct**, the most frequent of any **2,896 original words** occurs only **five** times, and across 55 rune-bearing physical pages only **four page-local repeat pairs** exist. With identical page word counts, five source-native English/Old English/Latin references have at least **195–1,194** page-internal repeat pairs in 1,200 fixed-seed draws each. A static encoder must map equal plaintext words to equal ciphertext words, regardless of its secret codebook. This excludes a finite natural-text reference class, while allowing deliberately nonrepeating plaintext, altered segmentation, changing nonce/state or other ciphers. [Original word-invariant gate and SFT](../im-results/lp2-global-wholeword-equality-source-gate.md).
- Every independent, **position-preserving per-word secret alphabet** fails a finite native-English word-pattern test on 39 of the 102 original 10–12-rune words. Within-word transposition or altered printed-word boundaries can remove this necessary-condition obstruction, so it is not a proof against all linguistic plaintexts. A separate source-frozen **global monoalphabetic Old English** gate also fails under intact word segmentation: 83 original one-rune words use 28 distinct types, versus eight types across five original-language Old English registers; original IoC 0.0344784 is below all 126,383 comparable native windows (minimum 0.0535338). These are key-independent invariants of a single GP29 bijection, and do not constrain changing-state or resegmented Old English. [Source and controls](../im-results/lp2-old-english-global-monoalphabetic-gate.md). [Original word-pattern test](../im-results/lp2-perword-alphabet-pattern-allsecret-gate.md).
- **Ciphertext-predecessor-indexed GP29 substitution rows**, with arbitrary secret permutations independently per previous rune and physical page, preserve an observable within-row collision statistic. Original page-keyed collision rates are **0.03553** (all within-page pairs) and **0.03535** (within-word); **144** native-GP word-length-matched English controls under fully reversible, doublet-suppressing secret row ciphers all score **at least 0.05881** and **0.06999** respectively. The source's conditional distribution therefore resists a simple static predecessor-state alphabet in this calibrated reference class, even though its singleton words alone permit such a model. This is **not** an all-keys/all-languages theorem: adversarial source-specific tables and more evolving state remain untested. [Original inverse-invariant test and controls](../im-results/lp2-previous-cipher-row-collision-allsecret-gate.md).
- Fixed secret **four- or five-rune block substitution** under tested page-local clocks has invariant repeated-block patterns inconsistent with source-native reference windows. Independently, the p15-adjacent p16–22 section defeats the bounded **input- and output-feedback Hill4** families, even when the 4×4 key is unknown under the specified source-native reference register. Those results do not exclude changing-state ciphers, a different register or alternative block boundaries. [Block test](../im-results/lp2-p15-fourblock-allsecret-collision-gate.md) · [CBC test](../im-results/lp2-p15-cbc4-source-difference-gate.md).
- **The proposed 41×316 matrix cannot be decoded into any of 88 source-native English windows by wholly secret stationary GP29 substitution alphabets** even when allowing an independent permutation for each of **41 rows, 41 opposite-orientation phase classes, or all 316 actual columns**. The original within-class equal-symbol pair counts are **69,661 / 70,220 / 8,916**, versus source-native minima **119,491 / 117,225 / 14,850**. More decisively, the maximum possible number of any one GP29 plaintext rune under arbitrary stationary class alphabets is exactly **726 / 746 / 1,346** for the three layouts, whereas all 88 GP-English controls contain **at least 1,428 literal E runes**. This is an exact key-independent capacity bound on the original ciphertext, including every secret alphabet choice per class. The 41×316 source selector itself is retrospective, and transposition, evolving state, altered register and unusual plaintext remain possible. [Consolidated source-shaped test and Windows SFT](../im-results/a194954_41x316_direct_key_gate.md).
- Explicit word-position cumulative-difference encoders, arbitrary phase alphabets and fixed or shared alphabet maps have further source-native entropy/equality exclusions. **Only their exact state clocks, conditioning and reference-language assumptions are excluded.** [Differential-map gates](../im-results/lp2-wordposition-delta-allsecret-page-gate.md) · [period16 source gate](../im-results/lp2-p15-period16-allsecret-source-gate.md).

**The historical plaintext-F exception is a much stronger local word constraint than a total-count constraint.** A ciphertext F need not be plaintext F. The 458 unknown ciphertext Fs permit the overall F counts of all 562,060 tested GP-English windows, correcting an earlier erroneous exclusion. But with the authentic solved-page plaintext-F bypass and unchanged printed word segmentation, **only ten of the original 448 two-rune ciphertext words end in F**. Every hypothetical native-GP plaintext OF or IF word must occupy one of these ten slots; word-length-matched Shakespeare, Gibran and Emerson reference registers contain approximately **47–76** F-ending two-rune words per 448, making this a strong **reference-conditional constraint on transferring p56's cipher to unsolved pages**. The single actual solved p56 OF is correctly preserved in its ciphertext and re-encodes exactly. A separate conditional probability calculation also disfavors independent uniform per-rune keys with authentic F bypass. Neither calculation excludes a different unknown cipher, modified plaintext-word boundaries, or unusual language. [Corrected total F report](../im-results/lp2-literal-f-fullbook-native-capacity.md) · [new two-rune original-source gate](../im-results/lp2-ftransparent-two-rune-word-end-f-capacity.md).

**These are reference-class tests.** Zero of N windows matching an invariant is not a theorem about arbitrary unknown English passages or arbitrary transformed plaintext. A 12,956-symbol synthetic positive plant demonstrates implementation power under its planted model; it cannot choose a historical model. For any true bijective cipher, wrong-key decode followed by the matching encoder exactly reproduces the ciphertext. Independent, source-derived key/state constraints and actual plaintext are mandatory.

## 4. Decisions with the shortest causal path to plaintext

1. **Recover original external continuation bytes or independently selected historical key material.** Solved p56 gives a 128-hex digest of an unidentified deep-web page, whose exact body and hash/serialization rule remain unknown. The 2014 Stage11 distributor onion is **not established** as that target. New [Arquivo.pt FAWP17 research](../im-results/archive-hash-preimage.md) has **recovered 261,198 payload bytes of an independent 27 April 2014 page** and reproduced its archived SHA-1/base32 digest exactly: this establishes a functioning period-original byte-preserving replay source, **not** capture of the unknown target. All **31 successful** source-pinned Cicada onion-host prefix CDX queries had zero captures (one further query failed transport), leaving the unknown target-host input unselected ([closed #814](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/814)). A proposed source-dated *inbound-link* discovery cannot yet be run on Arquivo's public inverted-link graph: its published source snapshots cover **1996–2009 and 2021, not 2014–2016** ([closed #818](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/818)). This is a **coverage/access limit**, not proof that the historical target never existed. Earlier public 2014 Common Crawl, Ahmia, OnionCity and 2016 academic-archive searches likewise yield only [bounded negative or metadata findings](../im-results/archive-hash-preimage.md); EURECOM's processed HTML may differ from on-wire bytes. An original target URL, date-authenticated unmodified HTTP body or independent author-selected hash/input procedure would change the decision immediately. Privately held raw-body custody inquiries still require [owner approval #784](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/784); no live Tor probing or unanchored hostname enumeration is justified. [Archive assessment](../im-results/archive-hash-preimage.md).
2. **Find a genuinely authored operation for a visible mathematical object.** The p15/p39 matrix coincidence and the 256-cell terminal grid constrain possible inputs but do **not** instruct a cipher. Progress requires an independent extraction coordinate, key/state, IV and punctuation/word clocks before scoring the unknown text. Prefer an original image clue or historically authenticated instruction over another unconstrained polynomial/phase search. [P15/p39 evidence](../im-results/lp2-p15-p39-signed-matrix-eigen-crosslink.md) · [integrated challenge](../workplace/integrated-original-inversion-challenge.md).
3. **Test a distinct lossless architecture only when its proposed encoder explains the source anomalies.** A new model must account for globally suppressed adjacent equals, its handling of all 29 runes, printed word and page transitions, and the 56-page source geometry. Document original-input heldout predictions; mere source-native reference-class difference is not original plaintext.

**Promotion rule.** A substantive solution claim requires exact original page/rune offsets, a source-supported key/operation and state clock, authentic GP or historically supported alternative orthography, coherent unknown plaintext on a meaningful contiguous interval, independent previously unselected original predictions, and exact forward re-encryption. Archive complete raw tests under [im-results](../im-results/) and reusable programs under [sims](../sims/); update this synthesis *by replacement* when the actual cryptanalytic frontier changes.
