# Full-manuscript blind solve-path test

You are given the complete original 58-image LP2 manuscript sequence in release order. Page numbers and current research hypotheses are intentionally withheld.

## Task

Using only these images and information that a player could reasonably have obtained by **11 May 2014**, investigate whether the manuscript itself selects a reproducible clue→operation path, including any source-derived operand/key, state or clock, and concrete continuation.

Do not assume that one operation must apply uniformly to every section. A route may be local or cross-page, but each transition must be independently selected by visible or period-authentic evidence.

If a route is selected, record:

1. Your first unprompted observations and the first clue that changed your next action.
2. The exact source clue for each transition, and why it selects the operation rather than a plausible rival.
3. The exact input, operand, key or seed, and the independent source from which each is obtained.
4. The complete operation(s): alphabet or representation, parameters, ordering, state/clock/reset rules, boundary handling, and any special-symbol behavior.
5. The exact predicted output or next object. State this before requesting or viewing any additional material.
6. One specific observation on an original source that would falsify each important transition.

If no unique route is selected, state the smallest missing source, value, or instruction that would make it executable. Distinguish a useful navigation clue from an actual decipherment of unknown text.

## Constraints

- Use no material first published after 11 May 2014.
- Do not use modern LP2 transcriptions, later plaintext interpretations, current research reports, or community hypotheses.
- Do not assume a cipher family, key source, visual traversal, number system, or uniform page partition in advance.
- Work from the images themselves; transcriptions may be created as working aids only after recording initial visual observations.
- Distinguish direct observation, mathematical derivation, historical source fact, and inference.
- Preserve dead ends and the point at which each candidate operation first becomes determined.
- If additional sources are necessary, identify the exact item and why; do not silently broaden the evidence set.

A useful result is a source-selected, reproducible path with locked predictions, or a precise account of why the available manuscript and period context remain underdetermined.
