# Blind manuscript solve-path test

You are given six consecutive original manuscript-page scans in their release order. The first four are the region under investigation; the final two are included as neighboring original context. Page numbers and current research hypotheses are intentionally withheld.

## Task

Using only these scans and information that a player could reasonably have obtained by **11 May 2014**, determine whether the first four scans select a concrete, reproducible operation or source-derived key/operand that advances the manuscript and explains a specific next step into the remaining scans.

If a route is selected, record:

1. Your first unprompted observations, before searching for explanations.
2. The exact clue that selects the operation, and why it favors that operation over at least one plausible alternative.
3. The exact input or operand, and the independent source from which it is obtained.
4. The complete operation: alphabet or representation, key/seed, parameters, ordering, state/clock/reset rules, and treatment of boundaries or exceptional symbols.
5. The exact predicted output or next object. State this before requesting or viewing any additional material.
6. One specific observation on an original source that would falsify the route.

If no unique operation is selected, state the smallest missing source, value, or instruction that would make the route executable. Do not fill gaps with guessed keys or post-selected exceptions.

## Constraints

- Use no material first published after 11 May 2014.
- Do not use modern LP2 transcriptions, later plaintext interpretations, current research reports, or community hypotheses.
- Do not assume a cipher family, visual traversal, number system, page partition, or source object in advance.
- Work from the images themselves; transcriptions may be created as working aids only after recording the initial visual observations.
- Distinguish direct observation, mathematical derivation, historical source fact, and inference.
- Preserve dead ends and the point at which a candidate operation first becomes determined.
- If additional pages or sources are necessary, identify exactly which item is needed and why; do not silently broaden the packet.

A useful result is either a short, source-selected solve path with a locked prediction or a precise account of why the available evidence is underdetermined.
