## A finite conditional baseline, with source-selection caution

Among the **720** three-rune words in the original **2,896** printed-word corpus, **16** have GP-prime sums that address a table cell with value142, namely one-based **cell81 or cell217**. The original WWP is exactly one of those 16. Among all **29³=24,389** possible GP three-rune tuples, **336** sum to81 or217. Both frequencies are finite *conditional comparisons*, not overall discovery-corrected odds; the WWP interpretation was identified after the GEMY navigation scheme, and the keypad/byte/next-index assumptions remain unauthenticated. The p51 first-complete-word and first-p50-cell coincidences are source geometry, while their *meaning as instructions* is a conjecture.

## Decision, scope, and repeatability

This is **not a decipherment**. It recovers no unknown LP2 plaintext, key, independently selected cipher, 100-rune original clear window or withheld 40-rune prediction. Base59 remains a candidate alternative to the conventional base60 interpretation and does not give the same graph spelling. A cell with q4 could simply be a fixed point rather than a reroute.
