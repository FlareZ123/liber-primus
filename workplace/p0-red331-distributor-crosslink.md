# Physical p0 red heading: a 331/32 source crosslink, no decoder

**Decision, 16 November 2026 (Agent3).** The original p0 **red 8|5 rune** heading has independently verified prime sums **347 and 315**. Their arithmetic is `347+315=662=2×331` and `347−315=32`. The source-dated May 2014 Stage11 index uses `<title>133</title>` and `<div id="331">`, and the manuscript's original p49–51 numeric table has **32 rows of eight cells**. These are correct, potentially intentional source-level correspondences. They do **not** recover unknown plaintext, the historical key, an operation, or an author-approved link from heading to table.

## Original-corpus specificity and its limit

The [single-file original-input SFT](../sims/lp2_p0_red331_html_table_sft.cjs), [executed successfully on the original repository with Node 22/Windows](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/43836230876), parses every original rune word across page carries and asserts 2,896 complete words / 12,956 runes and unchanged p0 first13. Of the 2,895 consecutive complete-word pairs, exactly **two** total662: p0's 347|315 (word lengths8|5) and a p43 498|164 pair (lengths6|4). Exactly **19** pairs have lengths8|5; p0 is the only such pair totaling662. Every one of the title's 1,287 possible 8-of-13 allocations is enumerated: **10** yield first-word sum347, **11** yield315, for conditional probability **21/1,287≈0.016317** of a 32-point absolute difference given this exact fixed title rune multiset.

These numbers are **not a corrected discovery probability**. The manuscript's GP prime-weight mean is **1480/29≈51.03448**; thirteen unconstrained letters have expected total **≈663.448**, remarkably close to the observed **662**. Thus the 331 mean falls near the expected value for an arbitrary 13-rune heading. The 32/table-dimension association was noticed retrospectively; selecting multiple numbers, section lengths and arithmetic operations after inspection creates additional chances for coincidence. A 2014 human could have noticed the title, the HTML `331`, and the later table, but **nothing source-authenticated directs a conversion from these to plaintext**.

## Cryptanalytic decision

An intended route, if present, would have to specify an actual **source-driven consumer**: which exact original p0 symbols encode which key or table coordinates, how the 331 and32 enter a reversible operation, and what original ciphertext must become readable *without* fitting new keys after seeing its output. A fixed direct application of the authentic p56 prime-totient cipher is already incompatible with eight of nine unknown chapters for all first5,000 starting prime positions ([original exact gate](../im-results/lp2-p56-native-F-transparent-ninechapter-impossibility.md)); 331 is no excuse to repeat that search.

**Completed original-handoff operator check (Director #1311, 16 November 2026, Agent31):** The source-authenticated 2014 Stage11 invitation, `resources/historical/cicada2014-stage11-message.asc`, supplies only the final onion destination and a greeting. Its period-preserved, unsigned 1,640-byte `index.html` is exactly the 58 ordered image references `0.jpg` through `57.jpg` wrapped in `<title>133</title>` and `<div id="331">`. The title's **133 is the literal digit-reversal of 331**, a possible visual hint to reverse, but neither element is an executable transformation: the document has no JavaScript, CSS, input form, receiver, address calculation, decoding grammar or ciphertext/key clock. Its historical archive tree has only the index and 58 JPEGs ([contemporaneous tree manifest](../resources/historical/stage11-2014-index-manifest.json)); no surviving sibling instruction is linked. The independently signature-verified 2013 TCP source documents `BASE29 3301 → 3QO` and a participant-directed DH operation, with no `331`, `32`, averaging, subtraction or LP2-table consumer ([TCP protocol source](../resources/external/2013-tcp-server-signed-source.md)). The future 2016 signed “numbers are the direction” clue leaves the same receiver unspecified and cannot authenticate a 2014 arithmetic rule.

**Disposition:** None of the independently dated first-party objects just listed fixes `(347+315)/2`, `347−315`, a p50 row/column address, a reversible GP29 operation, or an exact 331/32 key serialization. The numerical correspondence and the 133↔331 reversal remain source-visible clues **without a source-selected cryptographic consumer**, so this branch is closed against further arithmetic or key searches. This is a bounded historical-source negative, not proof that no lost original instruction ever existed. Reopen only upon a previously unexamined original first-party operand/receiver that uniquely predicts a held-out authentic rune interval. No unknown plaintext was recovered.
