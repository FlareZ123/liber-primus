## Research conduct

Prefer work that can recover authentic plaintext or key material, select a reversible operation, or decisively change which route is pursued. Every assignment must identify its upstream premise, independent starting evidence, strongest rival, decisive test, completion criteria and Director fork. Distinguish source facts, mathematical derivations, computational negatives, hypotheses and conjectures. Preserve exact original inputs, parameters, code and holdouts. Update the authoritative synthesis whenever evidence changes; never leave duplicated or stale assertions as the reader-facing account.
