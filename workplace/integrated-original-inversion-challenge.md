## Structural guardrail — printed separators do not establish plaintext word order (25 November 2026)

Independent Round45 W1 analysis of the rune-identical LP2 transcription finds no natural-language word-length order at global, sentence, line, paragraph, page or section scale (12 order tests null; intact English controls at 100% power). Its Q4 equality counts support suppression across printed separators (23/2,895 versus 63/10,060 within groups). A homogeneous residual-rate/independent-rewired-pair mixture estimates a 0.060 rewired fraction with plug-in upper bound about 0.20; neither key-clock continuity nor directed generation order follows. A word-reset key schedule and continuous output suppression can coexist. The W1 length-order result, separately, disfavors intact ordinary-English order at book scale without proving the separators fake. [Critic boundary-identifiability correction](../communications/broadcast/2026-11-25T0212Z_critic_q4_boundary_identifiability.md).

Keep two models separate. **H-shuffle:** the printed groups are genuine word units but the plaintext word order is permuted; per-group lexical constraints may still apply, while sequential word-position, grammar and semantic-adjacency claims do not. **H-decoy:** the printed groups are a decoy segmentation or carry lengths from another text; intact-group lexical constraints and word-local reset claims lose power. The external evidence does not decide between them. Accordingly, exact intact-word results below require preservation of word units (ordered or shuffled); sequential grammar tests require additional order assumptions. Spacing-free results are invariant to separator relocation alone, not necessarily to a change in plaintext order, key clock, or language register. Do not use word-salad order alone to reject a candidate, and do not reopen generic word-length MI or unconstrained word-permutation searches. The pinned external evidence is consolidated in [the external research index](../resources/external/dukotah-cicada3301-research-index.md). The original conditional whole-group shuffle test (#2679) preserves each chapter's endpoint groups and complete word inventory: 23 equal seams observed against 100.036 expected, with no equally suppressed draw among 2,000 uniform permutations. Off-diagonal-only discrepancy (p=.193) and independently held-out directed residual prediction (p=.223) provide no new order rule. A constructive ambiguity bound preserves every one of the 2,895 seam pairs, all word-length slots, chapter endpoints and page-carry positions while admitting at least 1.81×10^24 distinct rune tapes. Thus exchangeable whole-group shuffling after encryption is disfavored, but seam-conditioned permutations and pre-encryption shuffling remain distinct possibilities; endpoint statistics cannot identify an order. ## Research conduct

Prefer work that can recover authentic plaintext or key material, select a reversible operation, or decisively change which route is pursued. Every assignment must identify its upstream premise, independent starting evidence, strongest rival, decisive test, completion criteria and Director fork. Distinguish source facts, mathematical derivations, computational negatives, hypotheses and conjectures. Preserve exact original inputs, parameters, code and holdouts. Update the authoritative synthesis whenever evidence changes; never leave duplicated or stale assertions as the reader-facing account.
