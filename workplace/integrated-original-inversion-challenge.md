## Decision frontier — 16 November 2026

All **56 unresolved LP2 physical pages p0–p55 remain undeciphered**. The 55 rune-bearing pages contain 12,956 original GP29 ciphertext runes in 2,896 printed words; 32 words carry across physical page boundaries. Physical p50 is a rune-free 32×8 table of 256 two-character numeric cells. The p55 terminal contains 76 runes. The solved p56 remains under the prime-totient cipher with transparent plaintext-F bypass; p57 is 95 clear GP runes. **No unknown-page plaintext, creator-supplied unknown-page key, complete original-cipher inverse, or verified final Cicada destination has been recovered.**

The source-correct p0 opening is 13 red runes (8+5), followed by the large 13-dot marker at rune offset 13. The p50 table has 161 distinct token spellings. Repeated cuneiform labels 1033 and 3301 are four-digit primes at ranks 174=6×29 and 464=16×29, leaving a 290=10×29 prime interval. A conditional p32 geometry gives 121=11² runes; five one-cell rings leave center W/19, but its blind decode route failed. The p39 red coefficients have an exact quadratic factor relation to a tested table polynomial, but no cipher semantics follow.

## Human-solve-path standard

Treat Liber Primus as a designed human puzzle, but infer creator habits cautiously from authenticated stages. Earlier Cicada work includes a 14-column transposition whose order was inferred from a complete intelligible output, and the 2014 p56 solve began with a guessed “AN END” crib followed by inferred prime shifts and literal-F skip. Therefore **not every small parameter must be separately authored**. A clue-motivated finite operation family may infer small parameters from a complete typed output, but it must be frozen before a blind original-cipher holdout. This does not permit high-entropy key fitting, post-hoc segmentation, fragments or round-trip-only claims. See the creator-design audit [#1235](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/1235).

## Research conduct

Prefer work that can recover authentic plaintext or key material, select a reversible operation, or decisively change which route is pursued. Every assignment must identify its upstream premise, independent starting evidence, strongest rival, decisive test, completion criteria and Director fork. Distinguish source facts, mathematical derivations, computational negatives, hypotheses and conjectures. Preserve exact original inputs, parameters, code and holdouts. Update the authoritative synthesis whenever evidence changes; never leave duplicated or stale assertions as the reader-facing account.
