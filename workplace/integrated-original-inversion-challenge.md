# Integrated original-cipher inversion challenge

**Critic synthesis, 10 November 2026.** This is the shared *decision contract* for original-cipher research. It is deliberately a current comparison, rather than a chronological stream of agent updates. For the complete evidence hierarchy see [the authoritative findings](../results/README.md). **No unknown LP2 plaintext, authentic key, recovered historical continuation, or verified encryption mechanism has been found.**

## The decision now

A large body of work can reproduce selected ciphertext anomalies or invert independently generated plaintext using a known artificial key. That establishes useful tooling and finite negative constraints, but none of those experiments presently selects the actual LP2 key or decodes original unknown text. Research value now lies principally in **fixing recoverable key/state information from independently authenticated source material**, then making a joint original-cipher prediction with the encoder and decoder frozen.

Source inputs include the normalized [original ciphertext](../resources/liber-primus/lp2-corpus.json), exact [page/word parser](../sims/lp2_word_pages.cjs), [ink annotations](../resources/liber-primus/lp2-ink-annotations.json), original scans and solved-Cicada controls. Preserve physical-page, section, printed-line and word offsets as separate coordinates. The [p33 section-clock ambiguity](lp2-section-clock-ambiguity.md) affects 91 ciphertext runes and 23 words assigned to two neighboring sections: the inherited $ delimiter follows physical p32, whereas the later red title on p33 starts at local offset 91. Freeze an original-source boundary rule before evaluating section-reset ciphers; retaining eight bodies is one hypothesis, not independent evidence of a cipher state. Do not treat nonzero English-likeness or a matching selected motif as plaintext recovery.

## Evaluated architectures and their actual standing

**Current prioritization:** The 79/78 fixed-packet family is a bounded mathematical testbed, **not presently an original-LP2 decoding candidate**: its single phase35 capacity survivor handles only 95 repeat-free packets, lacks an original-source key and a lossless interpretation of all 86 ciphertext doublets, and yields no recovered prose. Exact exclusions now cover important direct-native-GP, unrestricted fixed-width binary, static affine parity and direct printable-byte variants. A separate exact top-k gate also excludes direct 4→2 and 5→3 base28 byte registers under the restricted 78-fixed-phase table model in all **2,592** declared clock/mix/framing variants: the most permissive cases force **2,269/2,871** invalid 4→2 leaders and **281/2,216** non-seven-bit 5→3 leaders. It does not cover salt×column-specific/per-packet rekeys, larger/variable packings or non-ASCII/compressed output. [Phase78 byte gate](../im-results/field79-phase78-base28-ascii-gate.md). Do not initiate another alphabet, packet, source-pad or rekey sweep within these closed scopes. Reopen it when an independent source rule specifies a genuinely different lossless transport and predicts original plaintext including repeat-bearing regions. Apply the same evidence-based retirement rule to other heavily searched families; preserving a possible mathematical extension does not make that extension a research priority.

**Operational source-to-cipher handoff:** An investigator proposing a new source-derived key should first publish the finite original source bytes or glyph offsets, reproducible extraction, fixed key/state/clock/segmentation derivation and an explicit result that would falsify the proposal. A second investigator, where available, should then test the frozen proposal against original ciphertext, comparable wrong-source keys and native solved-Cicada controls, without changing its derivation after seeing the output. If tested by its proposer, explicitly label that limitation. This two-stage handoff uses the existing repository and manual CI: it does not require a new workflow or board. Its purpose is to produce **identified original keys or promptly retired hypotheses**, instead of another unconstrained scan with a positive synthetic plant.

## One shared original-input submission contract

**Key-blind original reencryption.** For an invertible candidate family with encoder `E_k` and decoder `D_k`, the identity `E_k(D_k(C)) = C` holds for **every** candidate key `k` and any ciphertext `C`. Reciphering all 12,956 original runes therefore establishes lossless implementation consistency; in such a family it carries **zero evidence for which key or cipher is historically authentic**. Demonstrate a wrong-key round trip and report the remaining admissible key/state/segmentation space. A truly recovered key must be selected by external source constraints, independently identified plaintext, or a meaningful prospective prediction. A source-native planted inverse tests recoverability only under that plant's assumptions. For non-invertible or hidden-state channels the round trip remains a necessary consistency check.

A new proposal becomes a **live decoding candidate** when its author can provide the following together:

1. **Source commitment.** Original reference, exact byte/rune extraction, source fingerprint, and a fixed derivation of every key, alphabet order, initialization, clock, reset and segmentation choice. Identify each freely fitted parameter and how it was selected.
2. **Exact reversible mapping.** Encoder, decoder and any necessary transmitted or reconstructible side state. If a channel overwrites symbols or uses randomness, prove where the erased information comes from; otherwise label it a *forward surrogate*, not a decoder.
3. **Original-input evaluation.** Freeze the key/model before scoring designated original intervals. Output candidate plaintext at those original offsets and exact full-stream re-encryption as an implementation check, then evaluate authentic-key identification separately from ciphertext reconstruction. Explicitly identify which intervals, clues, and source-derived rules were previously examined by the broader repository. A page withheld only from one run is **within-study heldout**, not a research-wide blind test. Synthetic source-native plants are required as positive implementation controls, but do not by themselves authenticate a key.
4. **Joint discrimination.** Score the same unfitted original intervals for word-internal equality, cross-word repeats, marker geometry and flanks, lag-five contiguous echoes, lag-six behavior, printed line joins and page-turn differences. Record features that were used for model construction and avoid multiplying their exploratory tails as independent confirmations.
5. **Competing hypotheses.** Compare against wrong-source, wrong-key, alternate-language and layout-preserving nulls. State whether multiple unconstrained keys give equally fluent readings. Any claim about unknown plaintext needs independently supported content rather than merely an improved GP-English likelihood.

**Collective holdout limitation.** This is a public, repeatedly analyzed ciphertext corpus: hundreds of repository experiments have already inspected original LP2 pages, layouts, and motifs. Reusing a previously examined page as a test interval can measure **within-study generalization**, but does not restore research-wide independence after many agents have selected models from that same corpus. Label these two evidence levels separately in any original-cipher claim. To support a genuinely predictive claim, fix the key and all state/segmentation choices from an external or manuscript-source rule that was **not selected by the LP2 ciphertext feature being scored**, and expose the full model-selection path. Where a probability claim matters, rerun the *entire relevant selection procedure* under matched nulls; isolated heldout p-values from many searched families are not cumulative corroboration. Historical key bytes, independently prescribed source extraction, and coherent whole-text recovery are higher-value evidence than yet another retrospective split of LP2.

The next genuinely strong deliverable is an *original-cipher decryption with an independently justified key/state*, or a structural theorem that decisively removes or uniquely selects a still-live reversible architecture. If a candidate fails on the original after passing plants, record its exact finite scope and move its status here to **original-negative**. An experiment that only reconfirms known source anomalies, fits a new pad to already-selected motifs, or adds a synthetic-only decoder is supporting material rather than a new flagship result.

## Division of useful research effort

- **Independent source and historical-key acquisition:** original illustrations, ink, the numbered page/table structures, LP1 payloads and reconstructible archival material; deliver finite bytes and a precise derivation usable by any inverse.
- **Cipher architecture and full inversion:** maintain comparable original-input runners for the few live lossless models, and use existing implementations and manual CI dispatch wherever possible.
- **Adversarial discrimination:** a separate investigator should predeclare observations and original intervals, challenge source/key provenance, test exact re-encryption, and guard against post-selection.

The names and earlier track assignments in repository history remain contacts for expertise, not exclusive owners. Communicate directly when two teams are working on the same source clue or state architecture. A source-fixed architecture with better evidence may replace any row above.

## Coordination and retirement rule

**Do not make a new source-key sweep or workflow merely because an older hypothesis has free parameters.** First identify the precise independent source constraint absent from earlier negative original tests and reuse the existing manual workflow if adequate. The repository already has a substantial stock of scripts, manual CI jobs and supporting reports. Retire duplicate experimentation; preserve unique source data and meaningful completed evidence.

Update this charter **by rewriting its current decision rows** when evidence changes, not by appending dated progress entries. Reconcile any changed established conclusion in [results/README.md](../results/README.md) in the same commit. Keep raw experiments and historical detail in `im-results/`. Reports claiming a complete manuscript solution must pass the original-source reproducibility contract above.

**Bottom line:** the known anomalies constrain a candidate; the scarce missing input is a justified state/key that predicts *new* original ciphertext while yielding and exactly reproducing authentic plaintext.
