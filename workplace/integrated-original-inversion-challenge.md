## Structural guardrail — printed separators do not establish plaintext word order (25 November 2026)

Independent Round45 W1 analysis of the rune-identical LP2 transcription finds no detectable ordinary-English word-length order at global, sentence, line, paragraph, page or section scale (12 order tests null; intact English controls at 100% power). Its Q4 equality counts support suppression across printed separators (23/2,895 versus 63/10,060 within groups). The original tape has 45 independently reversible nonpalindromic single-line groups with identical endpoints, preserving all 841 directed seam counts and the Q4 repeat counts (2^45 distinct tapes). This is nonidentifiability, not an orientation-key hypothesis. [Exact witness](../im-results/lp2-r45-word-order-independent.md#exact-original-reversal-counterexample). A word-reset key schedule and continuous output suppression can coexist. The W1 length-order result, separately, disfavors intact ordinary-English order at book scale without proving the separators fake. [Critic boundary-identifiability correction](../communications/broadcast/2026-11-25T0212Z_critic_q4_boundary_identifiability.md).

Keep two models separate. **H-shuffle:** the printed groups are genuine word units but the plaintext word order is permuted; per-group lexical constraints may still apply, while sequential word-position, grammar and semantic-adjacency claims do not. **H-decoy:** the printed groups are a decoy segmentation or carry lengths from another text; intact-group lexical constraints and word-local reset claims lose power. The external evidence does not decide between them. In particular, the R47 b′ arrangement controls conserve sentence-level length inventories, which is a special case of H-decoy; arbitrary decoy segmentation need not conserve that inventory. Accordingly, exact intact-word results below require preservation of word units (ordered or shuffled); sequential grammar tests require additional order assumptions. Spacing-free results are invariant to separator relocation alone, not necessarily to a change in plaintext order, key clock, or language register. Do not use word-salad order alone to reject a candidate, and do not reopen generic word-length MI or unconstrained word-permutation searches. The GEB worked one-symbol gap displacement is now a scoped negative: exact all-key length/consuming-clock capacity (including F holds) excludes every contiguous native donor window at both book and p40–53 chapter lengths; [certificate and actual fixed-key inverse attempts](../im-results/lp2-wordlength-resegment-allsecret-original-gate.md#geb-one-symbol-gap-displacement-including-ambiguous-f-holds-agent18-28-november-2026). The pinned external evidence is consolidated in [the external research index](../resources/external/dukotah-cicada3301-research-index.md). The original conditional whole-group shuffle test (#2679) preserves each chapter's endpoint groups and complete word inventory: 23 equal seams observed against 100.036 expected, with no equally suppressed draw among 2,000 uniform permutations. Off-diagonal-only discrepancy (p=.193) and independently held-out directed residual prediction (p=.223) provide no new order rule. A constructive ambiguity bound preserves every one of the 2,895 seam pairs, all word-length slots, chapter endpoints and page-carry positions while admitting at least 1.81×10^24 distinct rune tapes. Thus exchangeable whole-group shuffling after encryption is disfavored, but seam-conditioned permutations and pre-encryption shuffling remain distinct possibilities; endpoint statistics cannot identify an order. ## Human-solve-path standard

Treat Liber Primus as a designed human puzzle, but infer creator habits cautiously from authenticated stages. Earlier Cicada work includes a 14-column transposition whose order was inferred from a complete intelligible output, and the 2014 p56 solve began with a guessed “AN END” crib followed by inferred prime shifts and literal-F skip. Therefore **not every small parameter must be separately authored**. A clue-motivated finite operation family may infer small parameters from a complete typed output, but it must be frozen before a blind original-cipher holdout. This does not permit high-entropy key fitting, post-hoc segmentation, fragments or round-trip-only claims. See the creator-design audit [#1235](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/1235).

For each genuinely different proposal, specify the visible clue a solver could act on, the operation or bounded choice it motivates, its exact input/output types, the weakest unsupported premise, and the strongest distinct rival. If a small family of parameters is inferred from coherent text, freeze the choice before examining a separate continuation. If a branch depends on lost historical material, name the actual missing bytes or authentication step instead of inventing an operand.

## Acceptance standard

1. **State the receiver.** Explicit alphabet, deterministic forward and inverse maps, operand/initial-state source, clock (including holds), frame boundaries, representation and any information needed to undo a transformation. If the sender is globally many-to-one, identify the actual available side information or justified restricted domain and show unique inversion there.
2. **Calibrate on authentic controls.** Test relevant known LP1/p56/p57 operations and blind planted controls of similar length, state uncertainty, repeat rate and boundary geometry where practical. Separate true-key/true-state oracles, partial recovery, exact recovery and output-only round trips. Challenge with independent wrong keys, wrong models and matched nulls. If calibration fails, fix the algorithm or stop; do not use its original fit as negative evidence about the whole family.
3. **Lock selection.** Record prior original-cipher exposures, candidate family, choice rule and inference data. Withhold original data from the current fit and test a frozen continuation. No familiar LP2 split is collectively fresh; independent source-derived predictions or authenticated external confirmation carry special weight. Distinguish a within-invocation held segment from genuinely unseen evidence.
4. **Produce original evidence.** Publish exact original coordinates, decoded sequence, key/state, and frozen continuation, with exact forward replay and competing-key diagnostics. Every bijective wrong key also round-trips: replay is an implementation invariant, not authentication. Language scoring and finite lexicon matches are evidence only in their calibrated domain; do not infer plaintext from hand-selected fragments.
5. **Make the result actionable.** A negative must state which scope is eliminated and what competing mechanism survives. A positive must identify the recovered information and how it constrains another original object. Store reusable code in sims, source material in resources, detailed evidence in im-results, and change authoritative results only if the causal frontier changes.
