# P57's “circumferences”: the source's literal referent and the missing grid operation

**Agent42, 18 November 2026. Scope:** original-art and independently solved-text reading for Director Issue [#1440](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/1440). **No recovered unknown LP2 plaintext, key, complete receiver, or destination.** The source-visible 76/180 numerical relationship is preserved as a conditional hypothesis.

## The historically available literal reading

The already solved first-release text defines its term *circumference* in an unusually explicit philosophical sense:

- Original solved LP1 p10: “THE CIRCUMFERENCE PRACTICES THREE BEHAVIORS WHICH CAUSE THE LOSS OF DIVINITY,” then names consumption, preservation, adherence.
- Original solved LP1 p14: “THE I IS THE VOICE OF THE CIRCUMFERENCE,” explained as the internal self-referential voice.
- Solved LP1 p03: the pilgrimage ends in “AN END TO SELF”; “LIKE THE INSTAR, IT IS ONLY THROUGH GOING WITHIN THAT WE MAY EMERGE.”
- Original clear LP2 p57: “LIKE THE INSTAR TUNNELING TO THE SURFACE / WE MUST SHED OUR OWN CIRCUMFERENCES / FIND THE DIVINITY WITHIN AND EMERGE.”

These are exact linked concepts in prior material a contemporary reader could have already solved before LP2. The natural primary referent of “our own circumferences” is the *I*/ego to be relinquished to recover divinity. This is direct source evidence for a semantic reading. A double-use as a geometric cipher command remains possible, but needs an additional physical selector and receiver.

## Which visible perimeter could a player actually peel?

The strongest geometric rival is documented independently: LP2 physical pp49–51 print **32×8 = 256** numeric tokens, with mathematically **76** outer cells and **180** strict inner cells. P55 has **76** original unknown runes; solved p56 and clear p57 have **85 + 95 = 180** runes. The original 2014 images confirm these counts and the floral side ornaments framing pp40–55. They do **not** draw a connected continuous line around the 32×8 table. The numeric rectangle spans *three separate pages*; its four corners are not marked as starts, and there is no indicated row/column, clockwise/counterclockwise or ring-to-terminal-rune correspondence. The repeated floral ornaments are placed to either side, not a closed enumerated border. P55's moth/tree artwork and p57's separate emergence tree similarly supply no ordered 76-cell contour.

An investigator can construct a lossless 256-slot **representation** by assigning p55's 76 runes to a chosen mathematical perimeter and p56/p57's 180 genuine GP runes to the interior. The construction is bookkeeping: one has to choose the first corner, winding order and inner placement independently. Even a perfectly invertible rearrangement of 256 ciphertext/known symbols does not supply a cipher, key, or plaintext for p55. Direct static byte→rune mappings, row/column masks, H/G codeword readings, linear 3×3 kernels and two-cell quadratic rules have previously failed on the 180 true known plaintext targets (see `im-results/p50-32x8-interior-known-plaintext-compatibility.md`, `im-results/lp2-p54-nine-rune-valid-convolution-gate.md`, `im-results/lp2-terminal256-quadratic-known180-gate.md`).

**Result of this one original-image decision:** the visible source selects neither a *physically drawn complete circumference* nor a typed invertible operation mapping its inside/outside registers to terminal plaintext. The independent solved LP1 definitions select an already coherent semantic reading. Promoting the 76/180 numerical coincidence into a decrypting command is presently unsupported.

## What would change the decision

An original-era authored outline, numbered corner/start, explicit stencil or solved-stage instruction linking that exact numeric rectangle with the two later pages would select a concrete geometric path. A reversible cipher reading would then have to account for all 76 p55 ciphertext runes, recover the complete known 180-runic interior under an unfit frozen operator, and predict a separate previously unexamined original ciphertext interval. Without that input, the exact 76/180 length identity remains a conditional observation and further unconstrained perimeter-key searches are a weak use of research resources.
