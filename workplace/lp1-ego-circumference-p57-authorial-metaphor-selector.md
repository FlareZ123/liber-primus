Original-text interpretation and solve-path gating; no cryptographic decoder or unknown plaintext.** The printed source text already provides an independent interpretation of the final cleartext PARABLE. ## First-party controls in original reading order

- Solved first-release LP1 original **p3** (archive scan `03.jpg`, also indexed in `resources/liber-primus/lp1-plaintext-reference.txt`) explicitly states: **“JOURNEY DEEP WITHIN AND YOU WILL ARRIVE OUTSIDE”** and **“LIKE THE INSTAR, IT IS ONLY THROUGH GOING WITHIN THAT WE MAY EMERGE.”** The instar/within/emerge triad precedes LP2 p57 by a complete release, already making *emergence* a spiritual/personhood metaphor.
- Solved LP1 original **p10** (scan `10.jpg`) titles its teaching **“THE LOSS OF DIVINITY”** and declares **“THE CIRCUMFERENCE PRACTICES THREE BEHAVIORS WHICH CAUSE THE LOSS OF DIVINITY”**. These are explicitly labeled consumption, preservation and adherence. The noun circumference is therefore defined as an aspect of the self in the author's philosophical vocabulary, rather than as a geometric pixel or word-border instruction.
- Solved LP1 original **p14** (scan `14.jpg`) states: **“THE I IS THE VOICE OF THE CIRCUMFERENCE.”** A dialogue discusses the internal voice saying “I.” This gives the precise psychological referent for “our own circumferences.”
- The solved LP2 original **p57** (scan `74.jpg`) reads: **“LIKE THE INSTAR TUNNELING TO THE SURFACE / WE MUST SHED OUR OWN CIRCUMFERENCES / FIND THE DIVINITY WITHIN AND EMERGE.”** The repeated proper noun *instar*, inside/outside imagery, divinity and verb *emerge* directly echo LP1 p3, p10 and p14.

The source evidence is unusually discriminating: the **same author's earlier already-solved texts explain the referents** of the final parable's very unusual words. A contemporary solver had access to these first-release passages along with LP2.

## Implication for an intended algorithm

Literal outside/inside stripping remains an **optional deliberate double meaning**. The author might have used this motif both philosophically and operationally. It cannot, however, be treated as a *unique first-party command* selecting first/last rune deletion, per-word interior GP sums, page-border cropping, two passes around the 32×8 table, one particular word `DIUINITY`, a radix, or a numeric p50 receiver. These require their own discriminating original-image clue and prospectively correct long ciphertext inverse. A unique `DIUINITY` numeric coincidence within a later-selected arithmetic/address family does not establish that the historical author intended that family.

The promising alternative human read is to treat the p57 passage as an **explicit callback to the established LP1 teaching** that defines self/circumference. That callback may instruct a *person* how to interpret the completion of the journey, while the concrete p56 hash instruction is a separate cryptographic continuation. This is a semantic inference, not evidence that all geometric ciphers are impossible.
