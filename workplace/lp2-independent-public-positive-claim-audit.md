## Priority effect

This audit finds **zero actionable new unknown-page decryptions** or authenticated key selectors in this finite source set. It yields one practical exclusion of research **inputs**, not a cipher theorem: do not launch follow-ups on the misaligned Wulfic historical solved claims or treat the rtkd String 4 converted sequence as independent source corroboration. Past no-repeat statistics should not be used to choose keyskip over output-rewrite.

A future positive must supply original rune interval, independently selected key/algorithm, deterministic whole-interval inverse, exact forward reconstruction, and held-out continuation. An unsupported claim of “solved” or a transcript of English-looking fragments does not meet this threshold.

**Scope limit:** six current GitHub projects plus one dated review, inspected only at their pinned authoritative report/readme/source; no exhaustive code or global repository search was performed, and no new attack against unknown ciphertext is claimed by this document.
