## Expanded source-pinned Round44 audit (Agent 3, 13 November 2026; Director #703)

[Full external Round44 snapshot at commit `9087dc36`](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/tree/9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29/liber-primus/analysis/round44) was compared at its campaign, synthesis, wave-one red-team audit, wave-two red-team audit, and substantive source-specific `RESULTS.md` and scripts. Its [17-cell wave-two audit](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29/liber-primus/analysis/round44/X1-redteam/REPORT-WAVE2.md) and [synthesis](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29/liber-primus/analysis/round44/SYNTHESIS.md) expressly report **zero newly deciphered LP2 plaintext**, source-selected decoding keys or destinations. Current live material includes the completed resumed C7 audio and C9 homophonic screens; older interim dead-job descriptions are superseded by their completed results.

The strongest apparently independent positive is [B3 T7](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29/liber-primus/analysis/round44/W2-B3-doublet-sites/RESULTS.md): repeat-event gap lag-one Pearson +0.3800, nominal p=0.0010 under a random-site null, and p≈0.00025 under an independent gap-permutation control. **This claim is already recognized and causally narrowed in the local [event-bearing-word identity study](../im-results/lp2-doublet-gap-source-boundary-conditional-gate.md)**: 86 same-page repeated-rune sites fall in 85 original printed words, and with those word IDs fixed the original +0.380 is ordinary (20,000 original-input permutations, p=0.25914). A new **whole-word event-pattern** null, independently tested for this audit, relocates complete repeat patterns among same-page same-length words without modifying source runes: p=0.02145 for page/length-matched profiles; p=0.00820 with original 13-dot fragments additionally held fixed ([single-file SFT](../sims/lp2_round44_t7_word_profile_shuffle_sft.cjs)). The latter residual leaves event-bearing-word selection unexplained but **does not fix a cipher key or stochastic state**. The external Round44 campaign correctly labels B3 a measured anomaly rather than a solve; its asserted process-drift interpretation is more specific than its flat-site null can distinguish.

**Reproducibility boundary on the exact external snapshot.** The GitHub Contents API listing of [the pinned Round44 directory](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/tree/9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29/liber-primus/analysis/round44) contains 13 `W2-` subdirectories: A1, A2, A4; B1, B2, B3; C2, C3, C5, C6, C7, C8, C9. It does **not** contain the four paths `W2-A3-reset-skip`, `W2-B4-trees`, `W2-C1-motifs`, or `W2-C4-stego` named in the synthesis. Those four reported Wave2 cells are therefore **secondary synthesis descriptions in this exact committed snapshot**, rather than directly inspectable code/results. In particular, the claimed p55 "ghost tree" T4 hit and figural-motif S1/S3 control are not independently reproduced from those absent folders. The original `72.jpg` LP2 p55 indeed shows a pale central tree; that visual fact is independently visible, but it is not evidence of an extractable hidden channel. No result or cipher claim is upgraded on the basis of the absent cell artifacts.

**Additional independently cataloged visual-source facts, not plaintext:** [B2's original ink census](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29/liber-primus/analysis/round44/W2-B2-cuneiform/RESULTS.md) finds seven occurrences of the same six-glyph cuneiform-like ornament bracketing pp33–39 (pp34–39 copies identical; p33 a resampled copy), and a five-dot ornament on original p7 mirrored on p23 with exact five x-centres and y-pair sums 3675 or 3675.5 pixels ([raw geometry](https://github.com/Dukotah/cicada3301/blob/9087dc36b5aa64c1786428e32180261ab9420c29/liber-primus/analysis/round44/W2-B2-cuneiform/results.json)). This makes an independent *five-star constellation address* interpretation weaker: the image is repeated symmetrical furniture. Original placement and layout may still be clues, but no author instruction maps the ornament to a complete inverse. The old Round44 alleged p27/p54 seven-rune repeat is the source-deduplicated p27/p55 six-rune `DJU BEI` collision plus the p54→p55 boundary and solved-p56 continuation, already closed under #689 and locally treated in [#562](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/562).

Other Wave1 and Wave2 channels have finite negative or measured-only outcomes under their own explicit decoders: complete red-ink selection, punctuation identity, p15 four-by-four `3301−prime` grid, onion byte pads, 2014 audio reductions, constant homophonic partitions, simple page-pair pads, p32/p55 tree comparison, direct 2013 hash-block stride and reset/skip variants. These do **not** collectively exhaust all key-source possibilities: many are partial family tests, and the external motif and ghost-tree hits were flagged without a decoder and with high matched-control false-positive rates. No known new source object in this snapshot supplies original unknown-page plaintext or a source-selected 29-symbol inverse; do not commission dependent sweeps on these flags alone.

**Decision for Director #703:** Round44 expands the useful reference inventory of original decorative ink, and the profile-shuffle SFT provides a stronger calibrated test of its main statistical anomaly. The leading uncertainty remains which ciphertext words carry repeat events. None of the external claims passes the original-input plaintext/key standard. This is an expanded *bounded* audit of the published Round44 program, rather than a proof against untested mechanisms or an assertion that every item in the repository history is exhausted.

One separate dated non-GitHub review, [SECRA, 15 Oct 2026](https://secra.tech/research/liber-primus-strongest-findings/), also expressly says pp0–55 remain unsolved; its insect, audio and square findings are visual/numerical observations, not a deterministic plaintext inverse. No reason was found to reproduce its decorative or statistical matches in the absence of a source-fixed cryptographic prediction.

## Priority effect

This audit finds **zero actionable new unknown-page decryptions** or authenticated key selectors in this finite source set. It yields one practical exclusion of research **inputs**, not a cipher theorem: do not launch follow-ups on the misaligned Wulfic historical solved claims or treat the rtkd String 4 converted sequence as independent source corroboration. Past no-repeat statistics should not be used to choose keyskip over output-rewrite.

A future positive must supply original rune interval, independently selected key/algorithm, deterministic whole-interval inverse, exact forward reconstruction, and held-out continuation. An unsupported claim of “solved” or a transcript of English-looking fragments does not meet this threshold.

**Scope limit:** six current GitHub projects plus one dated review, inspected only at their pinned authoritative report/readme/source; no exhaustive code or global repository search was performed, and no new attack against unknown ciphertext is claimed by this document.
