# Two original triple five-step echo words: conditional p36 and p51 cribs

**18 November 2026. Status: two independently located, reproducible word-pattern observations; neither is authenticated plaintext. The 2012 tapestry source does not supply a five-phase LP2 key or decoder. The first candidate is algebraically incompatible with every additive five-phase key, and a fixed additive key fitted to the second fails original held-out neighbors.**

An original printed ten-rune word at **LP2 p36**, visible in original **53.jpg**, is split only by a **line wrap**:

```text
ᛈᛟᛄᚪᛝᛈ/
ᚦᛈᚪᛝ
```

It has the Gematria transliteration `P OE J A NG P TH P A NG`. The words' five aligned pairs (rune positions 0/5 through 4/9) have equality pattern `[equal, different, different, equal, equal]`. This is the only ten-rune word with **three exact five-step equalities** in the source-matched printed-word census; a shorter, independent eight-rune word on p51 also has three returns. Its exact word is preserved in [the single-file reader](../sims/lp2_p36_lag5_tapestries_conditional_crib_sft.cjs); the line break is ignored while the word boundary is retained.

The extensively discussed **order-five within-word substitution** remains an *unproven cipher hypothesis*. If the same output alphabet recurs at positions five apart within a word, then ciphertext equalities at offset five imply identical **plaintext** GP runes at those positions. Under that conditional rule, the original word has an invariant plaintext letter pattern.

The repository already stores an independent English [29,157-word TextBlob spelling frequency list](../resources/control/english_wordfreq_textblob.txt). Re-encoding all its words in the original Gematria spelling convention (including ING as one rune and the documented alphabet merges), exactly **one** 10-rune English word matches all five equalities/inequalities:

**T A P E S T R I E S** (`TAPESTRIES`, TextBlob source frequency 1).

A larger **CMUdict** comparison, independently calculated locally, produced additional matches including `CONVECTIVE`, `SUPERSAVER`, `SCHERZINGER`, `SRIVASTAVA`, and `VANDEVELDE`; it therefore prevents treating `TAPESTRIES` as the unique possible English plaintext. The input CMUdict is a distinct modern English and proper-name lexicon, and was not part of the fixed repository SFT. Source-specific historical/archaic language could add alternatives.


## Independent second triple-return word on physical p51

The same canonical continuous printed-word parser identifies **exactly two words with at least three equalities at a five-rune displacement**, among **2,896 printed words and 104 word-local lag-five equalities**. There are nine words with at least two such equalities. The first is p36 word index **1967**, as described above. The second is **zero-based word index 2722**, on **physical p51 (original 68.jpg)**, with eight source runes:

```text
ᛋᛞᛝᚷᛚᛋᛞᛝ
S D NG G L S D NG
```

Its first three symbols repeat unchanged at offset five: equality mask **[equal, equal, equal]**. The same frozen 29,157-entry TextBlob list and GP29 spelling rule admit exactly one eight-rune lexical match: **MESDAMES** (frequency 1). This is a second *conditional* word-shape crib, not proof of a five-phase cipher or a French phrase in Liber Primus. Neither rarity nor uniqueness in a restricted lexicon establishes plaintext, and broader vocabularies may supply other matches. This observation is distinct from p36's earlier TAPESTRIES candidate because it is in a different chapter and has a different original length and equality mask.

**Language control rather than lexicon uniqueness:** The independent, source-pinned [Latin Vulgate GP29 whole-word corpus](../resources/control/latin-vulgate-gp-wholewords.txt) has **39,835 words** (7,305 distinct). The p36 shape has **zero** Latin matches. The p51 shape has **two occurrences of one distinct word**, `G14HIG14` in the fixed GP29 base-29 index notation, namely **TURBETUR** (the eight-rune Latin word *turbetur*). It has the same three repeated five-step positions as p51 and satisfies the additive period-five invariant. This supplies a concrete alternate p51 plaintext candidate from another already pinned historical language register; `MESDAMES` was unique **only in the chosen English TextBlob list**. A Latin key fitted to `TURBETUR`, **`[28,22,17,18,2]`**, gives **zero** 24-word exact Vulgate vocabulary matches under either word-reset or continuous original source clocks. Neither Latin nor TextBlob evidence supports a genuine cipher inverse or independent key.

**Stronger all-secret additive invariant at p36.** For any five-period additive GP29 cipher `C[j]=P[j]+K[j mod 5]`, the five differences `C[j+5]−C[j] (mod 29)` must exactly equal the same five differences of the plaintext, **regardless of all five unknown key values**. Original p36 yields **`[0,9,2,0,0]`**; `TAPESTRIES` yields **`[0,9,26,0,0]`**. The only p36 candidate that matches the weaker equality/inequality shape thus **fails the complete additive invariant**. Across both the pinned 29,157-word TextBlob lexicon and the distinct 39,835-word Latin Vulgate corpus, **zero** ten-rune words satisfy all five source p36 differences. This is a direct all-secret exclusion of *that exact period-five additive mechanism for a native-English p36 word*, while leaving arbitrary five-phase substitution permutations and other language material open. The p51 original differences are `[0,0,0]`; `MESDAMES` satisfies them, which is inevitable for any word with its first three letters repeated after five and provides no independent evidence of a key.

**Direct nearby original-cipher check.** Grant the p51 reading `MESDAMES` as a true plaintext and assume the simple additive GP29 clock `C[j]=P[j]+K[j mod 5]`. The eight original runes then determine a complete five-symbol key **`[25,5,6,12,25]`**, without any remaining free offsets on the candidate word. In the 24 other original words at word indices **2707–2731**, both natural extensions fail:

- Reinitialize the same five-symbol clock at each original word: exactly **one** decoded held-out word is in TextBlob, the one-rune `y` at index2723.
- Advance a single clock through all original runes, aligned at the candidate: exactly **two** held-out TextBlob matches, `eau` at index2710 and `i` at index2723. Both are short and cannot sustain a coherent reading.

The source-exact [independent two-word SFT](../sims/lp2_triple_lag5_two_cribs_sft.cjs) re-derives the full word inventory, exact shape counts, all three bilingual dictionary candidates, the p36 all-secret additive obstruction, both p51 period-five keys, original 24-word outputs and positive planted controls.
