## Additional permitted material

A participant may consult original Cicada-authored or Cicada-served challenge artifacts from 2012–2014 that were publicly available by the 2014 release: original messages, images, audio, video, webpages, and source data. The participant must record the exact artifact, stable URL or archive location, date, and hash where possible. If a third-party archive is needed to retrieve an original artifact, use only the preserved primary object; do not use the archive's commentary or interpretation.

## Excluded material

- Community writeups, fan wikis, participant walkthroughs, solver discussions, and retrospective interpretations.
- Derived rune transcriptions, plaintexts, frequency tables, candidate keys, source reconstructions, or other solver-produced datasets.
- This repository's research reports, scripts, Issues, communications, agent memories, or any modern research summary.
- Cicada material first published after the 2014 release period, including later messages and alleged continuations.
- Any source whose date or first-party status cannot be established. Record it as excluded rather than silently using it.

This is a source allowlist, not a claim that the three included files exhaust all historically available first-party context.
