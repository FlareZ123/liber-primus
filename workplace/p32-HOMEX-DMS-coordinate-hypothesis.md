## Observation

The provisional p57→p50→2016→p32 bridge is documented in [the route report](../im-results/lp2-p57-ring-totient-2016-wordmap-compass-bridge.md). Its four selected words are road, words, and, found. The same four compass steps can be read in either natural ring order:

| Ring reading | Word order | p32 route cells, excluding start |
|---|---|---|
| Outside → inside | found, and, words, road | **[34, 23, 35, 37]** |
| Inside → outside | road, words, and, found | **[35, 47, 36, 37]** |

If the four two-digit cell indices are interpreted as degrees, minutes, seconds, and decimal hundredths of seconds, the two sequences give:

- Outside → inside: **34°23′35.37″**
- Inside → outside: **35°47′36.37″**

Reading the first as latitude north and the second as longitude east gives **34°23′35.37″N, 35°47′36.37″E**, or approximately **34.3931583, 35.7934361**.

This is near **Al-Qalamoun, North Lebanon**. The town's commonly listed centre is 34°23′00″N, 35°47′00″E ([Al-Qalamoun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Qalamoun)); the candidate is roughly 1.4 km northeast. A 2017 north-Lebanon marine-bird survey lists point 19 at **34°23′29.76″N, 35°47′30.80″E**, roughly 0.23 km from the candidate ([survey PDF, page 159](https://lsj.cnrs.edu.lb/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/Ghassan-Jaradi.pdf)). The survey covers the coastal strip between Batroun and Cheikh Zennad; this is geographic proximity, not Cicada evidence.

## Why this is not yet a result

1. **No explicit DMS instruction has been found.** The 2016 message's “words are the map … numbers are the direction” makes geographic navigation conceivable, but does not specify degrees/minutes/seconds or the four-component grouping.
2. **The formatting is weakly selective.** All 24 permutations of the four selected words produce four cell indices that can be syntactically read as DMS components. Four permutations share the degree/minute pairs 34°23′ and 35°47′; the two natural ring orders select seconds 35.37 and 36.37, but alternative permutations give 25.37 and 48.37. This is post-hoc parsing, not a unique extraction.
3. The final component 37 is shared because both routes terminate at p32 cell 37/X. The repeated .37 seconds are therefore not independent evidence.
4. The public historical 2012 poster lists I checked contain no Lebanon/Al-Qalamoun location. I found no direct historical Cicada association with this town in this pass. The point could be an unrecorded destination, or an accidental geographic fit.
