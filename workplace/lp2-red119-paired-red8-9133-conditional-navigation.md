## Strongest objections and a concrete negative control

The prior red119 pairing and the matching 3/5 heading shapes are both real original-source properties. Their combination is still a **post-hoc interpretation** with insufficient specificity to identify a cipher. A direct shared-key, stateless-substitution reading would force the two titles to have matching rune equality patterns if they were the same plaintext phrase under one-to-one alphabets with a reused key schedule. They do **not**: p33:91 repeats index12 at positions 2 and 7 (zero-based), whereas p40:0 has distinct indices19 and8 at those positions. Even an arbitrary one-to-one symbol mapping from the entire p33 title to the p40 title cannot exist at those two positions. A changing key by chapter, independent plaintext phrases, or a nonstationary cipher survives.

Other easily proposed historical operations are already source-tested and failed: the literal 2014 period-seven transposition on the **p33–39 seven-page chapter**, including the exact 1,589-rune region starting at its red-eight rubric, with authentic LP2 prime-totient composition, produced no native-GP plaintext. [Prior original inverse](historical-period7-p33-p39-transposition-gate.md). Likewise source-heading-as-key Vigenère on the nine complete titled units was already a direct negative. [Heading key test](lp2-red-heading-self-key-vigenere-gate.md). Do not count the observed 3/5 profile as evidence for yet another unmotivated key search.
