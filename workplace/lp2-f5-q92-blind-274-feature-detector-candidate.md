## Motivation and independent algorithm

The previous Q92 Fridrich–Goljan–Hogea 2002 histogram estimator failed to separate Q85/Q95 positive/clean controls, and the fixed neural F5 model scored original-Q92 clean images and true positives identically. The known-cover DCT shared-stencil method passed real F5 controls but covers only 32/58 manuscript images. Director #736 specifically needs a **blind** method on previously unscorable page art, with no source-image alignment.

A distinct, historically available detection architecture is the **2007 Pevný–Fridrich 274-dimensional merged Markov/DCT JPEG feature detector**, optionally paired with **Kodovský–Fridrich 2009 calibration**. Its extractor derives features from a single JPEG's quantized DCT coefficient arrays and local dependencies; the supervised classifier distinguishes F5 carriers from source-matched clean controls. That is fundamentally different from the FGH-2002 one-dimensional cropped coefficient-histogram payload-size estimator and the pretrained neural classifier. The original researchers publish C++ and Matlab feature extractors at [Binghamton University official source](https://dde.binghamton.edu/download/ccmerged/) with original 2007 and 2009 research references. The publicly offered C++ package is named extract.tar.gz; check its stated use terms before importing, and record exact downloaded source hash.

The feature set predates the 2014 book and detects a historically compatible F5 wire, although our classifier would be a new 2026 calibration rather than an artifact-authenticated Cicada instrument. It cannot identify the correct extraction password on its own.

Preserve original quantization tables, component topology and F5 operation. Split *whole document/source-template families* between training and validation, to exclude learning the drawing rather than embedding. Use the published **274-feature C++ extractor without feature-selection search**.
4. Fit **one** prespecified regularized classifier (a standard SVM, fixed hyperparameters by training-only nested CV) to binary cover/F5 labels; hold aside distinct page/artwork families and each of the three payload-size strata as untouched test cases. Track RGB and grayscale sensitivity separately. The go/no-go requires at least 90% sensitivity at **the smallest 256-byte size** within both independently tested channel classes, with *no more than one* clean false positive among at least 100 strictly held-out matched clean carriers. Without both passes, stop before opening original images.
5. For an eventual 58-page original decision, use a predeclared *familywise max-score* null formed from many independent **clean** Q92 art renders with no shared training or artwork templates and compare all source originals to that fixed extreme-value cutoff. A 5% familywise false-positive claim needs a valid 58-test maximum distribution rather than treating one imagewise 5% score as a book-level positive. If a sufficient null cannot be built, output only exploratory ranks, no positive detection claims.
6. Only after a clean positive-control detection should a flagged original be investigated with a **source-selected F5 password** or genuinely keyless authenticated extraction. A classifier result by itself is no message, key or plaintext.
