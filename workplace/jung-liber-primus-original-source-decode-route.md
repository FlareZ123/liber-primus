## Complete original-source running-key inverse and exact title-key signatures

[Successful Windows original runs 44009228196](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/44009228196), [44009538094](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/44009538094) and [final revised 44009611908](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/actions/runs/44009611908) tested all **940,029** GP29 runes of the Jung PDF as a prospective direct additive running key. Original ciphertext is exactly the intact **729** runes of p0–2. All **939,301** possible 729-rune donor offsets and **both** GP29 inverse signs were evaluated, totaling **1,878,602** source offset/sign configurations. The first 80 unknown runes selected 2,500 offsets via independent Gibran rune-unigram likelihood, and a Gibran GP29 trigram model ranked them; the following **649 untrained original ciphertext runes** form an untouched discrimination set. No per-page clock, arbitrary key edits, reorderings or English word-segmentation changes were fitted.

**Native positive:** a planted genuine 729-GP-rune English passage encrypted using the actual Jung source key at hidden offset **3301**, inverse sign **−1** was recovered within the first 300 ranked candidates by the source’s own positive assertion. Its 649-rune heldout trigram log score is **−2.243771893** and heldout IoC **0.060924689**.

**Original outcome:** the best training-ranked candidate (start **306473**, inverse sign **+1**) shows heldout trigram log score **−4.383368922** and heldout IoC **0.034026708**; the other reported top-five head candidates have heldout IoCs **0.03448, 0.03474, 0.03490, 0.03484** and similarly weak trigram log likelihoods. None produces 649 continuous coherent GP English runes. The original's perfectly reversible additive inverse is arithmetically valid but is not evidence of a correct key.

The final revised source SFT additionally tested **all 940,029 donor positions** for *exact* contiguous candidate title-derived key sequences at the two original red inscriptions, under both additive signs. The fixed conditional cribs \`DIUINITY WITHIN\` at original p0 and \`THE DIUINITY\` at original p33 imply unique respective **13- and 10-rune** GP29 modular-additive key segments, explicitly enumerated in the SFT. Neither proposed plaintext is authenticated. **All four exact key-substring searches returned zero original donor matches**, while a separate planted true donor window passed. This excludes a simpler direct Jung-key use of those particular title cribs, even under a literal-F bypass because the proposed cribs contain no F. The failure remains conditional on the two unverified title plaintext hypotheses.

## Decision

Both independent frozen uses of the whole Jung text are **bounded original-input negatives**. Neither the 10-word-source match, an 80-rune fitted score, nor a title-name or page-number resemblance warrants an invented donor offset, ciphertext phase or new cipher layer. Preserve the completed source scripts and passing Windows Actions as exact reproducibility. Pursue another Jung-source operation only if a new **authenticated 2014 original clue** names the precise text location and reversible consumer, or if independently authentic unknown LP2 plaintext is actually recovered. No source-selected plaintext, key, complete inverse, hash preimage or destination emerged.
