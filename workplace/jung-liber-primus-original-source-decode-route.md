## Why this specific independent source?

C. G. Jung's *The Red Book: Liber Novus* (first published 2009; English Reader's Edition 2012) contains a named section **Liber Primus** before the independent 2014 Cicada manuscript of the same title. The section explicitly describes *meaning* through a *way* or *path*, says *the way is within us*, and discusses boundaries/circumference. These prepublication themes resemble the already-solved 2013/2014 Cicada verse about shedding one's circumferences and finding divinity within and the signed 2016 message about meaning as a road. The similarities may be generic spiritual literature; no original Cicada message points expressly to Jung, an edition, a book-code coordinate, a key length, or a word offset. The selection is independent of unknown ciphertext and does not imply authorship or direct borrowing.

The frozen text source is the [English *Red Book* PDF](https://logoilibrary.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/The-Red-Book-Jung.pdf), consumed **in memory** on manual Windows CI. Report hashes and source-length metadata; do not store or redistribute the copyrighted book. Earlier [complete exact-quote searches](../im-results/lp2-original-quote-native-wordlength-book-source-gate.md) examined 53,000 Gutenberg text records and 3.3 billion words without identifying an LP2 quote; that corpus does not cover this 2009 Jung work. Earlier [five-source whole-book tests](../im-results/lp2-wordlength-frozen-five-source-contiguous-retrieval.md) excluded Emerson, Shakespeare, Gibran, Beowulf and the Vulgate under unchanged GP word breaks. This is a newly chosen historical source with a title directly overlapping the puzzle name, not a repetition of their source datasets.

It searches **every uninterrupted seven-word original-source GP word-length window**, extending exact matches as far as possible within the original visual chapter. It includes an authentic 25-word Jung-source planted positive and 120 exact nine-chapter histogram-preserving null shuffles to measure selection effects. A **14+ consecutive-word** exact source-native signature, especially inside a predeclared visibly marked quotation, is a reason to freeze the candidate as possible original plaintext and attempt an exact ciphertext key inverse; it is not itself proof of decryption. **Short 7–11-word matches are expected** for generic English corpora and should not be promoted.

## Test B: can all actual Jung runes supply a running Vigenère key?

Cicada 2014 **already used GP29 Vigenère** on independently solved LP1 pages. The older rounds also used indexed external texts or media as code operands. The specific *source-text as a continuous running key* transfer to LP2 is an unverified hypothesis; this experiment tests its simplest repeat-free direct reversible implementation without adding an invented offset or cipher layer.

The [complete candidate-source running-key inverse](../sims/lp2_jung_liberprimus_runningkey_sft.py) reads **all 729 unmodified GP29 ciphertext runes of physical pp0–2**, concatenated exactly, and every source GP29 rune from the independently selected Jung PDF. It evaluates **every possible 729-rune Jung start offset, both additive inverse signs, and a continuous source-key clock**, without a variable F bypass. For each, source-independent Gibran GP29 unigram likelihood screens the first 80 original ciphertext runes. The best 2,500 candidates are rescored with a GP29 trigram model from the pinned Gibran corpus. The **remaining original 649 runes** are frozen holdout and never used to pick the source offset or arithmetic sign. Native source positivity is tested by planting genuine **729 GP runes of source Gibran English** under the real unmodified Jung key starting at hidden rune offset 3301; the blind search must recover the exact true offset/sign among its top 300 and show credible held-out linguistic score.

A **second, stronger, deterministic crib-to-key prerequisite** reuses the independently 2013-published poem's two matching red-title word shapes. The original p0 proposed plaintext `DIUINITY WITHIN` uniquely implies the 13-rune GP29 key `[21,27,17,22,26,9,11,3,8,16,16,11,25]`. The source-original p33 candidate `THE DIUINITY` uniquely implies the ten-rune GP29 key `[21,23,20,24,28,0,15,26,21,9]`. Neither title candidate is an authenticated recovered plaintext (their simple periodic continuation fails as separately recorded). Both candidate plaintexts contain **no GP F**, so a true book-based running Vigenère key must contain *exactly one of those contiguous inferred keys or its additive negation* at the selected offset, even under historically attested literal-F transparency. The script checks all **four** exact GP29 substrings against **every source rune position**, without guessing English or source offsets. Any match would immediately freeze a source-key offset for body testing; zero hits would be an exact exclusion **conditional on these two title-plaintext cribs and the direct running-key model**, independent of heldout language statistics. A distinct genuine source-window planted positive asserts the exact-substring finder works. This targeted source selector is much cheaper and more discriminating than optimizing arbitrary cipher parameters.

A valid unknown-source decryption would require a **sustained original 649-rune GP-language holdout**, a single frozen source offset and sign, and a full exact reencryption of the 729 original runes. A high first80 score alone is overfitting. Failure rules out only this exact non-F-transparent, no-resegmentation, untransposed direct GP29 running-key family and the reader's-edition text; it does not exclude Jung as another kind of key, Jungian paraphrase, a different spelling edition or a direct plaintext source after changing the printed word boundaries.
