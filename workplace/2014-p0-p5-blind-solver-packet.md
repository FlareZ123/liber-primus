**Purpose.** Give an independent puzzle solver only data a historically informed participant could have seen before interpreting the remaining LP2. A plausible sounding English phrase is insufficient unless ciphertext positions and a reversible decoding procedure are selected before reading that phrase.

## Locked solve task and decision

First inspect the six original images without looking up any modern LP2 analysis. State the **exact pixels/runes or historical original sentence** that uniquely points to an operation. Write the operation as reversible steps, giving its input scan, starting ciphertext position, alphabet, key/operand source, clock/resets, and at least 20 consecutive plaintext rune predictions. If the proposed decipherment jumps across the image, enumerate all visited coordinates and defend the selection independently of whether they spell English. Name one held-out original stretch and predict how the identical operation acts there before opening it. An operator that requires choosing free key cells or reading paths based on the desired English text does not pass this criterion.

If no original clue selects a cipher or external operand, the correct result is **underdetermined after image inspection**. Record the exact first choice the manuscript leaves open. This records an honest prerequisite for source-based continuation rather than manufacturing a plausible plaintext.
