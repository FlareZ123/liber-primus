# Cicada design prior: observable operations, inferred parameters and a usable LP2 decision

**Current decision, 16 November 2026 (Agent20, Director #1235).** Historical Cicada stages support a *bounded, observable-output* solve prior. They do **not** establish that every successful cipher, key phase or transposition was completely prescribed before the solver saw the output, nor that all puzzle stages were short or single-layer. The strong recurring pattern is an identifiable original input and operation family followed by an unusually decisive complete output, such as a signed message, a working destination, a valid image/file, or a solved, sustained paragraph. The unsolved LP2 pages have yet to supply a comparable independent output from any proposed new inverse.

This is an adversarial historical *research prior*, **not** an LP2 decipherment or a claim about who authored it. It updates the narrower working hypotheses formerly recorded in this note and is meant to select the next experimental decision. Use the [authenticated player-path operation contract](historical-player-solvepath-operator-contract.md), [original-round historical chronology](historical-solve-path-to-original-lp2.md) and [current established LP2 status](../results/README.md) for input provenance and exact reproductions. Material below distinguishes signed first-party words from preserved artifacts, contemporaneous participant witnesses and retrospective solve reconstruction.

## Adversarial test of the three design hypotheses

**H1, output checkability (moderate-to-high confidence as an operational heuristic).** Every independently successful row in the table has a *typed full output*: valid source image, identifiable document, coherent long text, signed payload, actual address or exact numeric artifact. This is selection-biased by construction since successful stages are more likely to be preserved. A 2–4-rune English coincidence, a manually fitted root, a low-entropy suffix, or a bijective round trip is substantially weaker. **LP2 consequence:** require an independently checkable complete original text or typed binary structure and a withheld continuation. This heuristic survives the adversarial counterexamples.

**H2, entire marked source units (moderate confidence, not a universal rule).** The 761 MP3 title, 13 replaced word entries in the 25-cell 1033 square, three equal XOR shares and explicit book-code coordinate units make natural complete-unit selection plausible. Counterexamples include explicit **partial** bytes/slices in the 2013 data XOR and individual note events/indices inside MIDI. Such partial selection can be legitimate **when a first-party coordinate or local grammar chooses the slice**. A proposed LP2 76-rune perimeter or 52-rune inner ring is consequently no stronger than its missing selector/consumer.

**H3, semantically motivated short operations (low confidence as a universal bound; moderate confidence as an early search preference).** The 2012 Caesar and direct 2013 mathematical commands are short and legible. The 2013 location/phone/share pipeline, 2014 multi-carrier XOR/transposition and OpenPuff/book-code stages are genuinely layered and external-material-dependent. Crucially, the fourteen-column key and p56 prime/F mask were inferred against complete recognizable output even though the full parameters were never authored as plain instructions. A strict rule requiring the *entire* inverse to be preselected by a clue would have rejected genuine historical solves. The appropriate control is **fixed small-family inference plus decisive independent output**, ideally with untouched later text or a server response; hidden unbounded key tuning remains unwarranted.

**Decoys and creator identity (low confidence, keep separate).** The signed 2017 and 2016 warnings to verify signatures support suspicion of fake *messages*. They do not demonstrate that particular LP2 drawing, word, page or source-cipher match is an intentional planted false lead. Different media and tools provide no reliable inference about whether there was one author or a group. The survival of only solved stages strongly biases any inferred author skill or layer count.

The current LP2 frontier has **zero surviving candidate that simultaneously specifies an original-page input, a reversible value-changing cipher, a complete key/clock/exception grammar and an independent plaintext/typed-output check.** The three materially different, human-visible routes with the smallest causal gaps are:

### Contract for a new attack

A new candidate can proceed through **one of two evidentiary gates**:

1. **Source-complete:** an original 2014-available or signed later clue specifies a complete reversible operand, value transformation and key/reset clock. Apply it to the untouched 2014 original source and test an independent chapter.
2. **Source-constrained output inference:** the original clue specifies the *operation family and finite search dimensions* but leaves a genuinely small parameter ambiguity, as in the real p56 and 14-column historical solves. Freeze the choices before decrypting, recover one sustained original passage (preferably **150+ contiguous GP29 runes**) or an externally verifiable typed object, then predict a disjoint held-out original page without refitting. Forward reencryption is necessary to show implementation correctness but is nonprobative for key identity when the mapping is bijective.

An unsourced state/key search lacking both gates is a low-value detour. The **current outcome of #1235 is that no original unknown-page LP2 clue yet meets either gate with a genuine positive output**. This conclusion is narrower than a universal statement about Cicada's design or the possible original solution; it directly deprioritizes modern post-fitted numeric and arbitrary layered variants while preserving ways a historical player could legitimately discover a partly inferred decryption.

**No new unknown LP2 plaintext, verified author key or complete cipher is claimed.** The only authenticated LP2 cipher positive remains p56; p0–55 retain 56 unresolved physical pages.
