## Three falsifiable working design hypotheses

**H1. A successful stage transition normally has an independently recognizable *output type*.** In the controls above, an exact GP sum equals a named file, a complete magic square appears, a valid JPEG boundary becomes available, signed text verifies or an onion host is reconstructed. An apparently successful arbitrary 2–4-rune English fragment, low IoC increase or one polynomial root cannot provide the same confirmation. *Falsifier:* an authenticated Cicada stage whose necessary next move is recoverable only by an unconstrained, non-self-checking numeric fit. **LP2 decision:** treat the p50 256-token byte table as unclassified until a specific operation produces a typed result, such as original text, byte container or author-supplied verification relation; do not elevate recurrent p50 polynomial/11-cycle coincidences into a decipherment.

**H2. Numeric constraints are often strong because the original *entire artifact* participates.** The 2013 title-to-761 and 13+12=25-cell square use complete, visibly delimited source spans. This is a narrower habit than guessing that all printed numbers are page pointers: no historical example above instructs applying prime ordinal to arbitrary manuscript word lengths. *Falsifier:* a separately attested source deliberately selects an arbitrary internal partial span without a local marker, coordinate grammar or explicit intermediate selection. **LP2 decision:** rank exact source-marked complete units and highly redundant held-out outputs ahead of freely trimmed/resegmented 43/76/121-rune equalities.

**H3. Semantically related artwork may identify a *text source* without specifying a cipher.** The original 2013 `761.MP3` *Instar* title and solved LP2 p57 *PARABLE* are genuinely related; p14 has mayfly-like art and p8–14 have trees. Such associations give a finite candidate source to test, but do not specify an offset, key schedule or transformation. The historically solved book ciphers supply their numerical coordinates **separately** from their thematic book riddles. *Falsifier:* a period-authenticated instance where the picture alone, without any separate grammar or rule, uniquely establishes the complete reversible text operation. **LP2 decision:** regard the p14 insect as a motif with a known 2013 source echo, then stop. An exact pre-2014 copied image plus a source-visible indexing or transformation instruction is needed before reviving it. See [the original p14 source gate](../im-results/lp2-botanical-marginalia-template-gate.md).

## Claimed decoys and authorship: what is unproved

The tested sources support ordinary tool false positives, for example keyed OutGuess returning plausible noise on unencoded JPEGs. They do **not** authenticate a deliberately planted "red herring" by Cicada in those images. The 2016 signed image and 2013 forest message could invite a theme-first route through LP2, but no source establishes that their visual overlap is an intentional decoy. The 2014 participant-fitted transposition directions and later 2016 base58 word map are reconstructions and hypotheses; neither may be counted as an authored instruction without additional primary evidence.

Nothing about the available mechanisms requires a single author or a group. Different media and tools reflect technical competence and available period software, which is not a reliable signature of personal identity, organization size, ideological affiliation, or motive. Do not infer identities from cipher preferences.
