# Cicada design prior: source-selected operations, inferred parameters, and current LP2 routes

**Current decision — 19 November 2026 (Agent31, Director Issue #1910).** Historical Cicada evidence supports a human-solvable path in which a visible clue selects an input, operation family, or external receiver; some nontrivial parameters may then be inferred from a recognizable output. It does **not** support a universal “one simple cipher” rule, nor does it require every parameter to be printed in advance. Conversely, a long English-looking output, exact round trip, or attractive numerical closure is not enough when the operation/receiver family was chosen after seeing that output.

This is a cautious research prior, not a claim about creator identity, number of authors, deliberate decoys, or a solution to LP2. The detailed [player-visible operator contract](historical-player-solvepath-operator-contract.md), [historical-to-LP2 chronology](historical-solve-path-to-original-lp2.md), [current authoritative LP2 synthesis](../results/README.md), and [external-research index](../resources/external/dukotah-cicada3301-research-index.md) contain the underlying source records and reproductions.

## Evidence labels and custody rules

- **First-party signed:** the preserved bytes verify under the stored Cicada OpenPGP key. This authenticates the signed bytes relative to that key; GnuPG reports unknown owner trust, so it is not independent proof of the signer's real-world identity.
- **Original artifact:** a period image, binary, or source object whose bytes are preserved and checked. A modern archive commit dates the surviving copy, not necessarily the original puzzle event.
- **Contemporaneous participant witness:** dated IRC, email, forum, or solver notes from the period. It can establish what a participant saw or did, but does not automatically authenticate the server, source code, or participant identity.
- **Participant reconstruction:** a successful solve account or archived decoder. It may be correct and useful without being a Cicada-authored instruction.
- **Analyst inference:** a derived operation, parameter, or relationship not fixed by the preceding source. It must be labeled as such even if its arithmetic is exact.

The evidence set is survivorship-biased: successful and interesting stages are much more likely to have been archived than failed attempts. It is not a random sample of all Cicada stages, and it cannot establish statistical frequencies of the authors' design habits. Do not infer author identity or intentional decoys from the preserved examples.

## Revised design prior

1. **Causal selection outranks simplicity.** Prefer a human-visible clue that identifies an input, operation family, operand, or receiver. Do not require every parameter to be printed: the 14-column order and p56 prime/F rule show that a solver can infer parameters from output. But every inferred parameter must belong to a small, frozen family motivated by the clue.
2. **Output inference is legitimate only with independent validation.** Require the complete original input, exact reversible method, complete typed output (signed payload, valid image, real destination, or sustained coherent passage), authentic positive controls, matched nulls, and an untouched holdout. Forward re-encryption proves implementation consistency, not key identity. A few English runes, a checksum noticed after a word, or a bijective round trip is not enough.
3. **Layering and external dependencies are plausible.** The 2013 phone/ISO/share route and 2014 GZIP/OutGuess/OpenPuff/book-code stages disprove a universal short/single-layer prior. Keep the search family finite at each layer; every additional layer needs its own source clue or a discriminating output/holdout.
4. **Whole units are a preference, not a law.** Full images, equal-length shares, whole books and complete printed words are common natural inputs. But MIDI note events, byte offsets and book coordinates show that partial slices are legitimate when a source grammar fixes them. An LP2 perimeter, fragment union, or selected diagonal is not justified merely because it forms a neat unit after inspection.
5. **Instructions can be genuine progress without being page plaintext.** The p23 five-dot/totient work produces two source-constrained instruction candidates, THE ORIGINAL and THE ONE RISE; p28 yields READ OUT and the continuation THEIR NUMBERS ARE THE DIRECTION; p22/p32 add a square/branch navigation route. These are more causally constrained than an arbitrary English fit, but remain analyst reconstructions and do not decrypt a full unknown page. Conversely, the modern 27×27 p0–p2 claim reports 118 selected runes and an ending DIE, yet its later transition family was selected retrospectively from thousands of predicates. The final DIE block reproduces exactly on original corpus values (source C/K–D–IA, aligned key D–IA–C/K, ciphertext B–H–D, plaintext D–I–E); this confirms one three-rune inverse, not the route or endpoint. See the [p23 instruction reconciliation](../im-results/lp2-p23-instruction-reconciliation.md), [p28 route synthesis](../results/README.md), [post-cutoff external audit](../resources/external/dukotah-cicada3301-research-index.md), and [local 27×27 holdout](../im-results/lp2-2026-square27-frozen-geometric-plaintext-holdout.md).
6. **Survivorship and attribution remain limits.** Preserved successful solves cannot estimate how often authors used a particular method. A valid signature is not real-world identity proof; participant logs are not original server code; a modern walkthrough is not first-party instruction. Warnings about fake signatures do not prove that a particular LP2 image or word is an intentional decoy. Do not infer one creator versus a group, technical background, motive, or deliberate misdirection from this sample.

## At most three next experiments

These are bounded causal tests, not invitations to reopen closed parameter sweeps.

1. **Blind p28 body continuation under the AL31 exponent schedule.** Human-visible chain: p23 yields THE ORIGINAL | AL; the pre-2014 Liber AL III:47/New Comment tradition gives AL=31; p28's literal DH line and the solved-p57 FIND/DIVINITY selector fix the H/cube family; the candidate schedule is [3,27]. Receiver: one untouched p28 body span or a precommitted p0 continuation outside the READ OUT packet. Strongest rival: AL31 was connected after READ OUT, and the two-part schedule was selected after the output. Decisive test: freeze the exact operation, offsets and controls before opening ciphertext; require a sustained readable segment and exact forward re-encryption. No new split/orientation search.
2. **Find a source-selected consumer for the p32 terminal state.** Human-visible chain: p32's source-constrained physical branch fixes offsets93–97 and fragment15; the HOME cell selects p50[48]=4D→hex77; the authenticated MEANING/FALSE collision at positions20/39 gives H(20,39)=[10,1]=[K*g,a−g]. Candidate operation: use this fixed two-rune state pair in a source-specified key update or receiver selector. Receiver: one precommitted original p32/p0 word or packet, not a searched window. Strongest rival: [10,1] may be only a source-register checksum, not a cipher key; no consumer is currently selected. Decisive test: recover a source clue that maps [10,1] to one exact receiver and operation, then obtain a blind complete word and exact forward reconstruction.
3. **Derive one common source-selected key scheduler for the p0 ROAD/AND/THEIR/NUMBERS packets.** Human-visible chain: the signed 2016 “THE ROAD, AND THEIR NUMBERS ARE THE DIRECTION” phrase; GP0/p50 addresses and the p28 DH state select the adjacent [3,4] receiver and the distant seven-rune NUMBERS receiver. Candidate operation: one finite DH/H state-to-key rule that produces both AND and THEIR keys and distinguishes THEIR from the competing PATHS inverse. Strongest rival: the current keys are target-aware formulas, and the OPENPGP key fails the independent ROAD/NUMBERS holdout. Decisive test: derive the rule from source values and fixed word boundaries alone, before opening a new receiver; if it cannot predict a fresh authenticated-target hit, retire the shared-key hypothesis.

The exact Wikidot lead from the gHOST3301 interview and the missing 965-byte Stage10 CGI remain conditional archival dependencies, not current cipher tasks. Resume them only if an archive-capable browser, period capture, independent mirror, or operator backup becomes available.

**No broad cipher sweep is justified by the current evidence.** The revised prior relaxes an unjustified simplicity constraint, but it does not relax source selection, reproducibility, or independent plaintext validation.
