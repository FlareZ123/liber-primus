3. A metaphor, imperative, or deictic reference without such typing is **clue/semantic**. It may cue an operation to a human, but the rule must abstain from its exact identification. A bounded adjacent data object remains data; do not supply a cipher based on proximity alone.
4. Formulaic sign-off (Good luck followed by 3301) at an actual signed-body terminus is **confirmation/sign-off**, not a computational operand; identical strings elsewhere have no automatically inherited role.

Rival A: any imperative or operation-like noun selects a computational operation, without requiring typed inputs.
