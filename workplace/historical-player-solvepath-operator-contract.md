## Next decision and what is actually missing

A human could reasonably attempt the recognized **word prime-sums** and the original **p32 tree map**. The independent success check is strong for reconstructing a table (2013 words→2014 5-by-5 grid) or locating a page (2016 image dimensions→p32), while neither gives a procedure to recover a single unknown LP2 rune.

After checking the documented 2012–2016 sources and existing exact inverses, **no genuinely new, executable, reversible decoder route is identified**. Creating a cipher with free start coordinates, rotating compass layouts or unbounded word-to-byte reductions would repeat eliminated postselection. The two specific missing inputs are:

1. **A source-original p32 movement instruction:** whether the number source is the 2013 prime sequence or p32 rune weights; which marked cell starts; how successive gaps become motion (including why to divide by two and use 0=N clockwise); how boundaries advance; where and why the path stops; and how X selects an actual keyed inverse.
2. **A source-original table transport contract:** the intended 32-by-8 numeral interpretation, a specified byte-to-GP mapping/clock, and a fixed key or a way to obtain it.

A newly recovered source clue satisfying either input should be tested on untouched original ciphertext with a **frozen** first step and then a held-out coherent plaintext prediction, including exact reencryption. Historical source hunting should be proportional to its chance of supplying those missing instructions. This assignment identifies historical operation gates, and closes the unsupported branches without multiplying speculative cipher menus.
